Having regard to the above application lodged on 30 January 2014,
Having deliberated, decides as follows:

FACTS AND PROCEDURE

The applicants, Mr Farhad Aydin oglu Valiyev and Mr Kamran Aliyev, 
Azerbaijani nationals living in Baku, were represented by Mr I. Aliyev, a 
lawyer based in Azerbaijan.

The applicants’ complaints under Article 11 of the Convention 
concerning their freedom of association were communicated to the 
Azerbaijani Government (“the Government”) 11 October 2016.

On 26 July 2019 the applicants informed the Registry that they wanted to 
withdraw the application to the Court since their association had been 
registered by the authorities.

THE LAW

In the light of the foregoing, the Court concludes that the applicants may 
be 
regarded 
as 
no 
longer 
wishing 
to 
pursue 
the 
application 
(Article 37 § 1 (a) of the Convention). Furthermore, in accordance with 
Article 37 § 1 in fine, the Court finds no special circumstances regarding
