The European Court of Human Rights (Third Section), sitting on

Peeter Roosma, President,

Andreas Zünd, judges,
and Viktoriya Maradudina, Acting Deputy Section Registrar,

Having regard to the above applications lodged on the various dates 
indicated in the appended table,

Having deliberated, decides as follows:

FACTS AND PROCEDURE

The list of applicants is provided in the appendix.
The applicants’ complaints under Articles 10 and 11 of the Convention, 
concerning their convictions for participating in the activities of “undesirable 
organisations”, were communicated to the Russian Government.

By letters sent on 2 June and 26 July 2023, through the Court’s Electronic 
Communication Service (eComms), the applicants were informed that the 
period allowed for submitting their observations had expired on 26 May 2023, 
and that no time extension had been requested. The applicants’ attention was 
drawn to Article 37 § 1 (a) of the Convention, which provides that the Court 
may strike an application out of its list of cases where the circumstances lead 
to the conclusion that the applicant does not intend to pursue the application. 
However, no response has been received to date.

THE LAW

In the light of the foregoing, and in the absence of any special 
circumstances regarding respect for the rights guaranteed by the Convention

and the Protocols thereto, the Court, in accordance with Article 37 § 1 (a) of

Decides to join the applications;

Decides to strike the applications out of its list of cases.
