Having regard to the above application lodged on 24 September 2014,
Having deliberated, decides as follows:

FACTS AND PROCEDURE

The applicants, Mr Teymur Alakbar oglu Mirzayev, living in Barda, and 
Mr Khalid Vahid oglu Kazimov, living in Baku, were represented by 
Mr Y. Agazade, a lawyer based in Azerbaijan.

The applicants’ complaints under Article 11 of the Convention 
concerning their freedom of association were communicated to the 
Azerbaijani Government (“the Government”) on 11 October 2016.

On 2 August 2019 the applicants informed the Registry that they wanted 
to withdraw the application to the Court since their association had been 
registered by the authorities.

THE LAW

In the light of the foregoing, the Court concludes that the applicants may 
be 
regarded 
as 
no 
longer 
wishing 
to 
pursue 
the 
application 
(Article 37 § 1 (a) of the Convention). Furthermore, in accordance with 
Article 37 § 1 in fine, the Court finds no special circumstances regarding
