THIRD SECTION

DECISION

Application no. 25934/20
Timur Orkhanovich KARAYAKA

against Russia

The European Court of Human Rights (Third Section), sitting on 
9 September 2021 as a Committee composed of:

Darian Pavli, President,
Dmitry Dedov,
Peeter Roosma, judges,
and Viktoriya Maradudina, Acting Deputy Section Registrar,

Having regard to the above application lodged on 22 June 2020,
Having deliberated, decides as follows:

FACTS AND PROCEDURE

The applicant, Mr Timur Orkhanovich Karayaka, was born in 2000.
The applicant was represented by lawyers of the Memorial Human 
Rights Centre, a non-governmental organisation based in Moscow.

The applicant’s complaints under Article 5 § 1, Article 6 §§ 1 and 3 (d) 
and Article 11 of the Convention were communicated to the Russian 
Government (“the Government”).

On 29 January 2021 the applicant informed the Registry that he wanted 
to withdraw the application and discontinue the proceedings before the 
Court.

THE LAW

In the light of the foregoing, the Court concludes that the applicant may 
be 
regarded 
as 
no 
longer 
wishing 
to 
pursue 
the 
application 
(Article 37 § 1 (a) of the Convention). Furthermore, in accordance with 
Article 37 § 1 in fine, the Court finds no special circumstances regarding 
respect for human rights as defined in the Convention and the Protocols 
thereto which require the continued examination of the application.


========================================

KARAYAKA v. RUSSIA DECISION

2

Accordingly, the case should be struck out of the list.

For these reasons, the Court, unanimously,

Decides to strike the application out of its list of cases.
