Having regard to the above application lodged on 19 August 2013,
Having deliberated, decides as follows:

FACTS AND PROCEDURE

The applicants, Ms Gunel Bakhtiyar gizi Bashirli and Mr Rashad Ali 
oglu Bashirli, Azerbaijani nationals living in Shirvan, were represented by 
Mr I. Aliyev, a lawyer based in Azerbaijan.

The applicants’ complaints under Article 11 of the Convention 
concerning their freedom of association were communicated to the 
Azerbaijani Government (“the Government”) on 26 January 2017.

On 26 August 2019 the applicants informed the Registry that they 
wanted to withdraw the application to the Court since their association had 
been registered by the authorities.

THE LAW

In the light of the foregoing, the Court concludes that the applicants may 
be 
regarded 
as 
no 
longer 
wishing 
to 
pursue 
the 
application 
(Article 37 § 1 (a) of the Convention). Furthermore, in accordance with 
Article 37 § 1 in fine, the Court finds no special circumstances regarding
