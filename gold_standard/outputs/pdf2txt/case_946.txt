Having regard to the above applications lodged on the various dates

Having deliberated, decides as follows:

FACTS AND PROCEDURE

1.  A list of the applicants is set out in the appendix.
2.  The Azerbaijani Government (“the Government”) were represented 
by their Agent, Mr Ç. Asgarov.

3.  The applicants complained under Article 11 of the Convention that 
the refusals by the State authorities to register associations which they had 
founded had amounted to a violation of their right to freedom of association. 
They also complained that the authorities had seized the case files relating 
to their applications from the office of Mr I. Aliyev, their representative, 
infringing their right of individual application under Article 34 of the 
Convention. In addition, the applicants in applications no. 34151/14 and 
51860/14 further complained under Article 6 of the Convention that they 
had not been notified of the Supreme Court hearings in the civil proceedings 
they had instituted in connection with the refusals to register their 
associations.

4.  The applicants’ complaints detailed above were communicated to the 
Government.

5.  On 17 February 2017 the applicants in application no. 51860/14 
informed the Court that they wanted to withdraw the application since the 
matter had been resolved and the association they had founded had been 
registered. On 27 April 2017 they further informed the Court that they no 
longer wished to be represented by Mr I. Aliyev.

6.  On 20 February 2017 the applicants in applications nos. 59591/13 and 
34151/14 also informed the Court that they wanted to withdraw the 
applications since the matter had been resolved because the associations 
they had founded had been registered. On 28 April 2017 they further 
informed the Court that they no longer wished to be represented by 
Mr I. Aliyev.

7.  In a separate letter, Mr I. Aliyev confirmed that the applicants in the 
above-mentioned applications wanted to withdraw their applications.

THE LAW

8.  The Court considers that, in these circumstances, the applicants may 
be regarded as no longer wishing to pursue their application, within the 
meaning of Article 37 § 1 (a) of the Convention. Furthermore, in accordance 
with Article 37 § 1 in fine, the Court finds no special circumstances 
regarding respect for human rights as defined in the Convention and its 
Protocols which require the continued examination of the case.

Accordingly, the cases should be struck out of the list.

For these reasons, the Court, unanimously,

Decides to join the applications;

Decides to strike the applications out of its list of cases.

Done in English and notified in writing on 11 January 2018.
