Having regard to the above application lodged on 8 May 2013,
Having deliberated, decides as follows:

FACTS AND PROCEDURE

The applicants, Mr Alasgar Jalal oglu Huseynov and Ms Aytakin 
Mehman gizi Abuzarova, are Azerbaijani nationals who were born in 1957 
and 1980 respectively. They were represented by Mr I. Aliyev, a lawyer 
based in Azerbaijan.

The applicants’ complaints under Article 11 of the Convention 
concerning the freedom of association as well as under Article 34 
concerning the right of individual application without hindrance were 
communicated to the Azerbaijani Government (“the Government”).

On 10 October 2019 the applicants informed the Registry that they 
wanted to withdraw the application to the Court since their association had 
been registered by the authorities.
