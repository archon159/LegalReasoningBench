Having deliberated, decides as follows:

FACTS AND PROCEDURE

The first applicant, Mr Mubariz Arshad oglu Tagiyev, was an 
Azerbaijani national who was born in 1965. The second applicant, 
Mr Sayyad Shirin oglu Mammadov, is an Azerbaijani national who was 
born in 1967. They were represented by Mr I. Aliyev, a lawyer based in 
Azerbaijan.

The applicants’ complaints under Article 11 of the Convention 
concerning the freedom of association as well as under Article 34 
concerning the right of individual application without hindrance were 
communicated to the Azerbaijani Government (“the Government”).

On 10 October 2019 the first applicant’s wife, Ms Tagiyeva Vusala 
Safarali gizi, informed the Registry that the first applicant had died and that 
his application to the Court would not be pursued. On the same date the 
second applicant informed the Registry that he wanted to withdraw the 
application to the Court since the association in question had been registered 
by the authorities.


========================================

TAGIYEV AND MAMMADOV v. AZERBAIJAN DECISION

2

THE LAW

In the light of the foregoing, the Court concludes that the first applicant’s 
heir and the second applicant may be regarded as no longer wishing to 
pursue the application (Article 37 § 1 (a) of the Convention). Furthermore, 
in accordance with Article 37 § 1 in fine, the Court finds no special 
circumstances regarding respect for human rights as defined in the 
Convention and the Protocols thereto which require the continued 
examination of the application.

Accordingly, the case should be struck out of the list.

For these reasons, the Court, unanimously,

Decides to strike the application out of its list of cases.

Done in English and notified in writing on 24 September 2020.
