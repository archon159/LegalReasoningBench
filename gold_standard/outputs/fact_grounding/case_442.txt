Here is the analysis of the factual grounding for each element the Court discussed or implied:

---

**(a) Scope**

- *Assembly exists*: The only factual grounding is the phrase "public assemblies" and "dispersal of these assemblies" from the opening paragraph: "disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies, namely their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies." The Court does not elaborate on the nature, size, location, or purpose of these assemblies.

- *Participation*: The only factual grounding is the phrase "participants of public assemblies" from the same sentence. No further facts are provided.

- *Peaceful*: **No factual grounding.** The Court does not state any facts establishing that the assemblies were peaceful. This is implied structurally but rests on no explicitly stated facts in this reasoning section.

---

**(b) Interference**

The factual grounding is explicit and comes from the opening paragraph: "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies, and their conviction for administrative offences." These are the two concrete governmental acts identified as constituting the interference.

---

**(e) Necessity in a democratic society**

**No specific facts are stated.** The Court's conclusion rests entirely on reference to prior case law ("Having examined all the material submitted to it, the Court has not found any fact or argument capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion") and the leading cases of *Frumkin*, *Navalnyy and Yashin*, and *Kasparov*. The Court does not identify any specific facts from the present case that ground its necessity assessment beyond the bare description of the interference noted under (b) above.

---

**Overall observation:** The reasoning is strikingly thin on factual grounding. The Court relies almost entirely on the repetition of prior case law rather than on case-specific facts. The only facts explicitly stated in the reasoning are (i) that the applicants participated in public assemblies, (ii) that those assemblies were dispersed, (iii) that the applicants were arrested, and (iv) that they were convicted of administrative offences. No further facts are provided to ground any of the canonical elements.
