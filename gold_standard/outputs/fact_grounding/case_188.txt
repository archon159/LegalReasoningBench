**Factual grounding for each element discussed:**

**(a) Scope (implied)**

The only factual grounding available in the text is the following phrase:

> "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences"

And:

> "participants of public assemblies"

These are the only facts the Court relies upon (or implies reliance upon) to establish that assemblies existed and that the applicants participated. There is no factual grounding for the peacefulness sub-element — the Court implies it without grounding it in any stated fact.

**(b) Interference (explicit)**

The factual grounding is the same passage as above:

> "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences"

These are the concrete governmental acts identified as constituting the interference. No further factual detail is provided in this reasoning section.

**(c) Prescribed by law**

Not discussed. No factual grounding to identify.

**(d) Legitimate aim**

Not discussed. No factual grounding to identify.

**(e) Necessity in a democratic society**

This is the central finding, yet the reasoning text provides **no independent factual grounding** for the necessity assessment within this section. The Court states:

> "Having examined all the material submitted to it, the Court has not found any fact or argument capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion as to the admissibility and merits of these complaints."

This sentence acknowledges that material was examined, but does not identify or quote any specific facts drawn from that material. The Court's conclusion rests entirely on a reference to prior case-law:

> "In the leading cases of Frumkin v. Russia, no. 74568/12, ECHR 2016 (extracts), Navalnyy and Yashin v. Russia, no. 76204/11, 4 December 2014, and Kasparov and Others v. Russia, no. 21613/07, 3 October 2013, the Court already found a violation in respect of issues similar to those in the present case."

So the necessity finding is grounded in the similarity to prior cases, not in any independently stated facts about the present case.

Not discussed. No factual grounding to identify.
