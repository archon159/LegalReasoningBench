## Factual Grounding for Each Doctrinal Element

---

### (a) Scope

**i) Assembly exists**
The Court does not explicitly state facts establishing the existence of an assembly. The closest factual grounding is implied by the Court's reference to "the demonstration" in the interference paragraph: *"the non-acquiescence by the police in the demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of the applicant's rights under that provision."* The word "demonstration" is used as a given, not established through explicit factual findings in this section.

**ii) Participation**
Same passage as above: *"the non-acquiescence by the police in the demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of the applicant's rights under that provision."* The Court treats the applicant's attempt to participate as a given, but does not separately establish it through explicit factual findings in this section.

**iii) Peaceful**
No factual grounding identifiable in the reasoning text. The Court does not make any factual finding on this point.

---

### (b) Interference

The Court grounds its finding of interference in the following facts:

*"the non-acquiescence by the police in the demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of the applicant's rights under that provision, as the individualised application of the original police decision referred to by the Government."*

The factual basis is thus: (1) the police did not acquiesce in the demonstration, and (2) this non-acquiescence was the individualised application of the original police decision. No further elaboration of these facts is provided within this section of the reasoning.

---

### (c) Prescribed by law

This is the most factually grounded element. The Court draws on the following facts:

**On the original measure:**
*"the police declared it a 'security operational zone' in 2006, and it remained so throughout the material period."*

**On the quashing of the legal basis:**
*"on 11 November 2010 the Budapest Regional Court quashed the underlying police decisions, reproaching those authorities for failing to assess the necessity and proportionality of the measure as maintained subsequent to 22 November 2006."*

**On the subsequent proportionality assessment:**
*"on 4 April 2011 the Budapest Commander carried out the requisite scrutiny and found that the proportionality of the measure had not been proved."*

**On the consequence for lawfulness:**
*"For the Court, these court rulings have effectively, if retroactively, removed the legal basis of the impugned measure."*

**On the immateriality of the proceedings being initiated by a third party:**
*"It is true that the above two decisions were adopted in a procedure initiated by Mr K. rather than the applicant. For the Court, however, this is immaterial when it comes to the notion of lawfulness in the context of Article 11 § 2."*

---

### (d) Legitimate aim

No factual grounding identifiable — the Court explicitly declined to examine this element.

---

### (e) Necessity in a democratic society

No factual grounding identifiable — the Court explicitly declined to examine this element.

---

### (f) Positive obligation

No factual grounding identifiable — this element was not discussed.
