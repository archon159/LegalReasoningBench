Here is the factual grounding for each element, drawn only from what the Court itself stated or explicitly accepted:

---

**(a) Scope**

**(i) Assembly exists:**
The Court grounds this on the following fact, stated in its own assessment:
> "the applicant was refused permit to attend a meeting held on 20 June 1997 in southern Cyprus"

and describes it as:
> "a bi-communal meeting"

**(ii) Participation:**
The Court grounds this on:
> "the refusal of the authorities to grant a permit to the applicant barred her participation in a bi-communal meeting there, preventing her consequently from engaging in peaceful assembly with people from both communities"

This establishes both that the applicant sought to participate and that she was prevented from doing so.

**(iii) Peaceful assembly:**
The Court does not make a separate factual finding establishing the peaceful nature of the assembly. It uses the phrase "peaceful assembly" descriptively when characterising what the applicant was prevented from doing, but does not ground this characterisation in any specific identified fact. **No distinct factual grounding can be identified.**

---

**(b) Interference**

The Court grounds its finding of interference on:
> "the refusal of the authorities to grant a permit to the applicant barred her participation in a bi-communal meeting there, preventing her consequently from engaging in peaceful assembly with people from both communities"

and on the legal principle it explicitly adopts:
> "that hindrance can amount to a violation of the Convention just like a legal impediment (see Loizidou v. Turkey, judgment of 18 December 1996, Reports, 1996-VI, § 63)"

The Court also grounds this in its prior findings in related cases:
> "the Court reiterates at the outset its findings in the cases of Cyprus v. Turkey and Djavit An v. Turkey in relation to the rigorous approach taken by the 'TRNC' authorities to bi-communal contacts after the second half of 1996 by the imposition of restrictions and, indeed, prohibitions"

---

**(c) Prescribed by law**

**(i) Foreseeability (explicit):**
The Court grounds its negative finding on the following facts it itself identifies:

> "the respondent Government referred to general restrictions concerning crossing of the 'green line' between the 'TRNC' and southern Cyprus. They did not refer to any law or measures in the 'TRNC' regulating the issuance of permits to Turkish Cypriots living in northern Cyprus to cross the 'green line' into southern Cyprus for the purposes of attending bi-communal meetings. Furthermore, they did not provide any indication as to when refusal of such permits is allowed."

Note: the Court here is characterising what the Government failed to produce, and draws its factual conclusion from that absence. It then states its own conclusion:
> "there seems to be no law applicable in the present case regulating the issuance of permits to Turkish Cypriots living in northern Cyprus to cross the 'green line' into southern Cyprus in order to engage in peaceful assembly with Greek Cypriots"

The Court also grounds this in its prior jurisprudence:
> "Bearing in mind its finding in the Djavit An v. Turkey case (cited above, § 67) on a similar issue and having regard to the circumstances of the individual case before it..."

**(ii) Accessibility (implied):**
No distinct factual grounding can be identified. The Court's finding that there is no applicable law at all subsumes this, but no separate facts are marshalled for it.

**(iii) Safeguards against abuse (implied):**
The closest factual grounding is the Court's own observation that the Government "did not provide any indication as to when refusal of such permits is allowed," but the Court does not frame this as a finding about safeguards specifically. **No distinct factual grounding for this sub-element as such can be identified.**

---

**(d) Legitimate aim**
Not discussed. No factual grounding to identify.

**(e) Necessity in a democratic society**
Not discussed. No factual grounding to identify.

**(f) Positive obligation**
Mentioned in general principles but not applied. No factual grounding to identify.
