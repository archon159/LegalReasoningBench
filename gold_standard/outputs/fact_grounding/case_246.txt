**(a) Scope**

The only factual grounding is the phrase "public assemblies" in the opening sentence:

No further factual detail about the nature, size, location, or purpose of the assemblies is provided in this reasoning section.

**ii) Participation**
The factual grounding is the same sentence:
> "disproportionate measures taken against them as **organisers or participants** of public assemblies, namely their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."

**iii) Peaceful**
There is **no factual grounding** for this element in the reasoning text. The Court implies the assemblies were peaceful by proceeding with the Article 11 analysis, but no fact is cited to support this. I cannot identify any factual basis stated in this section.

---

**(b) Interference**

The factual grounding is:
> "disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers or participants of public assemblies, namely **their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences**."

These two concrete acts — arrest/dispersal and administrative conviction — are the only facts cited as constituting the interference.

---

**(e) Necessity in a democratic society**

There is **no factual grounding** specific to the necessity assessment in this reasoning section. The Court makes its necessity finding entirely by reference to prior case law:
> "Having examined all the material submitted to it, the Court has not found any fact or argument capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion as to the admissibility and merits of these complaints. Having regard to its case-law on the subject, the Court considers that in the instant case the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not 'necessary in a democratic society'."

The Court references "all the material submitted to it" but does not identify or quote any specific facts from that material. The necessity conclusion is grounded in the comparison with prior leading cases (*Frumkin*, *Navalnyy and Yashin*, *Kasparov and Others*) rather than in any independently stated facts about the circumstances of the interference.
