Here is the factual grounding for each element the Court discussed or implied, drawn strictly from the reasoning text:

---

**(a) Scope (implied)**

The only factual grounding in the reasoning text is the following statement from the admissibility section:

> "the applicant was arrested, detained on remand and subsequently convicted of having attended a demonstration."

This single sentence provides the factual basis for inferring that an assembly existed and that the applicant participated in it. There is no factual grounding in the reasoning text for the peaceful nature of the assembly. The Court does not address this, and no facts are stated that would support or undermine it.

---

**(b) Interference (implied)**

The factual grounding is the same sentence as above:

> "the applicant was arrested, detained on remand and subsequently convicted of having attended a demonstration."

The Court also refers to "the interference resulting from the application of Article 220 § 6," but this is a legal characterization rather than a statement of fact. The underlying facts supporting the existence of an interference are the arrest, remand detention, and conviction described above.

---

**(c) Prescribed by law (explicitly discussed)**

The Court's assessment on this element is not grounded in any newly stated facts from the reasoning text. Instead, the Court relies entirely on its prior jurisprudence:

> "the foreseeability of Article 220 § 6 has been examined in a similar case (see, Işıkırık, cited above, §§ 56-70), where the Court concluded that the aforementioned provision was not 'foreseeable' in its application since it did not afford the applicant the legal protection against arbitrary interference with his right under Article 11 of the Convention."

And:

> "In the present case, the Court sees no reason to deviate from this finding."

There are no case-specific facts stated in the reasoning text that ground the prescribed-by-law analysis. The Court simply transplants the conclusion from *Işıkırık* without identifying any particular facts of the present case that independently support it.

---

**(d), (e), (f)**

As noted previously, these elements are not discussed. Accordingly, there is no factual grounding to identify for them.
