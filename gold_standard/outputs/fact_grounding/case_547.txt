## Factual Grounding for Each Canonical Element

---

### (a) Scope

**i) Assembly exists**

The Court grounds this on the following facts:

- "the two largest trade unions, namely the DİSK (Devrimci İşçi Sendikaları Konfederasyonu – Confederation of Revolutionary Workers' Trade Unions) and the KESK (Kamu Emekçileri Sendikaları Konfederasyonu – Confederation of Public Employees' Trade Unions) had made an announcement well in advance that their members would be gathering in Istanbul to celebrate the Labour Day and to commemorate their friends who had lost their lives during the demonstrations of 1 May 1977"

- "even before it became an official holiday in 2009, 1 May (Labour Day) was a highly symbolic day for trade union members, and was considered as a day of commemoration and celebration"

**ii) Participation**

The Court grounds this on the following facts:

- "the applicant was a civil servant in the Istanbul Municipality. He was also a member of the KESK, namely one of the trade unions which had organised and announced the demonstration of 1 May 2008"

- "disciplinary proceedings were initiated against the applicant for being absent from work without leave and in the course of the disciplinary proceedings against him, the applicant explicitly stated that he had been absent because he had participated in the demonstration organised by his trade union to celebrate the Labour Day"

- "the applicant, a civil servant working in the Istanbul Municipality, was absent on 1 May 2008 to participate in Labour Day demonstrations that were organised and announced by his trade union, namely the KESK"

**iii) Peaceful assembly**

No factual grounding can be identified. The Court does not cite any facts going to the peaceful character of the assembly.

---

### (b) Interference

The Court grounds this on the following facts:

- "the applicant was subsequently given a warning, under Law no. 657, as a disciplinary sanction owing to his absence without leave"

- "the disciplinary sanction complained of, although very light, was such as to dissuade trade union members from participation in trade union activities"

---

### (c) Prescribed by law

As noted in the prior analysis, this element is not discussed as a distinct analytical step. The Court mentions the legal basis in passing:

- "the applicant was subsequently given a warning, under Law no. 657, as a disciplinary sanction owing to his absence without leave"

- "whether it was justified to issue a warning pursuant to Section 125 of Law no. 657 in the particular circumstances of the case"

However, the Court does not ground any analysis of foreseeability, accessibility, or safeguards in these or any other facts. No factual grounding for a prescribed-by-law assessment can be identified.

---

### (d) Legitimate aim

This element is not discussed. No factual grounding can be identified.

---

### (e) Necessity in a democratic society

The Court grounds its necessity assessment on the following facts:

- "the demonstration was announced at the national level in advance" and "the demonstration in question was announced at the national level in advance. Moreover, it is of importance for the Court, in its assessment of the necessity of the interference under Article 11 § 2 of the Convention, that in the course of the investigation, the applicant had explained the reason of his absence by stating that he had participated in a trade union activity"

- "the disciplinary bodies were thus made explicitly aware of the reasons for his absence. Nevertheless, without the reasons for his absence being taken into account, the applicant was subsequently given a warning"

- "in their evaluation the disciplinary authorities failed to take into consideration, either explicitly or in substance, whether it was justified to issue a warning pursuant to Section 125 of Law no. 657 in the particular circumstances of the case"

- "the disciplinary sanction complained of, although very light, was such as to dissuade trade union members from participation in trade union activities"

---

### (f) Positive obligation

This element is not discussed. No factual grounding can be identified.
