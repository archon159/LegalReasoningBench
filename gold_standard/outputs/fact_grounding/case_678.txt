**Factual Grounding for Each Discussed Element**

**(a) Scope**

- **Assembly exists / Applicant participation:** The only factual grounding is the following phrase from the opening paragraph: "disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies, namely the dispersal of these assemblies, as well as the applicants' arrest followed by their conviction for administrative offence."

This is the Court's own characterisation of the situation, not merely a party submission. It establishes that public assemblies took place and that the applicants were participants in them.

- **Peaceful nature:** There is no factual grounding whatsoever. The Court does not mention any facts relating to the peaceful or non-peaceful character of the assemblies. The peaceful nature is implied structurally by the Court's willingness to proceed with the Article 11 analysis, but no facts are cited to support it.

**(b) Interference**

The factual grounding is the same passage as above: "the dispersal of these assemblies, as well as the applicants' arrest followed by their conviction for administrative offence." These are the concrete governmental acts the Court identifies as constituting the interference. No further factual detail is provided.

**(e) Necessity in a democratic society**

This is the central finding, yet the Court provides no independent factual analysis of its own.
