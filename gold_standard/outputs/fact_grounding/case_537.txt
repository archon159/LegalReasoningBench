**(a) Scope**

**ii) Participation**
The factual grounding for the implied conclusion that the applicant participated is the same passage:
> "the decision to sanction the applicant with a fine for his organising of a public meeting on 21 September 2012"

**iii) Peaceful**
No factual grounding can be identified. The Court does not refer to any facts establishing the peaceful nature of the assembly. It uses the phrase "freedom of peaceful assembly" but this is a quotation of the legal standard, not a factual finding. I cannot identify any factual grounding for this element.

---

**(b) Interference**

The Court grounds its finding of interference on the following fact, which it describes as "undisputed between the parties" and which it explicitly agrees with:
> "the decision to sanction the applicant with a fine for his organising of a public meeting on 21 September 2012 amounted to an 'interference by [a] public authority' with the applicant's right to freedom of assembly under the first paragraph of Article 11"

---

**(c) Prescribed by law**

The Court's finding that the interference was not prescribed by law is grounded in the following statement:
> "no elements in the present case allow the Court to consider that there was a legal basis for limiting the applicant's right to freedom of peaceful assembly"

This is a negative factual finding — the Court found an absence of any legal basis. The Court does not point to specific facts that positively establish this absence; rather, it notes that nothing in the case materials supports the existence of a legal basis. No further factual detail is provided.

**(f) Positive obligation (Moldova)**

The Court's assessment of Moldova's positive obligation is grounded on two distinct factual findings:

On the first aspect (re-establishing control), the Court relies on its prior finding in *Mozer* and the absence of new evidence:
> "Since the parties did not adduce any evidence to show that the Moldovan Government has changed its position concerning the Transdniestrian region in the years preceding the facts of the present case, the Court sees no reason to reach a different conclusion"

On the second aspect (ensuring respect for the applicant's rights), the Court grounds its conclusion on the following fact:
> "the applicant adduced no evidence to the effect that he had informed the Moldovan authorities of his problem. In such circumstances, the non-involvement of the Moldovan authorities in the case cannot be held against them."

---

**(f) Russia's responsibility (leading to the violation finding)**

The Court grounds Russia's responsibility on the following factual finding, which it cross-references to earlier paragraphs of the judgment:
> "the Court has established that Russia exercised effective control over the 'MRT' during the period in question (see paragraphs 19-20 above)"

And further:
> "By virtue of its continued military, economic and political support for the 'MRT', which could not otherwise survive, Russia's responsibility under the Convention is engaged as regards the violation of the applicants' rights."
