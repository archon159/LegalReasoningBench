# Factual Grounding for Each Canonical Element

---

## (a) Scope

### i) Assembly exists

The Court grounds this on the following facts:

> 'the demonstration of 14 July 2008 was organised by the DTP to protest about the conditions of detention of Abdullah Öcalan.'

### ii) Participation

The Court grounds this on the following facts:

> 'the applicant was arrested, detained on remand and subsequently convicted on the ground of having attended a demonstration and thrown stones at the security forces during that demonstration.'

And, accepting the applicant's account as uncontested:

> 'the applicant claimed that when he first joined the demonstrators, he started walking and chanting slogans with them. Thus, he had the intention of showing support for Mr Öcalan, but not of behaving violently when he started demonstrating, and these submissions were not contested by the Government.'

### iii) Peaceful assembly

The Court grounds this on the following facts:

> 'nothing in the case file suggests that this demonstration was not intended to be peaceful or that the organisers had violent intentions.'

> 'the applicant claimed that when he first joined the demonstrators, he started walking and chanting slogans with them. Thus, he had the intention of showing support for Mr Öcalan, but not of behaving violently when he started demonstrating, and these submissions were not contested by the Government.'

> 'there is nothing in the domestic courts' decisions showing that the applicant had violent intentions when he joined the demonstration.'

> 'the charges against the applicant did not concern infliction of any bodily harm on anyone.'

---

## (b) Interference

The Court grounds this on the following facts:

> 'the applicant was convicted under Article 314 § 2 of the Criminal Code, on the basis of Articles 220 § 6 and 314 § 3 of the same Code; section 7(2) of Law no. 3713; and sections 23(b) and 33(c) of Law no. 2911 by the judgment of Diyarbakır Assize Court and sentenced to a total of seven years and six months' imprisonment.'

> 'the applicant was detained on remand for three months and twenty days between 21 July and 11 November 2008 and partly served his prison sentence arising from the judgment of Diyarbakır Assize Court between 11 November 2008 and 25 July 2010. Thus, the applicant was deprived of his liberty for more than two years within the context of the criminal proceedings brought against him.'

> 'the judgments of 11 November 2008 and 20 December 2012 and the applicant's detention, both pending trial and for the execution of his sentence, entailed real and effective restraint and had a deterrent effect on the applicant's exercise of his right to freedom of assembly.'

> 'the juvenile court's judgment also had a deterrent effect on the applicant's future exercise of his right guaranteed under Article 11, since the pronouncement of the applicant's convictions under section 7(2) of Law no. 3713, sections 33(1), 32(1) and (2) of Law no. 2911 and Article 265 § 1 of the Criminal Code was suspended on condition that he did not commit another wilful offence and any failure on the applicant's part to comply with that condition would lead to the pronouncement of these convictions and the execution of the sentences.'

---

## (c) Prescribed by law

### i) Foreseeability

The Court grounds its discussion (though not its final conclusion) on the following facts:

> 'Diyarbakır Assize Court considered that the applicant had taken part in the demonstration of 14 July 2008 and committed the offences proscribed under sections 23(b) and 33(c) of Law no. 2911 and section 7(2) of the Prevention of Terrorism Act (Law no. 3713) on behalf of the PKK, given that the latter had made a general call for participation in this demonstration. The applicant was therefore convicted of membership of the PKK and sentenced to four years and two months' imprisonment.'

> 'This interpretation of Articles 220 § 6 and 314 §§ 2 and 3 of the Criminal Code is in accordance with the reasoning contained in the decision of the Plenary Court of Cassation of 4 March 2008. According to this line of interpretation, at the material time, if a demonstrator took part in a demonstration or a march for which the PKK had made a general call to participate, and committed one or more offences proscribed under the criminal codes during that event, he or she would be liable to be punished not only for the individual offences committed but also for membership of the PKK.'

### ii) Accessibility

The Court grounds this on the following facts:

> 'there is no doubt that the aforementioned provisions of Laws nos. 5237, 3713 and 2911 were accessible.'

> 'As to the decision of 4 March 2008, the Court observes that while this decision was not published in the Official Gazette, it was available on the Internet.'

### iii) Safeguards

No factual grounding identified. The Court does not discuss this sub-element under the 'prescribed by law' heading.

---

## (d) Legitimate aim

The Court does not ground its finding on specific facts — it states the conclusion briefly and without factual elaboration:

> 'The Court is of the opinion that, in the present case, the national authorities may be considered to have pursued the legitimate aims of preventing disorder and crime and the protection of the rights and freedoms of others.'

No specific facts are cited by the Court in support of this finding. No factual grounding can be identified beyond the bare assertion.

---

## (e) Necessity in a democratic society

The Court grounds its finding of disproportionality on several distinct factual bases:

**Lack of reasoning by domestic courts for PKK membership conviction:**

> 'Diyarbakır Assize Court concluded that the applicant had participated in the march as a result of the calls of the PKK published on two websites when it convicted the applicant of membership of the PKK, without providing any reason for that conclusion.'

> 'there is no justification in the first-instance court's judgment for the conclusion that the applicant participated in the demonstration and acted pursuant to the PKK's purposes or on behalf of that organisation upon its specific instructions to him.'

**Lack of reasoning for propaganda conviction:**

> 'the judgment of Diyarbakır Assize Court does not contain any information as to the reasons for which the applicant was found guilty of disseminating propaganda in support of a terrorist organisation. The Assize Court did not explain which of the acts of the applicant, a fifteen-year-old boy at the material time, constituted the offence proscribed by section 7(2) of Law no. 3713.'

> 'the assize court noted in its judgment that in his statements before the national authorities the applicant had accepted that he had made propaganda in support of an illegal organisation, whereas there is nothing in the case file to substantiate this finding.'

> 'Both before the Diyarbakır public prosecutor and the Fifth Division of Diyarbakır Assize Court the applicant maintained that he had participated in the demonstration, chanted the slogan "Long live President Öcalan" and thrown stones at the police when they intervened. On no occasion did he state that he had disseminated propaganda in support of the PKK.'

> 'Diyarbakır Juvenile Court also failed to offer an explanation for the applicant's conviction under section 7(2) of Law no. 3713.'

**The applicant's age and failure to consider it:**

> 'there is nothing in the case file to show that the national courts sufficiently took the applicant's age into consideration in ordering and continuing his detention on remand or in imposing a prison sentence.'

> 'the applicant was a minor at the relevant time' [implied from the reference to him as 'a fifteen-year-old boy at the material time' and the involvement of Diyarbakır Juvenile Court].

**Severity of penalties:**

> 'the extreme severity of the penalties imposed on the applicant by Diyarbakır Assize Court pursuant to Article 314 § 2 of the Criminal Code and section 7(2) of Law no. 3713, that is, a total of four years, eight months and twenty days of imprisonment, a sentence that the applicant partly served for a period of one year and eight months before he was released.'

> 'the applicant was detained pending trial for almost four months and the Government did not argue that alternative methods had been considered first or that the applicant's detention had been used only as a measure of last resort.'

**Stone-throwing and proportionality:**

> 'both the applicant's statements before the national authorities and the photographs in the case file reveal that he threw stones at the security forces and was thus involved in an act of violence.'

> 'the Court cannot overlook the harshness of the sentence imposed on the applicant by Diyarbakır Assize Court pursuant to sections 23(b) and 33(c) of Law no. 2911, that is to say, two years, nine months and ten days' imprisonment, a sentence that the applicant partly served, or the lengthy period during which he was detained pending trial.'

---

## (f) Positive obligation

No factual grounding identified. The Court does not discuss positive obligations in its own assessment.
