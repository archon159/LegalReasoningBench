## Factual Grounding for Each Doctrinal Element

---

### (a) Scope

**Assembly exists / Applicant's participation**

The Court does not explicitly state facts grounding these elements independently. The closest factual grounding is embedded in the interference analysis and the framing of the case. The Court accepted the following as established:

> "the non-acquiescence by the police in the demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of the applicant's rights under that provision, as the individualised application of the original police decision referred to by the Government"

This sentence presupposes both that a demonstration was planned and that the applicant sought to participate in it, but the Court does not separately identify facts grounding these elements. **No distinct factual grounding can be identified for scope as a standalone element.**

**Peaceful character**

No factual grounding identifiable. The Court does not point to any facts to establish that the assembly was peaceful. It is treated as uncontested. **No distinct factual grounding can be identified.**

---

### (b) Interference

The Court grounds its finding of interference on the following fact:

> "the non-acquiescence by the police in the demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of the applicant's rights under that provision, as the individualised application of the original police decision referred to by the Government"

The underlying factual basis for what that "original police decision" consisted of is captured in the 'prescribed by law' section:

> "the police declared it a 'security operational zone' in 2006, and it remained so throughout the material period"

These are the facts the Court treats as established and on which the interference finding rests.

---

### (c) Prescribed by law

This is the most factually grounded element in the reasoning. The Court relies on the following facts:

**The original measure and its duration:**
> "the police declared it a 'security operational zone' in 2006, and it remained so throughout the material period"

**The domestic court's quashing of the underlying police decisions:**
> "on 11 November 2010 the Budapest Regional Court quashed the underlying police decisions, reproaching those authorities for failing to assess the necessity and proportionality of the measure as maintained subsequent to 22 November 2006"

**The subsequent administrative finding on proportionality:**
> "on 4 April 2011 the Budapest Commander carried out the requisite scrutiny and found that the proportionality of the measure had not been proved"

**The Court's legal conclusion drawn from these facts:**
> "For the Court, these court rulings have effectively, if retroactively, removed the legal basis of the impugned measure."

**The Court's treatment of the fact that these proceedings were initiated by a third party (Mr K.) rather than the applicant:**
> "It is true that the above two decisions were adopted in a procedure initiated by Mr K. rather than the applicant. For the Court, however, this is immaterial when it comes to the notion of lawfulness in the context of Article 11 § 2."

---

### (d) Legitimate aim and (e) Necessity in a democratic society

The Court explicitly declines to examine these elements:

> "It is therefore not necessary to embark on an examination of its legitimate aim or necessity in a democratic society."

**No factual grounding is offered or identifiable for either element**, as the Court stops its analysis at the 'prescribed by law' stage.

---

### (f) Positive obligation

Not discussed. **No factual grounding identifiable.**
