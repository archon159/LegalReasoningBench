I will go through each element that was found to be either explicitly discussed or implied, and identify the factual grounding from the reasoning text.

---

**(a) Scope**

**i) Assembly exists**
The only factual grounding is: "their arrest followed by their conviction for administrative offence in view of their participation in **public demonstrations**."
The Court does not elaborate further on the nature of the demonstrations.

**ii) Participation**
The factual grounding is the same sentence: "their arrest followed by their conviction for administrative offence in view of their **participation** in public demonstrations."

**iii) Peaceful**
There is no factual grounding in the reasoning text. The Court says nothing about the character of the demonstrations. The peaceful nature is implied structurally by the Court's application of Article 11, but no fact is cited to support it.

---

**(b) Interference**

The factual grounding is: "their **arrest** followed by their **conviction for administrative offence** in view of their participation in public demonstrations."
These are the concrete governmental acts the Court identifies as constituting the interference.

---

**(e) Necessity in a democratic society**

The Court's necessity finding is not grounded in any specific facts drawn from the instant case. Instead, the Court relies entirely on prior case-law:

"In the leading cases of **Frumkin v. Russia**, no. 74568/12, 5 January 2016, **Navalnyy and Yashin v. Russia**, no. 76204/11, 4 December 2014, and **Kasparov and Others v. Russia**, no. 21613/07, 3 October 2013, the Court already found a violation in respect of **issues similar to those in the present case**."

And: "Having examined all the material submitted to it, the Court has not found **any fact or argument** capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion."

The Court thus explicitly declines to identify specific facts from the instant case that ground the necessity assessment, instead treating the similarity to prior cases as sufficient. No individual facts from the present case are quoted or relied upon in the necessity analysis.
