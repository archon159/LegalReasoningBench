LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: the applicant was convicted and sanctioned (ultimately by a fine) for participating in a protest rally, with no finding of any specific reprehensible conduct on his part, and in the absence of any legal framework for authorising or prohibiting demonstrations in Armenia at the time. These are the facts that drove the violation finding.

-> F008 (the applicant attended the opposition protest rally on 23 February 2003 — the factual basis for the conviction)
-> F015 (the charge was grounded in participation in the demonstration and violation of public order — establishes that the conviction was for assembly participation, not for any specific act)
-> F031 (the Court of Appeal's review referred only to attending an unauthorised demonstration and violating public order, with no details of reprehensible conduct — the critical fact for the necessity analysis)
-> F033 (the penalty was changed to an administrative fine — establishes that a sanction was maintained, grounding the interference and the necessity failure)
-> F054 (the relevant domestic law, as summarised in Galstyan, established the absence of any applicable legal act on authorisation of demonstrations — grounds the finding that the 'unauthorised' characterisation was legally baseless)
