LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on: (1) the existence of peaceful assemblies; (2) the applicants' participation in or intended participation in those assemblies; (3) the state's dispersal of those assemblies and arrest of the applicants; (4) the political/expressive nature of the assemblies (relevant to the weight of the right and the chilling effect on open political debate). The Court found a violation because peaceful assemblies were forcibly dispersed and participants arrested and subjected to administrative proceedings, producing a chilling effect on political participation, without justification meeting the Convention standard.

Irreducible core: F009 (2 April demonstration existed), F036 (second applicant participated), F039 (police dispersed and arrested), F010 (17 April demonstration existed), F037 (first applicant participated), F038 (third applicant participated), F040 (police dispersed and arrested), F044 (fourth applicant arrested before 18 October 2011 rally), F045 (first applicant arrested before 20 October 2012 demonstration), F022 (demonstrations were peaceful), F023 (political nature — democratic reforms demanded, relevant to chilling effect on open political debate)
