LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following key facts:

1. F025: The applicants were on their way to participate in demonstrations against the G8 summit — establishes that they were exercising their right to freedom of assembly.

2. F033, F034: The banners found in the van bore the inscriptions 'Freedom for all prisoners' and 'Free all now' — these are the sole basis for the authorities' concern and the entire justification for the detention.

3. F036: The banners appear to have been seized by the police — establishes that a less intrusive measure was already available and in fact used, fatally undermining the necessity of detention.

4. F037, F038: The domestic courts ordered the applicants' detention for the full duration of the G8 summit (until 9 June 2007, 12 noon) — establishes the severity of the interference.

5. F070: The Court of Appeal itself conceded that the slogans could be understood in different ways — establishes that deliberate incitement to violence was not proven, only possible negligent incitement.

6. F088: The applicants were released only after the summit ended — confirms that the detention was coextensive with the summit, making participation entirely impossible.

7. F102: No criminal proceedings were brought against the applicants for incitement — confirms that the feared crime was never established, supporting the Court's finding that the measure was disproportionate.

8. F022: More than 1,000 demonstrators were detained during the summit — establishes that the banners addressed a matter of genuine public interest, giving the assembly significant weight in the proportionality balance.

These eight facts are the irreducible core: they establish (i) that the applicants were exercising assembly rights (F025), (ii) the sole basis for the interference (F033, F034), (iii) that a less intrusive alternative existed (F036), (iv) the severity of the interference (F037, F038, F088), (v) that violent intent was not proven (F070, F102), and (vi) the public interest weight of the assembly (F022).
