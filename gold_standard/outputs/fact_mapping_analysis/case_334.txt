LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court's finding that the gatherings were assemblies within Article 11 and that the applicant participated in them is grounded in: F039 (ten marches and demonstrations listed in indictment), F075 (Assize Court found applicant attended ten marches and demonstrations between 19 February 2005 and 16 February 2006). The peaceful character is implied by the Court's own characterisation of the applicant as 'a politician and a peaceful demonstrator', grounded in: F003/F004 (applicant was head of local party branches), F074 (Assize Court found same). The most critical representative facts are F039, F075, F003.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The interference finding rests on the criminal conviction and the sentence imposed. The strictly necessary facts are: F054 (conviction under Article 314 § 2 on basis of Article 220 § 7), F056 (sentence of six years and three months), F090 (sentence reduced and served).

The Court's finding that the interference was not prescribed by law rests on several distinct pillars. The strictly necessary facts are:
- Text of the provision lacking definitions: F099 (Article 220 § 7 text), F098 (Article 220 § 6 text for comparison)
- Inconsistent domestic case-law: F129 (Plenary Court of Cassation judgment of 4 March 2008), F085 (Ninth Criminal Division upheld applicant's conviction on 13 April 2010)

- Contrast with standalone Article 314 criteria: F113 (Court of Cassation criterion of continuity, diversity and intensity)
- Severity of sanction reinforcing chilling effect: F105 (Article 314 § 2 provides five to ten years), F056 (sentence imposed)
- Timing issue (two meetings before Criminal Code in force): F092 (Criminal Code entered into force 1 April 2005), F057 (event of 19 February 2005), F058 (event of 31 March 2005)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the prescribed-by-law analysis. The Court found that Article 220 § 7, as applied to the applicant, was not foreseeable and did not afford sufficient protection against arbitrary interference. The irreducible factual core is:

1. The applicant attended ten public meetings and made speeches — this is what triggered the conviction and is the conduct at issue: F039, F075
2. The applicant was a local politician (head of party branches), not a member of the PKK's hierarchical structure — this is what makes the extensive interpretation problematic: F003, F074
3. The Assize Court convicted him under Article 220 § 7 on the basis of continuity of attendance alone, without requiring proof of actual membership or hierarchical involvement: F083, F054
4. The text of Article 220 § 7 contains no definition of 'aiding knowingly and willingly' and does not require hierarchical membership: F099
5. The domestic courts lacked consistent case-law — the Plenary Court of Cassation's 2008 judgment and the Ninth Criminal Division's 2010 judgment upholding the applicant's conviction pointed in different directions: F129, F085
6. The sentence imposed was five to ten years under Article 314 § 2, which is strikingly severe relative to the conduct: F105, F056
