LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Scope**
Quote: "it is not disputed between the parties that the applicants participated in the rally of 15 April 2007 and that their conduct was peaceful."
- F006: The rally of 15 April 2007 existed as an anti-government rally organised by a coalition of opposition groups.
- F007: The rally was part of a series of protest demonstrations called a 'March of Dissenters'.
- F008: The St Petersburg city administration authorised the rally.
- F013: The applicants attended the rally on 15 April 2007 at Pionerskaya Square.

Quote: "It has not been alleged that they were among those who had, according to the Government, disturbed the public order during the assembly."
- F021: According to the Government, a group of approximately 60 persons under the flags of the National Bolshevik Party attempted to break through the police cordon — not the applicants.
- F022: That group attempted to lead an unauthorised march — not the applicants.
- F023: Persons in that group shouted slogans — not the applicants.
- F024: Persons in that group threw bottles — not the applicants.

**Interference**
Quote: "the Court notes that it has found a violation of Article 3 of the Convention on account of the first and the third applicants' ill-treatment by police during the public assembly"
- F026: The first applicant was approached by a riot police officer.
- F027: The first applicant was severely beaten by a riot police officer.
- F028: The riot police officer hit the first applicant on the chest, ribs and shoulders.
- F029: The riot police officer left the first applicant lying unconscious.
- F032: The first applicant was diagnosed with a closed fracture of the seventh rib, traumatic pneumothorax, a closed craniocerebral injury, and brain concussion.
- F033: The third applicant was hit by a riot police officer with a rubber truncheon to the face.
- F034: The third applicant started falling unconscious.
- F036: The third applicant was diagnosed with a craniocerebral injury, concussion, closed fractures of the nose bones and the right cheek bone.

Quote: "the domestic courts established that the police had exceeded their powers during the operation on 15 April 2007"
- F065: The Oktyabrskiy District Court noted that on 15 April 2007 some unidentified police officers had exceeded their official powers and beaten a number of protesters.

**Prescribed by law**
The Court simply accepts this without factual grounding. No atomic facts are cited. The only potentially relevant background fact is:
- F075: A summary of relevant domestic law provisions governing use of force by the police can be found in Annenkov and Others v. Russia.
However, the Court does not cite this in its reasoning on "prescribed by law." No factual grounding can be identified.

**Legitimate aim**
The Court accepts this without factual grounding. The only contextual facts that could inform the aim of "prevention of disorder and crime" are the Government's submissions about the provocateurs:
- F021: A group attempted to break through the police cordon.
- F022: That group attempted to lead an unauthorised march.
- F023: Persons in that group shouted slogans.
- F024: Persons in that group threw bottles.
- F025: The riot police had to use force to disperse the particular group and restore public order.
However, the Court does not explicitly cite these facts when accepting the legitimate aim — it simply accepts it. These facts are at best implicit background.

**Necessity**
Quote: "given its above findings as regards the ill-treatment in respect of the first and the third applicants, and the findings of the domestic courts as regards the excessive use of power by police during the public assembly on 15 April 2007"
- F026: The first applicant was approached by a riot police officer.
- F027: The first applicant was severely beaten by a riot police officer.
- F028: The riot police officer hit the first applicant on the chest, ribs and shoulders.
- F029: The riot police officer left the first applicant lying unconscious.

- F033: The third applicant was hit by a riot police officer with a rubber truncheon to the face.
- F034: The third applicant started falling unconscious.
- F036: The third applicant was diagnosed with a craniocerebral injury, concussion, closed fractures of the nose bones and the right cheek bone.
- F065: The Oktyabrskiy District Court noted that on 15 April 2007 some unidentified police officers had exceeded their official powers and beaten a number of protesters.

**Positive obligation**

LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each doctrinal element

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish: (i) there was an assembly, (ii) the applicants participated, (iii) their conduct was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F006: Establishes the existence of the rally as an organised public assembly.
- F013: Establishes that the applicants attended and participated.
- F021 and F022: Establish that the group accused of disorder was a distinct group (approximately 60 persons under National Bolshevik Party flags attempting an unauthorised march), not the applicants — this is the critical factual basis for the finding that the applicants' conduct was peaceful and they were not among the troublemakers.

**Interference**
The Court's finding of interference rests on the Article 3 ill-treatment finding and the domestic court's finding of excess of powers. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F027: The first applicant was severely beaten — the core act of ill-treatment.
- F033: The third applicant was hit with a rubber truncheon — the core act of ill-treatment.
- F065: The domestic court established that police exceeded their official powers — the institutional confirmation of unlawful interference.

**Prescribed by law**
The Court accepts this without citing any facts. No atomic facts are strictly necessary from the list provided.

**Legitimate aim**
The Court accepts this without citing any facts. No atomic facts are strictly necessary from the list provided.

**Necessity**
The disproportionality finding rests on the severity of the ill-treatment and the domestic court's finding of excess of powers, cross-referenced from the Article 3 analysis. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F027: The first applicant was severely beaten — severity of force used.
- F032: Serious injuries diagnosed — demonstrates gravity of the force.
- F033: The third applicant was hit with a rubber truncheon to the face.
- F036: Serious injuries diagnosed for the third applicant — demonstrates gravity of the force.
- F065: The domestic court established that police exceeded their official powers — the key institutional finding underpinning disproportionality.
- F013: The applicants were peaceful participants — necessary to show the force was applied against persons who posed no threat.

**Positive obligation**
Not discussed. Empty.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: the applicants were peaceful participants in an authorised assembly; they were subjected to serious physical violence by riot police; the domestic courts confirmed the police exceeded their powers; and the applicants were not among those who had caused disorder. These are the facts without which the Court could not have reached its conclusion:

- F006: Establishes the existence of an authorised public assembly — necessary to bring the case within Article 11 scope.
- F013: The applicants participated in the rally — necessary for standing under Article 11.
- F021: The group accused of disorder was a distinct group of approximately 60 persons — necessary to establish the applicants' peaceful conduct by contrast.
- F027: The first applicant was severely beaten by riot police — the core act establishing both interference and disproportionality.
- F033: The third applicant was hit with a rubber truncheon — the core act establishing both interference and disproportionality.
- F032: Serious injuries to the first applicant — establishes the gravity of the force, underpinning the disproportionality finding.
- F036: Serious injuries to the third applicant — establishes the gravity of the force, underpinning the disproportionality finding.
- F065: The domestic court found that police exceeded their official powers — the institutional confirmation that the force was unlawful and disproportionate, which the Court explicitly relies on for both interference and necessity.
