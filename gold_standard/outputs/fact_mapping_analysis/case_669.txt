LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**
The Court needed to establish that there was a planned peaceful assembly and that the applicants were participants. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F016 or F017: The applicant associations notified the Ministry of their intention to hold a peaceful public rally (establishes the assembly and the applicants' role as organisers/participants).
- F018: The event would take the form of a silent twenty-minute flash mob (establishes peaceful character).
- F048: Participants started gathering at Pushkin Square on 17 May 2013 (establishes that participation was actually attempted).
- F071: Counter-demonstrators broke through cordons and headed towards the applicants (establishes disruption, confirming the assembly was underway and peaceful until disrupted by others).

**Interference (via positive obligation framing)**
The Court frames interference as the state's failure to protect. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F001: The cases concern the State's alleged failure to protect the applicants' public rally from homophobic/transphobic violence (establishes the nature of the complaint).
- F064: Clergymen led the crowd to break through the police cordon with no resistance from the police (establishes the state's failure to act).
- F071: Hundreds of counter-demonstrators got through the cordons and headed towards the applicants (establishes the consequence of the failure).

**Prescribed by law**
Not discussed — no atomic facts applicable.

**Legitimate aim**
Not discussed — no atomic facts applicable.

**Necessity**
The Court's necessity-adjacent reasoning (within the positive obligation framework) focuses on the inadequacy of planning and the dispersal-only approach. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F040: Ministry of the Interior stated more than 10,000 people were planning to take part in the counter-demonstration (establishes prior knowledge of the scale of the threat).
- F047: Some 35,000 to 40,000 counter-demonstrators were actually gathered (establishes the actual magnitude of the risk).
- F050: The LGBT demonstrators were separated only by removable metal fences and a thin cordon of unarmed and unequipped police patrol officers (establishes inadequacy of protective measures).
- F053: No riot squads could be seen at Pushkin Square (establishes inadequacy of protective measures).
- F074: The group of twelve retreated by boarding two buses provided by the police in advance for dispersal purposes (establishes that dispersal was the only plan).
- F075: None of the applicants knew anything about the dispersal plan (establishes that the plan was not communicated and was not oriented toward enabling the event).

**Positive obligation**
The Court's positive obligation finding rests on the failure to plan adequately and the active facilitation of the breach. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F040: Prior knowledge of the scale of the threat (more than 10,000 expected).
- F047: Actual scale of the threat (35,000-40,000 counter-demonstrators).
- F050: Inadequate protective measures deployed (removable fences, unarmed/unequipped officers).
- F053: No riot squads present.
- F064: Police offered no resistance as clergymen led the crowd through the cordon.
- F066: Police officers actively disassembled cordons and removed metal fences.
- F068: The removal of metal fences was pursuant to an order from a senior official (deputy head of State Security Agency).
- F074: Dispersal by bus was the only plan.
- F075: Applicants were unaware of the dispersal plan.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the positive obligation under Article 11 read with Article 14. The Court found a violation because: (a) the authorities knew of a massive, credible threat to a peaceful LGBT assembly; (b) they failed to plan adequate protective measures; (c) they instead planned only for dispersal; and (d) police officers actively facilitated the breach of the cordon. The irreducible facts are:

- F016 (or F017): The applicant associations notified the authorities of their intention to hold a peaceful public rally — establishing the assembly and triggering the positive obligation.
- F018: The event was planned as a silent flash mob — establishing its peaceful character.
- F040: The Ministry of the Interior knew that more than 10,000 counter-demonstrators were expected — establishing prior knowledge of the threat.
- F047: Some 35,000 to 40,000 counter-demonstrators actually gathered — establishing the magnitude of the risk materialised.
- F050: The applicants were protected only by removable metal fences and a thin cordon of unarmed, unequipped officers — establishing the inadequacy of protective measures.
- F053: No riot squads were present — corroborating the inadequacy of protective measures.
- F064: Police offered no resistance as clergymen led the crowd through the cordon — establishing the state's active failure.
- F066: Police officers actively disassembled the cordons and removed the metal fences — establishing active facilitation of the breach.
- F074: The only plan was dispersal by bus — establishing that enabling the event was never a real priority.
- F075: The applicants were unaware of the dispersal plan — corroborating that the plan was not oriented toward protecting the assembly.
