LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish: (i) there was an assembly, (ii) the applicants participated, (iii) it was peaceful.
- Assembly: F013 (Niğde gathering), F025 (Mardin gathering)
- Participation: F014 (first applicant read out statement), F026 (second applicant read out statement) — these also confirm participation of all applicants since F013 and F025 establish the gatherings they attended
- Subject matter (public interest): F014, F016 — establish the content of the demonstration as a matter of public concern
- Peaceful: F027, F028 (no one prevented from entering), F029, F030 (police did not interfere with the demonstration itself)

Most critical: F013, F025, F014, F026, F027, F028, F029, F030

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the right.
- F035 (Mardin conviction and fine), F036 (Niğde conviction and fine) — these are the core interference facts
- F033, F034 (prosecutions) — also part of the interference (chilling effect reasoning)

Most critical: F035, F036

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court declines to conclude on this element. The only fact it identifies is the legal basis:
- F044 (section 32 of Misdemeanours Act)

Most critical: F044

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts the Government's aims without independent factual analysis. The facts that make those aims plausible:
- F031, F032 (orders given for public order and safety — the stated reason for the orders)
- F044 (the statutory provision itself references public safety, public order and public health)

Most critical: F031, F032

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity analysis turns on several factual pillars:
1. The peaceful nature of the demonstration: F027, F028, F029, F030
2. The subject matter being of public interest: F014, F016
3. The fact that the applicants were sanctioned merely for participating: F035, F036
4. The failure of domestic courts to conduct proportionality balancing: F035, F036, F038, F039
5. The prohibition area and police orders: F031, F032, F050, F051

Most critical: F027, F028, F029, F030, F035, F036, F038, F039, F031, F032

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts strictly necessary — element not applied.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality analysis. The Court found a violation because: (a) the demonstration was peaceful and caused no disruption; (b) the applicants were sanctioned merely for participating; (c) the domestic courts conducted no proportionality balancing. The prohibition and the police orders are also necessary to understand why the applicants were prosecuted at all.

Irreducible core:
- F013, F025: the gatherings took place (without these, there is no assembly and no case)
- F027, F028, F029, F030: the demonstration was peaceful and caused no disruption (the central factual basis for the disproportionality finding)
- F031, F032: police orders to disband were given (necessary to understand the charge of disobeying orders)
- F035, F036: convictions and fines imposed (the interference that must be justified)
- F038, F039: domestic courts dismissed appeals without proportionality reasoning (the Court's key finding on necessity — domestic courts failed to balance rights)
