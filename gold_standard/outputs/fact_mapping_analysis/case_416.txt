LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish that there was an assembly, that the applicant participated, and that it was peaceful. The most critical facts are:
-> F068 (three persons decided to stage a static demonstration — establishes assembly and participation)
-> F073 (applicant placed herself on pavement in front of building — establishes participation in assembly)
-> F089 (police report characterised it as a demonstration — confirms assembly exists in domestic record)
-> F104 (Justice of the Peace found applicant failed to end non-authorised demonstration — confirms assembly and participation in domestic findings)
Peacefulness is presumed by the Court without specific factual grounding beyond the absence of any finding of violence; the most relevant underlying fact is:
-> F068 (static demonstration — by nature a non-violent form of protest)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needed to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's assembly rights. The critical facts are:
-> F079 (police compelled applicant to follow to police station — dispersal)
-> F102 (sentence of seven days detention — penalty imposed)
-> F089 (administrative offence report linking arrest to the demonstration)
-> F104 (domestic court findings linking conviction to the demonstration)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) the demonstration was small and peaceful; (2) the domestic proceedings were contradictory and failed to establish a clear legal basis for dispersal; (3) the proceedings against the other participants were discontinued; (4) the domestic courts imposed a detention penalty without assessing Convention principles or the conditions for administrative arrest. These are the facts the conclusion actually turned on:

-> F068 (three persons staging a static demonstration — small, peaceful assembly; applicant's participation)
-> F073 (demonstration on pavement — context; no disruption established)
-> F071 (notification by telegram — basis of Government's notification argument, which the Court found unsupported by domestic findings)
-> F089 (administrative offence report characterising offence as holding non-authorised demonstration — one horn of the contradiction)
-> F080 (arrest record characterising offence as refusal to comply with police orders — other horn of the contradiction)
-> F104 (Justice of the Peace findings — no clear finding on Public Gatherings Act breach, no Convention assessment)
-> F123 (appeal court findings — amended description, no clear finding on PGA breach)
-> F126 (appeal court noted section 8 but drew no conclusions for applicant's case — absence of clear domestic finding)
-> F128 (proceedings against Ms I. discontinued — no violations established against co-participant)
-> F133 (proceedings against Ms B. discontinued — no violations established against co-participant)
-> F102 (seven days detention imposed — severity of penalty without Convention assessment)
-> F163 (Article 27.3 CAO requires exceptional circumstances — domestic courts did not assess this condition)
