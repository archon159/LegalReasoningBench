The factual grounding identified in the previous step consisted entirely of one phrase drawn from the Frumkin citation: "disproportionate measures taken by the authorities against organisers and participants of public assemblies." No other facts were independently stated in the reasoning text.

Looking at the three atomic facts available:

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on the finding that disproportionate measures were taken against organisers and participants of public assemblies. The only atomic fact that is strictly necessary to support this conclusion — because it is the source of all case-specific facts about the assemblies, the participation, and the measures taken — is F001. F003 confirms that Article 11 complaints were raised as "other complaints" and thus that the conclusion of violation pertains to these applicants, but F001 is the irreducible core because it contains the substantive details. F003 is also necessary in the minimal sense that it establishes that these applicants raised Article 11-type complaints at all, making the conclusion applicable to them. However, since F001 already encompasses the details of the applications (including the nature of the complaints), F003 adds no independent factual content beyond what F001 already covers. The irreducible core is therefore F001.
