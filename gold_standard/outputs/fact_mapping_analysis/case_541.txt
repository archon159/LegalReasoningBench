LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) the applicants participated in peaceful assemblies organised by an opposition party on a matter of public interest; (2) they were fined for doing so without authorisation; (3) the legal basis for requiring authorisation was itself disputed and unclear; (4) none of the government's justifications for the sanction were relevant and sufficient — the assemblies were peaceful, the presence of children was not attributable to the CDPP, and the slogan was not a call to violence; (5) the sanction (fine) was disproportionate given the absence of any pressing social need.

Irreducible facts:
- F001: applicants are members/sympathisers of CDPP (establishes who was sanctioned and their connection to the assemblies)
- F002: CDPP is a parliamentary political party (establishes the political/democratic context narrowing the margin of appreciation)
- F003: CDPP was in opposition (further narrows margin of appreciation)
- F004: Government's intention to make Russian compulsory (establishes the public interest subject matter)
- F006: heated public debate (establishes public interest context)
- F023: gathering actually held on 9 January (establishes assembly existed)
- F024: further gatherings held (establishes ongoing assemblies)
- F026: CDPP did not seek authorisation (establishes the factual basis for the sanction)
- F020: Municipal Council noted contradictory provisions (establishes legal uncertainty undermining the prescribed-by-law and necessity analysis)
- F022: Municipal Council suspended its decision pending interpretation (establishes that even the authorising body was uncertain)
- F050: slogan alleged to be call to violence (the only non-peaceful element alleged — rejected by Court)
- F084: fine of MDL 450 on first applicant (establishes the interference/sanction)
- F085: fine of MDL 450 on second applicant
- F086: fine of MDL 90 on third applicant
- F087: fine of MDL 90 on fourth applicant
