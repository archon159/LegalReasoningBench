LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**: The Court's willingness to proceed under Article 11 requires that there were assemblies and that the applicants were organisers or participants. The strictly necessary atomic facts are F002 and F003. F002 establishes the organisers dimension; F003 establishes the participants dimension. Both are needed because the applicant group includes both categories.

**Interference**: The Court explicitly identifies arrest and conviction as the governmental acts constituting interference. These acts are directed at organisers (F002) and participants (F003). Both F002 and F003 are strictly necessary since the interference finding covers both categories.

**Prescribed by law**: Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

**Legitimate aim**: Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

**Necessity**: The necessity finding — that the interferences were not necessary in a democratic society — is grounded in the same measures (arrest and conviction of organisers and participants). F002 and F003 are strictly necessary. Without knowing that these measures were taken against organisers and participants of public assemblies, the necessity assessment has no factual anchor.

**Positive obligation**: Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the fact that governmental measures — arrest and conviction — were taken against the applicants as organisers and participants of public assemblies, and that these measures were found not to be necessary in a democratic society. The irreducible factual core is therefore F002 and F003. F002 (organisers subjected to disproportionate measures) and F003 (participants subjected to disproportionate measures) are the facts without which the Court could not have found a violation: they establish both the Article 11 scope (assemblies, participation) and the interference (the measures taken), which together with the necessity finding produce the violation conclusion. F001 and F004 are not part of this core — F001 is merely a procedural reference to the appended table, and F004 concerns unrelated complaints.
