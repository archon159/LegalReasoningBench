LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that an assembly took place and that it was peaceful. The most critical facts are:
- F019: establishes that members of DISK and KESK gathered in front of DISK headquarters — this is the assembly.
- F054: no prosecution initiated against applicants — this is the most direct factual basis for the Court's finding that the assembly was peaceful and that no violent conduct was established.
- F022: the group argued they were merely waiting in a pedestrian area and not violating the Act — supports the peaceful character finding.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that the police intervention prevented the applicants from exercising their right of assembly:
- F023: police dispersed the group using pressurised water, paint and tear gas — the core act of interference.
- F029: the group broke up of its own accord at 10.30 a.m. to forestall further violence — establishes that the assembly did not take place as a result of the intervention.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court needs to identify the legal basis:
- F057: Section 22 of Law no. 2911 — the primary provision cited.
- F059: Section 24 of Law no. 2911 — the dispersal provision cited.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts prevention of disorder without independent factual analysis. The most relevant fact supporting the Government's submission (which the Court did not contest) is:
- F009: authorities held the demonstration would be unlawful on account of possible provocations and disruption of traffic and public order.

--- NECESSITY ---
The core facts for the proportionality assessment are:
- F020: police asked group to disperse at 6.30 a.m. — establishes that intervention preceded the march.
- F019: gathering began at approximately 6 a.m. — establishes the timeline showing intervention was before the march commenced.
- F023: police used pressurised water, paint and tear gas — establishes the nature and intensity of force used.
- F025: some demonstrators were injured — establishes the consequences of the force.
- F054: no prosecution initiated against applicants — establishes absence of violent conduct by demonstrators.
- F022: group argued they were merely waiting and not violating the Act — supports absence of violent resistance.
- F026: injured demonstrators chased by police while trying to reach hospital — establishes disproportionate pursuit.
- F027: demonstrators subjected to gas attacks within hospital premises — establishes use of force in hospital.
- F031: around 20-30 demonstrators entered hospital and opened a banner — the accepted factual basis for the hospital incident.
- F032: police entered hospital premises and used gas bomb in garden — establishes the specific act found disproportionate.
- F029: group broke up at 10.30 a.m. to forestall further violence — establishes outcome and that assembly was prevented.
- F041: Minister of Interior refused to authorise prosecution — supports finding of no domestic investigation.
- F042: public prosecutor decided not to proceed — supports finding of no domestic investigation.

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary here as the Court makes no factual finding on positive obligations.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality assessment. The Court found the interference disproportionate because: (1) the police intervened before the march even began; (2) there was no evidence of violence by demonstrators; (3) the force used was extreme (gas, water cannon, beatings, pursuit into a hospital); and (4) no domestic investigation was conducted. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

- F019: establishes the assembly and its timing (gathering began at 6 a.m.) — necessary to show intervention preceded the march.
- F020: police asked group to disperse at 6.30 a.m. — establishes that intervention occurred before the march commenced, which is the Court's central proportionality finding.
- F023: police dispersed group using pressurised water, paint and tear gas — establishes the nature of the interference and the force used.
- F025: some demonstrators were injured — establishes the severity of the intervention.
- F054: no prosecution initiated against applicants — the single most important fact establishing that demonstrators were not violent, directly undercutting the Government's justification.
- F027: demonstrators subjected to gas attacks within hospital premises — establishes the specific act the Court found could not be considered necessary or proportionate.
- F032: police entered hospital premises and used gas bomb in garden — the accepted factual basis for the hospital finding.
- F029: group broke up at 10.30 a.m. to forestall further violence — establishes that the assembly was entirely prevented as a result of the intervention.
- F041: Minister of Interior refused to authorise prosecution — establishes absence of domestic investigation, which the Court explicitly noted as a factor in its assessment.
