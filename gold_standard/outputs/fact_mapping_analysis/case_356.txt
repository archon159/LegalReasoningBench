LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two pivotal findings: (1) the assembly was peaceful and the dispersal lacked sufficient justification (necessity), and (2) the applicants were convicted for the mere act of participating in an unauthorised but peaceful assembly, with harsh sanctions producing a chilling effect. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

- F006: Applicants participated in an assembly on 6 May 2014 — establishes the Article 11 activity at stake.
- F009: The protest arose spontaneously in response to the court judgment — establishes the spontaneous character, which is critical both for the prescribed-by-law doubt and for the necessity analysis.
- F010: The protest was brief, spontaneous and peaceful (applicants' account, accepted by Court) — establishes peacefulness, which is the predicate for Article 11 protection.
- F011: Police forcibly dispersed the demonstration immediately after protesters started chanting — establishes the interference and the absence of any disorder justifying it.
- F012: Applicants were arrested during the dispersal — establishes the interference.
- F017: The administrative-offence reports charged the applicants with participation in an assembly organised not in accordance with the law — establishes that the basis of the prosecution was mere participation, not any violent or disorderly conduct.
- F039/F040: The Nasimi District Court found the applicants had participated in an unauthorised demonstration — confirms that conviction rested on participation alone.
- F043: First applicant sentenced to thirty days administrative detention — establishes the harshness of the sanction.
- F044: Second applicant sentenced to twenty days administrative detention — establishes the harshness of the sanction.
- F054: No prior written notification was required for spontaneous assemblies under Azerbaijani law — establishes the doubt about lawfulness of the dispersal and the absence of a valid legal basis for treating the assembly as unlawful.
- F062: Administrative detention under Article 298.2 increased to up to two months — establishes the legislative context of escalating sanctions contributing to the chilling effect.
