LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) there was a peaceful, authorised assembly; (2) the applicants participated in it; (3) they were arrested immediately after it; (4) administrative proceedings followed; and (5) these measures had a chilling effect on assembly rights, as established by analogy with prior judgments. The strictly necessary atomic facts are:

- F008: The demonstration was authorised — establishes the assembly was lawful and peaceful, making the subsequent arrests particularly unjustifiable.
- F011: The demonstration was conducted in a peaceful manner — confirms the assembly was within Article 11 protection.
- F013: All three applicants participated — connects the applicants to the protected assembly.
- F014: The applicants were arrested after the demonstration — the core interference fact and the factual basis for the chilling effect finding.
- F017: Administrative proceedings similar to Huseynli and Others — necessary for the Court's reliance on that judgment's necessity findings.
- F020: Administrative proceedings similar to Huseynov and Others — necessary for the Court's reliance on that judgment's necessity findings.
