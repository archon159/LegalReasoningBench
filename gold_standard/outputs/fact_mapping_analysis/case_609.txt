LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that there was a peaceful assembly and that the applicant participated in it.
- F008: The demonstration was held on 26 November 2005 at Galaba Square — establishes the assembly.
- F009: The demonstration was scheduled from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. — establishes the notification and the temporal framework the Court relies on.
- F015: The applicant and her family participated in the demonstration — establishes participation.
- F012: The dispersal was triggered solely because the demonstration continued beyond the authorised time, not because of violence — establishes peacefulness by negation (no violence alleged as cause).

--- INTERFERENCE ---
- F011: Shortly after 5 p.m. police began a forcible dispersal operation — establishes the governmental act constituting interference.
- F015: The applicant participated in the demonstration — establishes that the interference affected her specifically.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
- F092: Article 7 of the Law on Freedom of Assembly — provides the legal basis for restrictions.
- F094: Article 14.II of the Law on Freedom of Assembly — provides the legal basis for police use of force to disperse.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
- F012: The dispersal was because the demonstration continued beyond the authorised time — this is the factual hook for the aims of preventing disorder and protecting the rights of others, which the Court accepts without further factual elaboration.

--- NECESSITY ---
- F011: Police began a forcible dispersal operation shortly after 5 p.m. — establishes the nature and timing of the intervention.
- F012: The sole reason for intervention was that the demonstration continued beyond the authorised time — establishes the insufficiency of the justification.
- F013: The dispersal was conducted by riot police units — establishes the intensity of the intervention.
- F014: Riot police were fully equipped with helmets, shields and truncheons — establishes the intensity of the intervention.
- F016: The applicant was walking slowly to the edge of the square to go home — establishes her specific conduct, accepted by default due to Government's failure to rebut.
- F010: The demonstration was held to protest alleged election irregularities — establishes the democratic importance of the subject matter.

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts strictly necessary; element not discussed.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis: the Court found that forcible dispersal of a peaceful demonstration, solely because it ran slightly over time, with no evidence of danger to public order, was disproportionate. The irreducible facts are:

- F008: The demonstration was held at Galaba Square on 26 November 2005 — establishes the assembly.
- F009: The demonstration was scheduled from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. — establishes the authorised time frame and the basis for the police action.
- F011: Police began a forcible dispersal operation shortly after 5 p.m. — establishes the interference.
- F012: The sole reason for the dispersal was that the demonstration continued beyond the authorised time — this is the critical fact: it shows the intervention lacked sufficient justification (no violence, no disorder).
- F013: The dispersal was conducted by riot police units — establishes the coercive character of the intervention.
- F014: Riot police were fully equipped with helmets, shields and truncheons — establishes the intensity/severity of the intervention.
- F015: The applicant participated in the demonstration — establishes her standing and that the interference affected her.
- F010: The demonstration was held to protest alleged election irregularities — establishes the democratic importance of the subject matter, which the Court explicitly weighs against the authorities' impatience.
- F016: The applicant was walking slowly to the edge of the square to go home — establishes her individual conduct, accepted by default, reinforcing the disproportionality finding.
