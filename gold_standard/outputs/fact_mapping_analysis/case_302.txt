LIST 1: Factual grounding mapped to atomic facts

**Scope — Assembly exists**
The Court's reasoning text refers to 'demonstrations' and 'political rallies' without making an independent factual finding, but treats their existence as given. The atomic facts that correspond to this are:
- F007: 'The applicants were arrested and convicted under different provisions of the Code of Administrative Offences with a view to either punishing them for participating in peaceful demonstrations or preventing them from doing so.'
- F009: 'The applicant, together with a number of other members of the Popular Front Party of Azerbaijan, was arrested during the dispersal of an unauthorised demonstration held on 23 November 2006.'
F007 is the general fact applicable to all applicants; F009 is the specific fact for application no. 39919/07.

**Scope — Participation**
The Court's reasoning text refers to the applicants 'participating in demonstrations.' The atomic facts that correspond to this are:
- F007: same as above — the arrests were with a view to punishing participation or preventing it.
- F009: the applicant was arrested during the dispersal of a demonstration, implying presence and participation.
- F005: 'The applicants were members of various opposition parties and movements at the material time.' This supports the plausibility of participation but is not directly cited by the Court.

**Scope — Peaceful**
The Court makes no explicit factual finding on peacefulness. The only atomic fact that touches on this is:
- F007: which refers to 'peaceful demonstrations.'

**Interference**
The Court explicitly grounds interference in the arrest and administrative proceedings. The atomic facts that correspond to this are:
- F006: 'The applicants were arrested and convicted under different provisions of the Code of Administrative Offences.'
- F007: 'The applicants were arrested and convicted under different provisions of the Code of Administrative Offences with a view to either punishing them for participating in peaceful demonstrations or preventing them from doing so.'
- F009: arrest during dispersal of demonstration (for application no. 39919/07).
- F010: conviction by Sabail District Court (for application no. 39919/07).
- F011: sentence of ten days' administrative detention (for application no. 39919/07).

**Necessity (implied)**
The Court's finding of a chilling effect and discouragement from participating in political rallies is grounded in the same facts as interference, plus the purposive element:
- F007: the arrests were with a view to punishing participation or preventing it — this is the key fact supporting the finding that the measures had a chilling effect.
- F006: the arrests and convictions under the Code of Administrative Offences.
- F009, F010, F011: specific facts for application no. 39919/07.

**Prescribed by law, Legitimate aim, Positive obligation**
No factual grounding can be identified in the reasoning text for these elements, and no atomic facts correspond to them in the Court's reasoning.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**
The strictly necessary fact is the one that establishes that there were peaceful demonstrations and that the applicants participated in them:
- F007: this is the most critical and representative fact, as it applies to all applicants and explicitly refers to 'peaceful demonstrations' and the applicants' participation (or intended participation).

**Interference**
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing the arrest and administrative proceedings as acts of the state directed at the applicants:
- F007: establishes that the arrests and convictions were with a view to punishing participation or preventing it — this is the purposive element that makes the acts an interference with assembly rights rather than merely incidental law enforcement.
- F006: establishes the fact of arrest and conviction under the Code of Administrative Offences.

**Prescribed by law**
No atomic facts are strictly necessary — the Court does not discuss this element.

**Legitimate aim**
No atomic facts are strictly necessary — the Court does not discuss this element.

**Necessity**
The strictly necessary fact is the one that supports the finding of a chilling effect and disproportionate impact on assembly rights:
- F007: the purposive element — arrests were with a view to punishing participation or preventing it — is what drives the finding that the measures had a chilling effect and were not necessary in a democratic society. This is the single most critical fact.

**Positive obligation**
No atomic facts are strictly necessary — the Court does not discuss this element.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two essential points: (1) that there were peaceful demonstrations in which the applicants participated or sought to participate, and (2) that the state arrested and convicted the applicants with the purpose of punishing or preventing that participation, thereby producing a chilling effect on freedom of assembly. The single atomic fact that captures both of these points most precisely and is strictly necessary for the conclusion is:
- F007: 'The applicants were arrested and convicted under different provisions of the Code of Administrative Offences with a view to either punishing them for participating in peaceful demonstrations or preventing them from doing so.'

This fact is the irreducible core: it establishes the existence of peaceful demonstrations (scope), the applicants' participation (scope), the state act (interference), and the punitive/preventive purpose (necessity/chilling effect). Without this fact, the Court could not have reached its conclusion. F005 (opposition membership) provides background context but is not strictly necessary. F006 establishes the arrest and conviction but without the purposive element of F007 it would not suffice. F009-F011 are application-specific instantiations of F007 for one applicant only and are subsumed by F007 at the general level.

The irreducible core is therefore: F007, supported by F006 as the factual basis for the state act itself.
