LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Scope (peaceful)**
The Court's grounding quote: 'none of the official records or the domestic court decisions submitted by the parties suggests that the demonstration of 2 April 2011 was violent. Therefore the Court accepts the assertion of the applicants in applications nos. 67977/11, 69411/11 and 69421/11 that that demonstration had been intended to be peaceful and was conducted in a peaceful manner up to the point of their arrest.'
Atomic facts: F018 (demonstrations intended to be peaceful), F019 (demonstrations conducted in a peaceful manner), F024 (police started to disperse shortly after demonstrations began — implies no prior violence), F025 (applicants arrested during dispersal).

The Court's further grounding quote (adopted from Gafgaz Mammadov): 'there was a lack of relevant and sufficient reasons justifying the dispersal of the demonstration, which had been intended to be peaceful and had been conducted in a peaceful manner (ibid., § 61)'
Atomic facts: F018, F019.

**Scope (participation)**
The Court's grounding: 'the act of participating in an unauthorised peaceful demonstration was by itself protected by Article 11 of the Convention (ibid., §§ 63-64)' and the framing of the complaint around arrest and conviction.
Atomic facts: F023 (each applicant attended one of the demonstrations), F025 (applicants arrested during dispersal), F054 (applicants convicted under Article 310.1 CAO).

**Scope (assembly exists)**
The Court's grounding: references to 'the demonstration of 2 April 2011' and 'the dispersal of the demonstrations by the police.'
Atomic facts: F003 (31 July 2010 demonstration planned), F004 (2 April 2011 demonstration planned), F005 (17 April 2011 demonstration planned), F017 (organisers decided to hold demonstrations in centre of Baku notwithstanding refusals), F024 (police dispersed demonstrations shortly after they began).

**Interference**
The Court's grounding: 'the dispersal of the demonstrations by the police and their arrest and conviction for an administrative offence.'
Atomic facts: F024 (police dispersed demonstrations), F025 (applicants arrested during dispersal), F054 (applicants convicted under Article 310.1 CAO), F055 (applicants sentenced to administrative detention).

**Prescribed by law**
The Court's grounding (adopted from Gafgaz Mammadov):
'(i) there were serious concerns about the foreseeability and precision of the legislation governing public assemblies, and about the possibility of public assemblies being abusively banned or dispersed (ibid., § 55)'
Atomic facts: F072 (relevant domestic law provisions summarised in Gafgaz Mammadov), F009 (BCEA refused to authorise 31 July 2010 demonstration), F013 (BCEA refused to authorise 2 April 2011 demonstration), F014 (BCEA refused to authorise 17 April 2011 demonstration).

'(ii) there were doubts about the credibility of the formal grounds, namely Article 310.1 of the CAO, relied on by the authorities to arrest and convict the participant in an unauthorised demonstration (ibid., §§ 56 and 62)'
Atomic facts: F030 (administrative offence reports stated applicants had committed offence under Article 310.1 CAO by deliberately failing to comply with lawful police orders), F053 (courts found applicants had failed to stop participating in unauthorised demonstrations), F054 (applicants convicted under Article 310.1 CAO), F072.

**Necessity**
The Court's grounding (adopted from Gafgaz Mammadov):
'(iii) the authorities had failed to take into consideration the fact that the demonstration had been notified (ibid., § 60)'
Atomic facts: F006 (notice given for 31 July 2010 demonstration), F007 (notice given for 2 April 2011 demonstration), F008 (notice given for 17 April 2011 demonstration).

'(iv) there was a lack of relevant and sufficient reasons justifying the dispersal of the demonstration, which had been intended to be peaceful and had been conducted in a peaceful manner (ibid., § 61)'
Atomic facts: F018, F019, F024.

'(v) there had been no acknowledgment that the act of participating in an unauthorised peaceful demonstration was by itself protected by Article 11 of the Convention (ibid., §§ 63-64)'
Atomic facts: F023, F053, F054.

Additionally, the Court's own finding on peacefulness feeding into necessity: 'none of the official records or the domestic court decisions submitted by the parties suggests that the demonstration of 2 April 2011 was violent.'
Atomic facts: F018, F019.

**Legitimate aim**
No factual grounding identified in the Court's own assessment. The Government's assertion is recorded in submissions but not adopted by the Court. No atomic facts can be mapped here from the Court's own reasoning.

**Positive obligation**
Not discussed. No atomic facts to map.

LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of law

**Scope**
For assembly exists: F017 (organisers held demonstrations despite refusals — establishes that assemblies actually took place), F024 (police dispersed them — confirms they were underway).
For participation: F023 (each applicant attended one of the demonstrations).
For peaceful: F018 (demonstrations intended to be peaceful — the Court explicitly accepted this), F019 (demonstrations conducted in a peaceful manner — the Court explicitly accepted this). The absence of violence in official records is the Court's stated basis; F018 and F019 are the applicants' assertions the Court accepted precisely because no official record contradicted them.

**Interference**
F024 (police dispersed demonstrations), F054 (applicants convicted under Article 310.1 CAO), F055 (applicants sentenced to administrative detention — the Government itself noted the sanction was administrative detention, and the Court treats this as the interference).

**Prescribed by law**
F072 (the relevant domestic law is summarised in Gafgaz Mammadov — this is the Court's own reference point for the legislative framework), F030 (administrative offence reports relied on Article 310.1 CAO — basis for doubts about credibility of formal grounds), F013 (BCEA refused to authorise 2 April 2011 demonstration — illustrates the authorisation system the Court found problematic).

**Legitimate aim**
No atomic facts strictly necessary — the Court made no independent finding on this element.

**Necessity**
F007 (notice given for 2 April 2011 demonstration — the Court specifically found authorities failed to consider that the demonstration had been notified; this is the most directly relevant notification fact given the Court's focus on the 2 April 2011 demonstration), F018 (demonstrations intended to be peaceful), F019 (demonstrations conducted in a peaceful manner), F024 (police dispersed demonstrations — the act whose justification was found lacking), F053 (courts found applicants had failed to stop participating in unauthorised demonstrations — shows no acknowledgment of Article 11 protection), F054 (applicants convicted under Article 310.1 CAO — confirms no acknowledgment that peaceful participation is protected).

**Positive obligation**
No atomic facts — not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on: (1) the demonstrations were peaceful and the authorities had no sufficient justification for dispersing them; (2) prior notification had been given but ignored; (3) the applicants were arrested and convicted merely for participating in an unauthorised but peaceful demonstration, with no acknowledgment that such participation is protected by Article 11.

The strictly necessary atomic facts are:
F007 (notice given for 2 April 2011 demonstration — authorities failed to consider this),
F017 (organisers held demonstrations despite refusals — establishes the 'unauthorised' character that triggered the response),
F018 (demonstrations intended to be peaceful),
F019 (demonstrations conducted in a peaceful manner),
F023 (each applicant attended one of the demonstrations),
F024 (police dispersed demonstrations),
F054 (applicants convicted under Article 310.1 CAO — the conviction is the core interference),
F055 (applicants sentenced to administrative detention — the sanction imposed).
