LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of law

**Scope:**
- F003: Establishes that a demonstration took place — necessary to confirm an assembly existed.
- F013: Establishes that the applicant attended the demonstration — necessary to confirm participation.
- F009: Establishes the peaceful intent of the demonstration — necessary for the peaceful character finding.
- F010: Establishes the peaceful conduct of the demonstration — necessary for the peaceful character finding.
- F008: Establishes the unauthorised nature of the demonstration — necessary context for the Court's finding that participation in an unauthorised peaceful assembly is itself protected.

**Interference:**
- F014: Establishes the dispersal by police — the primary act of interference with the assembly.
- F015: Establishes the applicant's arrest — direct interference with his individual right.
- F064: Establishes the final conviction under Article 310.1 — the legally operative interference (the second set of proceedings produced the final conviction).
- F065: Establishes the sentence of administrative detention — the concrete consequence of the interference.

**Prescribed by law:**
- F006: The BCEA's refusal to authorise the demonstration — illustrates the operation of the legislative framework and its potential for arbitrary application.
- F008: The organisers proceeded despite the refusal — establishes the context in which the law was applied.
- F023: The applicant was charged under Article 310.1 — identifies the specific legal provision whose credibility the Court doubted.
- F027: The applicant's name allegedly added later in different handwriting — supports the finding of doubts about the credibility of the formal grounds and lack of safeguards against abuse.
- F075: Discrepancy between times of arrest in the two official documents — further supports doubts about the credibility of the formal grounds.

**Legitimate aim:**
No atomic facts strictly necessary — the Court made no independent finding on this element.

- F010: The demonstration was conducted in a peaceful manner — necessary for the same finding.
- F014: The police dispersed the demonstration — the act whose necessity was assessed.
- F064: The final conviction under Article 310.1 — the act whose necessity was assessed at the individual level.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following facts:

1. F003: A demonstration took place — without this, there is no assembly and no Article 11 issue.
2. F013: The applicant attended the demonstration — without this, he has no standing under Article 11.
3. F009 and F010: The demonstration was peaceful — this is the critical qualifier that brings it within Article 11 protection and undermines the justification for dispersal.
4. F004: Prior notice was given to the BCEA — the Court explicitly identified the authorities' failure to consider this as a key reason for the violation.
5. F006: The BCEA refused to authorise the demonstration at the requested location — establishes the unauthorised character and the operation of the legislative framework.
6. F014: The police dispersed the demonstration — the primary act of interference.
7. F015: The applicant was arrested — the act of interference at the individual level.
8. F064: The applicant was convicted under Article 310.1 — the legally operative conclusion of the interference, and the provision whose credibility the Court doubted.
9. F027: The applicant's name allegedly added later in different handwriting — supports the finding of doubts about the formal grounds for arrest and conviction, undermining the prescribed-by-law and necessity analysis.
10. F075: Discrepancy between the times of arrest in the two official documents — further supports the same finding.
