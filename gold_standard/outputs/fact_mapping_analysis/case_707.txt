LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion, the Court must establish at minimum: (1) that there were public assemblies in which the applicants participated or which they organised (scope), and (2) that governmental measures were taken against them in that context (interference). Both F002 and F003 are strictly necessary: F002 establishes that some applicants were participants subjected to measures, and F003 establishes that others were organisers subjected to measures. Without both, the Court could not address the full applicant group. F001 and F004 are not necessary — F001 merely references an appended table, and F004 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions. The irreducible core is therefore F002 and F003.
