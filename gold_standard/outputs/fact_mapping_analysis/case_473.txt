LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts per element

**Scope**
The Court needed to establish: (i) a gathering took place (assembly exists); (ii) the applicant participated; (iii) it was peaceful.
- F003: seven people decided to hold a flash mob — establishes the collective nature and number of participants.
- F005: positioned at Gorbatyy Bridge — establishes the stationary, public character of the gathering.
- F006: each participant held a blank sheet of paper — establishes the non-threatening, expressive character.
- F007: each participant had mouth covered with adhesive tape — establishes the silent, non-violent character.
- F032: present for ten to fifteen minutes — establishes the brief, non-disruptive character (peaceful sub-element).

**Interference**
- F008: police ordered dispersal — the first act of interference.
- F013: basis was failure to notify — establishes the nature and legal ground of the interference.
- F014: conviction by justice of the peace — the formal sanction.
- F015: fine of 1,000 roubles (EUR 22) — the concrete penalty imposed.

**Prescribed by law (implied concerns)**
- F043: definition of static demonstration — the Court's critique of breadth rests on this definition.
- F044: one or more citizens with means of visual expression — the Court's observation that blank sheets could qualify as 'means of visual expression' rests on this.
- F045: notification required no later than three days before — the Court's observation about the rigid prior-notification requirement.
- F048: no notification for one-person static demonstrations — the Court explicitly references this exemption to illustrate the breadth of the rule.
- F049–F057 (represented by F049 as the most critical): notification must contain a lengthy list of elements — the Court explicitly references this list to illustrate the burden.

**Legitimate aim**
No atomic facts strictly necessary — the Court does not formally resolve this element and adopts no facts of its own to ground it.

**Necessity**
- F003: seven people — small number, relevant to absence of threat.
- F006: blank sheets of paper — non-threatening means of expression.
- F007: mouths taped — silent, non-violent conduct.
- F013: sole basis for sanction was failure to notify — critical to the Court's finding that enforcement was an end in itself.
- F014: conviction — the domestic judicial act whose proportionality is assessed.
- F022: justice of the peace found voluntary participation in static demonstration in breach of Public Events Act — establishes that the domestic court focused only on formal unlawfulness.
- F027: District Court upheld the conviction — confirms that neither level of jurisdiction conducted a proportionality assessment.
- F033: District Court found actions met criteria of Article 20.2 § 2 — confirms the purely formal basis of the domestic judicial reasoning.

**Positive obligation**
No atomic facts — element not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two interlocking findings: (1) the assembly was peaceful and caused no disorder, yet (2) the authorities dispersed it and sanctioned the applicant solely on the basis of failure to notify, without any proportionality assessment. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

- F003: seven people held a flash mob — establishes the assembly and its small scale.
- F006: blank sheets of paper — establishes the non-threatening, expressive character of the assembly.
- F007: mouths taped — establishes the silent, peaceful character.
- F008: police ordered dispersal — the State's interference.
- F013: sole basis for the charge was failure to notify — the critical fact showing enforcement was an end in itself.
- F014: conviction by justice of the peace — the formal sanction.
- F015: fine imposed — the concrete penalty (also relevant to admissibility).
- F022: justice of the peace found voluntary participation in static demonstration in breach of Public Events Act — establishes that the domestic court focused exclusively on formal unlawfulness, not on proportionality.
- F027: District Court upheld the conviction — confirms the absence of any proportionality review at either level of jurisdiction.
