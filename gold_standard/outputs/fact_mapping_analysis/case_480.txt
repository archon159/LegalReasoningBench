LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of law

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish: (i) an assembly existed, (ii) the applicant participated, (iii) it was peaceful.
- Assembly: F002 (group gathered), F003 (march held) — these establish the assembly.
- Participation: F005 (applicant delivered speech at end of march) — most direct evidence of participation; F004 (applicant was Vice-President of BDP) is background but F005 is the critical fact.
- Peaceful: F006 (content of speech — no violent content) is the most critical fact establishing peaceful nature of applicant's conduct. F010 (slogans) is relevant only insofar as the Court dismisses them as non-violent and not attributable to the applicant.

Strictly necessary: F002, F003, F005, F006, F010

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's assembly rights.
- F016 (conviction) is the core governmental act constituting interference.
- F017 (sentence) establishes the severity/reality of the interference.
- F018 (suspension of pronouncement) is necessary because the Government contested victim status on this basis and the Court explicitly addresses it.

Strictly necessary: F016, F017, F018

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court simply names the statutory provision.
- F008 (indicted under section 28(1) of Law no. 2911) and F016 (convicted under same provision) together establish the legal basis.
- F016 is the most critical (it is the act that constitutes the interference and is grounded in the law).

Strictly necessary: F016 (conviction under section 28(1)); F008 also supports this.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court asserts prevention of disorder without grounding it in specific facts. The most relevant contextual facts are:
- F019 (no prior notification) — the absence of notification is the domestic legal basis for unlawfulness, which connects to disorder prevention.

Strictly necessary: F019 (as the factual basis for the unlawfulness finding that underlies the disorder-prevention aim)

--- NECESSITY ---
This is where the case turns. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F019 (no prior notification — the sole or main reason for unlawfulness, which the Court finds insufficient to justify conviction)
- F006 (content of speech — establishes no incitement to violence, key to finding the demonstration peaceful and the conviction disproportionate)
- F010 (slogans — raised by Government, dismissed by Court as not attributable to applicant and not constituting incitement)
- F017 (sentence of one year and three months — establishes severity of sanction, key to disproportionality finding)
- F018 (suspension of pronouncement — Court explicitly notes this does not eliminate the threat, relevant to proportionality)

Strictly necessary: F006, F010, F017, F018, F019

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. No atomic facts apply.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality analysis. The Court found a violation because: (a) the demonstration was peaceful and the only basis for unlawfulness was lack of prior notice; (b) the applicant's own conduct (speech) contained no violent or inciting content; (c) the slogans were not attributable to him and were not incitement; (d) despite suspension, the applicant faced a severe threatened sanction. The existence of the assembly and the applicant's participation are threshold facts without which there is no Article 11 case at all.

Irreducible core:
- F003: march held (assembly existed — threshold fact)
- F005: applicant delivered speech (participation — threshold fact)
- F006: content of speech (peaceful nature of applicant's conduct, no incitement — decisive for necessity)
- F010: slogans chanted (raised by Government as justification; dismissed by Court — relevant to necessity)
- F016: conviction (the interference — without this there is no case)
- F017: sentence of one year and three months (severity of sanction — decisive for disproportionality)
- F018: suspension of pronouncement (Court explicitly holds this does not eliminate the threat — necessary to counter Government's victim status objection and relevant to proportionality)
- F019: no prior notification (sole/main basis for unlawfulness — Court holds this alone insufficient to justify interference)
