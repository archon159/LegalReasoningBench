LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Scope (assembly exists, participation, peaceful)**
The factual grounding quote was: "disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers or participants of public assemblies, namely their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."
This maps to:
- F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers of public assemblies." — directly corresponds to the "organisers" part of the quote.
- F003: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies." — directly corresponds to the "participants" part of the quote.
Note: The reference to "public assemblies" and their "dispersal" is contained within F002 and F003 as the context for the measures. There is no separate atomic fact establishing the peaceful character of the assemblies; F004 is too generic to serve as grounding.

**Interference**
The factual grounding quote was: "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."
This maps to:
- F002 and F003 again, as these are the only atomic facts that describe the measures taken against the applicants in the context of public assemblies. The specific acts (arrest, dispersal, conviction) are embedded in the Court's own characterisation, which draws on F002 and F003.

**Necessity**
The factual grounding was the Court's statement that it had "examined all the material submitted to it" and found no new fact or argument distinguishing the case from the leading cases, combined with the general characterisation of the measures as arrests, dispersals, and administrative convictions.
This maps to:
- F002 and F003, as the only atomic facts describing the nature of the measures and the context of public assemblies.
- F001 could be noted as the source of case-specific details ("the appended table"), but since the Court does not reproduce those details in the reasoning section and the appended table is not provided, F001 cannot be directly mapped to any specific factual grounding quote.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on two things: (1) that there were public assemblies in which the applicants were involved as organisers or participants, and (2) that the state took measures against them (arrest, dispersal, administrative conviction) in that context, which the Court found not necessary in a democratic society by analogy with leading cases. The irreducible factual core is therefore:
- F002: establishes that applicants were organisers of public assemblies subjected to disproportionate measures — essential for the scope and interference findings.
- F003: establishes that applicants were participants of public assemblies subjected to disproportionate measures — essential for the scope and interference findings.
Without F002 and F003, the Court could not have found that there was an assembly, that the applicants participated, or that there was an interference — all of which are prerequisites for the necessity finding and the ultimate violation conclusion. F001 and F004 are not strictly necessary for the conclusion: F001 is a procedural reference to the appended table, and F004 concerns other complaints unrelated to Article 11.
