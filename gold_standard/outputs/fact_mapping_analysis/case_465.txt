LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality assessment. The violation was found because: (a) the domestic courts failed to properly assess the circumstances of the applicant's conduct, relying on late police testimony and dismissing defence evidence without explanation; (b) the applicant's conduct was of limited gravity and reactive in nature; (c) the sentence was disproportionately severe, with the mass disorder conviction adding punishment for the same acts already covered by the Article 318 conviction; and (d) the chilling effect on assembly rights.

The irreducible factual core is:
F012: Establishes that there was an assembly and the applicant participated — necessary for Article 11 to be engaged at all.
F064: Applicant found guilty — necessary to establish the interference.
F085: Sentence of two years and six months — necessary to establish severity of interference and disproportionality.
F086: Sentence structure — necessary to establish that the Article 212 sentence was more severe than Article 318 and that the same acts were double-counted.
F069: Violence did not endanger life or health — necessary to establish limited gravity of applicant's acts.
F022: Police testimony given years after events and after watching video — necessary to establish procedural deficiency in domestic proceedings.
F062: Ms N. testified applicant was pulled out of crowd — necessary to establish existence of dismissed exculpatory evidence.
F063: Ms N. testified applicant had neither acted aggressively nor resisted arrest — necessary to establish content of dismissed exculpatory evidence.
F084: Court dismissed Ms N.'s testimony without comparative assessment — necessary to establish failure of procedural safeguards.
F066: Mass disorder conviction grounded in same acts as Article 318 conviction — necessary to establish double-counting and disproportionality of the Article 212 sentence.
F080: Video showed applicant approaching police already arresting someone else — necessary to establish that applicant was not among initial aggressors.
F013: Applicant found himself surrounded by police beating protestors — necessary to establish reactive/spontaneous nature of conduct.
