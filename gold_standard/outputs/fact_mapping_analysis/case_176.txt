Scope: F002 is strictly necessary. It establishes that the applicants were participants in public assemblies that were dispersed — this is the minimum factual basis for the Court to imply that an assembly existed, that the applicants participated, and that the Court proceeded to analyze the case (implying peacefulness). F001 is not strictly necessary for the legal reasoning itself.

Interference: F002 is strictly necessary. The description of dispersal, arrest, and conviction for administrative offences is the factual basis for the implied finding of interference.

Prescribed by law: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element was not discussed.

Legitimate aim: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element was not discussed.

Necessity: F002 is strictly necessary. It provides the factual context (participation in public assemblies, dispersal, arrest, conviction) against which the Court assessed necessity by reference to its prior case-law. Without this, there would be no factual basis for the necessity conclusion.

Positive obligation: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element was not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The final conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on the fact that the applicants were participants in public assemblies that were dispersed and that they were arrested and convicted for administrative offences — i.e., that there were state measures interfering with their assembly rights. This is captured entirely by F002. F001 merely identifies the applicants and F003 concerns other complaints irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, the irreducible factual core is F002 alone.
