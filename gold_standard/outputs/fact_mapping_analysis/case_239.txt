LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turns on: (i) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope, implied); (ii) the existence of state interference in the form of dispersal, arrest, and conviction (interference, implied from the same fact); and (iii) the finding that this interference was not necessary in a democratic society (necessity, grounded in case-law rather than new facts). The only atomic fact that is strictly necessary to reach this conclusion — as grounded in the Court's own assessment — is F002, which establishes both the assemblies and the measures taken against the applicants. Without F002, the Court could not have identified either the assemblies or the interference, and the violation conclusion would have no factual foundation. F001 is procedurally necessary to identify the applicants but does not bear on the substantive conclusion. F003 is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
Atomic facts: F002
