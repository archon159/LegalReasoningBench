--- SCOPE ---

Quote: "the dismissal by the police of the applicant's notification of his intention to hold a demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of his rights under Article 11 of the Convention."
Atomic facts: F009 (applicant and others intended to hold a demonstration), F011 (applicant notified the Budapest Police Department), F012 (Police Department dismissed the notification).

Quote: "In the present case, there is no indication – nor has it been argued by the Government – that the applicant or other potential participants would have had violent intentions."
Atomic facts: F009 (intended demonstration), F010 (purpose was to call on the President not to assent — a political, non-violent purpose). No atomic fact directly states the absence of violent intentions; this is a negative finding. F009 and F010 are the closest factual anchors, as they describe the nature and purpose of the intended assembly.

--- INTERFERENCE ---

Quote: "the dismissal by the police of the applicant's notification of his intention to hold a demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of his rights under Article 11 of the Convention."
Atomic facts: F011 (notification submitted), F012 (notification dismissed on the evening of 11 March 2013).

Quote: "the President of Hungary had five days – that is to say, until 16 March 2013 – to assent to the Amendment of 11 March 2013 and that the area in front of the President's residence was closed down for the entirety of this period."
Atomic facts: F008 (President had five days to assent), F013 (Anti-Terror Centre closed the area from 8 to 16 March 2013).

Quote: "the only option left to the applicant would have been to hold an unauthorised gathering, that is, without a presumption of legality... Inevitably, the state of affairs complained of resulted in a chilling effect on the freedom of assembly."
Atomic facts: F012 (dismissal of notification), F013 (area closed), F014 (area declared unavailable for demonstrations). These facts together establish that the applicant had no lawful avenue to proceed.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---

Quote: "the Police Department in essence argued that the fact that the Anti-Terror Centre had closed that area down for the period in question meant that it was excluded from the scope of the law on peaceful assembly."
Atomic facts: F013 (Anti-Terror Centre closed the area from 8 to 16 March 2013), F014 (Police Department stated the area was no longer a public area available for demonstrations).

Quote: "on 18 March 2013 the Budapest Administrative and Labour Court quashed the impugned police decision, reproaching the Police Department for failing to provide the precise reasons for the underlying measure taken by the Anti-Terror Centre."
Atomic facts: F017 (court quashed the police decision on 18 March 2013), F018 (court held that the Police Department failed to provide precise reasons why the Anti-Terror Centre had closed the area).

Quote: "In the Court's view, that court ruling effectively, if retroactively, removed the legal basis of the impugned measure... the interference was devoid of a basis in domestic law and cannot as such be regarded as 'prescribed by law'."
Atomic facts: F017 (court quashed the decision), F018 (court's reasoning: failure to provide precise reasons), F019 (court held the Police Department had erroneously characterised the demonstration as outside the scope of the law on peaceful demonstrations).

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish: (i) an assembly was planned; (ii) the applicant sought to participate; (iii) it was peaceful.
Strictly necessary: F009 (intended demonstration by applicant and others), F010 (political, non-violent purpose — grounding the peacefulness finding), F011 (applicant notified police, confirming his participation).

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needed to establish that a governmental act prevented the applicant from exercising his Article 11 rights, and that the judicial remedy did not remove victim status.
Strictly necessary: F012 (dismissal of notification — the core act of interference), F008 (five-day window — establishing the time-sensitive nature making the interference irreversible), F013 (area closed for the entire relevant period — establishing the practical impossibility of holding the demonstration), F017 (court ruling came after the window closed — establishing the remedy was ineffective in time), F022 (damages claim dismissed — establishing no compensatory redress).

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court needed to establish that the legal basis for the interference was defective, as confirmed by the domestic court.
Strictly necessary: F013 (Anti-Terror Centre closure — the asserted legal basis), F014 (Police Department's characterisation of the area as unavailable — the operative decision), F017 (domestic court quashed the decision — removing the legal basis), F018 (domestic court's reasoning: failure to provide precise reasons — the specific defect identified).
F019 is also relevant (court found erroneous characterisation) but F018 is the more direct grounding for the Court's conclusion that no valid legal basis existed.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
Not examined. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

--- NECESSITY ---
Not examined. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following chain: (1) the applicant planned a peaceful, time-sensitive political demonstration; (2) the police dismissed his notification based on an area closure; (3) the domestic court later quashed that decision for lack of adequate legal reasoning; (4) by the time the court ruled, the demonstration had become impossible; (5) no compensatory remedy succeeded. The violation was found solely on the 'prescribed by law' ground, with the ineffectiveness of the remedy also relevant to victim status.

Irreducible facts:
- F009: applicant and others intended to hold a demonstration (establishes the assembly)
- F010: purpose was political and non-violent (grounds the peacefulness finding)
- F011: applicant notified the police (establishes his attempt to participate lawfully)
- F012: police dismissed the notification (the act of interference)
- F008: President had five days to assent (establishes the time-sensitive window making the interference irreversible)
- F013: Anti-Terror Centre closed the area from 8 to 16 March 2013 (the asserted legal basis for the interference)
- F017: domestic court quashed the police decision on 18 March 2013 — after the window closed (removes the legal basis and establishes the remedy was too late)
- F018: domestic court found the Police Department failed to provide precise reasons (the specific legal defect establishing the interference was not prescribed by law)
- F022: damages claim dismissed (establishes no compensatory redress, supporting victim status)
