LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish: (i) an assembly existed; (ii) the applicant participated; (iii) the assembly (and the applicant) was peaceful.

For assembly existence: F018 (assembly approved), F023 (working meeting confirming planning), F089 (march began), F090 (march proceeded peacefully)
For applicant's participation: F315 (applicant arrived at Bolotnaya Square), F316 (applicant stood in front of stage within designated area)
For peaceful character of assembly generally: F107 (sit-in involved only 20-50 people), F228 (no incidents in sector adjacent to stage), F317 (area around applicant remained peaceful)
For peaceful character of applicant personally: F316, F317, F319 (remained within authorised area in general confusion), F329 (charged only with disobeying orders, not violence)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needed to establish that governmental acts restricted the applicant's Article 11 rights.

Key facts: F097 (park excluded from venue, restricting assembly space), F098 (riot police cordon barred access to park), F123 (meeting declared over at 6 p.m.), F003 (applicant arrested during dispersal), F004 (detained at police station), F005 (found guilty of failure to obey lawful police orders), F006 (sentenced to fifteen days' detention), F342 (conviction upheld)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court only briefly touched on this element in the context of the termination procedure.

Key facts: F362 (section 15 of Public Events Act requiring suspension before termination), F366 (section 17(3) emergency termination provision), F123 (meeting declared over without prior suspension step)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court assumed rather than found a legitimate aim, referencing public safety and the 'fear of Maidan'.

Key facts: F033 (warning against erecting tents), F034 (warning against camping tents), F073 (park cordoned off to prevent campsite), F296 (Zdorenko testified park cordoned off to prevent campsite)

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court found the measures disproportionate both as to the termination of the assembly and as to the applicant's arrest and conviction.

Key facts: F117 (disorder localised at Malyy Kamennyy Bridge), F123 (meeting declared over), F228 (no incidents in sector adjacent to stage), F316 (applicant within designated peaceful area), F317 (area around applicant remained peaceful), F319 (applicant remained in general confusion, unable to leave immediately), F322 (no traffic at time of arrest), F329 (charge based on printed template), F342 (sentenced to fifteen days), F156 (656 people detained, broad media coverage)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
The Court's primary finding of violation rested on the failure to communicate with assembly organisers.

Key facts: F027 (organisers claimed agreement to replicate 4 February 2012 layout), F029 (park included in 4 February 2012 venue), F039 (published police map included park), F040 (published map marked access through park), F042 (security plan not shared with organisers), F066 (no officer designated to liaise with assembly organisers), F096 (leaders found layout did not correspond to expectations), F097 (park excluded from venue on 6 May 2012), F098 (riot police cordon barred access to park), F104 (cordon officers did not discuss with protest leaders, no senior officer delegated), F107 (sit-in peaceful and localised), F110 (no senior police or municipal officials came to sit-down protest between 5.20 and 5.45 p.m.), F169 (security plan excluded park), F171 (security plan not shared with organisers), F274 (Biryukov told Ombudsman it was outside his powers), F294 (Makhonin arrived only after mass disorder began)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two separate violations: (1) failure to discharge positive obligation to ensure peaceful conduct of assembly; (2) disproportionate measures against the applicant personally.

For violation 1 (positive obligation):
- F029: Park was included in the 4 February 2012 venue, establishing the baseline expectation
- F039: Police published a map including the park, creating a legitimate expectation
- F042: Security plan (which excluded the park) was not shared with organisers
- F097: Park was in fact excluded from the venue on 6 May 2012, contrary to expectations
- F098: Riot police cordon barred access to park
- F104: No senior officer was delegated to negotiate with protest leaders at the cordon
- F110: No senior police or municipal officials came to the sit-down protest between 5.20 and 5.45 p.m.
- F274: Biryukov told the Ombudsman the decision on the cordon was outside his powers
- F294: Makhonin arrived only after mass disorder had already begun

For violation 2 (disproportionate measures against applicant):
- F315: Applicant arrived at Bolotnaya Square at around 6 p.m. to take part in the meeting
- F316: Applicant stood in front of stage within designated meeting area
- F317: Area around applicant remained peaceful
- F319: Applicant remained within authorised area in general confusion
- F322: No traffic at Bolotnaya Square at time of arrest
- F342: Applicant sentenced to fifteen days' administrative detention
- F156: 656 people detained, attracting broad media coverage (chilling effect)
