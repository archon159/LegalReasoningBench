LIST 1: Factual grounding for each element, mapped to atomic facts

**Scope (assembly exists, participation, peaceful)**

The Court's factual grounding for scope was drawn almost entirely from the prior judgment Mushegh Saghatelyan. In this judgment, the Court states: "it has already examined and dismissed a similar claim by the Government, finding that there was not sufficient and convincing evidence to conclude that the organisers and the participants of the assembly at Freedom Square had had violent intentions and that the assembly in question had not been peaceful." It also states: "the applicant, who was the main leader of the demonstrations held at Freedom Square and was, moreover, on site during the dispersal."

- Assembly exists: The assembly at Freedom Square is referenced throughout. The relevant atomic facts establishing that an assembly took place are: F009 (applicant called supporters to gather at Freedom Square), F012 (nationwide daily protest rallies from 20 February 2008), F013 (main meeting place was Freedom Square), F014 (rallies attracted tens of thousands), F015 (several hundred demonstrators stayed around the clock).
- Participation: The Court explicitly states the applicant was "the main leader of the demonstrations held at Freedom Square and was, moreover, on site during the dispersal." Relevant atomic facts: F016 (applicant participated as opposition leader), F017 (applicant gave speeches), F018 (applicant called on supporters to continue), F019 (applicant stayed at Freedom Square around the clock), F039 (applicant walked to statue during police operation), F043 (applicant addressed demonstrators during police operation).
- Peaceful: The Court relies on the prior finding that there was no sufficient evidence of violent intentions. The relevant atomic facts supporting the peaceful nature (and rebutting the Government's claim of violence) are: F043 (applicant urged demonstrators to be peaceful and keep distance), F044 (demonstrators followed his request), F046 (police attacked without prior warning), F147 (police made no audible demand to disperse).

**Interference**

The Court states: "the dispersal of the assembly at Freedom Square had interfered with the right to freedom of assembly of its participants" and "the applicant, who was the main leader of the demonstrations held at Freedom Square and was, moreover, on site during the dispersal." Relevant atomic facts: F035 (police arrived at Freedom Square on 1 March 2008), F046 (police attacked demonstrators without warning), F047 (police beat demonstrators and destroyed camp), F048 (demonstrators pushed out of Freedom Square), F016 (applicant participated as leader), F019 (applicant stayed at Freedom Square), F039 (applicant was on site during dispersal).

**Prescribed by law**

The Court explicitly declined to decide this element. No factual grounding was identified in the reasoning. The Government cited Section 14 § 1(4) of the Assemblies Act (F126) but the Court did not adopt or assess this. No atomic facts map to this element.

**Legitimate aim**

The Court explicitly declined to decide this element. No factual grounding was identified. No atomic facts map to this element.

**Necessity**

The Court states: "its dispersal was without sufficient justification and took place under somewhat dubious circumstances, apparently without warnings to disperse and with unjustified and excessive use of force, and that it was a disproportionate measure which went beyond what it was reasonable to expect from the authorities when curtailing freedom of assembly." This is drawn from Mushegh Saghatelyan. The relevant atomic facts supporting these findings are: F046 (police attacked without prior warning or orders to disperse), F047 (police violently beat demonstrators with rubber batons and destroyed camp), F043 (applicant urged demonstrators to be peaceful), F044 (demonstrators followed his request and kept distance), F147 (police made no audible demand to disperse), F150 (riot police attacked without warning using rubber truncheons, iron sticks, electric shock batons), F148 (police pushed demonstrators with shields causing panic).

**Positive obligation**

Not discussed. No atomic facts map to this element.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on: (1) there was a peaceful assembly at Freedom Square in which the applicant participated as leader and was present during the dispersal; (2) the police dispersed the assembly without prior warning and with excessive force; (3) the demonstrators had been urged to and did remain peaceful. These are the facts that drove the necessity finding, which was the decisive element.

Irreducible core: F012 (assembly existed), F016 (applicant participated as leader), F039 (applicant on site during dispersal), F043 (applicant urged demonstrators to be peaceful and keep distance), F044 (demonstrators complied), F046 (police attacked without prior warning or orders to disperse), F047 (police violently beat demonstrators and destroyed camp).
