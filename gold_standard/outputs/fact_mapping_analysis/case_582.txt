LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on: (i) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated as organisers or participants, and (ii) the fact that disproportionate measures (arrest, dispersal, administrative conviction) were taken against them. Both of these are captured in F002. F001 is a background fact identifying the applicants but adds no substantive content beyond what F002 already establishes for the legal analysis. The irreducible factual core is therefore F002 alone.
