LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on: (1) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated, and (2) the fact that governmental measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) were taken against them in that context, which the Court found not necessary in a democratic society by reference to precedent. Both of these points are captured exclusively by F002. F001 is purely administrative and adds nothing to the legal conclusion. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.

Therefore, the irreducible factual core is: F002.
