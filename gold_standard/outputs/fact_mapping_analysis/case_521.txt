LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each doctrinal element

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish: (i) there was an assembly — F003 (applicant joined demonstration); (ii) the applicant participated — F003; (iii) the assembly was not peaceful — the core facts are the UN SG's findings about the demonstrators forcing their way through UN lines, which the Court explicitly adopted: F072, F073, F074. These three facts are the irreducible core for the non-peaceful finding. F099 is also necessary to establish the border violation aspect of the violent character finding.

Most critical: F003, F072, F073, F074

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's assembly right. The core facts are: the demonstration was dispersed (F075 — Turkish-Cypriot forces apprehended 111 persons) and the applicant specifically was arrested (F031 — applicant taken to court and remanded). F003 is also necessary to establish she was participating.

Most critical: F003, F075, F031

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court identifies specific legal provisions. The most critical are the provisions that directly empowered the arrest and dispersal: F108 (section 71 — participation in unlawful assembly is a misdemeanour, the substantive offence) and F111 (section 14(1)(b) — power of arrest without warrant for offence committed in presence, the procedural power). F105 is also necessary as it defines the unlawful assembly that section 71 penalises.

Most critical: F105, F108, F111

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court does not independently establish facts for legitimate aim; it flows from the same facts as necessity. The violent character of the demonstration (F072, F073, F074) and the border violation (F099) are the factual basis for the aims of prevention of disorder, prevention of crime, and public safety. However, since the Court does not separately ground legitimate aim in facts, and it merges with necessity, the most critical facts are the same as for necessity: F072, F073, F074.

Most critical: F072, F073, F074

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's proportionality finding turns on: (i) the violent character of the demonstration established by the UN SG report (F072, F073, F074); (ii) the entry into 'TRNC' territory established by trial witness evidence (F099); (iii) the fact that the intervention was provoked by violence and border violation rather than political content (F072, F073, F074, F075, F099). The charges (F090, F091) and the underlying laws (F113, F114, F115) are relevant context but the Court's proportionality reasoning turns on the factual character of the demonstration.

Most critical: F072, F073, F074, F075, F099

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary here — the Court's reference is purely to legal principle. Empty list.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (no violation of Article 11)

The case turned on whether the interference with the applicant's assembly right was justified. The Court found no violation because: (a) the demonstration was violent (not merely unlawful), which justified the interference; (b) the demonstrators had entered 'TRNC' territory, committing criminal offences; (c) the intervention was provoked by violence and border violation, not by the political content of the demonstration; (d) there was a legal basis for the arrest and dispersal.

The absolutely essential facts for this conclusion are:
- F003: establishes the applicant participated in the demonstration (necessary for Article 11 to be engaged at all)
- F072: demonstrators forced their way into the UN buffer zone (core of the violence/non-peaceful finding)
- F073: demonstrators broke through UNFICYP barrier and destroyed observation post (core of the violence finding)
- F074: demonstrators broke through UNFICYP soldiers' line and entered school complex (core of the violence finding)
- F075: Turkish-Cypriot forces apprehended 111 persons (establishes the interference and the response to the violence)
- F099: according to map at trial, area entered was 'TRNC' territory (establishes the border violation that the Court found it could not cast doubt upon, supporting the criminal offence basis for the intervention)
- F105 and F108: define unlawful assembly and the offence of participating in one (legal basis for the interference)
- F111: power of arrest without warrant (legal basis for the arrest)

The most irreducible core — what the case actually turned on — is the violent character of the demonstration (F072, F073, F074) combined with the border violation (F099), the fact of the applicant's participation and arrest (F003, F075), and the legal basis (F105, F108, F111).
