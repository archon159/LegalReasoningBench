LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The case turned on two findings: (1) the interference with freedom of assembly failed the prescribed-by-law test, and (2) it was not necessary in a democratic society. Both findings presuppose that there were governmental restrictions on public events — without this, there is no interference and no Article 11 issue at all. The restrictions on location (F002), time (F003), and manner (F004) are the irreducible factual core. These are the facts that make the case an Article 11 case and that the Court's violation finding is ultimately grounded in. F005 and F006 relate to other complaints (effective remedy and unlawful arrest) that are not the basis of the Article 11 violation found here. F001 is purely administrative background. The prescribed-by-law and necessity analyses have no additional independent factual grounding in the atomic facts beyond the existence of the restrictions themselves. Therefore, F002, F003, and F004 are the strictly necessary facts for the conclusion.
