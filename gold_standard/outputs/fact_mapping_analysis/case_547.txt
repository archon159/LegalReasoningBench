LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
For the assembly to exist and be identified as a trade union assembly within Article 11 scope, the Court needed to establish that a real, organised gathering was announced and took place. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F006: DİSK announced a large-scale demonstration for 1 May 2008
- F007: KESK announced a large-scale demonstration for 1 May 2008
- F008: The gathering was to celebrate Labour Day
- F009: The gathering was to commemorate those who died on 1 May 1977
For participation, the strictly necessary facts are:
- F003: The applicant was a civil servant in the Istanbul Municipality
- F005: The applicant's union (Tümbel-Sen) was affiliated to KESK
- F012: The applicant stated he participated in the demonstration

Note: F004 (membership of Tümbel-Sen) is necessary to establish the link to KESK (F005), but F005 is the operative link the Court relied on. F021 supports the contrast between the situation in 2008 and the later designation as a public holiday, which the Court used to contextualise the symbolic importance of the day, but it is not strictly necessary for the scope finding itself.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
- F013: The applicant was given a warning as a disciplinary sanction for being absent without leave
- F014: The sanction was imposed pursuant to section 125 of Law no. 657

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court did not conduct a prescribed-by-law analysis. The only relevant atomic fact referenced in passing is F014, but it does not ground any substantive legal assessment. List is effectively empty for analytical purposes, though F014 is the sole reference.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
Not discussed. Empty.

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity analysis turned on whether the disciplinary authorities adequately weighed the trade union dimension of the applicant's absence. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F012: The applicant explained he participated in a trade union demonstration (the authorities were informed of the reason)
- F013: The warning was issued regardless
- F014: The warning was issued under section 125 of Law no. 657 (the legal basis the authorities applied without considering the trade union context)
- F016: The Disciplinary Board dismissed the applicant's objection
- F017: The Disciplinary Board found the decision was in accordance with the law (without engaging with the trade union dimension)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. Empty.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two pivotal points: (1) the applicant participated in an organised trade union assembly, and (2) the disciplinary authorities sanctioned him without taking into account the trade union nature of his absence. The irreducible facts are:

- F005: The applicant's union was affiliated to KESK — establishing the trade union character of his participation
- F007: KESK had publicly announced the demonstration in advance — establishing that the authorities could not have been unaware of the trade union dimension
- F012: The applicant explicitly told the disciplinary authorities he had participated in a trade union demonstration — establishing that the authorities were directly informed
- F013: The applicant was given a warning as a disciplinary sanction — establishing the interference
- F017: The Disciplinary Board found the decision was in accordance with the law without engaging with the trade union dimension — establishing the failure to balance, which is the core of the necessity finding
