The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on the finding that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion, the Court needed to establish: (1) that there were public assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope), and (2) that governmental measures were taken against them in connection with those assemblies (interference).
