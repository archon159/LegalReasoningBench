LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The conclusion that Article 11 was violated turns on the fact that the applicants were administratively convicted for participation in public events (F004). This is the single fact that establishes: (i) the existence of a public assembly, (ii) the applicants' participation in it, and (iii) the state measure (conviction) that constitutes both the interference and the disproportionate response. Without F004, the Article 11 violation cannot be established on the facts as presented. F003 (taken to police stations as administrative suspects of offences related to public events) is also necessary as it establishes the procedural chain leading to conviction and forms part of the interference, but F004 is the essential core fact. Both F003 and F004 are strictly necessary: F003 establishes the initial deprivation of liberty as part of the enforcement action, and F004 establishes the ultimate administrative conviction — together they constitute the interference that the Court found disproportionate.
