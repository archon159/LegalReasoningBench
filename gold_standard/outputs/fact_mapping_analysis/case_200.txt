LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Scope / Peaceful nature of the assembly**

The Court stated: "the applicants' protests were staged in accordance with the old and new laws concerning assemblies" — this maps to:
- F012: The demonstration of 27 March 2008 had been authorised by the Chișinău Municipality (i.e., staged in accordance with the old law).
- F025: On 22 April 2008 a new Assemblies Act entered into force under which no authorisation was needed for spontaneous gatherings.
- F026: On 22 April 2008 a new Assemblies Act entered into force under which no authorisation was needed for gatherings with a limited number of participants.
- F042: The Buiucani District Court found that the first, second and fourth applicants had a legal right to protest in front of the residence of the President of Moldova without any authorisation on 22 April 2008.
- F121: On 22 February 2008 the Parliament of Moldova adopted a new Assemblies Act under which no authorisation was needed for the holding of demonstrations with less than fifty participants.

The Court stated: "they remained peaceful" and "they did not disturb public order in any way" — this maps to:
- F019: The applicants complied with the police officer's order to follow him to the police station without any resistance (27 March 2008).
- F067: The Chișinău Court of Appeal concluded that the applicants' demonstration on 30 April 2008 had been peaceful.
- F043: The Buiucani District Court found that the charges concerning resisting police officers brought against the first, second and fourth applicants in connection with the events of 22 April 2008 were groundless.
- F044: The Buiucani District Court found that the charges concerning insulting police officers brought against the first, second and fourth applicants in connection with the events of 22 April 2008 were groundless.

The Court stated: "This conclusion is supported by the videos attached to the case-file" — this maps to:
- F017: In a video filmed by the applicants, one of the police officers can be seen requesting identity papers from one of the participants in the demonstration of 27 March 2008.
- F018: In the video filmed by the applicants, after an identity card was presented to the police officer, one of the police officers ordered the applicants to follow him to the police station.
- F019: In the video filmed by the applicants, the applicants complied with the police officer's order to follow him to the police station without any resistance.
- F053: One of the first, second or third applicants was filming the event on 30 April 2008 in front of the concert hall.
- F066: The Chișinău Court of Appeal reached its acquittal decision on 18 June 2008 after viewing the video of the event of 30 April 2008.
- F084: The applicants submitted a video of the events involving the second applicant on 18 December 2008.

The Court stated: "by the conclusions of the domestic courts which acquitted the applicants" — this maps to:
- F021: On 26 May 2008 the Buiucani District Court finally acquitted the applicants of all charges arising from the events of 27 March 2008 in view of a lack of evidence against them.
- F022: The Buiucani District Court found that the accusations made against the applicants in connection with the events of 27 March 2008 were groundless as they were not confirmed by any of the witnesses.
- F041: On 8 May 2008 the Buiucani District Court acquitted the first, second and fourth applicants of all charges arising from the events of 22 April 2008 in view of a lack of incriminating evidence against them.
- F042: The Buiucani District Court found that the first, second and fourth applicants had a legal right to protest in front of the residence of the President of Moldova without any authorisation on 22 April 2008.
- F043: The Buiucani District Court found that the charges concerning resisting police officers brought against the first, second and fourth applicants in connection with the events of 22 April 2008 were groundless.
- F044: The Buiucani District Court found that the charges concerning insulting police officers brought against the first, second and fourth applicants in connection with the events of 22 April 2008 were groundless.
- F065: On 18 June 2008 all the applicants and O.B. were finally acquitted by the Chișinău Court of Appeal of all charges arising from the events of 30 April 2008 in view of a lack of incriminating evidence against them.
- F067: The Chișinău Court of Appeal concluded that the applicants' demonstration on 30 April 2008 had been peaceful.
- F085: On 18 December 2008 the first applicant was acquitted in view of a lack of incriminating evidence against him in connection with the events of that date.

**Interference**

The Court stated: "the applicants' arrest constituted 'interference by [a] public authority' with their right to freedom of assembly" — this maps to:
- F013: At approximately 11.30 a.m. on 27 March 2008 the first and fourth applicants were arrested.
- F035: The first, second and fourth applicants were arrested during the demonstration on 22 April 2008.
- F060: O.B. and the first, second and third applicants were immediately arrested on 30 April 2008 and taken to the police station.

- F082: On 18 December 2008 the second applicant was near a trolleybus stop when a group of six plain-clothes police officers forced him into a trolleybus.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope (peaceful nature):**
The most critical facts establishing that the assemblies were peaceful and conducted in accordance with the law are the domestic court acquittals and their specific findings, which the Court explicitly relied upon. The video evidence is also cited but is secondary to the court findings. The key facts are:
- F012: Authorisation for 27 March 2008 demonstration — establishes compliance with old law.
- F025/F026/F121: New Assemblies Act — establishes legal basis for 22 April 2008 protest without authorisation.
- F042: Domestic court finding of legal right to protest on 22 April 2008 — directly establishes lawfulness.
- F067: Court of Appeal finding that 30 April 2008 demonstration was peaceful — directly establishes peaceful nature.
- F021: Acquittal on 27 March 2008 charges — establishes no wrongdoing.
- F041: Acquittal on 22 April 2008 charges — establishes no wrongdoing.
- F065: Acquittal on 30 April 2008 charges — establishes no wrongdoing.
- F085: Acquittal on 18 December 2008 charges — establishes no wrongdoing.

**Interference:**
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that arrests occurred — one per incident is sufficient to establish the pattern the Court relied upon:
- F013: Arrest of first and fourth applicants on 27 March 2008.
- F035: Arrest of first, second and fourth applicants on 22 April 2008.
- F060: Arrest of first, second and third applicants on 30 April 2008.
- F078: Arrest of first applicant on 18 December 2008.
- F082: Forced detention of second applicant on 18 December 2008 (functionally equivalent to arrest for interference purposes).

**Prescribed by law:**
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that the protests were lawful under domestic law and that the domestic courts confirmed this — making the arrests unlawful:
- F012: Authorisation for 27 March 2008 — protests staged in accordance with old law.
- F025 or F026 or F121: New Assemblies Act removing authorisation requirement — establishes legal basis for subsequent protests.
- F042: Domestic court finding of legal right to protest on 22 April 2008 — the most direct judicial confirmation of lawfulness.
- F067: Court of Appeal finding of peaceful demonstration on 30 April 2008.
- F021: Acquittal on 27 March 2008 — confirms no lawful basis for arrest.
- F041: Acquittal on 22 April 2008 — confirms no lawful basis for arrest.
- F065: Acquittal on 30 April 2008 — confirms no lawful basis for arrest.
- F085: Acquittal on 18 December 2008 — confirms no lawful basis for arrest.

**Legitimate aim, Necessity, Positive obligation:** Empty — not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two essential points: (1) the applicants were arrested while exercising their right to peaceful assembly, and (2) those arrests were unlawful because the assemblies were conducted in compliance with applicable law and the domestic courts confirmed this. The irreducible core is:

- F013: Arrest on 27 March 2008 (interference established).
- F035: Arrest on 22 April 2008 (interference established).
- F060: Arrest on 30 April 2008 (interference established).
- F078: Arrest on 18 December 2008 (interference established).
- F012: Authorisation for 27 March 2008 demonstration (lawfulness under old law).
- F025 or F121: New Assemblies Act removing authorisation requirement (lawfulness of subsequent protests).
- F042: Domestic court finding of legal right to protest on 22 April 2008 (judicial confirmation of lawfulness — the clearest single fact establishing unlawfulness of arrest).
- F021: Acquittal on 27 March 2008 (judicial confirmation that arrest was without legal basis).
- F041: Acquittal on 22 April 2008 (judicial confirmation that arrest was without legal basis).
- F065: Acquittal on 30 April 2008 (judicial confirmation that arrest was without legal basis).
- F085: Acquittal on 18 December 2008 (judicial confirmation that arrest was without legal basis).
- F067: Court of Appeal finding that 30 April 2008 demonstration was peaceful (establishes peaceful nature, which is a precondition for Article 11 protection and undermines any lawful basis for dispersal).
