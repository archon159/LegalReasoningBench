LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis — specifically, whether the domestic courts gave relevant and sufficient reasons to justify convicting the applicant for membership of a terrorist organisation on the basis of his participation in assemblies. The irreducible factual core is:

- F003 / F004 / F026 / F027: The applicant participated in demonstrations and press conferences, and did not deny doing so — establishing that the conviction was based on assembly-related conduct falling within Article 11's scope.
- F029: The applicant was convicted of membership of an armed terrorist organisation.
- F032: The applicant was sentenced to nine years' imprisonment — establishing the severity of the interference and the chilling effect.
- F030 / F031: The conviction was based on participation in demonstrations and press conferences on the basis of PKK website calls — establishing the link between the assembly conduct and the conviction, and the absence of any finding of violence.
- F034: The Assize Court found participation per PKK instructions without characterising the nature of the events — establishing the failure to conduct an Article 11 assessment and the absence of any violence finding.
- F028 / F060: The applicant argued the events were legal and the YDGM was the legal youth wing of the DTP — establishing the submissions the Assize Court failed to address.
- F046: Nawruz celebrations — the one event where the PKK call correspondence was found, used by the Court to highlight that for all other events the correspondence was absent or post-dated, making the lack of additional scrutiny all the more problematic.
- F063: Court of Cassation upheld the conviction — confirming the interference was final and not remedied on appeal.
