LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two linked findings: (1) the demonstration was peaceful and the dispersal was unjustified because it was based on location rather than violence; and (2) the criminal sanctions imposed on the applicants were disproportionate given the absence of reprehensible individual conduct (first group) or the sporadic and reactive nature of the conduct and severity of the sentence (second group). The irreducible facts are:

- F032: notice was given to the authorities — establishing the demonstration was not clandestine and the authorities were aware
- F036: the authority refused to authorise the proposed location — establishing the basis for dispersal was location, not violence
- F046: video recordings show peaceful gathering, marching and chanting before dispersal — establishing the peaceful character of the assembly
- F047: police dispersed by arresting demonstrators and forcing them into vehicles — establishing the nature of the interference
- F048: only isolated clashes resulted from dispersal — establishing that violence was not the cause of dispersal
- F056: first group detained pending trial — establishing severity of interference
- F064: first group sentenced to imprisonment — establishing severity of sanction
- F070: domestic court relied on statements about unidentified participants — establishing lack of specific individual factual basis for convictions
- F071: domestic court relied on police testimony about aggressive behaviour — establishing lack of specific individual factual basis
- F113: second group sentenced up to three years — establishing severity of sanction
- F126: video shows Alishli kicking bus used to transport arrested demonstrators — establishing the reactive/contextual nature of Alishli's conduct
- F152: Guliyev sentenced to three years — establishing severity of sanction
- F153: Guliyev elbowed officer during arrest — establishing the reactive/contextual nature of Guliyev's conduct
- F154: officer sustained no injuries — establishing the limited severity of Guliyev's conduct
