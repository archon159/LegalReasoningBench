LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that there was an assembly, that the applicants participated, and (implicitly) that it was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F035: organisers opted to picket Tverskaya Square on 27 November 2005 (establishes the assembly)
- F051: organisers decided to hold gathering in Tverskaya Square on 27 November 2005 (confirms the assembly took place)
- F054: applicants taken to police station (confirms their presence/participation)
- F026: decision to hold gathering to mark opposition to values of Right March (establishes the political/expressive character of the assembly)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that state action negatively affected the applicants' exercise of their right. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F052: special security squad arrested some participants (dispersal)
- F053: squad did not give participants time to disperse (prompt dispersal, no time to manifest views)
- F054: applicants taken to police station
- F055: applicants remained at police station until 7 p.m.
- F057/F058: judgments finding applicants guilty of administrative offence
- F059: fines imposed (1,000 RUB)
- F085: legal basis making the event unlawful (section 5 § 5 of Public Gatherings Act)
- F094: Code of Administrative Offences — punishability of participation

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court simply asserts the legal basis without detailed sub-analysis. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F083: Public Gatherings Act empowers authority to propose alternative venue/timing
- F085: section 5 § 5 — event cannot take place without approved alternative
- F094: Code of Administrative Offences — fine for violation

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts the obstruction of passage as the relevant and sufficient reason. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F044: district authority cited security of participants
- F045: district authority cited need to avoid obstruction to pedestrians and vehicles

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's proportionality analysis turns on several key points. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F042: district authority suggested alternative venue (Tverskaya Zastava Square)
- F043: district authority suggested shorter timing
- F047: organisers expressed disagreement but did not accept the proposal
- F050: organisers did not challenge conditions in court before the event
- F051: organisers proceeded with gathering in Tverskaya Square despite the legal position
- F085: section 5 § 5 — event could not lawfully take place
- F039: notice given on 23 November 2005 (showing advance planning, no spontaneity/urgency)
- F059: fines of 1,000 RUB (small fines, relevant to proportionality)
- F060: ninth applicant fined 500 RUB

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts applicable.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (no violation of Article 11)

The case turned on the proportionality of the interference. The Court found no violation primarily because: (1) the organisers were offered a lawful alternative venue and timing but refused it without valid reason or judicial challenge; (2) the law clearly made the event unlawful once the alternative was not accepted; (3) the reasons given by the authorities (obstruction of passage, security) were relevant and sufficient; (4) there was no urgency or spontaneity justifying non-compliance; and (5) the sanctions were small. The irreducible factual core is:

- F042: district authority suggested Tverskaya Zastava Square as alternative venue
- F043: district authority suggested shorter timing (1 hour instead of 2)
- F045: district authority cited obstruction to pedestrians and vehicles as reason
- F047: organisers expressed disagreement but did not accept the proposal
- F050: organisers did not challenge conditions in court before the event
- F051: organisers proceeded with gathering in Tverskaya Square despite legal position
- F085: section 5 § 5 — event could not lawfully take place without approved alternative
- F039: notice given on 23 November 2005 (advance planning — no spontaneity/urgency)
- F059: fines of 1,000 RUB (small, relevant to proportionality finding)
