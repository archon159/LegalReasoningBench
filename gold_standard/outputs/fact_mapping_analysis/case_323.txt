LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The final conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court needs at minimum: (i) that there were public assemblies (scope); (ii) that the applicants participated in them (scope/participation); and (iii) that the state took measures against them in that context (interference). All three of these points are captured exclusively in F002. F001 merely identifies the applicants procedurally and is not a substantive factual predicate for the violation finding. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, F002 is the sole atomic fact that is strictly necessary for the Court to reach its conclusion.
