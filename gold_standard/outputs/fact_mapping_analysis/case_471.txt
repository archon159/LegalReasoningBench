LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish: (i) there was an assembly; (ii) the applicants participated; (iii) it was initially peaceful.
- F005: KESK decided to organise meetings — establishes the assembly existed as an organised event
- F012/F013/F014/F015/F016/F017/F018/F019: applicants gathered in Kızılay — establishes participation (most representative: F012 for 7 June, F017 for 25 June)
- F006: aim was to draw public attention — establishes the expressive/political character of the assembly
- F020: police intervened while president was making press statements — establishes the assembly was initially peaceful (no violence had occurred at the moment of intervention)

Most critical/representative: F005, F012, F017, F006, F020

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act negatively affected the applicants' exercise of their right.
- F024: police used truncheons — core act of forcible dispersal
- F026: police used tear gas — core act of forcible dispersal
- F057: charges brought against two applicants — establishes the chilling effect dimension
- F030: applicants were wounded — establishes they were directly and negatively affected

Most critical: F024, F026, F057, F030

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court needs to establish a legal basis for the interference.
- F074: section 22 of Law no. 2911 prohibited demonstrations on public streets — the specific provision cited by the Court
- F079: section 24 provided for forcible dispersal of non-compliant demonstrations — the enforcement provision
- F080: Kızılay avenue not among permitted areas per Ankara Governorship circular — establishes the specific legal basis for the location-based intervention

Most critical: F074, F079, F080

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts the Government's submission without independent factual analysis. The only contextual facts that could ground the aims of public safety and prevention of disorder are:
- F022: demonstrators blocked the main street — most directly relevant to public safety/disorder
- F023: demonstrators attempted to march towards the Prime Minister's Office — relevant to public order concern

Most critical: F022

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity analysis turns on the proportionality of the police response relative to the actual threat posed.
- F020: police intervened while president was still making press statements — establishes the swiftness and timing of intervention (before serious disorder had developed)
- F024/F026: police used truncheons and tear gas — establishes the considerable force used
- F022: demonstrators blocked the main street — establishes the limited nature of the disruption caused
- F027: some demonstrators attacked security forces — but only after police intervention, relevant to the Court's finding that the intervention caused the escalation
- F006/F007: aims of the demonstration were political/expressive — relevant to weighing the importance of the assembly

Most critical: F020, F024, F026, F022, F027

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary here; the Court raises this only as an abstract principle without applying it to the facts. List is empty.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality analysis. The Court found a violation because: (a) the assembly was initially peaceful and posed no serious danger to public order; (b) the police intervened swiftly and with considerable force at an early stage; (c) this disproportionate intervention caused the escalation rather than responding to pre-existing violence. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary for this conclusion:

- F005: KESK organised the meetings — establishes the assembly existed
- F020: police intervened while president was making press statements — establishes the assembly was peaceful at the moment of intervention and that the intervention was swift
- F024: police used truncheons — establishes the considerable force used
- F026: police used tear gas — establishes the considerable force used
- F022: demonstrators blocked the main street — establishes the limited nature of the actual disruption (the only concrete disorder before police intervention)
- F027: some demonstrators attacked security forces — establishes that violence followed the police intervention (causation: intervention caused escalation)
- F030: applicants were wounded — establishes direct negative impact on applicants, confirming interference and victim status
- F057: charges brought against applicants — establishes the chilling effect and confirms interference
- F074: section 22 of Law no. 2911 — establishes the legal basis (prescribed by law satisfied, so the case turns on necessity)
- F080: Kızılay avenue not among permitted areas — establishes the specific ground on which police acted, and thus what the Court had to assess for proportionality
