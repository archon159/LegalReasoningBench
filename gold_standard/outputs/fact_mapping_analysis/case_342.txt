**Scope:** The Court's implicit finding that there were public assemblies in which the applicants participated requires F002 (organisers) and F003 (participants). **Interference:** The Court's finding of interference rests on the same facts: the applicants (as organisers or participants) were subjected to disproportionate measures including arrest and conviction. LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rested on: (1) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants were organisers or participants (scope, implied); (2) the imposition of disproportionate measures against them (interference and necessity). The entire chain of reasoning — from scope through interference to the necessity finding — is grounded exclusively in F002 and F003. F001 and F004 are procedural or ancillary and do not bear on the substantive Article 11 conclusion. The irreducible factual core is therefore F002 and F003.
