LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turns on the finding that there were public assemblies, that the applicants were organisers or participants, and that disproportionate measures were taken against them — with no new material distinguishing the case from prior violations found in Frumkin, Navalnyy and Yashin, and Kasparov. The irreducible atomic facts are F002 and F003: these establish both the factual premise for the Article 11 analysis (assemblies, participation) and the existence of the measures that constituted the interference found not to be necessary in a democratic society.
