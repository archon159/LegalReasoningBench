LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion, the Court needed to establish: (1) that there were public assemblies, (2) that the applicants were organisers or participants, and (3) that measures were taken against them. All three points are captured by F002 and F003. F002 covers organisers and F003 covers participants — together they are the irreducible factual core. F001 merely identifies that there are applicants and refers to an appended table; it is not itself a substantive fact driving the conclusion. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, the irreducible core is F002 and F003.
