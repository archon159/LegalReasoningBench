LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Interference element:**
The Court's factual grounding quote was: "the fine imposed on the applicant by the decision of the President of the Criminal and Military Court of Appeal of 1 March 2003 undoubtedly interfered with his right to freedom of peaceful assembly."
Atomic facts pointed to:
- F027: On 1 March 2003 the President of the Criminal and Military Court of Appeal reviewed the applicant's conviction.
- F031: The President of the Criminal and Military Court of Appeal changed the applicant's penalty to an administrative fine of 1,000 Armenian drams (AMD), which was approximately 1.5 euros (EUR) at the material time.

The scope elements are implied by the interference finding and by the Court's reference to "his right to freedom of peaceful assembly." The underlying facts are:
- F008: On 21 February 2003 the applicant participated in a demonstration held in Yerevan. (assembly exists; applicant participated)
- F016: Judge H. found that on 21 February 2003 the applicant participated together with a group of people in an unauthorised demonstration in Yerevan. (assembly exists; participation; no finding of violence, implying peaceful)
- F029: The President of the Criminal and Military Court of Appeal found that on 21 February 2003 the applicant participated in an unauthorised demonstration and street march, during which he violated public order. (assembly; participation — note: "violated public order" is mentioned but the Court still treats the assembly as falling within Art. 11 scope, so peaceful character is implied)

**Prescribed by law element:**
The Court's factual grounding quotes were:
1. "the applicant was convicted under Article 180.1 of the CAO for 'violation of the prescribed rules for organising or holding assemblies, rallies, street marches and demonstrations'." — Atomic facts: F015 (conviction under Art. 180.1), F028 (President of Court of Appeal's finding referencing the same provision), F029 (finding of participation in unauthorised demonstration).
2. "there was no legal act applicable in Armenia which contained the 'prescribed rules' referred to in that provision. The USSR Law of 28 October 1988 was no longer applicable..." — This is a legal finding drawn from Mkrtchyan (F037), not directly from the atomic facts of this case. No single atomic fact in the list directly establishes this; it is a legal conclusion imported from prior case law. F037 (reference to Mkrtchyan judgment containing relevant domestic law summary) is the closest atomic fact.
3. "a new law on assemblies and rallies was adopted only on 28 April 2004." — Again drawn from Mkrtchyan/F037. No specific atomic fact in the list states this directly; F037 is the closest.
4. "the interference in the present case similarly took place before the enactment of a new law on assemblies and rallies." — The temporal fact that the interference occurred before 28 April 2004 is grounded in: F008 (demonstration on 21 February 2003), F027 (Court of Appeal decision on 1 March 2003), F031 (fine imposed on 1 March 2003).

LEGITIMATE AIM, NECESSITY, POSITIVE OBLIGATION: The Court did not examine these elements and no atomic facts were cited in connection with them. Lists are empty.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope (implied):**
The minimum facts needed to establish that there was a peaceful assembly and that the applicant participated:
- F008: The applicant participated in a demonstration on 21 February 2003. This is the core fact establishing both assembly existence and participation. The Court's implicit treatment of the assembly as peaceful (despite F029's reference to "violated public order") means no additional fact is needed to establish peacefulness — the Court simply does not contest it.

**Interference:**
The minimum facts needed to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's assembly rights:
- F027: The President of the Criminal and Military Court of Appeal reviewed and upheld the conviction on 1 March 2003.
- F031: The penalty was changed to an administrative fine — this is the specific act the Court identifies as the interference.

**Prescribed by law:**
The minimum facts needed:
- F015 or F028: The applicant was convicted under Article 180.1 of the CAO. F028 is slightly more precise as it reflects the Court of Appeal's own characterisation, which is the decision under review. Either establishes the legal basis invoked.
- F037: The summary of relevant domestic law in Mkrtchyan, which the Court relies on to establish that the USSR Law of 28 October 1988 was no longer applicable and no replacement law existed at the material time.
- F027 or F031: The interference occurred on 1 March 2003, i.e., before 28 April 2004 when the new law was enacted — establishing the temporal gap. F031 is most direct as it identifies the fine (the interference) as occurring on 1 March 2003.

**Legitimate aim:** Empty — not examined.
**Necessity:** Empty — not examined.
**Positive obligation:** Empty — not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on two pivotal points: (1) there was an interference with assembly rights (the fine), and (2) that interference was not prescribed by law because no applicable legal framework existed in Armenia at the time. The irreducible facts are:

- F008: The applicant participated in a demonstration — establishes the assembly right was engaged.
- F031: The fine was imposed by the Court of Appeal decision of 1 March 2003 — establishes the interference.
- F028: The conviction was for violation of the prescribed rules for organising/holding assemblies under Art. 180.1 CAO — establishes the legal basis invoked.
- F037: The Mkrtchyan judgment (incorporated by reference) establishes that no applicable legal act in Armenia contained the "prescribed rules" referred to in Art. 180.1 CAO at the material time — this is the decisive fact for the "not prescribed by law" conclusion.

F027 is subsumed by F031 (the fine is the specific act of the Court of Appeal decision). F029 adds detail but is not strictly necessary beyond what F008 and F028 already establish. F015 is superseded by F028 as the relevant conviction for the decision under review is the Court of Appeal's.
