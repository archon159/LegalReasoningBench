LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that there was a peaceful assembly and that the applicants participated in it.
- F006: The applicants took part in an anti-war demonstration in Istanbul on 17 October 2003. (establishes participation)
- F014: Fifty to sixty persons gathered in a square in Istanbul. (establishes assembly exists)
- F070: The Bakırköy Criminal Court found the applicants exercised their democratic rights and committed no offences. (establishes peaceful character — the Court relies on this to confirm no violence)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicants' assembly rights.
- F008: The applicants were arrested during the dispersal of the demonstration. (core act of interference)
- F020: The police used force against a number of demonstrators. (establishes use of force, grounding injury to two applicants)
- F026: The first applicant had bruising on her lower lip. (establishes injury, confirming force was used against her)
- F028: The fourth applicant had bruising on the left side of his lower back. (establishes injury to fourth applicant)

Note: F026 and F028 are the minimum needed to establish that "two of the applicants" were injured. F027, F029, F030, F031 are additional injuries to the same two applicants and are not strictly necessary beyond confirming injury occurred.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court accepts the legal basis without independent analysis. The only strictly necessary fact is:
- F068: Criminal proceedings were instigated under sections 33, 36 and 40 of the Meetings and Demonstration Marches Act (Law no. 2911). (identifies the legal instrument)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts the Government's submission. The same fact supports this:
- F068: Criminal proceedings instigated under the Meetings and Demonstration Marches Act for unlawful demonstration. (contextualizes the aim of preventing disorder)

--- NECESSITY ---
The necessity analysis turns on three key points: (1) the nature and scale of the assembly, (2) the disproportionate police response including injury, and (3) the domestic court's finding that the applicants broke no law.
- F014: Fifty to sixty persons gathered in a square. (scale of assembly — small, non-threatening)
- F017: Police warned using loudspeakers about traffic disruption. (nature of the only identified harm — traffic)
- F018: Police asked demonstrators to disperse but were unsuccessful. (establishes sequence before force)
- F008: Applicants were arrested. (core police action)
- F020: Police used force. (establishes disproportionate response)
- F026: First applicant had bruising. (injury resulting from force)
- F028: Fourth applicant had bruising. (injury resulting from force)
- F068: Proceedings brought under the Meetings and Demonstration Marches Act. (basis for arrest)
- F069: Bakırköy Criminal Court of First Instance acquitted the applicants. (domestic court rejected the legal basis for arrest)
- F070: Court held applicants exercised democratic rights and committed no offences. (directly undermines the Government's necessity argument)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. Empty.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (a) the applicants participated in a small, peaceful assembly; (b) the police forcibly dispersed it and arrested the applicants, injuring two of them; (c) the only identified disruption was to traffic flow; (d) a domestic court subsequently found the applicants had broken no law and had merely exercised their democratic rights. These four points are what drove the finding of disproportionality.

- F006: The applicants took part in an anti-war demonstration. (establishes the assembly and participation — the right being exercised)
- F014: Fifty to sixty persons gathered in a square. (scale: small assembly, low threat level)
- F017: Police warned about traffic disruption only. (the only harm identified was minor — traffic)
- F008: Applicants were arrested. (the core interference)
- F020: Police used force. (the means of interference)
- F026: First applicant had bruising. (injury — confirms force used against her)
- F028: Fourth applicant had bruising. (injury — confirms force used against him)
- F069: Bakırköy Criminal Court acquitted the applicants. (domestic vindication — no unlawful conduct)
- F070: Court held applicants exercised democratic rights and committed no offences. (the decisive finding undermining necessity of the police intervention)
