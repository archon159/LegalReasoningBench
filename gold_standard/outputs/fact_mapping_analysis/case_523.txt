LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the finding that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly — specifically the arrest, dispersal, and administrative conviction of organisers and participants of public assemblies — were not necessary in a democratic society. The irreducible factual core is:
- F002: establishes that organisers of public assemblies faced disproportionate measures — this is the minimum fact needed to ground the Article 11 analysis for organiser-applicants.
- F003: establishes that participants of public assemblies faced disproportionate measures — this is the minimum fact needed to ground the Article 11 analysis for participant-applicants.

Without F002 and F003, there is no factual basis for the existence of assemblies, the applicants' connection to them, or the governmental measures that constituted the interference found to violate Article 11. F001 and F004 are not strictly necessary for the violation conclusion: F001 merely identifies the applicants by reference to a table, and F004 concerns other complaints not relevant to the Article 11 finding.
