LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish: (i) an assembly existed; (ii) the applicants participated; (iii) it was peaceful.
- Assembly existed: F018, F019, F020 (permits issued for assemblies), F023, F024 (demonstrations started and farmers gathered in designated areas)
- Participation: F080 (applicants convicted in connection with their actions during the assembly)
- Peaceful nature: F033 (police record: no serious conflicts), F038, F039 (vehicles allowed through, showing restraint and cooperation)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that the criminal conviction constituted an interference.
- F080 (conviction under Article 283 § 1)
- F108 (sixty-day custodial sentence)
- F110 (suspended sentence)
- F111 (restriction on leaving residence)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court assumes this without resolving it. The only strictly necessary fact is the legal basis for conviction:
- F180, F181 (Article 283 § 1 as the applicable criminal provision)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court assumes prevention of disorder and protection of rights of others. The strictly necessary facts establishing that there was at least some disruption (justifying the assumption):
- F025, F026, F027 (road blockades on three highways)
- F034 (pecuniary damage reported by transport companies)

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's proportionality analysis turns on several key factual pillars:
1. The demonstration was peaceful and restrained: F033, F038, F039
2. Negotiations were ongoing: F028, F029
3. Only one civil claim was actually pursued: F070, F101
4. The domestic courts failed to conduct a proper proportionality analysis (riot framing): F080, F116, F151
5. Inconsistent treatment compared to A.D.: F044, F045, F046, F047, F129
6. Severity of sanction on applicants: F108, F110, F111

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts from the case are relied upon. Empty.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the disproportionality of the criminal conviction. The irreducible facts are:

1. The assembly was permitted and peaceful, with the applicants showing restraint: F018, F019, F020 (permits), F033 (no violence), F038, F039 (vehicles allowed through)

2. Negotiations were ongoing during the blockade, showing the protest was part of a dialogue: F028, F029

3. The actual harm caused was limited — only one civil claim was pursued: F070, F101

4. The domestic courts framed the case as riot without conducting a proper proportionality analysis: F080, F116, F151

5. The inconsistent treatment of A.D. — who did essentially the same thing and received only an administrative fine — demonstrates the criminal sanction against the applicants was disproportionate: F044, F045, F046, F047, F129

6. The severity of the sanction imposed on the applicants (criminal conviction, custodial sentence, residence restriction): F080, F108, F110, F111
