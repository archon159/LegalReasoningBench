**Necessity**

LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of legal doctrine

**Scope**
The Court needed to establish that the applicant was an organiser of a collective assembly (not a solo demonstrator). The strictly necessary facts are:
→ F018 (applicant notified the mayor on behalf of the Macedonian Initiative Committee — establishes his organiser role and the collective nature of the planned event), F024 (applicant sought judicial review — confirms his active role as organiser)

**Interference**
The Court needed to establish that a governmental act restricted the applicant's right. The strictly necessary fact is:
→ F021 (mayor replied that the rally could not proceed — this is the ban itself), F033 (the rally was called off as a result — confirms the ban had real effect)

**Prescribed by law**
Not examined. No atomic facts strictly necessary.
→ []

**Legitimate aim**
Not examined. No atomic facts strictly necessary.
→ []

**Necessity**
The Court needed to establish: (i) what justifications the domestic authorities gave; (ii) that those justifications were thin and unsubstantiated; (iii) that the same justifications had been found insufficient in prior cases. The strictly necessary facts are:
→ F022 (mayor's justification: the municipal concert), F028 (court's justification: risk to rights and freedoms of others), F031 (court's reasoning about sensitivity of political statements — captures the "controversial statements" rationale), F032 (court's reasoning about Bulgarian society's sensitivity — the core of the "protection from exposure to controversial statements" justification). For the pattern of prior cases: F003, F004, F008, F010 (the four prior judgments cited by the Court as materially identical cases). For the absence of logistical detail: this is captured by the same facts (F022, F023, F028) which show the totality of the reasoning offered — no additional atomic fact records logistical analysis because none was provided.

Not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) there was a planned collective assembly; (2) it was banned by the mayor and that ban was upheld by the court; (3) the applicant was an organiser personally affected; (4) the only justifications offered were the existence of a parallel municipal event and the sensitivity of the rally's political content; (5) those justifications had already been found insufficient in materially identical prior cases; (6) no additional logistical or security reasoning was provided.

The irreducible atomic facts are:
→ F018 (applicant organised and notified the rally — establishes scope and his standing), F021 (mayor banned the rally — the interference), F022 (mayor's sole justification: the municipal concert), F028 (court's justification: risk to rights and freedoms of others), F031 (court's reasoning about forcing music lovers to hear political speeches not accepted unequivocally — the "controversial statements" rationale that the ECtHR found insufficient), F032 (court's note about Bulgarian society's sensitivity to Macedonian minority claims — the core of the sensitivity justification), F033 (rally was called off — confirms real effect of the ban), F003, F004, F008, F010 (the four prior judgments establishing that identical justifications had already been found insufficient — this is what the necessity analysis actually turned on).
