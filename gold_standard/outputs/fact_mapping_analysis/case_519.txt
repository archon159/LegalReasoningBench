LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish that there was a peaceful assembly and that the applicants participated in it. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F008: the demonstration was organised and held on 3 February 2009
- F009: approximately twenty individuals participated (establishes it was a real gathering; also relevant to the notification exemption)
- F010: the third applicant was among the participants
- F011: the demonstrators were attacked (implies they were not themselves the aggressors, supporting peaceful character)

F015 and F016 are relevant to participation but are more critical to the positive obligation element. F012 and F013 are more relevant to the severity of the attack (positive obligation). For scope, F008, F009, F010, and F011 are the core.

**Interference (state omission)**
The strictly necessary facts establishing the state's failure to act are:
- F020: police were present and observing but did not intervene
- F024: no police officer intervened when the clash began
- F025: police were called but gave no assistance
- F026: police arrived only one and a half hours later
- F036: no notification obligation existed for fewer than fifty participants (rebuts the Government's justification for inaction)
- F009: approximately twenty participants (establishes the notification exemption applied)

**Prescribed by law**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

**Legitimate aim**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

**Necessity**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

**Positive obligation**
The strictly necessary facts for the positive obligation finding (both failure to protect and failure to investigate) are:
- F011: attack by six masked men (establishes the threat requiring protection)
- F012: physical assault (severity of attack)
- F013: tear gas sprayed (severity of attack)
- F015: third applicant sustained injuries (concrete harm)
- F016: third applicant needed medical treatment (concrete harm)
- F020: police present but did not intervene (core failure to protect)
- F024: no police officer intervened (core failure to protect)
- F025: police called but gave no assistance (failure to respond)
- F026: police arrived only after one and a half hours (failure to respond promptly)
- F036: no notification obligation for fewer than fifty participants (rebuts Government's excuse)
- F009: approximately twenty participants (establishes exemption applied)
- F019: one attacker admitted being paid — no investigation into sponsor (failure to investigate)
- F031: all six attackers identified (establishes investigation capacity)
- F032: only two convicted (failure to prosecute fully)
- F021: filming by plain-clothes individuals — no investigation into their identity (failure to investigate)
- F033: no action on complaint about police inaction (failure to investigate)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two things: (1) the state's failure to protect the demonstrators despite having the means and opportunity to do so, and (2) the failure to effectively investigate. The irreducible facts are:

- F008: a demonstration was held — establishes the assembly whose protection was at issue
- F011: the demonstrators were attacked by masked men — establishes the threat the state failed to address
- F020: police were present and observing but did not intervene — the most direct evidence of failure to protect
- F024: no police officer intervened when the clash began — confirms the failure to protect
- F025: police were called but gave no assistance — confirms the failure to respond
- F026: police arrived only one and a half hours later — establishes the unreasonable delay
- F036: no notification obligation for fewer than fifty participants — rebuts the only justification the Government offered for inaction
- F009: approximately twenty participants — establishes the notification exemption applied
- F032: only two of six attackers convicted — establishes failure to investigate fully
- F019: attacker admitted being paid, no investigation into sponsor — establishes failure to investigate the organised nature of the attack
