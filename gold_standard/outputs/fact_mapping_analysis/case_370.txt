LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the finding that the applicants — as organisers or participants of public assemblies — were subjected to measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) that were not necessary in a democratic society. The irreducible factual core is:
- F002: establishes that organisers of public assemblies were subjected to disproportionate measures — this is the minimum fact needed to ground the Article 11 complaint and the violation finding for that category.
- F003: establishes the same for participants.

Both F002 and F003 are strictly necessary because the Court's conclusion covers both organisers and participants, and removing either would leave part of the applicant group without factual grounding. F001 (the appended table) and F004 (other complaints) are not necessary for the Article 11 conclusion itself. The violation conclusion rests entirely on F002 and F003.
