LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) there were peaceful assemblies in which the applicant participated; (2) the State dispersed them and arrested and convicted the applicant; (3) in the first case, notice had been given but was disregarded; (4) in both cases, the authorities gave no relevant and sufficient reasons for the dispersal and failed to acknowledge Article 11 protection for peaceful unauthorised assemblies; (5) the sanction in the second case was disproportionately severe relative to the minimum wage, producing a chilling effect.

The strictly necessary facts are:
- F005: demonstration of 17 April 2011 took place (assembly exists, first case)
- F006: demonstration of 26 January 2013 took place (assembly exists, second case)
- F014: demonstrations intended to be peaceful (peaceful character)
- F015: demonstrations conducted in a peaceful manner (peaceful character)
- F019: applicant participated in 17 April 2011 demonstration
- F020: applicant participated in 26 January 2013 demonstration
- F021: police dispersed 17 April 2011 demonstration (interference)
- F022: police dispersed 26 January 2013 demonstration (interference)
- F007: notice was given for 17 April 2011 demonstration (necessity — failure to take notification into account)
- F053: applicant convicted under Article 310.1 in first case (interference and necessity)
- F054: sentenced to five days' detention in first case (necessity — chilling effect)
- F055: applicant convicted under Article 298.1 in second case (interference and necessity)
- F056: fined AZN 2,000 in second case (necessity — disproportionate sanction)
- F090: minimum wage was AZN 93.5 (necessity — severity of fine relative to minimum wage)
