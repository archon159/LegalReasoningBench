LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the existence of public assemblies, the participation of the applicants as organisers or participants, and the fact that disproportionate measures were taken against them. These are the facts that, combined with the Court's necessity analysis by reference to prior case-law, drove the violation finding. F002 and F003 are the irreducible core: they establish both that the applicants were engaged in protected assembly activity and that governmental measures were taken against them in that context. F004 is not necessary for the Article 11 conclusion. F001 is not strictly necessary as an atomic fact — it is a pointer to a table rather than a substantive fact. The violation conclusion rests entirely on F002 and F003.
