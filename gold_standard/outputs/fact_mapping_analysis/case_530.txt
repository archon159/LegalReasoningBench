LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court must at minimum establish: (1) that there were public assemblies, (2) that the applicants participated in or organised them, and (3) that governmental measures interfered with their freedom of assembly. All three of these points are grounded in F002 and F003. F001 is a procedural fact about the table of applicants and is not substantively necessary for the legal conclusion. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. The irreducible core is therefore F002 and F003 — without these, there is no assembly, no participation, and no interference to assess, and the violation finding cannot stand.
