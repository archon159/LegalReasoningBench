LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. For this conclusion to be reached under the doctrinal structure of Article 11, the Court must at minimum establish: (1) that there were assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope), and (2) that governmental measures were taken against them in that context (interference). The necessity finding was reached by analogy with precedent and does not depend on additional case-specific atomic facts beyond these.

F002 and F003 together constitute the irreducible factual core: F002 establishes the applicants as participants subject to measures; F003 establishes the same for organisers. Both are needed because the applicant group includes both categories. Without either, the Article 11 analysis cannot proceed to a violation finding.

F004 is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. F001 is purely procedural background.
