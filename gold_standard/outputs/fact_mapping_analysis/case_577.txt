LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Scope (assembly exists, participation)**

The Court's grounding quotes refer to UMO Ilinden – PIRIN planning rallies and the applicants being ordered not to participate. The relevant atomic facts are:
- F007: All five applicants are members of UMO Ilinden – PIRIN (establishes who the applicants are and their connection to the organisation planning the rallies)
- F008: All five applicants are followers of UMO Ilinden – PIRIN (reinforces connection)
- F009: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN is a political party founded in 1998 (establishes the organising entity)
- F010: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN is based in south-western Bulgaria, in an area known as the Pirin region (establishes the geographic context of the planned rallies)

Note: The atomic facts provided do not include facts about the specific rallies planned for 22 April 2007, the Rozhen Monastery, Melnik, or the police orders. These facts appear in the circumstances of the case section of the judgment but are not among the atomic facts provided. Therefore, the factual grounding for the Court's interference and scope findings (the ban, the police orders, the vehicle checks, the road block) cannot be mapped to any of the provided atomic facts F001–F018.

**Interference**

The Court's grounding quotes concern: (1) the Regional Governor's ban on the rallies; (2) the police orders to the applicants not to organise or participate; (3) the vehicle checks; (4) the road block. None of these specific facts appear in the provided atomic facts F001–F018. The only atomic facts that are even tangentially relevant are:
- F007: membership of the applicants in UMO Ilinden – PIRIN (establishes who was subject to the orders)
- F009: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN is a political party (establishes the organising entity)

**Prescribed by law**

The Court's grounding quotes concern: (1) the 1990 Meetings and Marches Act not envisaging a role for regional governors; (2) the Regional Governor relying on irrelevant grounds including UMO Ilinden – PIRIN's lack of registration; (3) section 55(1) of the 2006 Ministry of Internal Affairs Act being vague; (4) no legal basis for the road block. The reference to UMO Ilinden – PIRIN's lack of registration as a ground relied upon by the Governor connects to:
- F011: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN was declared unconstitutional by the Bulgarian Constitutional Court on 29 February 2000
- F012: As a result, UMO Ilinden – PIRIN was dissolved
These facts explain why the organisation lacked registration — it had been dissolved. The other prescribed-by-law grounding quotes (about the 1990 Act, section 55(1), the road block) relate to national law provisions not captured in the provided atomic facts.

**Legitimate aim**

The Court declines to determine legitimate aim. The grounds stated by the Governor (unregistered status, conjectural threat to public order, propaganda against sovereignty and territorial integrity) are mentioned but not assessed. The reference to unregistered status connects to:
- F011: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN was declared unconstitutional
- F012: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN was dissolved
No other provided atomic facts map to this element.

**Necessity**

The Court's grounding quotes concern: (1) the Regional Governor relying on the same grounds as previous bans already found deficient by the Court in three prior judgments; (2) the specific grounds (unregistered status, conjectural threat to public order, meetings of other organisations, propaganda against sovereignty and territorial integrity); (3) the Governor's disregard for prior ECtHR judgments. The relevant atomic facts are:
- F011: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN was declared unconstitutional (explains the 'unregistered status' ground)
- F012: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN was dissolved (same)
- F013: The ECtHR found in 2005 that the dissolution of UMO Ilinden – PIRIN had been in breach of the party's right to freedom of association (one of the prior judgments the Court refers to when saying the Governor's grounds had already been found deficient)
- F014: The ECtHR found in 2005 that the dissolution had been in breach of members' right to freedom of association (same)
- F016: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN apparently has links with UMO Ilinden (relevant because the Court refers to prior violations found in cases concerning UMO Ilinden as well as UMO Ilinden – PIRIN)
- F017: UMO Ilinden advocates similar ideas (same contextual relevance)

The Court also references Stankov and the United Macedonian Organisation Ilinden and United Macedonian Organisation Ilinden and Ivanov as prior judgments finding violations — these concern UMO Ilinden, not UMO Ilinden – PIRIN directly, which is why F016 and F017 are relevant.

**Positive obligation**

No factual grounding was identified in the merits analysis. No atomic facts map to this element.

**Interference**
The Court's interference finding rests on the ban, police orders, vehicle checks, and road block. None of these specific events are captured in the provided atomic facts F001–F018. The only atomic fact that is strictly necessary as background is:

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis: the Regional Governor banned the rallies using grounds that the ECtHR had already found deficient in prior cases concerning the same or linked organisations. The irreducible atomic facts for this conclusion are:
- F007: The applicants are members of UMO Ilinden – PIRIN (establishes standing and connection to the banned rallies)
- F011: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN was declared unconstitutional and dissolved (explains the 'unregistered status' ground the Governor relied upon, which was the basis for the necessity finding)
- F012: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN was dissolved (same)
- F013: The ECtHR had already found that the dissolution of UMO Ilinden – PIRIN breached freedom of association (establishes that the Governor's grounds had already been found deficient by the Court, which is the core of the necessity finding)
- F016: UMO Ilinden – PIRIN has links with UMO Ilinden (necessary to connect the prior UMO Ilinden judgments — Stankov, United Macedonian Organisation Ilinden and Ivanov — to the present case, since the Court relies on those judgments as establishing that the Governor's grounds were already found deficient)
