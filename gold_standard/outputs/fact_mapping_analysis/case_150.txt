LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rested on: (i) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated as organisers or participants (scope, implied), (ii) governmental interference with those assemblies through arrest, dispersal, and administrative conviction (interference, explicit), and (iii) the finding that this interference was not necessary in a democratic society (necessity, explicit, by reference to prior case-law). The factual core that is strictly necessary — i.e., without which the conclusion cannot stand — is the existence of public assemblies and the applicants' involvement in them as the basis for the interference finding. This is captured by F002 and F003. F001 is a reference to an appended table and does not itself establish any substantive fact. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, the irreducible core is F002 and F003.
