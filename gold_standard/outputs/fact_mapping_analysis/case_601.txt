LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

(a) Scope

i) Assembly exists
The reasoning text states: "disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers and/or participants of public assemblies."
This maps to: F002 (organisers of public assemblies) and F003 (participants of public assemblies). Both facts establish that public assemblies existed.

ii) Participation
The reasoning text states: "measures taken against them as organisers and/or participants of public assemblies."
This maps to: F002 (organisers) and F003 (participants). These are the only atomic facts that speak to the applicants' role in the assemblies.

iii) Peaceful character
As noted previously, there is no factual grounding in the reasoning text for this sub-element. None of the atomic facts F001-F004 address the peaceful character of the assemblies. This element remains implied with no atomic fact support.

(b) Interference
The reasoning text states: "the dispersal of these assemblies, as well as their arrest followed by their conviction for administrative offences."
This maps to F002 and F003, which describe the applicants as having been subjected to disproportionate measures as organisers and participants respectively. F004 (other complaints) does not correspond to this element. No atomic fact specifically mentions dispersal, arrest, or conviction — these details appear only in the reasoning section, not in the atomic facts provided. The closest atomic facts are F002 and F003, which refer to "disproportionate measures" against organisers and participants.

(e) Necessity in a democratic society
The reasoning text states: "the Court has not found any fact or argument capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion" and refers to "issues similar to those in the present cases" in the leading cases.
The factual grounding for the necessity assessment, to the extent it exists in the atomic facts, is again F002 and F003, which describe the measures as "disproportionate" — a characterisation that directly feeds into the necessity analysis. No atomic fact addresses the proportionality analysis in any greater detail.

Legitimate aim: Not discussed; no atomic facts correspond.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion, the Court must establish: (1) that there were public assemblies, (2) that the applicants participated in them (as organisers or participants), and (3) that measures were taken against them. All three points are captured by F002 and F003 together. F002 covers the organisers dimension and F003 covers the participants dimension; both describe the measures as disproportionate, which is the factual characterisation that drives the necessity finding. F001 and F004 are not strictly necessary for the conclusion — F001 is a procedural reference and F004 concerns unrelated complaints. The irreducible core is therefore F002 and F003.
