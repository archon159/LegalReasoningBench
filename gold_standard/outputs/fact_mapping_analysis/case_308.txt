LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: a civil servant was dismissed because he attended peaceful political demonstrations while on leave, his participation was purely passive, and the domestic court itself found no evidence of any breach of political restraint — yet the dismissal stood (reinstatement was refused). The Court found this disproportionate.

The irreducible facts are:
- F010: The applicant was on leave when he attended the rallies — this is critical because it undermines any claim that he used his official position or performed duties in a partisan way.
- F021: Participation in the rally on 26 February 2008 — one of the two acts that triggered dismissal.
- F022: Participation in the rally on 28 February 2008 — the other act that triggered dismissal.
- F032: The dismissal itself — the interference with Article 11 rights.
- F034: The stated reason (breach of political restraint) — establishes the causal link between assembly attendance and dismissal.
- F045: Administrative Court found the conclusion was based on assumptions — establishes that the domestic authority's own assessment undermined the justification for dismissal.
- F046: Administrative Court found no evidence of breach — the key domestic finding that the applicant had not actually violated political restraint, making the dismissal unjustified.
- F056: Text of section 24 § 1(d) — defines the restriction (use of official position in interests of political parties while performing service duties), which the Court implicitly uses to assess whether passive attendance on leave could constitute a breach.
