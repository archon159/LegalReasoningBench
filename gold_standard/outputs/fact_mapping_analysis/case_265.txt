LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court's implicit finding that there was an assembly and that the applicant participated in it rests on:
F006 (a demonstration was organised on 15 December 2009 in Muş — establishes the assembly)
F004 (the applicant's conviction was based on his participation in the demonstration — establishes participation as the factual premise accepted by the Court)

The peaceful character of the assembly is not discussed, so no atomic facts are strictly necessary for that sub-element.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
F033 (the applicant was sentenced to ten months' imprisonment for disseminating propaganda under section 7(2) of Law no. 3713 — this is the interference)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
F033 (the conviction was under section 7(2) of Law no. 3713 — the only factual anchor for the assumed legal basis; no further analysis is conducted)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
F033 (conviction under anti-terrorism legislation — the nature of the law is the only factual basis for assuming the aims of national security and prevention of disorder and crime)

--- NECESSITY ---
F033 (the conviction and sentence under section 7(2) — the interference whose necessity is assessed)
F054 (the trial court's reasoning equating participation in the demonstration and incitement to stone-throwing with the propaganda offence — this is the specific reasoning the Court finds insufficient)
F056 (the Court of Cassation upheld the judgment — confirming that no additional reasoning was provided at appellate level)
F059 (execution of sentence suspended — the Court notes this in the context of proportionality, having already found the conviction itself disproportionate)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts applicable.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) there was a demonstration (assembly); (2) the applicant participated in it; (3) he was convicted and sentenced to imprisonment specifically for disseminating propaganda under anti-terrorism law; (4) the sole factual basis offered by the domestic courts for that conviction was his participation in the demonstration and his alleged incitement to stone-throwing; and (5) the domestic courts failed to connect those acts to the propaganda offence. These are the facts without which the violation finding cannot stand.

F006 (the demonstration existed — necessary to establish scope/assembly)
F004 (conviction based on participation and inciting stone-throwing — the factual premise of the entire necessity analysis)
F033 (conviction and ten-month prison sentence under section 7(2) — the interference and the disproportionate sanction)
F054 (trial court's reasoning equating participation and incitement with the propaganda offence — the specific reasoning the Court found insufficient to justify the conviction under section 7(2))
F056 (Court of Cassation upheld without adding reasoning — confirms domestic courts as a whole failed to give relevant and sufficient reasons)
