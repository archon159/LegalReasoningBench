LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on the finding that there were interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly that were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on the fact that measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) were taken against the applicants as organisers and participants of public assemblies. This is captured by F002 and F003. F001 is merely a procedural reference to the appended table and adds no substantive content. F004 concerns other complaints irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.

The irreducible core is therefore F002 and F003 — without these facts, there is no assembly, no participation, no interference, and no basis for the necessity analysis or the violation finding.
- F002, F003
