LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two findings: (1) that there were interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly (which required establishing that assemblies existed and that governmental restrictions were imposed), and (2) that those interferences failed the "quality of law" and "necessity" tests (which the Court resolved by reference to Lashmankin without independent factual grounding in the atomic facts provided). The irreducible factual core from the atomic facts is therefore:
- F003 (restrictions on location of public religious events — establishes both the existence of assemblies and the governmental interference)
- F004 (restrictions on time of public religious events — same)
These two facts are the only ones from the atomic list that are strictly necessary for the Court to reach its conclusion: without them, there is no assembly, no interference, and no violation to find. F002 provides useful background but is not strictly necessary for the legal conclusion once F003 and F004 are established. F005 relates to a separate complaint about remedies and does not bear on the Article 11 merits conclusion. F001 is purely procedural.
