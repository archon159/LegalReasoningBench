LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) the assemblies were peaceful and the association did not incite violence; (2) the bans were systematic and sweeping; (3) the only real justification was the anti-constitutional character of the association and anticipated separatist speech, which the Court held insufficient; (4) there was no real foreseeable risk of violent action.

Irreducible facts:
F055 — systematic practice of bans since 1992 (establishes sweeping, disproportionate interference)
F162 — no criminal proceedings ever brought (establishes absence of real risk of violence or incitement)
F011 — statute expressly rejected violent means (establishes peaceful character of association)
F136 — memorandum expressly rejected violence (corroborates peaceful character)
F032 — factions differed in views (isolates violent statements from the association as a whole, preventing attribution)
F018 — courts found aims directed against unity of nation (establishes the anti-constitutional finding that was the primary basis for bans)
F020 — courts found UMO Ilinden dangerous for territorial integrity (same)
F043 — declaration demanded autonomy and withdrawal of Bulgarian armies (establishes separatist element — the anticipated speech that authorities sought to prevent)
F044 — declaration stated Ilinden would appeal internationally for unification of Macedonia (same)
F144 — Constitutional Court observed repeated calls for autonomy/secession (establishes that separatist suspicion was not unreasonable, but Court held this insufficient to justify ban)
F157 — flyer bore no signature and concerned a different organisation (arms allegation not attributable to UMO Ilinden)
F161 — Government provided no further information on arms documents (arms allegation unsubstantiated)
F083 — supporters not allowed to bring placards/banners (establishes significance/sweeping nature of interference)
F084 — supporters not allowed to make speeches (same)
F172 — only 3,019 self-identified Macedonians in 1992 census (small size of association, disproportionality of sweeping bans)
