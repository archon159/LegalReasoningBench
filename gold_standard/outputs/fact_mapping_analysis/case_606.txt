LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

--- SCOPE ---
The Court's implied factual grounding for scope rests on the reference to 'the demonstration' and the applicant's 'rights under that provision.' The relevant atomic facts are:
- F003: The applicant announced his intention to organise a demonstration on 9 December 2006 on Kossuth Square in Budapest, in front of Parliament. This is the assembly in question.
- F004: The announcement was made as required by the Assembly Act 1989, confirming the applicant sought to exercise a formal right of assembly.
- F003 also establishes the applicant's attempt to participate, as he was the organiser.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court's grounding quote: 'the non-acquiescence by the police in the demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of the applicant's rights under that provision, as the individualised application of the original police decision referred to by the Government.'
- F005: The Budapest Police Department refused to deal with the applicant's application to organise a demonstration on Kossuth Square.
- F006: The Budapest Police Department declared the area of Kossuth Square a 'security operational zone' for an indefinite period.
- F008: The Budapest Police Department observed that the designation placed it outside its jurisdiction as regards acquiescence in a demonstration.
These three facts together establish both the original measure and its individualised application to the applicant.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court's grounding quotes map to the following atomic facts:

(1) 'the police declared it a "security operational zone" in 2006, and it remained so throughout the material period.'
- F006: On 23 October 2006, the Budapest Police Department declared the area of Kossuth Square a 'security operational zone' for an indefinite period.

(2) 'on 11 November 2010 the Budapest Regional Court quashed the underlying police decisions, reproaching those authorities for failing to assess the necessity and proportionality of the measure as maintained subsequent to 22 November 2006.'
- F038: On 11 November 2010, the Budapest Regional Court issued judgment no. 27.K.31.354/2010/9., quashing both the first-instance and the second-instance administrative decisions.
- F040: The Budapest Regional Court pointed out that the impugned administrative decisions did not contain any concrete elements establishing the necessity and proportionality of maintaining the 'security operational zone' after the prolongation of 22 November 2006.

(3) 'on 4 April 2011 the Budapest Commander carried out the requisite scrutiny and found that the proportionality of the measure had not been proved.'
- F042: On 4 April 2011, the Budapest Police Commander partly sustained Mr K.'s complaint.
- F043: On 4 April 2011, the Budapest Police Commander noted that, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, the proportionality of the 'security operational zone' measure had successfully been challenged.

(4) 'these court rulings have effectively, if retroactively, removed the legal basis of the impugned measure.'
- This is the Court's legal conclusion drawn from F038, F040, F042, F043 above.

(5) 'the above two decisions were adopted in a procedure initiated by Mr K. rather than the applicant. For the Court, however, this is immaterial when it comes to the notion of lawfulness in the context of Article 11 § 2.'
- F027: Mr K. challenged the police's decision to declare Kossuth Square a 'security operational zone'.
- F038: The Budapest Regional Court quashed the administrative decisions in Mr K.'s case.
- F042: The Budapest Police Commander partly sustained Mr K.'s complaint.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that there was an intended assembly and that the applicant sought to participate in it:
- F003: The applicant announced his intention to organise a demonstration on 9 December 2006 on Kossuth Square. This single fact establishes both the existence of an intended assembly and the applicant's role as organiser/participant.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing the individualised application of the original police decision to the applicant:
- F005: The Budapest Police Department refused to deal with the applicant's application — this is the direct act of non-acquiescence.
- F006: The declaration of Kossuth Square as a 'security operational zone' — this is the original police decision of which F005 was the individualised application.
- F008: The Police Department's observation that the designation placed it outside its jurisdiction — this explains the mechanism by which the original decision produced the interference.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that the legal basis was retroactively removed:
- F006: The original declaration of Kossuth Square as a 'security operational zone' (the measure whose legal basis is in question).
- F038: The Budapest Regional Court quashed the underlying administrative decisions in Mr K.'s case.
- F040: The court found the decisions lacked any concrete elements establishing necessity and proportionality after 22 November 2006.
- F043: The Budapest Police Commander found that the proportionality of the measure had not been proved.

Not discussed. Empty.

Not discussed. Empty.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned entirely on the 'prescribed by law' test. The Court found a violation because the legal basis for the ban on Kossuth Square was retroactively removed by domestic court rulings and a subsequent administrative decision. The irreducible facts are:

- F003: The applicant sought to organise a demonstration on Kossuth Square — without this, there is no Article 11 claim at all.
- F006: The police declared Kossuth Square a 'security operational zone,' which is the measure that prevented the demonstration and whose lawfulness is the central question.
- F038: The Budapest Regional Court quashed the underlying administrative decisions, retroactively removing the legal basis of the measure.
- F040: The court found the decisions lacked concrete elements establishing necessity and proportionality — this is the specific reason the legal basis was found deficient.
- F043: The Budapest Police Commander found that proportionality had not been proved — this confirms the absence of a valid legal basis at the administrative level as well.

F005 and F008 are important for establishing interference but are not strictly necessary for the final conclusion once F006 and F003 are established, since the Court treats F005 as the individualised application of F006. F027 and F042 are relevant context but not strictly necessary once F038, F040, and F043 are included, as those facts already capture the outcome of Mr K.'s proceedings that the Court relied upon.
