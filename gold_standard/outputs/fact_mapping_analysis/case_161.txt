LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on: (i) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated as organisers or participants, and (ii) the fact that disproportionate measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) were taken against them. These are the facts that activate Article 11 protection and ground the necessity finding. F002 and F003 are the only atomic facts available that are strictly necessary. F001 is a procedural reference and F004 concerns other complaints unrelated to Article 11, so neither is essential to the Article 11 conclusion. The irreducible core is therefore F002 and F003.
