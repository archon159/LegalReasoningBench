LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality analysis. The Court found a violation because: (a) the sole reason for arrest and conviction was lack of authorisation for the demonstration; (b) the other justifications offered (Red Square security threat, traffic obstruction) were not reflected in domestic court reasoning and were factually implausible; (c) the authorities had in fact anticipated and prepared for the rally, so the arrest was not necessitated by unpreparedness; (d) the march was peaceful and modest in size. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

- F034: no march was authorised — the regulatory basis that triggered the arrest
- F082: police report and domestic court finding that applicants were arrested for walking in a group shouting slogans (= unauthorised demonstration) — establishes that lack of authorisation was the sole operative reason
- F091: Justice of the Peace conclusion based solely on unauthorised demonstration
- F092: conviction under Article 20.2 — the punitive measure constituting the interference
- F084: Mr I.'s testimony that authorities anticipated the rally and had specific instructions at 19 Tverskaya Street — directly undermines the Government's claim of being unprepared and supports finding that arrest was not necessitated by disorder
- F040: OMON reinforcements deployed on 14 April 2007
- F041: OMON brought from twenty-nine regions
- F042: stated purpose to protect public order — confirms advance preparation, making arrests disproportionate
- F085: group of fifty to sixty people — supports finding that Red Square threat was not imminent and march was modest/manageable
- F044: group marching and shouting slogans with no violence — supports peaceful character finding, which is central to the tolerance principle the Court applied
