- F016: the municipality issued the warning document on 23 November 2009 — this is the first interference

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two things: (1) the existence of a state interference with the first applicant's freedom of assembly, and (2) the complete absence of adequate reasoning by the domestic authorities to justify that interference. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

- F014: the first applicant planned and notified a static demonstration near the FSB office — establishes the assembly and the applicant's participation (scope)
- F019: the first applicant held the demonstration as planned — confirms participation and that the interference was real, not hypothetical
- F016: the municipality issued a warning document on 23 November 2009 referring to Article 294 of the Criminal Code and section 12 § 2 of the Public Events Act — establishes the first interference and the absence of substantiated reasoning
- F020: the first applicant was convicted under Article 20.2 § 2 of the Code of Administrative Offences — establishes the second interference (prosecution)
- F022: the justice of the peace's reasoning was limited to the failure to abstain despite the warning — establishes the absence of adequate necessity/legitimate aim reasoning at first instance
- F025: the Town Court stated the goal of the demonstration amounted to indirect interference with the administration of justice — establishes the absence of adequate reasoning at appeal level, and that the courts did not engage with the specific factual circumstances required to justify the interference
