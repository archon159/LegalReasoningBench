LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court's implied finding that the events fall within Article 11 scope rests on: the nature of the events as peaceful marches and picketing for human rights (F004, F005, F006), the submission of formal notices (F029, F083), and the absence of violent intent by participants. The most critical facts are F004, F005, F006 (establishing the peaceful, rights-promoting character of the events).

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The interference finding rests strictly on: the bans imposed in 2006 (F035, F036), 2007 (F085, F090, F091), and 2008 (F108, F110), and the enforcement through arrest and administrative charges (F065, F066, F095, F096). The most critical and representative facts are F035, F036, F065, F066, F085, F090, F091, F108.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court does not decide this element but notes that the morals-based justification had no basis in domestic law and was not raised in domestic proceedings. The critical facts are: F022 (the first deputy's own acknowledgment that the Assemblies Act did not provide for a ban), F036 (the ban was justified on public order/safety grounds in domestic proceedings), and F130, F139, F141 (the domestic legal framework, which provided only for venue/time changes, not outright bans).

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court declines to decide but engages with both alleged aims. For public safety/disorder: F037, F038 (petitions received, nature of opposition), F064 (approximately 100 counter-protesters actually present). For protection of morals: F013, F014, F016, F017, F018, F044, F046, F047 (mayor's statements showing the real motivation was moral disapproval of homosexuality). Critical facts: F036, F037, F038, F064, F013, F014, F016, F044, F046, F047.

--- NECESSITY ---
The necessity analysis is the core of the judgment. Critical facts:
- No risk assessment was carried out: F064, F069 (actual scale of counter-protest was about 100 persons)
- The petitions were of mixed character, most not threatening unlawful action: F037, F038
- The authorities banned rather than protected: F036, F065, F067, F068, F085, F090, F091
- The real motivation was moral disapproval, not security: F013, F014, F016, F017, F018, F026, F044, F046, F047
- The events were peaceful in purpose and form: F004, F005, F006
- The ban was repeated over three years across many proposed formats and venues: F035, F036, F085, F090, F091, F107, F108, F110, F111, F112, F113, F114, F115, F116, F117
- European consensus on the right to demonstrate: F147, F148, F149, F150

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Critical facts: F037, F038 (petitions indicating counter-protest intentions, which should have triggered protective measures), F064, F067, F068, F069 (actual violence by counter-protesters when events did occur, showing the threat was real but manageable), F036, F085, F090, F091 (authorities banned rather than protected).

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) the applicant organised peaceful assemblies promoting gay rights (F004, F005, F006); (2) the authorities repeatedly banned them over three years across many proposed formats (F035, F036, F085, F090, F091, F108); (3) the bans were enforced through arrest and administrative sanctions (F065, F066, F095, F096); (4) the real motivation for the bans was the mayor's and authorities' moral disapproval of homosexuality, not genuine security concerns (F013, F014, F016, F017, F018, F026, F044, F046, F047); (5) no proper risk assessment was carried out, and the actual scale of counter-protest was manageable (F064, F069); (6) the authorities banned the events rather than taking protective measures, including in the face of actual violence against participants (F067, F068); (7) the domestic legal framework did not even provide for outright bans (F022, F139, F141). The most irreducible core facts — those the conclusion strictly turned on — are: F004, F005, F006, F035, F036, F085, F090, F091, F108, F013, F014, F016, F044, F046, F047, F026, F064, F069, F067, F068, F022.
