LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The case turned on the existence of public assemblies, the applicants' involvement as organisers or participants, and the measures taken against them — which the Court found not necessary in a democratic society. The atomic facts that are absolutely essential for reaching the conclusion that Article 11 was violated are:
- F002: establishes that there were public assemblies and that the applicants were organisers subjected to disproportionate measures (the core factual predicate for scope, interference, and necessity)
- F003: establishes the same for participants

F001 and F004 are not strictly necessary for the Article 11 conclusion: F001 is a procedural reference to the appended table, and F004 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions, which are not part of the Article 11 analysis.
