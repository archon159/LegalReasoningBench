The general narrative facts establishing that a demonstration took place are: F001 (notice posted for demonstration), F044 (organisers decided to hold it despite ban), F048 (group gathered on outskirts of Usukhchay), F084 (groups gathered near village administration). LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
Assembly exists: F001 (notice posted for demonstration — establishes that a planned public assembly existed), F044 (organisers decided to hold it despite ban — establishes the assembly actually took place), F048 (approximately 1,000 people gathered on outskirts of Usukhchay — establishes the assembly as a concrete event).

Participation: F045 (first applicant took part), F046 (third applicant took part), F047 (second applicant was detained and could not participate), F071 (first and third applicants present at scene but left when clash started), F034 (second applicant arrested on 21 April — reason he could not attend), F210 (third applicant's arrest connected to 19 April events, not 25 April demonstration — relevant to interference analysis for third applicant's arrest).

Peaceful assembly (first phase): F001 (notice stated peaceful intent), F009 (organisers asked police for protection — consistent with peaceful intent), F048 (gathering on outskirts, no violence described in morning).
Peaceful assembly (individual applicants, second phase): F168 (first applicant suspected of incitement but charges dropped — F190), F190 (case discontinued for lack of evidence), F205, F206 (video showed applicants calling for calm), F210 (third applicant never suspected of violence on 25 April).
Collective violence (second phase, establishing non-sporadic character): F093 (road blocked with barricades), F097 (protesters threw stones at police), F099 (protesters armed with iron rods), F107, F108, F109 (police injuries), F192 (police officer wounded by hunter's gun).

--- INTERFERENCE ---
F051 (police blocked demonstrators moving towards centre of Usukhchay — blockade of village), F080 (mixed squad took position at roadblocks — police operation implementing blockade), F165 (first applicant arrested on 29 April for participation in organisation of unauthorised demonstration — arrest as interference), F003 (applicants not among organisers — reason decision of 17 April did not personally affect them as interference), F034 (second applicant arrested for 19 April events — reason his arrest was not interference with Art. 11 re 25 April), F210 (third applicant arrested for 19 April events — same reason).

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
F260 (section 7 of Public Gatherings Act — notice to be lodged not earlier than 15 and not later than 10 days before event — the ambiguous word "lodging"), F012 (payment slip showing notice posted on 10 April — organisers' interpretation of "lodging" as sending), F013 (notice received by district administration on 17 April — administration's interpretation of "lodging" as receipt), F261 (procedure for lodging notices to be defined in regional legislation — no regional clarification referred to by Government).

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
F074, F075, F076 (Order No. 7 — police feared disorder, road blocking, attacks on State institutions — establishes legitimate aim of preventing disorder and crime for blockade), F020, F024, F025, F026, F027 (events of 19 April — armed group attacked police roadblock — factual basis for security fears justifying police presence), F168, F170, F171 (suspicion that first applicant incited crowd to use firearms — legitimate aim of preventing crime for his arrest).

--- NECESSITY ---
For the ban (decision of 17 April):
F001, F012, F013 (notice posted 10 April, received 17 April — ambiguity and reasonable effort to comply), F014 (Mr A. — the very target of criticism — made the decision), F015 (Mr A. cited belated notice as reason), F016 (Mr A. cited park capacity as reason), F017, F018 (Mr A. cited falsity of accusations as reason — content-based restriction), F004, F005, F006 (subject matter of demonstration was political criticism of local administration — strong protection required), F090, F091 (alternative venue offered only at last moment on day of demonstration — not at time of ban).

For the blockade of Usukhchay:
F048 (crowd gathered peacefully on outskirts — no actual violence in morning), F051 (police blocked demonstrators), F077 (182 police officers deployed — situation under control), F080 (police took positions at roadblocks), F090, F091 (alternative venue offered only at last moment — inappropriate), F015, F016, F017, F018 (reasons for ban were unconvincing — blockade implemented an unjustified ban), F020, F024, F025, F026, F027 (19 April violence — some basis for security concern but not sufficient to justify complete blockade given peaceful morning behaviour).

For the Miskindzha dispersal (no violation):
F093, F094 (road blocked with barricades — federal road, major disruption), F097, F099 (protesters attacked police with stones and iron rods), F107, F108, F109, F192 (police injuries including gunshot wound), F193, F194, F195 (cartridges not belonging to police found at scene), F077 (police outnumbered despite 182 officers), F106, F235, F247 (some officers fired grenades in breach of instructions but no evidence of deliberate intent to kill).

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated in part)

The case turned on two distinct findings: (A) violation regarding the blockade of Usukhchay, and (B) no violation regarding the Miskindzha dispersal. The following facts are strictly necessary for the Court to reach these conclusions:

For the VIOLATION (blockade of Usukhchay):
- F001: Notice posted on 10 April — establishes organisers made reasonable effort to comply with notification requirement.
- F012: Payment slip showing posting on 10 April — establishes the organisers' interpretation of "lodging" as sending.
- F013: Notice received 17 April — establishes the ambiguity and the short transit time.
- F014: Mr A. (the target of criticism) made the decision — establishes content-based restriction by the very authority targeted.
- F015: Mr A. cited belated notice — first reason for ban, found to lack firm legal basis.
- F016: Mr A. cited park capacity — second reason, found insufficient to justify total ban rather than alternative venue.
- F017, F018: Mr A. cited falsity of accusations — third reason, content-based restriction, found unjustifiable.
- F004, F005, F006: Subject matter was political criticism of local administration — requires strong protection.
- F048: Approximately 1,000 people gathered peacefully on outskirts of Usukhchay in the morning — no actual violence.
- F051: Police blocked demonstrators from entering village centre — the interference itself.
- F077, F080: 182 police officers deployed and situation under control — blockade was not necessary for safety.
- F090, F091: Alternative venue (municipal garage) offered only at last moment on day of demonstration, declined — not a genuine alternative proposed at time of ban.

For the NO VIOLATION (Miskindzha dispersal):
- F093, F094: Road blocked with barricades — federal road, major disruption beyond ordinary demonstration inconvenience.
- F097, F099: Protesters attacked police with stones and iron rods — protesters initiated violence.
- F107, F108, F109, F192: Police injuries including gunshot wound — intensity of violence was considerable.
- F193, F194, F195: Cartridges not belonging to police found at scene — corroborates that protesters had firearms.
- F106, F235, F247: Some officers fired grenades in breach of instructions but no evidence of deliberate intent to kill — limits the finding to no Art. 11 violation (while acknowledging unprofessional conduct).
