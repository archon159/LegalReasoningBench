LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish that assemblies took place, that the applicants participated, and that the assemblies were peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F005, F006 (participation in 20 October 2012 demonstration)
- F011, F012, F013 (participation in subsequent demonstrations)
- F016 (peaceful character — the only factual basis available in the text, even though it comes from the applicants' account, as the Court does not contradict it)

**Interference**
The Court needs to establish that state action interfered with the applicants' assembly rights. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F022, F023, F024, F025 (police dispersal of each demonstration as soon as protesters began to gather — this is the core act of interference)
- F026, F028 (arrests and administrative proceedings — the measures producing the chilling effect)

**Prescribed by law**
No atomic facts strictly necessary — not discussed in this excerpt.

**Legitimate aim**
No atomic facts strictly necessary — not discussed in this excerpt.

**Necessity**
The Court's necessity reasoning turns on the chilling effect of the arrests and administrative proceedings. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F026, F028 (arrests and administrative proceedings — the measures whose proportionality is assessed)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on: (1) the applicants participating in assemblies that were peaceful; (2) the police immediately dispersing those assemblies; and (3) the applicants being arrested and subjected to administrative proceedings, producing a chilling effect on political participation. These are the facts without which the violation conclusion cannot stand.

- F005, F006 (participation in 20 October 2012 demonstration — establishing scope for first and second applicants)
- F011, F012, F013 (participation in subsequent demonstrations — establishing scope for second and third applicants)
- F016 (peaceful character of demonstrations — necessary for Article 11 protection to apply)
- F022, F023, F024, F025 (immediate police dispersal — core act of interference)
- F026, F028 (arrests and administrative proceedings — the measures producing the chilling effect and grounding the violation finding)
