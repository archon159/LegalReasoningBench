LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

(a) Scope
The Court needed to establish that there was a peaceful assembly and that the applicant participated in it. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F013: The applicant started a silent protest — this is the core fact establishing the assembly and participation.
- F105: No authorisation needed for fewer than fifty participants — this is what makes the protest "in accordance with the law concerning assemblies."
- F040: Taken to police van without resistance — most direct factual support for the finding that the applicant did not resist (peaceful throughout).
- F099: Court found applicant had not resisted arrest — judicial confirmation of peacefulness.
- F098: Court found applicant had not insulted police — judicial confirmation of peacefulness.

(b) Interference
The strictly necessary fact is the arrest itself:
- F039: Two uniformed police officers apprehended the applicant.
- F040: Taken to police van without resistance — confirms the arrest was completed.

(c) Prescribed by law
The Court's reasoning turns on the absence of any lawful basis for the arrest given the applicant's entirely lawful conduct. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F013: Silent protest — the assembly itself was lawful.
- F105: No authorisation needed — legal basis confirming the protest was lawful.
- F019: The applicant stated he is a journalist on hunger strike and refused to stop filming — filming was not unlawful.
- F040: No resistance from the applicant — no lawful ground for arrest on that basis.
- F098: Not insulting police — removes the factual basis for the charge.
- F099: Not resisting arrest — removes the factual basis for the charge.

(d) Legitimate aim: Not discussed by the Court; no atomic facts to list.

(e) Necessity: Not discussed by the Court; no atomic facts to list.

(f) Positive obligation: Not discussed by the Court; no atomic facts to list.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on whether the arrest of the applicant during a lawful, peaceful protest had any legal basis. The Court found it did not. The irreducible facts are:
- F013: The applicant started a silent protest — establishes the assembly.
- F105: No authorisation needed for fewer than fifty participants — establishes the protest was lawful.
- F039: Two uniformed police officers apprehended the applicant — establishes the state interference.
- F099: The applicant had not resisted arrest — removes the factual predicate for the arrest charge.
- F098: The applicant had not insulted the police — removes the factual predicate for the insult charge.
- F040: Taken to police van without resistance — confirms both the interference and the peaceful nature of the applicant's conduct throughout.
