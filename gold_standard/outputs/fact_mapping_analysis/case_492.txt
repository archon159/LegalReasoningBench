LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish: (i) that assemblies took place; (ii) that the applicant participated; (iii) that the assemblies were peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F017: nationwide daily protest rallies were held (assembly exists)
- F018: main meeting place was Freedom Square (assembly exists, location)
- F012/F013: applicant was active supporter and member of Ter-Petrosyan campaign (participation)
- F019: rallies attracted tens of thousands (scale of assembly)
- F021: detailed description of demonstrations and police intervention breaking up the camp on 1 March 2008 (peaceful character and its disruption)
- F023: escalation of tensions and resulting violence on 1 March 2008 (context for peacefuleness assessment)
- F068: domestic court finding that applicant woke up campers and called on them to arm themselves — which the ECtHR rejected as unsubstantiated (peaceful character contested)
- F070: domestic court finding about weapons discovered — which the ECtHR rejected (peaceful character contested)

Most critical/representative: F017, F018, F012, F021, F023, F068, F070

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needed to establish that the conviction was based on protected assembly activity. Strictly necessary:
- F040: applicant charged under Article 225 (organising mass disorder)
- F060: applicant found guilty under Article 225 § 1
- F061: sentenced to seven years' imprisonment
- F063: conviction based on involvement in post-election protests and speeches at Freedom Square

Most critical/representative: F040, F060, F061, F063

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two pivotal questions: (1) was the applicant's conviction based on protected assembly activity (interference with a peaceful assembly), and (2) did the domestic courts provide relevant and sufficient reasons to justify that interference (necessity)? The irreducible facts are:

- F017: protest rallies were held — establishes the assembly
- F018: Freedom Square was the main meeting place — identifies the protected assembly
- F012/F013: applicant was an active participant and organiser — establishes his connection to the assembly
- F060: applicant convicted under Article 225 § 1 — the interference
- F061: sentenced to seven years' imprisonment — severity of the interference
- F063: conviction based on involvement in protests and speeches at Freedom Square — links the interference to protected assembly activity
- F066: domestic court finding on arms build-up — the key factual allegation the ECtHR found unsubstantiated as to the applicant's involvement
- F074: domestic court finding that applicant organised arming and assaults — the core finding of the domestic courts, found by ECtHR to lack specific detail
- F078: domestic court finding on concrete instructions (barricades, stones, batons) — the most concrete domestic finding, still found insufficiently detailed
- F108, F109: PACE condemnation of charges as politically motivated — establishing the pattern and the need for close scrutiny that ultimately drove the finding of violation
