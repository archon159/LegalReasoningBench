LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that there was an assembly, that the applicants participated, and that it was (initially) peaceful.
-> F015: establishes the assembly (gathering in a public park on 31 July 2000)
-> F016: establishes the subject matter of the protest (F-type prisons)
-> F017: establishes the subject matter of the protest (Bergama Prison events)
-> F019: establishes the peaceful, expressive intent (reading out a press statement)
-> F027: establishes the non-violent character of the applicants' conduct (protest banners, no weapons)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court identifies two acts of interference: police intervention and criminal conviction.
-> F023: police arrested the applicants and took them into custody
-> F024: police used force to disperse the protesters
-> F039: applicants were convicted
-> F044: sentenced to one year and three months' imprisonment

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court grounds this solely in the existence of a statutory basis.
-> F054: section 22 of Law no. 2911 prohibited demonstrations in parks — this is the specific provision the Court names as the legal basis

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts prevention of disorder without independent factual analysis. The most relevant atomic fact establishing the context for this acceptance is:
-> F022: the group ignored the police warning — this is the closest factual anchor for the disorder-prevention aim, as it establishes non-compliance with lawful instructions
-> F033: the charge itself (participating in a demonstration without permission) reflects the disorder-prevention rationale

--- NECESSITY ---
This is the central element. The strictly necessary facts are:
-> F015: gathering in a public park in Ankara (establishes the context and the limited disruption likely)
-> F019: participants wanted to read out a press statement (establishes peaceful expressive intent)
-> F027: demonstrating with protest banners (establishes non-violent initial conduct)
-> F024: police used force to disperse the protesters (establishes the nature of the intervention)
-> F025: some protesters were injured as a result of police use of force (establishes the severity of the intervention)
-> F029: three police officers were injured during the clash (establishes that violence occurred but in the context of the police-initiated dispersal)
-> F044: sentenced to one year and three months' imprisonment (establishes the disproportionate sanction)
-> F045: enforcement suspended for most but not all applicants (establishes that the sentence was actually enforced against some)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts correspond to this element — the Court's mention is purely doctrinal.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality assessment. The Court found a violation because: (1) the gathering was initially peaceful and posed no serious danger to public order; (2) the police intervened with considerable force, escalating rather than managing the situation; and (3) the criminal sanction imposed was disproportionate. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary for this conclusion:

-> F015: the gathering was in a public park — establishes the limited disruption context
-> F019: participants wanted to read out a press statement — establishes the peaceful expressive purpose
-> F027: applicants were demonstrating with protest banners — establishes the non-violent initial conduct of the applicants specifically
-> F024: police used force to disperse the protesters — establishes the nature and severity of the interference
-> F025: some protesters were injured as a result of police use of force — establishes the real-world impact of the disproportionate intervention
-> F044: sentenced to one year and three months' imprisonment — establishes the disproportionate criminal sanction
-> F054: section 22 of Law no. 2911 — establishes the prescribed-by-law requirement (necessary to reach the necessity stage at all)
-> F039: applicants were convicted — necessary to establish that the interference included a criminal sanction, not just dispersal
