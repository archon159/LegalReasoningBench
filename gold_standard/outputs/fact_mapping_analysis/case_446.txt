LIST 3: Atomic facts strictly necessary for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the finding that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly — their arrest and conviction as organisers or participants of public assemblies — were not necessary in a democratic society. The irreducible factual core is:
- F002: establishes that applicants as organisers were subjected to disproportionate measures in the context of public assemblies.
- F003: establishes that applicants as participants were subjected to disproportionate measures in the context of public assemblies.

These two facts are the minimum necessary to ground the conclusion that Article 11 was violated: they identify the existence of assemblies, the applicants' involvement, and the governmental measures that constituted the interference. Without them, there is no factual basis for any element of the Article 11 analysis. F001 and F004 are not strictly necessary for the violation conclusion itself.
