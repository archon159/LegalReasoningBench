LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis — specifically the Court's finding that the reasons for the ban were not relevant or sufficient. The irreducible factual core consists of:

F003 — L.G. died while at a police station: establishes the non-political, commemorative purpose of the march, which is central to the Court's finding that the march had nothing to do with the post-election disorder.

F012 — State of emergency declared 1 March 2008: establishes the starting point of the post-election crisis period.

F013 — State of emergency for 20 days: establishes that the emergency had expired approximately seven weeks before the planned march, undermining the justification for the ban.

F016 — March planned for 12 May 2008: establishes the date of the planned event, which is critical both to the temporal gap argument and to the anniversary significance.

F020 — Mayor's ban decision of 8 May 2008: the act constituting the interference — without this there is no case.

F034 — Police prevented the organisers from holding the march: the physical enforcement of the ban, establishing that interference actually occurred.

F028 — Ban cited NSS opinion of 7 May 2008: establishes the evidentiary basis the Mayor relied upon.

F029 — NSS opinion claimed credible and verified data: the Court found this to be unsubstantiated and couched in general terms, which is the core of its necessity finding.

F001 — Applicant is a non-governmental human rights organisation: establishes the absence of any link between the applicant and the post-election disorder, supporting the finding that the ban lacked relevant and sufficient reasons.
