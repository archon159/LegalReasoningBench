LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The final conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on: (1) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated as organisers or participants (F002, F003), and (2) the disproportionate measures taken against them (F002, F003). F002 and F003 are therefore the irreducible factual core. F004 (other complaints) is not necessary for the Article 11 conclusion. F001 identifies the parties but is not itself a substantive fact that the legal conclusion turns on.
