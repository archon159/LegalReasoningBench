LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on a single, narrow point: the Municipality refused to authorise the demonstration for the full requested period (1-31 August 2006) for a reason — that one day of protest was sufficient — that (a) did not correspond to any of the legally permitted grounds for refusal under the Assemblies Act (sections 6 and 7), and (b) could not be considered a "relevant and sufficient" reason within the meaning of Article 11. No public order concern was ever raised. These are the facts the conclusion actually rests on:

- F034: Hyde Park applied for authorisation to hold a peaceful demonstration
- F035: The application covered 1-31 August 2006
- F038: The Municipality authorised only 1 August 2006 (the partial refusal)
- F039: The Municipality's stated reason was that one day of protest was sufficient
- F143: Section 12(6) — the only legal basis for refusal requires evidence of breach of sections 6 and 7 with serious consequences; the Municipality's reason did not meet this standard
- F130: Section 6(1) — the substantive content of the standard not invoked
- F131: Section 7 — the other substantive content of the standard not invoked
