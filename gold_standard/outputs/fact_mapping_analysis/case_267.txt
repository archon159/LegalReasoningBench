LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court must at minimum establish: (i) that there were assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope), and (ii) that governmental measures were taken against them in that context (interference). Both of these rest solely on F002. F001 is a procedural fact that does not bear on the substantive conclusion. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, the irreducible factual core is F002 alone.
