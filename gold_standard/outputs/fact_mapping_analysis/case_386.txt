LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court found a violation of Article 11 on the basis that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The entire chain of reasoning — scope (assembly, participation), interference, and necessity — rests on a single atomic fact: F002. This fact establishes (a) that there were public assemblies, (b) that the applicants were organisers or participants, and (c) that disproportionate measures were taken against them.
