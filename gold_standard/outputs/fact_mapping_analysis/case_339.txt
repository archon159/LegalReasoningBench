LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court implies scope (assembly exists, applicant participated) from the following strictly necessary facts:
- F013: presidential election held (establishes the trigger for the protest)
- F018: nationwide daily protest rallies from 20 February 2008 (establishes the assembly)
- F019: main meeting place was Freedom Square (establishes the specific assembly)
- F022: the applicant made a speech at the rally at Freedom Square on 22 February 2008 (establishes participation)
- F023: the applicant expressed support for Ter-Petrosyan (establishes the political nature of participation)
- F024: the applicant criticised the conduct of the presidential election (establishes the content of participation)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The following facts are strictly necessary to support the Court's finding of interference:
- F022: applicant's speech at Freedom Square (the act that triggered the chain of events)
- F028: General Prosecutor applied for dismissal on the same day as the speech
- F030: dismissal decree issued on 23 February 2008, the day after the speech and the day of the police operation
- F042: police announcement citing vague 'operative information' as trigger for the operation
- F055: the CZ-75 pistol was legally issued to the applicant (known to arresting officers, undermining the stated basis for arrest)
- F120: on 6 June 2008 the investigator questioned the applicant mainly about his speech at Freedom Square, not about the charges
- F121: the illegal weapons charge was dropped because the applicant had a valid licence (showing the charge was unfounded from the start)
- F123: a new charge of usurpation of power was brought, directly referencing the applicant's involvement in the protest movement
- F139: the usurpation of power charge was dropped for lack of evidence
- F115: the applicant's case was joined to the main opposition criminal case no. 62202608
- F174: the applicant was convicted solely on the assault charge
- F181: the conviction was based exclusively on police officer testimony
- F183: the court found the applicant's submissions unsubstantiated without independent analysis
- F201: PACE report noted that high-level officials arrested on seemingly artificial charges, leaving impression of political motivation
- F203: OSCE/ODIHR monitored approximately a hundred trials of opposition leaders and supporters
- F204: the applicant's case was included in the OSCE/ODIHR monitoring project

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The prescribed-by-law finding is grounded in the same facts as interference (the Court explicitly cross-references its earlier findings). The strictly necessary facts are the same core facts establishing the arbitrary/pretextual nature of the prosecution:
- F022: speech at Freedom Square as the true trigger
- F030: dismissal on the same day as the police operation
- F042: vague operative information as stated basis for arrest
- F121: weapons charge dropped because licence was valid all along
- F123: usurpation charge directly tied to protest participation
- F139: usurpation charge dropped for lack of evidence
- F174: conviction resting solely on the assault charge
- F181: conviction based exclusively on police testimony

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the finding that the prosecution and conviction were a disguised interference with the applicant's freedom of assembly, making the interference not prescribed by law and therefore a violation of Article 11. The irreducible facts are:

- F022: the applicant made a speech at the opposition rally at Freedom Square on 22 February 2008 (establishes the protected assembly activity)
- F028: the General Prosecutor applied for the applicant's dismissal on the very same day as the speech, on the ground of political activity (establishes the immediate official reaction to the speech)
- F030: the President issued the dismissal decree on 23 February 2008, the day after the speech and the same day as the police operation (establishes the temporal link between speech and prosecution)
- F042: the police operation was triggered by vague 'operative information' whose nature and source were never disclosed (establishes the pretextual basis for arrest)
- F121: the illegal weapons charge was dropped because the applicant had a valid licence — information that was available from the outset (establishes that the primary stated basis for arrest was unfounded)
- F120: during questioning on 6 June 2008, the investigator asked almost exclusively about the speech at Freedom Square, not about the charges (establishes the true focus of the prosecution)
- F123: a new charge of usurpation of power was brought directly referencing the applicant's participation in the protest movement (establishes the direct link between prosecution and assembly)
- F139: the usurpation charge was dropped for lack of evidence (establishes that this charge too was unfounded)
- F115: the applicant's case was joined to the main criminal case against opposition leaders (establishes institutional linkage to the political prosecution)
- F174: the applicant was convicted solely on the assault charge based exclusively on police testimony (establishes that the only surviving basis for conviction was the most easily fabricated charge)
- F181: the conviction rested exclusively on police officer testimony with no independent corroboration (establishes the structural similarity to other cases found to be politically motivated)
