LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish: (i) there was an assembly; (ii) the applicants participated; (iii) it was peaceful.
- F009: applicants took part in public demonstration at Chistyye Prudy — establishes assembly and participation
- F011: demonstration duly authorised — establishes legitimacy of the assembly
- F027: about sixty people began walking after the meeting — establishes the subsequent group movement as a perceived assembly
- F021: group of about one hundred protesters — establishes scale of the perceived march
- F024: no one in the surrounded group put up any resistance — establishes peaceful character

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needed to establish that a governmental act interfered with the right.
- F016: applicants were arrested — core act of interference
- F053: applicants charged with administrative offence — second act of interference
- F103: second applicant sentenced to fifteen days' administrative detention — third act of interference (punitive measure)
- F144: first applicant sentenced to fifteen days' administrative detention — third act of interference (punitive measure)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court declined to resolve this element, citing evidentiary insufficiency. The strictly necessary facts are those that explain why the Court could not resolve it.
- F054: charges based on identical statements of police officers I. and F. — explains the evidentiary basis
- F097: second applicant insisted he was arrested without any warning or orders
- F123: first applicant insisted he had not received any orders
- F102: Justice of the Peace dismissed testimonies of second applicant and witness B.
- F143: Justice of the Peace dismissed testimonies of first applicant and witnesses A. and T.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepted the aim of maintaining public order. The strictly necessary facts are those that made this plausible.
- F027: group of about sixty people walking along the streets after the meeting — basis for public order concern
- F028: group walking along road, obstructing traffic, chanting slogans — basis for public order and road safety concern

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court found the measures disproportionate. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F024: no one in the surrounded group put up any resistance — establishes peaceful/non-violent character of the march, underpinning the finding that interception was disproportionate
- F027: about sixty people — establishes the limited scale of the march
- F021: about one hundred protesters — establishes the limited scale of the march
- F160: Tverskoy District Court held the first applicant participated in a march that had not been notified — establishes that the sole reason for interception was lack of authorisation
- F186: Article 27.2(1) CAO: escort only if report cannot be drawn up at place of offence — establishes the legal standard against which the arrest was measured
- F035: both applicants taken to police bus after arrest — establishes that the report could have been drawn up on the spot (in the bus)
- F051: police reports drawn up only at Kitay-Gorod District police station — establishes that the escort was not carried out as quickly as possible and that the report was not drawn up at the place of offence
- F103: second applicant sentenced to fifteen days' administrative detention — establishes the severity of the sanction
- F144: first applicant sentenced to fifteen days' administrative detention — establishes the severity of the sanction
- F183: Article 19.3 CAO: maximum penalty of fifteen days — establishes that the maximum available penalty was imposed
- F004: both applicants are political activists and opposition leaders — establishes the chilling effect on political participation
- F158: Tverskoy District Court dismissed appeals and upheld first-instance judgment — establishes that domestic courts failed to balance interests

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. No atomic facts correspond to this element.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on three findings: (1) the interception of the march was disproportionate because it was peaceful and small-scale and the sole reason for stopping it was lack of authorisation; (2) the arrest was disproportionate because there was no pressing social need to escort the applicants to a police station; (3) the conviction was disproportionate because the maximum penalty was imposed for a trivial offence. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

- F009: applicants took part in the authorised public demonstration — establishes the assembly
- F011: demonstration duly authorised — establishes the legitimate starting point
- F027: about sixty people began walking after the meeting — establishes the perceived march
- F024: no one in the surrounded group put up any resistance — establishes the peaceful character, critical to the disproportionality finding on interception
- F160: Tverskoy District Court held the first applicant participated in a march that had not been notified — establishes that the sole reason for interception was lack of authorisation
- F016: applicants were arrested — core interference
- F186: Article 27.2(1) CAO: escort only if report cannot be drawn up at place of offence — legal standard for the arrest
- F035: both applicants taken to police bus after arrest — establishes that drawing up the report on the spot was feasible
- F051: police reports drawn up only at Kitay-Gorod District police station — establishes that the escort was not justified under domestic law
- F103: second applicant sentenced to fifteen days' administrative detention — establishes the severity of the sanction
- F144: first applicant sentenced to fifteen days' administrative detention — establishes the severity of the sanction
- F183: Article 19.3 CAO: maximum penalty of fifteen days — establishes that the maximum was imposed for a trivial offence
- F004: both applicants are political activists and opposition leaders — establishes the chilling effect, amplifying the disproportionality finding
- F158: Tverskoy District Court dismissed appeals — establishes failure of domestic courts to balance interests
