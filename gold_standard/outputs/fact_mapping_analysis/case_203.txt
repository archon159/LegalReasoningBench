LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (no violation of Article 11)

The case turned on two main issues: (1) whether the spontaneity exception applied (which would have required tolerance of the unnotified assembly), and (2) whether the applicant's conduct exceeded what is protected under Article 11 (making the criminal conviction proportionate). The irreducible facts are:

-> F004 (statutory destruction date was 20 July 2002 — defeats the spontaneity argument that was the applicant's main defence)
-> F028 (domestic court's finding that the law left no doubt about the destruction date — confirms rejection of spontaneity)
-> F008 (complete blockade of all six lanes of Erzsébet Bridge — establishes the disproportionate nature of the conduct)
-> F010 (refusal to comply with police orders — establishes lack of flexibility and cooperation)
-> F012 (massive traffic jam all over Budapest — establishes the scale of disruption caused)
-> F030 (traffic paralysed all over Budapest for several hours — establishes duration and magnitude of disruption)
-> F025 (conviction was for disturbance of public works, not for participation in the demonstration — establishes that the conviction targeted conduct, not assembly)
