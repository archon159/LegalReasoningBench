LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on one central fact: the domestic authorities imposed a ban on holding LGBT public assemblies (F002). This is the fact that triggers the Article 11 analysis, grounds the interference finding, and underpins the necessity assessment. Without F002, there is no interference and no violation to assess. F003 (discriminatory treatment of applications) is also relevant to the Article 14 conjunction finding but is not strictly necessary for the standalone Article 11 violation conclusion — the ban itself is sufficient. Therefore the irreducible factual core for the Article 11 violation conclusion is F002 alone.
-> F002
