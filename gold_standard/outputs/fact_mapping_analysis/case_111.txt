- F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers of public assemblies." — directly corresponds to the "organisers" part of the quote.
- F003: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies." — directly corresponds to the "participants" part of the quote.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

- Scope: F002 and F003 are strictly necessary. The Court's implied finding that there were assemblies and that the applicants were organisers or participants rests entirely on these two facts. Both are needed because the applicant group includes both organisers (F002) and participants (F003).
- Interference: F002 and F003 are again the necessary foundation. The interference (arrest, dispersal, conviction) is described in the same sentence that characterises the applicants as organisers or participants. Without F002 and F003, there is no factual basis for the interference finding.
- Prescribed by law: No atomic facts support this element — it was not discussed.
- Legitimate aim: No atomic facts support this element — it was not discussed.
- Necessity: No atomic facts from the provided list are strictly necessary to support the necessity reasoning, which rests on prior case-law rather than on any of the four atomic facts.
- Positive obligation: No atomic facts support this element — it was not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on: (i) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants were organisers or participants (scope, implied), (ii) governmental interference with those assemblies (interference, explicit), and (iii) that interference not being necessary in a democratic society (necessity, explicit). The necessity finding is driven by prior case-law rather than by any of the four atomic facts. However, the entire Article 11 analysis is only triggered because there were assemblies and applicants who organised or participated in them and suffered disproportionate measures. The irreducible factual core from the atomic facts is therefore:
- F002: establishes that organisers of public assemblies were subject to disproportionate measures — essential to ground both scope and interference for that sub-group.
- F003: establishes that participants of public assemblies were subject to disproportionate measures — essential to ground both scope and interference for that sub-group.
Without both F002 and F003, the Court could not have found that Article 11 was engaged at all, and therefore could not have reached a violation conclusion. F001 and F004 are not strictly necessary for the violation conclusion itself.
