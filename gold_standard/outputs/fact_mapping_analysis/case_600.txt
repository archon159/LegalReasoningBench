LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The case turned on: (1) the existence of a protest in which the applicants participated (F002), and (2) the measures taken against them in connection with that protest (F002). F002 is the single atomic fact that is strictly necessary across all the elements that led to the violation finding — it establishes scope, interference, and the factual premise for the necessity assessment. F003 (the anti-war nature of the protest) is contextually significant and would ordinarily bear on the weight of the assembly right in a necessity analysis, but the Court did not explicitly rely on it in its reasoning, preferring instead to rest its conclusion on prior case-law. It is therefore not part of the irreducible core as the Court actually reasoned. F001 and F004 are procedural/administrative facts that do not bear on the Article 11 conclusion.
