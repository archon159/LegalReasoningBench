LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on two distinct routes to violation:

For the first group (Art. 220 § 7 — not prescribed by law):
The violation turned on: (a) the vague text of Art 220 § 7; (b) the inconsistent domestic case-law; (c) the fact that participation in a legally authorised demonstration, with conduct that did not constitute an offence, was treated as sufficient to convict for membership of an armed organisation; and (d) the severity of the resulting sentences.

For the second group (s.7(2) Law 3713 — not necessary in a democratic society):
The violation turned on: (a) the legally authorised and peaceful nature of the demonstrations; (b) the conduct of the applicants (slogans, clothing, gestures) not amounting to advocacy of violence; (c) the absence of any reasoning by the domestic courts as to why the conduct incited violence; and (d) the severity of the sentences.

Irreducible core facts:
- F003: MLKP classified as illegal armed organisation (necessary backdrop for the charges)
- F021: demonstration of 17 Dec 2005 was legally authorised (peaceful/lawful assembly — critical for both routes)
- F022: demonstration of 19 Feb 2006 was legally authorised (same)
- F062: participation of applicants in 17 Dec 2005 demonstration (participation established)
- F063: Selçuk Mart's participation in 19 Feb 2006 demonstration
- F140: text of Art 220 § 7 — vague wording (core of prescribed by law finding)
- F167: Plenary Court of Cassation judgment of 4 March 2008 (inconsistent case-law — core of prescribed by law finding)
- F051: Assize Court judgment of 22 October 2008 convicting applicants (the impugned act)
- F107: conviction under Art 220 § 7 and Art 314 §§ 2 and 3 (first group)
- F118: conviction under s.7(2) Law 3713 (second group)
- F042, F043, F044, F045: prison sentences of first group (severity — chilling effect and lack of safeguards)
- F047: prison sentence of second group (severity — disproportionality)
- F064, F065, F066, F067: slogans chanted (conduct found not to advocate violence)
- F071, F072, F079, F080: wearing ESP clothes/pennants and red ribbons (conduct found not to advocate violence)
- F117: Assize Court's finding of propaganda inciting violence without reasoning (absence of relevant and sufficient reasons — core of necessity finding)
- F119: Court of Cassation upheld conviction (exhaustion of domestic remedies and confirmation of interference)
