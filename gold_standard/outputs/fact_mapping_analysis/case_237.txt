LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

(a) Scope: The Court needs to establish that there was a peaceful assembly, that the applicants intended to participate, and that the assembly was peaceful. The most critical facts are:
- F018 and/or F019: establish that the applicants intended to participate in peaceful demonstrations (covers assembly existence, participation, and peaceful character in one fact).
- F020 and/or F021: establish that the applicants were arrested while distributing leaflets for an authorised demonstration — the word 'authorised' reinforces the peaceful/lawful character and the participation attempt.
Of these, F018 and F020 (for the April 8 demonstrations) and F019 and F021 (for the April 22 demonstrations) are the most representative. Since the cases concern both dates, F018 and F019 together are the irreducible minimum for scope, with F020 and F021 adding the factual detail of the arrest context. For strict necessity, F018 and F019 suffice to establish scope.

(b) Interference: The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the right. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F020 and/or F021: the arrests themselves are the primary act of interference.
- F022: the convictions are the secondary act of interference.
F020 and F022 together (or F021 and F022) are strictly necessary — the arrest and the conviction are the two acts of interference identified.

(c) Prescribed by law: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element is not discussed.

(d) Legitimate aim: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element is not discussed.

(e) Necessity: The Court's reasoning rests entirely on similarity to prior cases. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F013 and F014 (similarity to Huseynli and Others) and/or F015 and F016 (similarity to Huseynov and Others): these are the direct factual basis for the Court's reasoning by reference. Since the Court cites both precedents, both pairs are relevant, but F013/F014 and F015/F016 are equivalent in function. The irreducible minimum is at least one pair — F013 and F014 suffice, but F015 and F016 are equally necessary given the Court cites both.
- F020 and/or F021 and F022: the underlying facts (arrests and convictions) that are being compared to the prior cases.
For strict necessity: F013, F014, F015, F016 (establishing similarity), and F020, F022 (the underlying facts being compared).

(f) Positive obligation: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element is not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on: (i) the existence of peaceful demonstrations that the applicants intended to join; (ii) the arrests and convictions as acts of interference; and (iii) the similarity to prior cases where a violation was found, which drove the necessity finding. The following facts are strictly necessary:
- F018 or F019: peaceful demonstrations existed and applicants intended to participate (scope).
- F020 or F021: applicants were arrested in connection with those demonstrations (interference — arrest).
- F022: applicants were convicted under administrative offences law (interference — conviction).
- F013 and F014 or F015 and F016: the circumstances were sufficiently similar to prior cases where a violation was found (necessity — the decisive element for the violation finding).

The irreducible core is: F018, F020, F022, F013, F014, F015, F016. F019 and F021 are parallel facts for the second demonstration date and are included for completeness but F018 and F020 alone would suffice for one set of applicants.
