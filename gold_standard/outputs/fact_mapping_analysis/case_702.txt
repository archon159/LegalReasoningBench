LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The only atomic fact that is strictly necessary to reach this conclusion is F002. It establishes: (1) that there were public assemblies, (2) that the applicants were organisers or participants (scope and participation), and (3) that disproportionate measures were taken against them (interference and the basis for the necessity finding). Without F002, the Court has no factual basis for any element of its Article 11 analysis. F001 is a purely formal fact (a reference to an appended table) and F003 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions, neither of which is necessary for the Article 11 conclusion. The irreducible factual core is therefore F002 alone.
