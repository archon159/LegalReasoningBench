LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts per element

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish: (i) an assembly existed — F048; (ii) the applicant participated — F049; (iii) the assembly was peaceful — the only conduct recorded in the arrest record was chanting political slogans (F052, F053, F054), and the domestic courts found only a procedural violation, not any violent conduct (F057). The most critical facts are F048, F049, F052, F053, F054, F057.

**Interference**
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's freedom of assembly. The arrest (F050) and the fine imposed following administrative proceedings (F057, F058) are the core facts. Most critical: F050, F057, F058.

**Prescribed by law** → Empty: element not discussed, no atomic facts.

**Legitimate aim** → Empty: element not adopted by the Court in its own reasoning, no atomic facts.

**Necessity**
The Court's necessity analysis turns on: (a) the arrest occurring thirty minutes into the rally solely because it was unauthorised (F050, F051); (b) absence of any violent or disruptive conduct by the applicant (F052, F053, F054, F057 — domestic findings limited to procedural violation only); (c) the authorities' failure to allow the assembly to complete and instead immediately arresting and fining the applicant (F050, F058). Most critical: F050, F051, F052, F053, F054, F057, F058.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The case turned on the following: the applicant participated in a rally (F049) that was held despite the authorities refusing to authorise it (F042, F048); he was arrested thirty minutes into the rally (F050) solely on the basis that the gathering was unauthorised (F051); the only conduct recorded was chanting political slogans (F052, F053, F054); the domestic court found only a procedural violation and imposed a fine (F057, F058); there was no finding of violence or disruption. These facts are the irreducible core: F042, F048, F049, F050, F051, F052, F053, F054, F057, F058.

F042 is included because the unauthorised nature of the rally — which was the sole basis for the arrest — flows directly from the refusal to authorise it, and this is what the Court identifies as the core problem (arrest for participation in an unauthorised gathering, without more). F048 confirms the rally took place as planned notwithstanding the refusal.
