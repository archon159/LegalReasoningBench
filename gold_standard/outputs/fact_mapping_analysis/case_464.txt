LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts per doctrinal element

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish: (i) an assembly existed and was authorised and peaceful; (ii) the applicant participated; (iii) the assembly and the applicant's conduct were peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F024: the authorised meeting took place at Triumfalnaya Square on 31 December 2010 — establishes assembly exists
- F015: the event was approved — establishes it was authorised
- F030/F033/F034: applicant addressed participants with speech — establishes participation (F030 is representative of the speech content; F033 and F034 are representative slogans)
- F036: applicant headed towards exit after speech — establishes he was still within the assembly context when arrested
- F117/F118: M.T.'s testimony about what applicant actually said at the cordon — establishes peaceful conduct
- F163: photographer T. testified applicant did not resist — establishes peaceful conduct at point of arrest

For List 2, the most critical and non-redundant facts are: F024, F015, F030, F036, F117, F118, F163

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needed to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's assembly right. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F038: applicant was arrested and put into a police vehicle — the core act of interference
- F074: applicant remained in detention until 2 January 2011 — establishes detention as interference
- F133: applicant found guilty of administrative offence — establishes administrative charges as interference
- F134: sentenced to fifteen days — establishes the punitive measure

For List 2: F038, F074, F133, F134

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court's finding of unlawfulness rested on: (a) the legal basis invoked (Article 19.3 CAO); (b) the Court's factual finding that the predicate facts for that provision were not established. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F196: text of Article 19.3 CAO — establishes what the legal basis required (disobeying a lawful order)
- F117/F118: M.T.'s testimony — the Court's key factual finding of what the applicant actually did (shouted Article 31, not anti-government slogans calling for a meeting)
- F163/F165: photographer T.'s testimony — corroborates that applicant did not disobey or agitate
- F133: applicant convicted under Article 19.3 — establishes the legal basis actually applied

For List 2: F196, F117, F118, F163, F165, F133

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court merged this with lawfulness and necessity and did not separately confirm any aim. No atomic facts are strictly necessary for this element in isolation. List 2 is empty for this element.

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity finding rested on: (a) what the applicant actually did (not a pressing social need to arrest him); (b) the chilling effect; (c) the applicant's public profile amplifying that effect. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F117/F118: what the applicant actually said — the core factual basis for finding no pressing social need
- F112: thirteen witnesses testified applicant made no calls to another meeting — corroborates absence of pressing need
- F163/F165: photographer T.'s testimony — corroborates peaceful conduct
- F074/F134: detention and sentence — the measures whose necessity is assessed
- F003/F007: applicant's public profile as opposition leader — grounds the chilling effect finding
- F072/F073: media coverage of arrest — grounds the chilling effect finding

For List 2: F117, F118, F112, F163, F165, F074, F134, F003, F007, F072, F073

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. Empty list.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) the applicant participated in an authorised, peaceful assembly; (2) he was arrested, detained and convicted; (3) the factual basis for the arrest (disobeying a lawful police order) was not established — what he actually did was shout that Article 31 guaranteed freedom of assembly; (4) the arrest and conviction therefore had no lawful basis and served no pressing social need; (5) the chilling effect was amplified by his public profile. The irreducible core facts are:

- F024: authorised meeting took place — establishes the assembly
- F015: meeting was approved — establishes it was lawful
- F030: applicant addressed participants — establishes participation
- F038: applicant was arrested — the core interference
- F133: applicant convicted under Article 19.3 — the legal measure applied
- F134: sentenced to fifteen days — the punitive consequence
- F196: text of Article 19.3 CAO — what the legal basis required
- F117: M.T. heard applicant asking why exit was blocked — key factual finding undermining the official account
- F118: M.T. heard applicant shouting Article 31 — key factual finding of what applicant actually did
- F163: photographer T. testified applicant did not resist — corroborates peaceful conduct and undermines official account
- F165: photographer T. testified applicant did not shout slogans — directly contradicts the legal basis for arrest
- F003/F007: applicant's public profile as opposition leader — grounds the chilling effect that made the violation particularly serious
