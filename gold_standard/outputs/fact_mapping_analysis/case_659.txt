LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on two separate violations. For the homes demonstration, the violation turned entirely on the absence of a legal basis: the police and courts used a dispersal provision (section 2(3)) to impose a prior ban, when only section 8(1) permitted prior prohibition and it did not include protection of private life as a ground. For the Kúria demonstration, the violation turned on the disproportionality of the ban: the authorities conducted no genuine balancing, relying solely on the vice-president's assertion, despite the organisers having amended their programme, reduced the duration to ten minutes, and the demonstration having a symbolic rather than targeted character.

Irreducible core facts:
- F006: establishes the public-interest subject matter of the demonstration (foreign currency loans), essential to the necessity analysis for the Kúria.
- F011: establishes the multi-venue character of the demonstration, essential to the symbolic/non-targeted character finding for the Kúria, and to the interference finding for both.
- F019: establishes the ten-minute duration at the Kúria after amendment, essential to the necessity finding.
- F023: establishes the amendment of the aim to 'raising public awareness', essential to both the peaceful/scope finding and the necessity finding.
- F024: establishes the ban on the Kúria demonstration — the interference for that part of the case.
- F025: establishes the ban on the homes demonstration (prime minister) — the interference for that part.
- F026: establishes the ban on the homes demonstration (CEO) — the interference for that part.
- F027: establishes that the police relied solely on the vice-president of the Kúria's statement — essential to the no-balancing finding under necessity.
- F036: establishes that the police relied on section 2(3) to ban the homes demonstration — the core of the 'not prescribed by law' finding.
- F056: establishes the Constitutional Court's clarification that section 2(3) is a dispersal ground, not a prior prohibition ground — essential to the 'not prescribed by law' conclusion.
- F082: establishes the text of section 8(1) as the exhaustive list of prior prohibition grounds — essential to both the 'prescribed by law' finding for the Kúria and the 'not prescribed by law' finding for the homes.
