The Court simply declares itself satisfied; no specific facts are cited. The contextual basis is:

- F023 (police view that airport would be rendered inaccessible, infringing passengers' right to leave)
- F032 (section 8(1) refers to disproportionate hindrance of traffic circulation)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) a ban was imposed on a planned peaceful assembly; (2) the organisers had given specific undertakings limiting the impact of the demonstration; (3) the authorities imposed an outright ban without exploring alternative arrangements; (4) the domestic courts endorsed this ban exclusively on traffic grounds without assessing alternatives; and (5) the location had symbolic importance for the demonstrators. These are the facts without which the Court could not have reached its conclusion of a violation:

- F006 (application to hold demonstration — establishes the assembly and the applicants' exercise of their right)
- F007 (hard shoulder only, not ordinarily used for traffic — the central factual basis for rejecting the traffic danger argument)
- F009 (two-hour duration — part of the undertakings limiting impact)
- F010 (fifty to 100 persons — part of the undertakings limiting impact)
- F020 (organisers would call off if not compliant — part of the undertakings)
- F021 (outright ban — the interference and the 'most radical measure')
- F016 (police suggested alternative location but did not explore workable alternatives — basis for the positive obligation violation)
- F031 (High Court endorsed police reasoning exclusively on traffic grounds — basis for finding domestic assessment inadequate)
- F008 (objective related to airport employees — basis for symbolic importance and the duty to seek alternatives)
