LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two findings: (1) the vicinity-of-court ban was overbroad and gave excessive discretion to local authorities; (2) the regional law ban on Stefanovskaya Square was a general, unlimited, unjustified prohibition with no case-specific assessment. The irreducible facts are:

- F010: the applicant notified a planned public event (establishing the assembly and triggering Article 11 protection)
- F025: the town administration refused to approve the venue (the interference)
- F151: section 5 of Komi Republic Law no. 91-RZ imposes a general prohibition on public events in Stefanovskaya Square (the unjustified general ban that the Court found not necessary)
- F158: decree no. 9/3483 described the perimeter of the zone in the immediate vicinity of the Constitutional Court (the legal basis for the vicinity-of-court ban)
- F159: the perimeter comprised the entire Stefanovskaya Square and adjacent streets including the crossroads of Lenin and Kommunisticheskaya streets (establishing the overbroad scope that the Court found incompatible with Article 11 § 2)
- F053: the assembly went peacefully and without any disturbance (confirming peaceful character, necessary for Article 11 scope)
