The factual grounding for necessity is the same as for interference — the arrests and convictions described in F002 and F003. No additional atomic facts are available.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on two things: (1) that there were public assemblies in which the applicants were organisers or participants, and (2) that the state took measures (arrests, convictions) against them in connection with those assemblies. Both of these are captured by F002 and F003. F001 merely identifies the applicants and F004 concerns other complaints unrelated to Article 11. The irreducible factual core is therefore F002 and F003.
