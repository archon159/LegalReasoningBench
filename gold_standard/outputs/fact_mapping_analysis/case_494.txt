**LIST 1: Factual grounding quotes mapped to atomic facts**

**Scope (implied)**
The Court's statement "the non-acquiescence by the police in the demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of the applicant's rights under that provision" presupposes a planned demonstration and the applicant's attempt to participate. The atomic facts underlying this are:
- F003: The applicant intended to organise demonstrations in order to call attention to the situation of those having sustained damage originating in a major bankruptcy case.
- F004: On 5 February 2007 the applicant applied to the Budapest Police Department for acquiescence in a demonstration on 10 March 2007 scheduled in Kossuth Square, in front of Parliament.
- F005: On 5 February 2007 the applicant applied to the Budapest Police Department for acquiescence in a demonstration of ongoing nature, from 10 March until 31 October 2007, scheduled in Kossuth Square, in front of Parliament.
No atomic fact explicitly addresses the peaceful character of the assembly; this is entirely ungrounded in the listed facts.

**Interference**
The Court's grounding quote: "the non-acquiescence by the police in the demonstration effectively interfered with the exercise of the applicant's rights under that provision, as the individualised application of the original police decision referred to by the Government."
And: "the police declared it a 'security operational zone' in 2006, and it remained so throughout the material period."
- F006: On 7 February 2007 the Budapest Police Department refused to deal with the applicant's application for acquiescence in a demonstration on 10 March 2007.
- F007: On 7 February 2007 the Budapest Police Department refused to deal with the applicant's application for acquiescence in an ongoing demonstration from 10 March until 31 October 2007.
- F008: On 23 October 2006 the Budapest Police Department declared the area of Kossuth Square, in front of Parliament, a "security operational zone" in view of the tumultuous events in Budapest in September 2006.
- F009: The "security operational zone" declaration was, after a prolongation, in place for an indefinite period of time.
- F010: The Budapest Police Department observed that the "security operational zone" measure remained outside the Budapest Police Department's competence as regards the prohibition of, or acquiescence in, a demonstration.

**Prescribed by law**
The Court's grounding quotes:
(i) "the police declared it a 'security operational zone' in 2006, and it remained so throughout the material period" → F008, F009.
(ii) "on 11 November 2010 the Budapest Regional Court quashed the underlying police decisions, reproaching those authorities for failing to assess the necessity and proportionality of the measure as maintained subsequent to 22 November 2006" → F026, F027, F028.
(iii) "on 4 April 2011 the Budapest Commander carried out the requisite scrutiny and found that the proportionality of the measure had not been proved" → F030, F031.
(iv) "these court rulings have effectively, if retroactively, removed the legal basis of the impugned measure" → F026, F030, F031.
(v) "the above two decisions were adopted in a procedure initiated by Mr K. rather than the applicant. For the Court, however, this is immaterial when it comes to the notion of lawfulness in the context of Article 11 § 2" → F015, F026, F030.

**LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning**

**Scope (implied)**
The minimum facts needed to establish that there was a planned assembly and that the applicant sought to participate:
- F004: The applicant applied for acquiescence in a demonstration on 10 March 2007 at Kossuth Square. (This is the most concrete and representative fact; F005 adds the ongoing nature but F004 is the core.)
- F005: The applicant also applied for an ongoing demonstration — necessary to show the full scope of what was refused.
F003 provides background motivation but is not strictly necessary for the legal analysis; the applications themselves (F004, F005) are what matter. Peaceful character: no atomic fact is strictly necessary here as it is uncontested and ungrounded.

**Interference**
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that the police refused to deal with the applicant's applications as a direct consequence of the security operational zone:
- F006 or F007 (either suffices to show refusal; both concern the same decision on the same date — F006 is representative for the single-day demonstration, F007 for the ongoing one; both are needed to capture the full interference).
- F008: The declaration of the security operational zone is the root cause of the interference and strictly necessary.
- F010: The police's stated reason for non-competence directly explains the mechanism of interference.

**Prescribed by law**
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that the legal basis was retroactively removed:
- F008: The original declaration of the security operational zone (establishes what measure is being assessed).
- F009: The indefinite continuation of the zone (establishes it was in place at the material time).
- F026: The Budapest Regional Court quashed the underlying police decisions on 11 November 2010 (the key judicial act removing the legal basis).
- F028: The Regional Court's finding that the decisions lacked concrete elements establishing necessity and proportionality after 22 November 2006 (the reason for quashing — strictly necessary to understand why the legal basis was removed).
- F030: The Budapest Police Commander partly sustained Mr K.'s complaint on 4 April 2011 (the administrative follow-up confirming the zone's disproportionality).
- F031: The Police Commander's finding that proportionality had not been proved (the substantive conclusion confirming absence of valid legal basis).
- F015: Mr K.'s original challenge (necessary to explain why these proceedings existed and why the applicant was not a party — the Court explicitly addresses this).

**Legitimate aim:** No atomic facts — not discussed.
**Necessity:** No atomic facts — not discussed.
**Positive obligation:** No atomic facts — not discussed.

**LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)**

The case turned entirely on the 'prescribed by law' element. The Court found that the security operational zone lacked a valid legal basis because domestic courts retroactively removed it. The irreducible facts are:

- F004: The applicant applied for acquiescence in a demonstration at Kossuth Square (establishes the applicant's attempt to exercise Article 11 rights — without this, there is no case).
- F008: The Budapest Police Department declared Kossuth Square a "security operational zone" in October 2006 (the measure that caused the interference and whose legal basis is in question).
- F006: The Budapest Police Department refused to deal with the applicant's application (the concrete act of interference with the applicant's rights).
- F026: The Budapest Regional Court quashed the underlying police decisions on 11 November 2010, finding they lacked assessment of necessity and proportionality (the key fact removing the legal basis retroactively).
- F031: The Budapest Police Commander found that the proportionality of the measure had not been proved (confirms the absence of valid legal basis, completing the chain that leads to the 'not prescribed by law' conclusion).
