LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity assessment. The Court found the interference was not necessary in a democratic society because: (1) the applicant was stopped and arrested at a distance from Freedom Square with no clear basis for suspicion beyond his participation in the assembly; (2) the charges were general and abstract and the most serious ones were dropped for lack of evidence; (3) the eventual conviction was a recapitulation of the charges, lacking detail, and based solely on the statements of the arresting officers. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary for this conclusion:

F014 - establishes the applicant as a known active demonstrator (relevant to the Court's inference that officers knew him as such)
F021 - establishes the applicant's presence at Freedom Square during the police operation
F037 - establishes that the applicant was stopped at a considerable distance from Freedom Square (Northern Avenue/Abovyan Street intersection), which is the factual anchor for the Court's finding that it was unclear on what grounds the officers assumed he had committed anything illegal
F045 - establishes that H.K.'s task was to pursue persons who had fled Freedom Square, confirming the link between the arrest and assembly participation rather than any specific criminal act
F050 and F051 - establish that the initial charges were framed in general and abstract terms under the same articles as in Mushegh Saghatelyan
F056 and F057 - establish that those charges were dropped for lack of evidence
F058 - establishes that a new, lesser charge was brought on the same facts
F077 - establishes that the conviction judgment's findings were stated in general terms without specific factual detail
F078 - establishes that the conviction rested solely on the statements of the two arresting police officers
F089 - establishes (by reference to Mushegh Saghatelyan) that the assembly was peaceful and within the scope of Article 11, which is a prerequisite for the entire analysis
