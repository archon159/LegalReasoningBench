LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. For this conclusion to be reached, the Court must at minimum establish: (i) that there were public assemblies (scope), (ii) that the applicants were organisers or participants (scope/participation), and (iii) that state measures were taken against them (interference). All three of these points are grounded exclusively in F002 and F003. F002 covers organisers; F003 covers participants. Together they are the irreducible factual core — without them, there is no assembly, no participation, and no interference to assess. F001 is merely administrative (identifying the applicants) and F004 concerns other complaints unrelated to Article 11. The necessity finding adds no new facts. Therefore the irreducible core is F002 and F003.
