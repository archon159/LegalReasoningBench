LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on three interlocking findings: (1) the BCEA refused authorisation on legally baseless or unevidenced grounds; (2) the domestic courts failed to scrutinise those refusals; and (3) the only alternative offered was wholly unsuitable, particularly given the political and Parliament-directed nature of the assemblies. The irreducible facts are:

F006 — applicants were members of a political group (Public Chamber): establishes political speech context and narrow margin of appreciation.
F016 — outright refusal of app. 69483/13: establishes the interference.
F018 — BCEA's 'to no purpose' reason: establishes the legally baseless ground for refusal in app. 69483/13.
F024 — proposed location (Tabriz cinema square) was on BCEA's own indicative list: establishes the arbitrariness of the refusal in app. 76319/13.
F034 — BCEA refused all three proposed locations in app. 76319/13: establishes the interference.
F035 — BCEA proposed driving school on outskirts as alternative: establishes the inadequacy of the alternative.
F043, F044, F045 — planned route of app. 30456/14 along Parliament Avenue to Parliament building: establishes the crucial importance of location.
F046 — purpose of app. 30456/14: demand early elections to Parliament: establishes why location was essential.
F047 — BCEA refused indicated location in app. 30456/14: establishes the interference.
F048 — BCEA proposed same driving school as alternative in app. 30456/14: establishes inadequacy of alternative given Parliament-directed purpose.
F067, F068, F069 — domestic courts dismissed all complaints accepting BCEA's findings: establishes failure of judicial scrutiny.
F077 — no court addressed the indicative list argument: establishes failure of judicial scrutiny on a key point.
F078 — no court addressed the no-evidence argument: establishes failure of judicial scrutiny on a key point.
F080 — no court addressed the Parliament-location argument: establishes failure of judicial scrutiny on a key point.
