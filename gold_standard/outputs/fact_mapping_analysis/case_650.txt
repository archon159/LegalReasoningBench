LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope:** F002 — the existence of public assemblies and the applicants' role as organisers or participants is the minimum factual predicate for the scope analysis. Without this, there is no assembly and no participation to speak of.

**Interference:** F002 — the reference to "disproportionate measures" taken against the applicants as organisers or participants of public assemblies is the factual basis for the interference finding. The specific measures (dispersal, arrest, conviction) are described in the reasoning section but trace back to this atomic fact as their source.

**Prescribed by law:** No atomic facts are strictly necessary here, as this element is not discussed.

**Legitimate aim:** No atomic facts are strictly necessary here, as this element is not discussed.

**Necessity:** F002 — the same fact underlies the necessity assessment, as the Court's conclusion that the interferences were not necessary in a democratic society is grounded in the nature of the measures taken against the applicants at public assemblies.

**Positive obligation:** No atomic facts are strictly necessary here, as this element is not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on the fact that governmental measures (dispersal, arrest, conviction) were taken against people participating in or organising public assemblies, and that these measures were disproportionate. The single atomic fact that is strictly necessary and sufficient to ground this conclusion — given the doctrinal structure of Article 11 — is:

- F002: This establishes (a) that there were public assemblies, (b) that the applicants were organisers or participants, and (c) that disproportionate measures were taken against them. These are the three indispensable factual elements: scope (assembly + participation), interference, and the proportionality deficit that drives the necessity finding.

F001 is purely administrative background. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore the irreducible core is F002 alone.
