LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality assessment. The Court found a violation because: the assembly was lawfully authorised; the group was peaceful and presented no danger; the police used force without issuing a prior warning as required by law; and the applicants were arrested. The irreducible facts are:

→ F012: The assembly was lawfully authorised by the Governor — this is foundational to the disproportionality finding.
→ F046: The group was walking silently, presented no danger to public order, and engaged in no violent activities — this is the core factual basis for finding the intervention unjustified.
→ F047: The police were legally obliged to warn the group out loud before dispersing — establishes the legal standard against which conduct is measured.
→ F048: The police failed to give the required warning — establishes the specific procedural failure that made the intervention disproportionate.
→ F017: The police used force to disperse the group — establishes the nature and fact of the interference.
→ F019: The applicants were arrested — establishes the severity of the interference.
