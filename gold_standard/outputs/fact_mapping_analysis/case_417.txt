LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Art. 11 was violated rests on: (i) the applicants being organisers or participants of public assemblies (scope/interference), and (ii) the authorities having taken disproportionate measures against them or subjected them to unlawful administrative arrest (necessity/lawfulness). Both of these findings are grounded exclusively in F001 (the appended table containing all applicant-specific details) and F003 (confirming that these are the complaints being adjudicated). F001 is the single most critical fact because without the applicant details in the appended table, the Court could not have identified who was affected, what happened to them, or why the Frumkin and Tsvetkova precedents applied. F003 confirms the existence and nature of the complaints. F002 is not relevant to the Art. 11 conclusion (it concerns impartiality of the tribunal, a different complaint).
