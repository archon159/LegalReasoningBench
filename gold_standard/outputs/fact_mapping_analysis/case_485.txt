LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on the finding that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The predicate facts for this conclusion are that the applicants were organisers or participants of public assemblies and that disproportionate measures were taken against them. This maps to F002 and F003. F001 is merely a procedural reference to the appended table and adds nothing substantive to the violation finding. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore the irreducible factual core is F002 and F003.
