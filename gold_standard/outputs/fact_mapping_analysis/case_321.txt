LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on: (1) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated as organisers or participants, and (2) the measures taken against them (arrest, dispersal, conviction for administrative offences). Both of these are captured in F002. F001 is not strictly necessary for the conclusion — it identifies the parties but does not bear on the legal outcome. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, the irreducible factual core is F002 alone.
