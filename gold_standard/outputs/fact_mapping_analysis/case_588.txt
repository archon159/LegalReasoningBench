LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court must establish at minimum: (i) that there were assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope), and (ii) that governmental measures interfered with that participation (interference). Both of these rest exclusively on F002. F001 merely identifies the applicants and refers to an appended table; it adds no substantive facts about assemblies or interference. F003 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.

Therefore, the irreducible factual core is: F002.
