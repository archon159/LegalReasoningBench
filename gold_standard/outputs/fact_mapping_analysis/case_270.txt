LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on three core issues: (1) the demonstration was peaceful and prior notice had been given, yet it was dispersed without adequate justification; (2) the applicant was arrested and convicted not for any specific reprehensible conduct but for participating in the demonstration; and (3) the penalty imposed (five days detention) was not available under the provision that actually covered assembly violations, and was applied through an arbitrary invocation of a different provision. The irreducible facts are:

F004: Prior notice was given to the BCEA — establishing that the organisers complied with the constitutional requirement, undermining the justification for dispersal.
F007: BCEA refused to authorise the demonstration — the trigger for the dispersal, and the fact that exposes the gap between the constitutional notification requirement and the authorisation system applied in practice.
F010: The demonstration was intended to be peaceful — relevant to the peaceful character of the assembly.
F011: The demonstration was conducted in a peaceful manner — directly establishes the peaceful character, critical to the necessity analysis.
F017: The police dispersed the demonstration shortly after it began — the core act of interference regarding the dispersal.
F018: The applicant was arrested during the dispersal — the core act of interference regarding the applicant personally.
F056: The Sabail District Court found that the applicant had failed to stop participating in the unauthorised demonstration — establishes that the real basis for conviction was participation, not disobedience of a specific order.
F057: The applicant was convicted under Article 310.1 CAO — establishes the legal characterisation used.
F058: The applicant was sentenced to five days administrative detention — establishes the severity of the sanction.
F080: Article 298 CAO provided only a reprimand or small fine for breach of assembly rules — establishes that the penalty actually applicable to assembly violations was minor.
F081: Article 310.1 CAO provided for fine or up to 15 days detention for disobeying police — establishes that the more severe provision was used to circumvent the lighter penalty available under the assembly-specific provision.
F066: Article 49 of the Constitution required only prior notification — establishes the constitutional standard against which the authorisation system is measured, critical to both the lawfulness and necessity analyses.
