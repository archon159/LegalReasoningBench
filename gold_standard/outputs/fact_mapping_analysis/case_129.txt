LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two distinct but related issues: (1) the unjustified use of force to terminate participation in the assembly (for Mr Annenkov, Ms Suprunova, Ms Guseva, Ms Zakharova), and (2) the deficiency of the domestic courts' reasoning in convicting and sentencing applicants to administrative detention without adequately establishing the factual and legal elements of the offence or assessing the proportionality of the interference with freedom of assembly (for Mr Khripunov, Mr Finskiy, Mr Khavantsev, Mr Buzov). The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

F008 — establishes the collective assembly (night shifts) as the context for all events
F014, F018, F019, F020 — arrests of Mr Annenkov, Ms Suprunova, Ms Zakharova, Ms Guseva on 7 August (forcible termination of participation, group 1)
F089 — arrest of Mr Buzov on 10 August (forcible termination of participation)
F122 — Officer Ku. struck Mr Annenkov (use of force establishing disproportionate interference for group 1)
F124 — Mr Annenkov's head injury (physical consequence of force)
F130 — Ms Suprunova's concussion and bruises (physical consequence of force)
F131 — Ms Zakharova's bruises (physical consequence of force)
F132 — Ms Guseva's bruises (physical consequence of force)
F174 — Regional Investigations Department found the refusal to prosecute contained insufficient assessment of factual circumstances and no plausible explanation for injuries (establishes inadequacy of domestic scrutiny of use of force)
F026 — Mr Finskiy sentenced to five days' detention (severity of penalty, group 2)
F031 — Mr Khripunov sentenced to five days' detention (severity of penalty, group 2)
F038 — Mr Khavantsev sentenced to ten days' detention (severity of penalty, group 2)
F095 — Mr Buzov sentenced to ten days' detention (severity of penalty)
F067 — District Court found record stated Mr Khripunov grabbed uniform and tried to escape (the only factual basis for conviction, found insufficient by ECtHR)
F071 — District Court dismissed witness P.'s testimony as refuted by other evidence (deficient evidential reasoning for Mr Khripunov)
F076 — Regional Court dismissed argument about failure to specify type of public event (deficient legal reasoning, applies to Mr Finskiy, Mr Khavantsev, Mr Khripunov)
F096 — justice of the peace found Mr Buzov disobeyed orders without specifying what order (deficient factual reasoning for Mr Buzov)
F111 — Regional Court held Mr Buzov was among most active participants without specifying his particular conduct (deficient factual reasoning for Mr Buzov)
F196 — gathering not subject to prior notification requirement (relevant legal aspect not examined by domestic courts, central to proportionality assessment for Mr Buzov)
