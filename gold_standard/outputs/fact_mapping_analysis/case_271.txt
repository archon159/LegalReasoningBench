**List 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion**

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. For this conclusion to be reached under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the following must be established as a minimum: (i) there were public assemblies, (ii) the applicants participated in or organised them, and (iii) the state interfered with their freedom of assembly. All three points are grounded in F002 and F003. These are the only atomic facts in the provided list that are strictly necessary for the conclusion. F001 and F004 are either purely procedural (F001) or concern other complaints (F004) and are not necessary for the Article 11 violation finding. The necessity finding itself, while central to the conclusion, is not grounded in any specific atomic fact from the list — it rests on precedent. Therefore the irreducible factual core is F002 and F003.
