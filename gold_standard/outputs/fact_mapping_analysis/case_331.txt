LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the prescribed by law analysis — specifically whether Article 220 § 6, as interpreted and applied, was foreseeable and provided sufficient safeguards against arbitrary interference. The violation was found because the provision was applied extensively to equate peaceful assembly participation with terrorist organisation membership, without sufficient legal precision or safeguards. The irreducible facts are:

- F004: the funeral was a real assembly (establishes scope)
- F021: the demonstration was a real assembly (establishes scope)
- F081: applicant attended the funeral (establishes participation)
- F087: applicant applauded during the demonstration (establishes participation)
- F086: not established that applicant chanted slogans (establishes peaceful nature / absence of concrete evidence of membership)
- F044: no charge of violence (establishes peaceful nature)
- F066: conviction for membership of illegal organisation (establishes interference and the outcome of the extensive application)
- F067: sentence of six years and three months (establishes severity of interference and chilling effect)
- F121: served four years and eight months (establishes concrete impact)
- F128: text of Article 220 § 6 — the provision that was found not foreseeable
- F151: Plenary Court of Cassation's extensive interpretation equating demonstration participation with acting on behalf of organisation
- F153: Plenary Court held calls need not target identified individuals — key to the breadth of the provision's application
- F088: funeral held per PKK instructions — the sole basis for treating attendance as acting on behalf of PKK
- F089: demonstration held per PKK instructions — the sole basis for treating attendance as acting on behalf of PKK
- F157: Venice Commission criterion of continuity, diversity and intensity — used to show that Article 314 alone requires more, making the combined application of Article 220 § 6 with Article 314 § 2 disproportionately extensive
