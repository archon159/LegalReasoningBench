LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on: (1) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants were organisers or participants, and (2) the fact that disproportionate measures (dispersal, arrest, conviction) were taken against them. These two points are what made the case analogous to Frumkin, Navalnyy and Yashin, and Kasparov, and what grounded the necessity finding. Both F002 and F003 are strictly necessary: F002 establishes the organiser dimension and F003 the participant dimension, and the Court addresses both roles. Without either, the factual basis for the Article 11 complaint — and thus the violation — would be incomplete. F001 is purely procedural and not part of the substantive factual core. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
