LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts per element

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that there was a peaceful assembly and that the applicant participated in it. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F013: nationwide daily protest rallies were held (establishes existence of assembly)
- F019: applicant was member of political council of opposition party (establishes his identity as opposition figure)
- F021: applicant was active participant in protest rallies (establishes participation)
- F022: applicant spent night of 23-24 February at Freedom Square (establishes participation immediately before arrest)
- F023: on 24 February applicant and companions were driving home from the rally (establishes participation and link to arrest)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The strictly necessary facts to support the Court's finding of interference (i.e., that the prosecution was linked to protest participation rather than genuine criminal conduct) are:
- F019: applicant was opposition political figure (establishes political identity)
- F021: applicant was active participant in rallies (establishes link between identity and protests)
- F022: applicant spent night before arrest at Freedom Square (establishes immediate connection to protests)
- F042: anonymous telephone call was the trigger for arrest, with no objective evidence it was received (establishes precarious basis for arrest)
- F052: drug test ordered solely on basis of baton possession, with no logical connection (establishes arbitrary character of proceedings)
- F068: decision to institute criminal case stated drug use suspicion with no evidentiary basis (establishes fabricated grounds)
- F086: toxicological examination revealed no traces of drugs (confirms no basis for drug suspicion)
- F099: applicant's case joined to main political case no. 62202608 (establishes political linkage)
- F100: joinder decision stated applicant had participated in organising unlawful demonstrations (establishes explicit political motivation in official documents)
- F101: contact with outside world restricted with reference to political case (establishes use of political case to restrict applicant)
- F104: detention extension application referred to materials of political case (establishes political case used to justify continued detention)
- F105: detention extension stated applicant conspired to usurp State power (establishes political charge used against applicant)
- F119: investigator acknowledged no sufficient evidence implicating applicant in political case (establishes that political case was used without evidentiary basis)
- F120: applicant's case disjoined from political case when no evidence found (establishes abandonment of political charge)
- F121: General Prosecutor's Office noted applicant charged only under Art. 316 § 1 (establishes that new charge was prompted externally)
- F122: General Prosecutor's Office instructed investigator to bring new charge under Art. 235 § 4 (establishes that weapons charge was added at this late stage)
- F125: new charge brought on 26 June 2008, four months after baton found (establishes suspicious delay)
- F154: applicant convicted on 19 November 2008 (establishes final punitive measure)
- F162: conviction based on police officer statements and applicant's own statement about baton (establishes exclusive reliance on police testimony)
- F184: PACE report noted arrests on seemingly artificial charges with impression of political motivation (establishes general context of politically motivated prosecutions)
- F186: OSCE/ODIHR monitored about a hundred trials of opposition leaders and supporters (establishes monitoring context)
- F187: applicant's case included in OSCE/ODIHR monitoring project (establishes that applicant's case was treated as part of political prosecutions)

- F125: new charge under Art. 235 § 4 brought four months after baton found (establishes formal legal basis for weapons charge)
- F100: joinder decision stated applicant participated in organising demonstrations (establishes true reason in official documents)
- F119: no sufficient evidence found implicating applicant in political case (establishes that political case was pretextual)
- F154: conviction (establishes that formal criminal process was completed)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on the finding that the prosecution and conviction were not genuinely motivated by the criminal acts charged but were linked to the applicant's participation in the protest movement, making the interference manifestly arbitrary and therefore not prescribed by law. The irreducible facts are:

- F013: nationwide daily protest rallies were held (assembly existed)
- F021: applicant was active participant in protest rallies (participation established)
- F022: applicant spent night before arrest at Freedom Square (immediate connection to protests)
- F042: anonymous telephone call was the sole trigger for arrest with no objective evidence it was received (precarious and unverifiable basis for arrest)
- F052: drug test ordered solely on basis of baton possession with no logical connection (arbitrary character of proceedings)
- F086: toxicological examination revealed no traces of drugs (no basis for drug suspicion)
- F099: applicant's case joined to main political case no. 62202608 (political linkage in official proceedings)
- F100: joinder decision stated applicant participated in organising demonstrations (explicit political motivation in official documents)
- F105: detention extension stated applicant conspired to usurp State power (political charge used to justify detention)
- F119: investigator acknowledged no sufficient evidence implicating applicant in political case (political case used without evidentiary basis)
- F122: General Prosecutor's Office instructed investigator to bring new weapons charge (charge added externally and belatedly)
- F125: new charge brought four months after baton found (suspicious delay undermining credibility of charge)
- F154: applicant convicted (punitive measure completed)
- F162: conviction based exclusively on police officer statements (sole evidentiary basis mirrors pattern of politically motivated prosecutions)
- F184: PACE report noted arrests on seemingly artificial charges with impression of political motivation (general context of politically motivated prosecutions)
- F187: applicant's case included in OSCE/ODIHR monitoring project of opposition-related trials (applicant's case treated as part of political prosecutions)
