LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on the finding that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The factual core for this conclusion is:
- That the applicants were organisers or participants of public assemblies (F002, F003) — this establishes that Article 11 is engaged at all and that the measures taken against them constituted an interference with a protected right.

F002 and F003 together are the irreducible factual minimum: without them, there is no assembly, no participation, no interference, and no violation to find. The Court's necessity conclusion is reached by reference to prior case law applied to these facts, so these two facts are what the entire conclusion rests on in terms of the factual record provided.

Note: The atomic facts provided are extremely sparse. F001 and F004 are not necessary for the violation conclusion — F001 is a purely administrative reference to the appended table, and F004 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions. They play no role in the Article 11 analysis.
