LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

Scope: F002 is strictly necessary. It establishes that the applicants were participants or organisers of public assemblies, which is the only factual basis for concluding that Article 11's scope is engaged (assembly exists, participation implied). Without F002, there is no factual basis for the scope finding.

Interference: F002 is strictly necessary. It describes the measures (disproportionate measures, arrest, dispersal, conviction) that constitute the interference. Without F002, there is no factual basis for the interference finding.

Prescribed by law: Not discussed; no atomic facts necessary.

Legitimate aim: Not discussed; no atomic facts necessary.

Necessity: The necessity finding rests on case-law comparison, not on atomic facts from the present case. However, F002 is the minimum factual anchor — it describes the nature of the measures, which is what the Court compared to the leading cases. Without F002, the Court could not have identified the "issues similar to those in the present case." F002 is therefore the closest necessary fact, though the reasoning is primarily jurisprudential.

Positive obligation: Not discussed; no atomic facts necessary.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The entire chain of reasoning — scope, interference, and necessity — rests on a single atomic fact: F002. This fact establishes (i) that there were public assemblies, (ii) that the applicants were participants or organisers, and (iii) that governmental measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) were taken against them. These are the only case-specific facts the Court relied upon. F001 is a formal reference to the appended table and adds no substantive content. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. F002 is therefore the sole irreducible factual foundation for the Court's conclusion.
