LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court must at minimum establish: (1) that there were assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope), and (2) that governmental measures were taken against them in that context (interference/necessity). Both of these rest on F002 and F003. F001 is a procedural reference and F004 concerns unrelated complaints. The irreducible core is therefore F002 and F003. Between these two, neither alone is sufficient — F002 establishes the organiser dimension and F003 the participant dimension, and the judgment covers both categories of applicants. Both are strictly necessary to cover the full scope of the conclusion as stated by the Court.
