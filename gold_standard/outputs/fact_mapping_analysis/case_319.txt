LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on three pivotal factual clusters:

1. The demonstration was small and peaceful, giving no justification for immediate dispersal: F009, F010, F011
2. The applicants were convicted not for any specific reprehensible conduct but solely for participating in an unauthorised peaceful demonstration, as confirmed by the domestic courts themselves: F049, F071, F096
3. The legal basis invoked (Article 310.1 CAO) was used to impose deprivation of liberty (detention) for conduct that, under the applicable assembly-specific provision (Article 298 CAO), could only attract a reprimand or fine — making the sanction disproportionate and unwarranted: F051, F073, F098, F121, F122
4. The applicants showed willingness to disperse, undermining the basis for treating them as having disobeyed a police order: F015

Core facts: F009, F010, F011, F015, F049, F051, F071, F073, F096, F098, F121, F122
