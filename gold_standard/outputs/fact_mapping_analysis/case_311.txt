LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**SCOPE**
The Court implies that a qualifying peaceful assembly existed and that the applicants participated or intended to participate. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F029: Prior notice of the demonstration of 2 April 2011 had been given to the BCEA (establishes the assembly existed and was formally notified)
- F007: At the material time the applicants were members of the main opposition parties or groups (establishes their connection to the assembly)
- F017: All three applicants participated in a number of opposition demonstrations (establishes participation pattern)
- F027: According to the applicants, they had intended to attend the demonstration scheduled for 2 April 2011 (establishes intended participation — adopted by the Court in its interference finding)
- F011: Opposition demonstrations were held in Azerbaijan on 2 April 2011 (establishes the assembly took place)

**INTERFERENCE**
The strictly necessary facts are those the Court explicitly relied upon to draw "strong, clear and concordant inferences":
- F008: 2011 was marked by an increased number of opposition demonstrations (political context)
- F007: Applicants were members of main opposition parties (profile)
- F017: All three applicants actively participated in various protests (profile)
- F029: Prior notice of the 2 April demonstration had been given to the BCEA (authorities' prior knowledge)
- F256: Ministry of Internal Affairs warned the public on 31 March 2011 that the protest would be prevented (authorities' prior knowledge and intent)
- F038: First applicant arrested for disobeying a lawful order of a police officer according to official records (timing and dubious grounds)
- F039: Police officers stated they stopped a person resembling someone on a wanted list (dubious grounds)
- F040: Police officers stated the person ignored their demand to produce identity document (dubious grounds)
- F043: Administrative-offence report charged first applicant under Article 310.1 (legal basis)
- F059: Nasimi District Court found first applicant guilty (domestic proceedings)
- F060: First applicant sentenced to seven days' administrative detention (sentence)
- F073: Second applicant arrested for minor hooliganism according to official records (dubious grounds)
- F075: Police officer T.R. stated he noticed a person making noise (dubious grounds — second applicant)
- F079: Administrative-offence report charged second applicant under Article 296 (legal basis)
- F097: Sumgait City Court found second applicant guilty under Article 296 (domestic proceedings)
- F099: Second applicant sentenced to seven days' administrative detention (sentence)
- F113: Third applicant arrested for minor hooliganism according to official records (dubious grounds)
- F114: Police officer N.M. stated he noticed a person swearing loudly without addressing anyone (dubious grounds — third applicant)
- F118: Administrative-offence report charged third applicant under Article 296 (legal basis)
- F132: Sumgait City Court found third applicant guilty under Article 296 (domestic proceedings)
- F133: Third applicant sentenced to seven days' administrative detention (sentence)
- F050: First applicant's hearing was very brief (quality of proceedings)
- F055: Only witnesses at first applicant's hearing were police officers (quality of proceedings)
- F057: Police officers' testimonies were virtually identical to their report (quality of proceedings)
- F087: Second applicant's hearing was very brief (quality of proceedings)
- F095: Only witness at second applicant's hearing was police officer T.R. (quality of proceedings)
- F130: Only witnesses at third applicant's hearing were police officers (quality of proceedings)
- F174: Commissioner for Human Rights drew attention to wave of arrests of activists in connection with March and April 2011 protests (systemic context)
- F194: European Parliament deplored practice of intimidating, arresting, prosecuting and convicting political activists on various criminal charges (systemic context)
- F195: European Parliament deplored arrest of around 200 people prior to and during 2 April 2011 protests (systemic context)

- F213: Human Rights Watch reported Azerbaijani authorities detained at least 10 opposition activists to prevent the 2 April rally (systemic context)
- F216: Activists quickly convicted in summary trials on charges of disobeying police orders (systemic context — parallel pattern)
- F033: First applicant was informed he was being detained in connection with the upcoming demonstration of 2 April 2011 (direct evidence of true purpose)
- F036: First applicant was asked about his intention to participate in the demonstration of 2 April 2011 during questioning (direct evidence of true purpose)

**PRESCRIBED BY LAW**
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing the mismatch between the legal basis used and the actual purpose:
- F043: Administrative-offence report charged first applicant under Article 310.1 ("failure to comply with a lawful order of a police officer")
- F079: Administrative-offence report charged second applicant under Article 296 ("minor hooliganism")
- F118: Administrative-offence report charged third applicant under Article 296 ("minor hooliganism")
- F033: First applicant was told he was being detained in connection with the upcoming demonstration (establishes the true purpose, making the legal basis a pretext)
- F036: First applicant was questioned about his intention to participate in the demonstration (same)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two interlocking findings: (1) that the arrests and convictions were in truth aimed at preventing the applicants from attending the 2 April 2011 demonstration and punishing them for opposition activity (interference), and (2) that the legal provisions invoked had no connection with that actual purpose (not prescribed by law). The irreducible facts are:

- F007: Applicants were members of main opposition parties (establishes their profile as targets)
- F017: All three applicants actively participated in opposition demonstrations (establishes pattern of participation)
- F029: Prior notice of the 2 April demonstration had been given to the BCEA (establishes the assembly existed and authorities knew of it)
- F256: Ministry of Internal Affairs warned on 31 March 2011 that the protest would be prevented (establishes authorities' intent to suppress the demonstration)
- F033: First applicant was told he was being detained in connection with the upcoming demonstration (direct evidence of true purpose of arrest)
- F036: First applicant was questioned about his intention to participate in the demonstration (direct evidence of true purpose)
- F038: First applicant arrested on official grounds of disobeying a lawful order (establishes the pretextual legal basis)
- F039: Police claimed to have stopped first applicant because he resembled a wanted person, without ever identifying that person (establishes dubious grounds)
- F043: First applicant charged under Article 310.1 of the CAO (establishes the legal provision used)
- F059: First applicant convicted by Nasimi District Court (establishes the punitive measure)
- F060: First applicant sentenced to seven days' administrative detention (establishes the detention preventing attendance)
- F073: Second applicant arrested on official grounds of minor hooliganism (establishes pretextual legal basis)
- F075: Police officer stated second applicant was making noise/swearing without addressing anyone in particular (establishes dubious and vague grounds)
- F079: Second applicant charged under Article 296 of the CAO (establishes the legal provision used)
- F097: Second applicant convicted by Sumgait City Court (establishes the punitive measure)
- F099: Second applicant sentenced to seven days' administrative detention (establishes the detention preventing attendance)
- F113: Third applicant arrested on official grounds of minor hooliganism (establishes pretextual legal basis)
- F114: Police officer stated third applicant was swearing loudly without addressing anyone in particular (establishes dubious and vague grounds)
- F118: Third applicant charged under Article 296 of the CAO (establishes the legal provision used)
- F132: Third applicant convicted by Sumgait City Court (establishes the punitive measure)
- F133: Third applicant sentenced to seven days' administrative detention (establishes the detention preventing attendance)
- F195: European Parliament deplored arrest of around 200 people prior to and during 2 April 2011 protests (establishes systemic pattern directly linked to the same demonstration)
- F201: Amnesty International reported at least 11 prominent political activists detained on 31 March 2011 ahead of 2 April protest (establishes systemic pattern on the same day as applicants' arrests)
- F209: Multiple activists given administrative detention for offences that had yet to take place (establishes the pattern of pre-emptive detention directly parallel to applicants' cases)
