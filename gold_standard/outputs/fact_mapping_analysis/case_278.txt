LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Scope (assembly exists, participation, peaceful):**
The factual grounding quote was: 'The applicant association complained of a violation of its right to peaceful assembly. It claimed that the ban imposed on it on holding a demonstration had not been in accordance with the law.'
Atomic facts pointed to:
- F001 (Genderdoc-M is an NGO based in Moldova — establishes the applicant)
- F003 (applicant applied for authorisation to hold a peaceful demonstration)
- F004 (demonstration was to be held in front of Parliament on 27 May 2005)
- F005 (purpose was to encourage adoption of laws protecting sexual minorities from discrimination)
- F006 (Chișinău Municipal Council rejected the application)
- F009 (Chișinău Mayor's Office refused permission)

**Interference:**
The factual grounding quote was: 'In their observations lodged on 15 September 2008 the Government agreed that there was an interference with the applicant's right to freedom of assembly' and 'the ban imposed on it on holding a demonstration.'
Atomic facts pointed to:
- F006 (Municipal Council rejected the application)
- F009 (Mayor's Office refused permission)
- F036 (Supreme Court upheld the refusal on 18 October 2006 — final domestic decision confirming the ban)

**Prescribed by law:**
The factual grounding quote was only the applicant's claim that the ban 'had not been in accordance with the law.' The Court did not independently establish facts on this element. However, the underlying facts that would be relevant to this element (and that the Court implicitly relied on by accepting the Government's concession of violation) include the grounds given by domestic authorities for the ban:
- F007 (ban said to be because demonstration was 'baseless')
- F008 (justification: law on protection of national minorities already adopted)
- F010 (Mayor's Office: law on national minorities already in place)
- F011 (Mayor's Office: legal provisions on legislative initiative)
- F037–F044 (Supreme Court: applicant failed to give various undertakings)
However, since the Court made no independent factual findings on this element and simply accepted the Government's concession, no atomic facts can be strictly said to be 'pointed to' by the Court's own reasoning on this element. The list is therefore empty for List 1 on this element.

**Legitimate aim:**
The factual grounding quote was only the applicant's claim that the ban 'had not pursued any legitimate aim,' and the Government's initial submission referencing the Orthodox Christian majority and popular petitions — but the Court did not adopt these. No atomic facts are pointed to by the Court's own reasoning.

**Necessity:**
No factual grounding identified. No atomic facts pointed to.

**Positive obligation:**
Not discussed. No atomic facts pointed to.

F001 is background; F006 and F009 are more relevant to interference. The most critical facts for scope are F003 and F004.

F036 is the most critical single fact, as it represents the final and definitive ban after all domestic remedies were exhausted.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) the applicant, an NGO representing the LGBT community, sought to hold a peaceful demonstration to advocate for anti-discrimination laws; (2) the domestic authorities banned that demonstration; (3) the ban was upheld by the highest domestic court; and (4) the Government ultimately conceded a violation. The atomic facts strictly necessary for the conclusion are:

- F003 (applicant applied for authorisation to hold a peaceful demonstration — establishes the exercise of the right)
- F005 (purpose was to encourage adoption of laws protecting sexual minorities — establishes the subject matter and context of the assembly, relevant to why the ban was imposed)
- F009 (Mayor's Office refused permission — the primary act of interference)
- F036 (Supreme Court upheld the refusal — the final, definitive interference exhausting domestic remedies)

These four facts establish: that there was a peaceful assembly the applicant sought to hold (F003), what it was about (F005), that it was banned (F009), and that the ban was definitively confirmed domestically (F036). Together they are the irreducible core on which the violation finding rests.
