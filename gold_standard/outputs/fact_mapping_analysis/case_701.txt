**List 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)**

The final conclusion that Article 11 was violated turns on: (1) the existence of assemblies in which the applicants were organisers or participants, and (2) the fact that governmental measures (dispersal, arrest, conviction) were taken against them in that context, and (3) that those measures were not necessary in a democratic society. The atomic facts that are absolutely essential are:

- F002 and/or F003: These establish that the applicants were organisers or participants of public assemblies against whom disproportionate measures were taken — this is the irreducible core of the entire complaint and the violation finding.
- F004: This establishes the nature of the assemblies (manifestations for fair elections to Mosgorduma), which is critical to the necessity assessment — political assemblies on electoral matters attract strong Article 11 protection, and this fact is what makes the interference presumptively unjustified in the absence of compelling reasons.

F001 and F005 are not strictly necessary for the Article 11 violation conclusion. F001 is purely administrative. F005 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
