LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on three interlocking findings: (1) the interference was not prescribed by law with sufficient foreseeability and the procedural safeguards were not applied; (2) the factual basis for the interference was unsubstantiated; and (3) the measures were grossly disproportionate. The irreducible facts are:

F010 — The picket had already started before the court action was lodged, making the application of Article 182 (designed for pre-event prohibition) to an ongoing assembly the core foreseeability problem.
F067 — Article 182(1) CAJ: the procedure was designed for pre-notification situations, not ongoing assemblies.
F068 — Article 182(2) CAJ: actions received on the day of the event shall be left without examination — the very provision that made application to an ongoing picket unforeseeable.
F024 — The only evidence of misconduct was complaints by V.Ch. and Y.S., which did not describe specific incidents.
F034 — V.Ch. and Y.S. gave false addresses and could not be identified, fatally undermining the evidentiary basis.
F040 — The District Court rejected the applicant's request to summon V.Ch. and Y.S., meaning the only evidence was never tested.
F041 — The District Court rejected the request to summon police officers present at the picket.
F043 — The District Court refused a site inspection that could have verified whether tents obstructed traffic or pedestrians.
F047 — The Court of Appeal simply endorsed the District Court's factual findings without independent assessment.
F054 — The Court of Appeal rejected the applicant's arguments without giving reasons.
F029 — The District Court imposed a blanket ban on all peaceful assemblies in Vinnytsia — the paradigmatic example of disproportionality.
F053 — The Court of Appeal replaced 'peaceful assemblies' with 'pickets' without defining the term, maintaining a broad citywide indefinite ban.
F033 — The protesters were actually dispersed by police, confirming the concrete and immediate nature of the interference.
