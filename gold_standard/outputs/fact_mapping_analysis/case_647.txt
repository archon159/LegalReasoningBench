As established in the previous step, the reasoning text contains no factual narrative about the applicants themselves. Therefore, the "factual grounding" identified in the previous step was acknowledged to be absent from the reasoning text itself.

However, now mapping to the atomic facts provided, we can identify which atomic facts correspond to the implied doctrinal elements:

- Scope (assembly exists, participation, peaceful): The atomic facts that correspond are F003 ("taken to police stations as administrative suspects of offences related to public events") and F004 ("administratively convicted for participation in different public events in Moscow"). These establish that there were public events and that the applicants participated in them. F004 is the most directly relevant as it explicitly references participation in public events.

- Interference: F002 ("taken to police stations"), F003 ("taken to police stations as administrative suspects"), and F004 ("administratively convicted") together establish that governmental measures were taken against the applicants. F004 is the most critical as it establishes the conviction — the clearest form of interference.

- Necessity (disproportionate measures): F004 ("administratively convicted for participation in different public events") is the key fact, as the Court's reference to "disproportionate measures" in the cited cases maps onto the convictions of these applicants for participating in public events.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The conclusion that Article 11 was violated turns on the following: there were public assemblies, the applicants participated in them, and the authorities took disproportionate measures against them (specifically, administrative convictions). The single atomic fact that is absolutely essential and irreducible is F004: "The applicants were subsequently administratively convicted for participation in different public events in Moscow." This fact alone establishes the assembly, the participation, and the governmental measure — the three elements that, when combined with the well-established case-law cited, drive the conclusion of a violation. F003 is also necessary as it establishes the link between the police action and the public events, forming the chain of interference. However, F004 is the most critical single fact, as it captures the completed governmental act (conviction) that constitutes the core of the Article 11 violation.
