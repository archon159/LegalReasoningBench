LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope:**
The Court needs to establish that (i) an assembly took place, (ii) the applicant participated, and (iii) the assembly was peaceful (implied). The strictly necessary facts are:
- F004: the demonstration of 26 October 2008 existed (assembly)
- F049: the national court found the applicant participated (participation)
- F055: no evidence applicant was actual PKK member (peaceful/distinction from criminal membership)
- F057: no evidence applicant involved in acts of violence (peaceful)
- F058: acquitted of charges relating to causing injury or damage (peaceful)

F050 and F051 (covering face, chanting slogans) are also established by the national court and referenced by the ECtHR, but they go more to the interference and prescribed by law elements. F056 is important but derivative of F049 and F055. For strict necessity on scope, F004, F049, F055, F057, F058 are the core.

**Interference:**
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's assembly rights. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F037: conviction under Art. 220 § 6 and 314 § 2 (the act of conviction)
- F038: sentence of six years and three months' imprisonment (the sanction)
- F049: participation in the demonstration was the basis for conviction (linking conviction to assembly activity)
- F060: applicant began serving sentence (concrete deprivation of liberty)
- F065: released 12 July 2012 (confirming actual imprisonment)

F056 is implied by F037 and F049 together. For strict necessity, F037, F038, F049 are the irreducible core; F060 and F065 establish the concrete interference was actually suffered (relevant to victim status, which the Court also addresses).

**Prescribed by law:**
The Court needs to establish that the legal provision was not foreseeable in its application and lacked sufficient safeguards. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F049: participation in demonstration was the sole factual basis for conviction
- F050: covering face during demonstration
- F051: chanting slogans
- F055: no evidence of actual PKK membership
- F056: conviction for PKK membership based solely on participation
- F057: no evidence of involvement in acts of violence
- F058: acquitted of charges relating to causing injury or damage
- F038: severe sentence of six years and three months imposed

The combination of F049+F055+F056 is the core: participation alone (without actual membership evidence) led to a membership conviction. F057 and F058 reinforce that the participation was peaceful. F038 establishes the severity of the sanction. F050 and F051 are the specific acts cited by the Court as the "sole basis" so they are necessary.

**Legitimate aim:** No atomic facts — not discussed.

**Necessity:** No atomic facts — not discussed as standalone element.

**Positive obligation:** No atomic facts — not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: the applicant was convicted and imprisoned for participating in a demonstration, on the basis of a legal provision (Art. 220 § 6) that equated mere participation in a demonstration with membership of an illegal armed organisation, in the absence of any concrete evidence of actual membership or violent conduct. This made the interference not prescribed by law (not foreseeable, insufficient safeguards), which was the ground on which the violation was found.

The irreducible facts are:
- F004: the demonstration of 26 October 2008 existed (there was an assembly)
- F037: the applicant was convicted under Art. 220 § 6 and 314 § 2 on the basis of that participation (interference)
- F038: a sentence of six years and three months' imprisonment was imposed (severity of interference)
- F049: the national court found the applicant participated in the demonstration (factual basis for conviction)
- F051: the applicant chanted slogans (the specific conduct found by national court)
- F055: no evidence the applicant was an actual PKK member (key to the foreseeability failure)
- F056: conviction for PKK membership based solely on participation in the demonstration (the legal mechanism that the Court found not prescribed by law)
- F057: no evidence of involvement in acts of violence (supports peaceful character and absence of legitimate basis for membership conviction)
- F058: acquitted of charges relating to causing injury or damage (confirms no violent conduct established)
