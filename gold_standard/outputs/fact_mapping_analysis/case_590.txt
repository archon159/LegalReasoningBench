LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

(a) Scope — Assembly, Participation, Peaceful character
The factual grounding quote was: "The applicants complained principally of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers or participants of public assemblies, namely their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."
This sentence maps to:
- F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers of public assemblies." — directly corresponds to the "organisers" limb of the quote.
- F003: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies." — directly corresponds to the "participants" limb of the quote.
The reference to "public assemblies" in the grounding quote supports the existence of an assembly (scope sub-element i) and participation (scope sub-element ii). There is no atomic fact corresponding to the peaceful character of the assemblies.

(b) Interference
The factual grounding quote was the same sentence as above, specifically the reference to "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."
This maps to:
- F002 and F003 again, as the measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) are described as having been taken against organisers and participants respectively.
F001 is not a factual grounding for interference as it merely refers to an appended table.

(e) Necessity in a democratic society
The Court stated: "Having examined all the material submitted to it, the Court has not found any fact or argument capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion..." and referred to prior case law. There is no specific atomic fact that the Court explicitly adopted as the factual basis for its necessity finding. The necessity conclusion is grounded in the general characterisation of the measures as disproportionate, which traces back to F002 and F003. No atomic fact corresponds to the specific content of the necessity assessment.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

Scope: The Court's implied finding that assemblies existed and that the applicants participated rests strictly on F002 and F003, which establish that the applicants were organisers and participants of public assemblies. Both are necessary because the Court's reasoning covers both categories. F001 is not necessary — it merely points to an appended table. Peaceful character has no atomic fact support.

Interference: The finding of interference (arrest, dispersal, conviction) is grounded in the same characterisation of the measures in F002 and F003. These are the only atomic facts that establish that something was done to the applicants in connection with the assemblies.

Prescribed by law: Not discussed; no atomic facts map to this element.

Legitimate aim: Not discussed; no atomic facts map to this element.

Necessity: The necessity conclusion is the operative finding. It rests on the characterisation of the measures as disproportionate, which is grounded in F002 and F003. No additional atomic fact is strictly necessary beyond these two, as the Court reasons by reference to precedent rather than specific facts.

Positive obligation: Not discussed; no atomic facts map to this element.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on the finding that there were interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly that were not necessary in a democratic society. The irreducible factual core for this conclusion requires: (1) that there were public assemblies (implied by F002 and F003); (2) that the applicants were organisers or participants (F002, F003); and (3) that disproportionate measures were taken against them (F002, F003). All of this is captured in F002 and F003. F001 is not strictly necessary — it is a procedural reference. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. The irreducible core is therefore F002 and F003.
