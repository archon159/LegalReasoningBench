LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish: (i) an assembly existed; (ii) the applicants participated; (iii) their participation was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F008: a public demonstration was held — establishes the assembly
- F010: it was approved by authorities — confirms it was a formal assembly within Art. 11 scope
- F020: both applicants took part — establishes participation
- F013, F014, F015: the authorities changed the venue layout unexpectedly, causing crowd pressure — this is the Court's accepted explanation for the cordon rupture, which is critical to finding the applicants' conduct peaceful
- F026: first applicant did not cause disorder — supports peaceful character
- F041: first applicant chanted political slogans — the Court explicitly accepts this as peaceful conduct
- F065: second applicant came closer to listen to Deputy Ponomarev — supports peaceful character of second applicant

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
- F017 or F018: the demonstration was terminated early by police — establishes the termination
- F021: both applicants were arrested at the venue — establishes the arrest
- F023: both applicants were convicted — establishes the conviction

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
No atomic facts strictly necessary — the Court did not examine this element separately.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court merged this into necessity and did not resolve it. The only facts referenced are those establishing what the applicants were charged with, which overlap with necessity:
- F039: first applicant charged under Art. 19.3 (disobeying lawful police order)
- F074: second applicant charged under Art. 19.3 (disobeying lawful police order)
These are the minimum facts needed to identify the putative aim (prevention of disorder/crime).

--- NECESSITY ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
- F037, F038: police reports against first applicant were templated with no individualised information — supports finding that domestic courts failed to establish key facts
- F041: first applicant charged partly for chanting political slogans — the Court found this was protected expression, not a lawful basis for sanction
- F047: application to examine arresting officers rejected — supports finding of inadequate fact-finding
- F050: justice of the peace relied solely on police reports — supports finding of inadequate fact-finding for first applicant
- F085: second applicant's sister's eyewitness testimony dismissed without adequate assessment — supports finding of inadequate fact-finding
- F086: officers stated second applicant broke cordon at around 8 p.m. — the chronology the Court found inconsistent
- F019: venue cleared by approximately 7.30 p.m. (two hours after dispersal began at 5.30 p.m.) — creates the chronological inconsistency the Court identified
- F067: Government stated second applicant arrested at 8.10 p.m. — the timing that sits ill with the cleared venue
- F054: first applicant sentenced to 24 hours' detention — establishes severity of sanction and chilling effect
- F089: second applicant sentenced to fine of 700 RUB — establishes sanction

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
- F013: barriers narrowed entrance to meeting venue
- F014: barriers restricted space allocated for meeting
- F015: police cordon forced protestors to remain within barriers
These three facts establish the authorities' conduct that the Court (via Frumkin) found to constitute a failure of the positive obligation.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on two pivotal issues: (1) whether the applicants' participation was peaceful (scope/protection of Art. 11), and (2) whether the interference was necessary — specifically whether the domestic courts adequately examined the facts before convicting the applicants. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

- F020: both applicants participated in the demonstration — without this, no Art. 11 claim arises
- F013/F015: the authorities changed the venue layout and used a cordon — the Court's accepted explanation for the cordon rupture, essential to finding the applicants' conduct peaceful
- F021: both applicants were arrested — the core interference
- F023: both applicants were convicted — the core interference
- F037/F038: police reports against first applicant were templated with no individualised information — essential to the finding that domestic courts failed to establish key facts
- F041: first applicant charged partly for chanting political slogans — essential to the finding that he was penalised for protected expression
- F047: application to examine arresting officers rejected — essential to inadequate fact-finding finding for first applicant
- F085: second applicant's eyewitness (sister) dismissed without adequate comparative assessment — essential to inadequate fact-finding finding for second applicant
- F019 + F067: venue cleared by ~7.30 p.m. but second applicant allegedly arrested at 8.10 p.m. for breaking cordon — the chronological inconsistency the Court found the domestic courts failed to address, essential to the necessity finding for second applicant
- F054: first applicant sentenced to 24 hours' detention — necessary for the chilling effect finding and proportionality assessment
