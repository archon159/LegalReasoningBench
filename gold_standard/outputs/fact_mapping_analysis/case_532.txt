LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's final conclusion — that Article 11 was violated — turns on: (i) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated as organisers or participants (scope, implied), and (ii) the State's interference with those assemblies through arrest and conviction (interference, explicit), which was found not necessary in a democratic society (necessity, explicit). The necessity finding itself was grounded in prior case law rather than new facts, so the factual core reduces to the facts establishing that there were assemblies and that the applicants were organisers or participants subjected to State measures. This is captured by F002 and F003. F001 is background and F004 concerns other complaints not relevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore the irreducible factual core is F002 and F003.
