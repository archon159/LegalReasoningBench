LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that there was an assembly, that the applicant participated, and that it was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F024: assembly at Kossuth Square established
- F030: applicant's participation established
- F035: peaceful character established (dispersed without force, no finding of illegal conduct by participants)
- F025: absence of prior notification (relevant to the peaceful/unlawful distinction the Court draws)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that the police dispersal interfered with the applicant's assembly rights. Strictly necessary:
- F035: the police broke up the demonstration
- F036: the applicant participated until dispersal

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court needs to establish the legal basis and safeguards. Strictly necessary:
- F056: Section 14(1) — the dispersal power and its conditions, including the no-notification trigger
- F044: the applicant did not deny absence of notification, confirming the legal basis was engaged
- F053: Section 6 — the 72-hour notification requirement (establishes the rule)
- F055: Section 9(1) — judicial review available (establishes the safeguard)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
No atomic facts strictly necessary as the Court does not ground this element in specific facts stated in its own voice.

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity analysis turns on several factual pillars. Strictly necessary:
- F009/F010: election results were final as of 4 May 2002 (no urgency justifying spontaneous response)
- F019/F022: the Erzsébet Bridge demonstration was illegal (blocking a bridge without notification)
- F027: the Kossuth Square demonstration's declared objective was to support the illegal Erzsébet Bridge demonstrators
- F024: demonstrators assembled at Kossuth Square at 1 p.m.
- F030: applicant joined at 7 p.m.
- F035: police dispersed at 9 p.m. without force (several hours of tolerance; peaceful dispersal)
- F025: no prior notification given (the trigger for dispersal)
- F031: traffic seriously disrupted (justifying the dispersal as proportionate)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts strictly necessary.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (no violation of Article 11)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality analysis. The Court found no violation because: (1) there were no special circumstances warranting an immediate spontaneous response since the election results were already final two months earlier; (2) the demonstration was linked to and supportive of an illegal demonstration; (3) the police showed tolerance by allowing the demonstration to continue for approximately eight hours before dispersing it without force; and (4) adequate legal safeguards existed. The irreducible facts are:

- F010: the result of the April 2002 elections had become final (on 4 May 2002) — defeats the spontaneity/urgency argument
- F016: two months elapsed between elections and the demonstration — reinforces absence of urgency
- F019: the Erzsébet Bridge demonstrators blocked a main bridge — establishes the illegal character of the originating event
- F022: the Erzsébet Bridge demonstrators had not given prior notice — establishes the illegal character of the originating event
- F027: the Kossuth Square demonstrators expressed support for the Erzsébet Bridge participants — links the Kossuth Square demonstration to the illegal event
- F024: demonstrators assembled at Kossuth Square at around 1 p.m. — establishes start of the tolerance period
- F035: police broke up the demonstration at about 9 p.m. without force — establishes both the duration of tolerance and the peaceful manner of dispersal
- F025: no prior notification given — the legal trigger for dispersal and the basis for the interference
- F055: judicial review available within three days — establishes the procedural safeguard
- F056: Section 14(1) — the legal basis for dispersal of unnotified assemblies
