LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

(a) Scope — assembly exists, participation, peaceful:
The reasoning text quotes: "participants of peaceful public assemblies" and "peaceful participants in the public assemblies."
These map to:
- F001/F009: "On the dates indicated in the appended table, the applicants participated in political demonstrations." — establishes both that assemblies existed and that the applicants participated.
- F002/F010: "The applicants were arrested/apprehended at the venue of the political demonstrations." — corroborates presence at the assembly.
The characterization "peaceful" is not independently grounded in any single atomic fact; the Court uses it as a descriptor without citing a specific finding. No atomic fact explicitly states the assemblies were peaceful, though none states they were violent either. The closest grounding is F001/F009 (participation in political demonstrations) combined with the absence of any finding of violence.

(b) Interference:
The reasoning text quotes: "their arrest followed by their conviction for an administrative offence."
These map to:
- F002/F010: arrest/apprehension at the venue.
- F003/F016: conviction of an administrative offence.
- F011: transfer to police stations (part of the arrest process).
- F014: first applicant released several hours after apprehension.
- F015: second applicant remained in detention until the court hearing the next day.
- F018: first applicant fined 10,000 roubles.
- F020: second applicant sentenced to fifteen days administrative imprisonment.

(e) Necessity:
The reasoning text quotes: "the measures applied to the applicants as peaceful participants in the public assemblies did not correspond to a pressing social need and were thus not necessary in a democratic society."
The factual grounding the Court relies on for this conclusion is:
- F001/F009: participation in political demonstrations (establishing the assembly context).
- F002/F010: arrest at the venue (the measure applied).
- F003/F016: conviction for an administrative offence (the measure applied).
- F017: first applicant convicted under Article 20.2 §5 (violating procedure for conduct of public assembly).
- F019: second applicant convicted under Article 19.3 §1 (refusal to obey lawful order of police officer).
- F022: domestic courts considered it unlawful that applicants shouted slogans.
- F023: domestic courts considered it unlawful that applicants participated in assembly without advance notification.
The Court also references its leading cases rather than making independent factual findings, but the above facts are the case-specific grounding.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

(a) Scope (assembly exists, participation, peaceful):
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that there were assemblies and that the applicants participated in them peacefully.
- F001/F009: participation in political demonstrations — this is the single most critical fact establishing both the existence of an assembly and the applicants' participation. F001 and F009 are duplicates; F001 suffices.
- The "peaceful" characterization has no single atomic fact as its grounding; the Court implies it from the overall context. No atomic fact is strictly necessary beyond F001 for this sub-element.
Most critical: F001.

(b) Interference:
The strictly necessary fact is that a governmental act was applied to the applicants in the context of the assembly. The arrest and conviction are the core interference.
- F002: arrest at the venue — most directly establishes the interference with the right to assemble.
- F003: conviction for an administrative offence — the legal consequence that compounds the interference.
Most critical: F002, F003.

(c) Prescribed by law: Not discussed; no atomic facts.

(d) Legitimate aim: Not discussed; no atomic facts.

(e) Necessity:
The Court's necessity assessment turns on whether the measures (arrest and conviction) applied to peaceful assembly participants corresponded to a pressing social need. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F001: participation in political demonstrations (establishes the assembly context and the peaceful nature).
- F002: arrest at the venue (the primary measure).
- F003: conviction for an administrative offence (the legal consequence).
- F022: domestic courts found it unlawful to shout slogans — this is the substantive basis for the conviction, relevant to assessing whether the measure was proportionate.
- F023: domestic courts found it unlawful to participate without advance notification — the other substantive basis for the conviction, equally relevant to proportionality.
F017 and F019 (the specific articles of the Code) are relevant context but the Court does not explicitly reason from them; F022 and F023 capture the operative factual basis more directly.
Most critical: F001, F002, F003, F022, F023.

(f) Positive obligation: Not discussed; no atomic facts.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on whether the arrest and conviction of peaceful assembly participants was necessary in a democratic society. The irreducible facts are:
- F001: the applicants participated in political demonstrations — establishes the Article 11 context (assembly, participation, peaceful nature implied).
- F002: the applicants were arrested at the venue — the primary state interference with the right.
- F003: the applicants were convicted of an administrative offence — the legal consequence that makes the interference serious and concrete.
- F022: the domestic courts found it unlawful to shout slogans — reveals the substantive basis for state action, directly relevant to whether the interference was proportionate.
- F023: the domestic courts found it unlawful to participate without advance notification — the other substantive basis, equally central to the proportionality assessment.
These five facts are the minimum necessary: without F001 there is no Article 11 scope; without F002 and F003 there is no interference; without F022 and F023 there is no basis for assessing whether the measures corresponded to a pressing social need.
