--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish: (i) there were assemblies; (ii) the applicant participated; (iii) the assemblies were peaceful.

- F004: opposition planned demonstration of 26 January 2013 (assembly exists, second case)
- F018: applicant attended demonstration of 17 November 2012 (participation)
- F019: applicant attended demonstration of 26 January 2013 (participation)
- F013: demonstrations intended to be peaceful (peaceful character)
- F014: demonstrations conducted in a peaceful manner (peaceful character)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's freedom of assembly.
- F020: police dispersed demonstration of 17 November 2012
- F021: police dispersed demonstration of 26 January 2013
- F022: applicant arrested during dispersal of 17 November 2012
- F023: applicant arrested during dispersal of 26 January 2013
- F048/F049: convicted and sentenced to five days detention (first case)
- F050/F051: convicted and sentenced to fine of AZN 500 (second case)
For List 2, the most critical are: F020, F021, F022, F023, F049, F051 (the dispersals, arrests, and sanctions are the core interference facts)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court raises concerns about foreseeability and safeguards (first case) and accepts lawfulness in the second case based on failure to establish spontaneous character.
- F005: notice given for 17 November 2012 demonstration (relevant to whether refusal was foreseeable/arbitrary)
- F008: BCEA refused to authorise demonstration of 17 November 2012 (basis for concerns about abusive banning)
- F009: BCEA stated in general terms not in accordance with law (lack of specific reasons, supports foreseeability/safeguards concerns)
- F006: no prior notice given for 26 January 2013 (basis for accepting lawfulness in second case)
- F061: no prior notification required for spontaneous assemblies (legal framework for second case)
- F063: Article 310.1 concerns deliberate failure to comply with police order (basis for doubts about credibility of formal ground)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) there were peaceful assemblies in which the applicant participated; (2) the police dispersed them and the applicant was arrested and convicted; (3) the authorities gave no relevant and sufficient reasons for the dispersal; (4) the authorities failed to acknowledge that participating in an unauthorised but peaceful demonstration is itself protected; (5) the sanctions were disproportionately harsh. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

- F003: assembly of 17 November 2012 existed
- F004: assembly of 26 January 2013 existed
- F018: applicant participated in assembly of 17 November 2012
- F019: applicant participated in assembly of 26 January 2013
- F013: demonstrations intended to be peaceful
- F014: demonstrations conducted in a peaceful manner
- F020: police dispersed demonstration of 17 November 2012
- F021: police dispersed demonstration of 26 January 2013
- F022: applicant arrested during dispersal of 17 November 2012
- F023: applicant arrested during dispersal of 26 January 2013
- F005: notice given for 17 November 2012 (authorities failed to take this into account — key to necessity finding in first case)
- F049: sentenced to five days administrative detention (sanction, chilling effect)
- F051: sentenced to fine of AZN 500 (harshness of sanction in second case)
- F075: minimum wage AZN 93.5 (contextualises disproportionality of fine)
