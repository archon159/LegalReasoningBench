LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Scope (assembly exists / participation)**
The reasoning text states: "organisers or participants of public assemblies" and "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences." The atomic fact that corresponds to this is F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers or participants of public assemblies." F001 is also relevant insofar as it establishes that there is a defined list of applicants whose details are in the appended table, but it does not itself establish the factual content of the assemblies. F002 is the direct match.

**Scope (peaceful)**
As noted previously, there is no explicit factual grounding for the peaceful character of the assemblies in the reasoning text, and none of the atomic facts address this either. No atomic fact corresponds.

**Interference**
The reasoning text states: "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences." This maps directly to F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers or participants of public assemblies." The word "disproportionate measures" in F002 encompasses the arrests, dispersals, and convictions mentioned in the reasoning. No other atomic fact addresses this.

**Prescribed by law**
Not discussed in the reasoning. No atomic facts correspond.

**Legitimate aim**
Not discussed in the reasoning. No atomic facts correspond.

**Necessity**
The reasoning text states: "Having examined all the material submitted to it, the Court has not found any fact or argument capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion" and refers to "issues similar to those in the present case" from prior leading cases. The only atomic fact that could ground this is F002, which establishes the nature of the complaints (disproportionate measures against assembly participants). F001 is tangentially relevant as it points to the appended table containing case-specific details, but the Court does not spell out what those details are in the reasoning section. F002 remains the primary anchor.

**Positive obligation**
Not discussed. No atomic facts correspond.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**: F002 is strictly necessary. It establishes that the applicants were organisers or participants of public assemblies, which is the minimum factual predicate for the scope of Article 11 to be engaged. F001 is not strictly necessary for the legal reasoning itself — it is an administrative reference to the appended table.

**Interference**: F002 is strictly necessary. The reference to "disproportionate measures" (arrests, dispersals, convictions) is the factual basis for the Court's finding that there was an interference with the applicants' freedom of assembly.

**Prescribed by law**: No atomic facts — not discussed.

**Legitimate aim**: No atomic facts — not discussed.

**Necessity**: F002 is strictly necessary. The Court's necessity finding is grounded in the characterisation of the measures as disproportionate, which is the substance of F002. Without this fact, the Court could not have concluded that the interferences were not necessary in a democratic society.

**Positive obligation**: No atomic facts — not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on: (1) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated as organisers or participants, and (2) the fact that disproportionate measures (arrests, dispersals, administrative convictions) were taken against them. Both of these are captured in F002. F001 is a procedural reference and does not itself establish any fact that the legal conclusion turns on. F003 is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, the irreducible factual core is F002 alone.
