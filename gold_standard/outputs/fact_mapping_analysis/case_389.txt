LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts per doctrinal element

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish: (i) an assembly existed; (ii) the applicant participated; (iii) the assembly was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- Assembly exists: F013 (fifty to sixty protesters continued marching spontaneously — this is the assembly at issue), F016 (it was spontaneous, explaining why it was unnotified).
- Participation: F006 (applicant took part in the demonstration), F048 (applicant was holding the placard in the first line — establishes her presence and participation in the post-official gathering).
- Peaceful: F091 (criminal judge no. 3 found protesters' behaviour did not justify indiscriminate force — the Court's key adopted finding on peacefulness), F092 (protesters had not impeded traffic), F094 (protesters had not tried to enter restaurant), F095 (protesters had not attacked police officers), F098 (no criminal proceedings ever initiated against applicant — confirms applicant's individual peaceful conduct).

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needed to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's exercise of her right. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F048 (applicant was struck by a police officer while holding the placard — the direct act of interference against her specifically), F049 (injuries to mouth), F050 (injuries to hand — establishes the physical reality of the interference), F036 (protesters were forcefully separated by police — the dispersal act itself).

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court found foreseeability satisfied based on the clarity of the legislative text. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F115 (section 5(a) Right of Assembly Act — grounds for dissolution), F116 (section 5(b) Right of Assembly Act — grounds for dissolution on public order), F120 (section 23(1) Protection of Public Safety Act — empowers dissolution), F123 (section 23(2) — proportionality requirement), F125 (section 23(3) — prior warning requirement). These are the provisions the Court cited as the legal basis.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court made no definitive finding and proceeded on assumption only. No atomic facts are strictly necessary to support a Court finding here. Empty.

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity analysis turned on: the peaceful character of the gathering, the minor level of disruption, the absence of justification for the force used, and the findings of domestic courts. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F006 (Sunday — contextualises minor disruption), F014 (pedestrian streets — contextualises minor disruption), F020 (content of placard — harmlessness of protest), F032 (protesters did not approach restaurant entrance), F033 (protesters did not enter restaurant), F091 (criminal judge no. 3: behaviour did not justify indiscriminate force — the Court's central adopted finding), F092 (protesters had not impeded traffic — directly contradicts Government's justification), F097 (protest dispersed without prior warning — relevant to proportionality and compliance with domestic law), F047 (applicant not arrested), F098 (no criminal proceedings against applicant), F048 (applicant struck by police officer), F049 (injuries to mouth), F050 (injuries to hand — establishes the concrete disproportionate impact on the applicant).

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two interlocking findings: (1) the gathering was peaceful and the dispersal was unjustified; (2) the force used against the applicant specifically was disproportionate. The irreducible core facts are:

- F006: The applicant took part in the demonstration on Sunday 2 February 2014 — establishes her presence and the temporal/contextual setting (Sunday).
- F013: Around fifty to sixty protesters continued marching spontaneously after the official end — establishes the assembly at issue.
- F014: The protesters marched mostly through pedestrian streets — establishes the minor nature of any disruption.
- F016: The post-official-end protest was spontaneous — explains the absence of notification without implying unlawfulness.
- F048: The applicant was holding the placard in the first line when struck violently by a police officer — the central fact establishing both her participation and the interference directed at her.
- F049 and F050: Injuries to mouth and hand — establishes the concrete disproportionate impact of the interference on the applicant.
- F091: Criminal judge no. 3 found that the attitude and behaviour of the protesters had not justified the indiscriminate use of force — the Court's key adopted domestic finding on disproportionality.
- F092: Protesters had not impeded the movement of traffic — directly undermines the Government's primary justification for dispersal.
- F097: The protest was violently dispersed without any prior warning — establishes that even the procedural requirements of domestic law (section 23(3) of the Protection of Public Safety Act) were not complied with, reinforcing disproportionality.
- F098: No criminal proceedings were ever initiated against the applicant — confirms the applicant's individual peaceful conduct and the absence of any legitimate basis for the force used against her specifically.
