LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

Scope: F002 and F003 are strictly necessary. The Court must establish that the applicants were organisers or participants of public assemblies before it can assess whether their Article 11 rights were engaged. These two facts together establish both the existence of assemblies and the applicants' participation in them.

Interference: F002 and F003 are strictly necessary. The Court's finding of interference rests on the fact that measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) were taken against the applicants as organisers and participants. Without these facts, there is no interference to assess.

Prescribed by law: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element is not discussed.

Legitimate aim: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element is not discussed.

Necessity: F002 and F003 are the most critical. The applicants' characterisation of the measures as "disproportionate" is the factual premise that triggers the necessity analysis. The Court's conclusion that the measures were not necessary in a democratic society is grounded in the existence of those measures against organisers and participants of assemblies.

Positive obligation: No atomic facts are strictly necessary — this element is not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The case turned on the fact that the applicants — as organisers and participants of public assemblies — were subjected to measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction for administrative offences) that the Court found were not necessary in a democratic society. The irreducible factual core is:
- F002: establishes that organisers of public assemblies were subjected to disproportionate measures.
- F003: establishes that participants of public assemblies were subjected to disproportionate measures.

These two facts are the essential foundation: without them, there is no assembly, no participation, no interference, and no necessity analysis. F001 and F004 are not strictly necessary for the Article 11 conclusion itself.
