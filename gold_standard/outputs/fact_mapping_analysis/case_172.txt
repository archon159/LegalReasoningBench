LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on whether disproportionate measures were taken against the applicants as organisers or participants of public assemblies. The final conclusion of a violation of Article 11 requires, at minimum, the existence of applicants who were organisers or participants of public assemblies and who were subjected to measures in that context. This is captured by:
- F002: organisers subjected to disproportionate measures
- F003: participants subjected to disproportionate measures

F001 (the appended table) and F004 (other complaints) are not strictly necessary for the Article 11 conclusion. F001 is procedural and F004 concerns other Convention provisions. The irreducible core is F002 and F003.
