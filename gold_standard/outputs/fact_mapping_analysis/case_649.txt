LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on: (1) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants were organisers or participants, and (2) the fact that disproportionate measures (arrest, conviction) were taken against them in connection with those assemblies. These two points are what distinguish this as an Article 11 case at all and what the necessity finding is directed at. Both F002 and F003 are strictly necessary — F002 covers organisers and F003 covers participants, and the judgment explicitly covers both categories. Without either, the factual basis for the violation finding would be incomplete. F001 is purely procedural and F004 concerns other complaints not relevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
