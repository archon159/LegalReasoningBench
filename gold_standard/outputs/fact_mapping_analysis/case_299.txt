LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court's acceptance that the assemblies were peaceful is strictly grounded in: F006 (intended to be peaceful) and F007 (conducted in a peaceful manner). The Court adopts these characterizations in its own language ("unauthorised peaceful demonstration"). F011 (each applicant attended) is necessary to establish participation, which is implied. F002, F003, F004 establish that demonstrations took place on the relevant dates.
Strictly necessary: F002, F003, F004, F006, F007, F011.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court's implied finding of interference rests on the dispersal, arrest, and conviction. Strictly necessary: F012 (dispersal), F013 (arrests), F046 (convictions).

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court's lawfulness finding turns on: (1) no prior notice was given (F005); (2) the spontaneous assembly exemption existed (F080); (3) the demonstrations were not spontaneous because the underlying issues were ongoing (F008 for 12 January and 10 March, F010 for 26 January). Strictly necessary: F005, F080, F008, F010.
F009 is subsumed by F008 (same subject matter, same reasoning applies).

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
Not assessed by the Court. Empty.

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity finding turns on: the absence of relevant and sufficient reasons for dispersal (grounded in the fact of dispersal and conviction without acknowledgment of Article 11 protection), the peaceful nature of the assemblies, and the chilling effect of the sanctions regime. Strictly necessary: F006, F007, F012, F046, F047, F084, F087, F097, F101.
F085 (administrative detention) is relevant context for the severity of sanctions but F087 (increased fines) and F084 (explicit criminalization of participation) are more directly operative. F097 and F101 are the international materials the Court draws on for the chilling effect finding.

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. Empty.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two pivotal issues: (1) whether the interference was prescribed by law (resolved against the applicants on the spontaneity point, but accepted as lawful), and (2) whether the interference was necessary in a democratic society (resolved in favour of the applicants — no sufficient reasons, chilling effect). The final violation finding rests on necessity. The irreducible facts are:

- F005: No prior notice given (establishes the basis for the interference and the lawfulness finding).
- F006 and F007: Demonstrations were peaceful (the Court's own characterization of "unauthorised peaceful demonstration" is central to the necessity finding — the authorities failed to acknowledge that participating in such a demonstration was protected).
- F012: Police dispersed the demonstrations (the act constituting interference).
- F046: Applicants convicted (the legal consequence that produces the chilling effect and establishes the full weight of the interference).
- F047: Applicants sentenced to fines (the concrete sanction, relevant to proportionality and chilling effect).
- F084: Participation in assembly organised in breach of rules explicitly punishable (the legal framework that enables the conviction and generates the chilling effect).
- F087: Fines significantly increased (the severity of the sanctions regime, central to the chilling effect finding).
- F097: PACE noted amendments likely to have further negative impact on freedom of assembly (international material supporting the chilling effect conclusion).
- F101: Commissioner noted sanctions coupled with blanket ban clearly have chilling effect (international material directly supporting the Court's chilling effect language).
