LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each doctrinal element

Scope (assembly exists, participation, peaceful):
- F004: The first applicant attended the unauthorised opposition demonstration on 2 April 2011 — strictly necessary to establish both that an assembly existed and that the applicant was present at it.
- F006: The demonstration was intended to be peaceful — strictly necessary for the peaceful character finding.
- F007: The demonstration was conducted in a peaceful manner — strictly necessary for the peaceful character finding.
- F036: The Nasimi District Court found that the first applicant had failed to stop participating in an unauthorised demonstration — strictly necessary to establish what the conviction was directed at (the factual anchor for participation).
- F037: The first applicant was convicted under Article 310.1 of the CAO — strictly necessary as the legal characterisation of the conviction that the Court uses to establish both participation and interference.

Interference:
- F014: The first applicant was arrested at Fountains Square — strictly necessary as the initial act of interference.
- F037: Convicted under Article 310.1 — strictly necessary as the core act of interference.
- F038: Sentenced to eight days' administrative detention — strictly necessary as the concrete sanction constituting interference.
- F050: Appeal dismissed — strictly necessary to confirm the interference was upheld and not remedied domestically.

Prescribed by law:
No atomic facts — not discussed in the reasoning.

Legitimate aim:
No atomic facts — not discussed in the reasoning.

Necessity:
- F014: Arrested — necessary to ground the chilling effect finding.
- F037: Convicted — necessary to ground the chilling effect finding.
- F038: Sentenced to eight days' administrative detention — necessary to ground the chilling effect finding (the severity of the sanction).

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following facts:
- F004: The first applicant attended the demonstration on 2 April 2011 — without this, there is no assembly-related activity to protect.
- F006 and F007: The demonstration was peaceful — without this, Article 11 protection (which covers only peaceful assembly) would not apply.
- F014: The first applicant was arrested at the demonstration — this is the triggering act of state interference.
- F037: The first applicant was convicted under Article 310.1 — this is the core interference the Court found to violate Article 11.
- F038: The first applicant was sentenced to eight days' administrative detention — this is the concrete sanction that produced the chilling effect and made the interference serious enough to constitute a violation.
- F050: The Baku Court of Appeal dismissed the first applicant's appeal — this confirms the interference was not remedied domestically and was upheld by the state, which is essential to the violation finding (no domestic remedy cured the breach).
