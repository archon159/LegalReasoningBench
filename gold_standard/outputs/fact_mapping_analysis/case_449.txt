LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on three interlocking findings: (1) the assembly was peaceful and the dispersal lacked sufficient justification and was carried out with excessive force without prior warning; (2) the applicant was arrested and initially detained essentially for participating in the assembly rather than for any specific reprehensible act; and (3) the domestic courts failed to provide relevant and sufficient reasons for the conviction, relying exclusively on police testimony without addressing crucial contradictions. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

F011 — assembly existed and was ongoing from 20 February 2008 (establishes the assembly)
F013 — camp set up, demonstrators present around the clock (establishes the assembly's character)
F014 — applicant was active participant (establishes participation)
F043 — decision instituting criminal case showed police took measures to forcibly end the assembly (contradicts Government's account of purpose)
F354 — Ombudsman: police in riot gear attacked demonstrators without warning (establishes excessive force and lack of prior order)
F365 — Ombudsman: no order to disperse given on morning of 1 March as required by law (establishes procedural failure)
F360 — Ombudsman noted contradictions in official statements about purpose of operation (establishes lack of credibility of official account)
F075 — arrest record cited organising demonstrations as reason for arrest (establishes that arrest was linked to assembly participation, not specific criminal act)
F099 — memorandum identified applicant as activist of assembly (corroborates that arrest was for participation)
F144 — initial charges drafted in general abstract terms without specific acts (establishes charges were not grounded in specific conduct)
F213 — charges under Art. 225.1 dropped because order was to inspect not disperse (establishes internal contradiction in prosecution)
F272 — domestic courts relied on same evidence as indictment, exclusively police testimony (establishes failure of domestic courts)
F249 — applicant argued he was already at police station at time of alleged assault (establishes crucial unaddressed contradiction)
F074 — record of bringing-in showed applicant at Kentron Police Station at 6.30 a.m. (corroborates the time contradiction)
F372 — PACE condemned arrests as de facto crackdown on opposition (establishes political context and broader pattern)
F380 — PACE: significant number of cases based solely on police testimony (establishes systemic pattern corroborating applicant's case)
F368 — no criminal case initiated against any police officer (establishes one-sided accountability)
