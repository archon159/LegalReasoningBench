LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

(a) Scope
The reasoning text quotes: "participants and organisers of public assemblies" and "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences." These phrases map to:
- F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies."
- F003: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers of public assemblies."
These are the only atomic facts that correspond to the scope-related language in the reasoning. There is no atomic fact establishing that the assemblies were peaceful.

(b) Interference
The reasoning text quotes: "disproportionate measures taken against them as participants and organisers of public assemblies, namely their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."
This maps to:
- F002: participants of public assemblies
- F003: organisers of public assemblies
No atomic fact specifically describes the arrest or conviction in isolation as a standalone fact; F002 and F003 are the closest available atomic facts. F001 references the appended table with details, which may contain more specifics, but those are not reproduced as atomic facts here.

(c) Prescribed by law
Not discussed. No factual grounding identified. No atomic facts map to this element.

(d) Legitimate aim
Not discussed. No factual grounding identified. No atomic facts map to this element.

(e) Necessity in a democratic society
The reasoning text states: "Having examined all the material submitted to it, the Court has not found any fact or argument capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion." The Court references similarity to leading cases but cites no specific facts from the present case. The only atomic facts that could be said to underlie the necessity assessment (as the factual premise for the interference being disproportionate) are:
- F002 and F003, which establish the context of the measures complained of.
No atomic fact independently establishes the specific conduct of authorities (dispersal, arrest, conviction) beyond what is implied by F002 and F003.

(f) Positive obligation
Not discussed. No atomic facts map to this element.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turns on the finding that there were interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly (in the form of measures taken against them as participants and organisers of public assemblies) that were not necessary in a democratic society. The entire legal chain — scope, interference, and necessity — rests on the same two atomic facts:
- F002: applicants as participants of public assemblies subjected to disproportionate measures
- F003: applicants as organisers of public assemblies subjected to disproportionate measures

F001 is a procedural reference to the appended table and is not itself a substantive fact the Court's reasoning turns on. F004 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore the irreducible core is F002 and F003.
