LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on two findings: (1) the unlawful apprehension of Mr Fedorov, Mr Prokopenko and Ms Levina preventing their participation (negative obligation violation), and (2) the failure of police to protect all applicants from counter-demonstrator violence at authorised demonstrations (positive obligation violation). The irreducible facts are:

- F009: The Rainbow flash mob was authorised — establishing the assembly was lawful and protected.
- F036: The Voronezh demonstration was authorised — same point.
- F059: The 29 June 2013 demonstration was notified in advance — same point.
- F078: Mr Fedorov, Mr Prokopenko and Ms Levina intended to participate in the 12 October 2013 demonstration — establishing attempted participation.
- F013: Police did not intervene when counter-demonstrators insulted participants at the Rainbow flash mob — core failure of positive obligation.
- F047: About forty police officers were present at the Voronezh demonstration but did not intervene — core failure of positive obligation.
- F064: Police did not intervene when counter-demonstrators attacked participants on 29 June 2013 — core failure of positive obligation.
- F083: Police did not intervene when counter-demonstrators pushed applicants on 12 October 2013 — core failure of positive obligation.
- F084: Police apprehended Mr Fedorov, Mr Prokopenko and Ms Levina five minutes before the demonstration started — core interference (negative obligation).
- F097: Domestic court found Mr Prokopenko's deprivation of liberty unlawful — corroborates unlawfulness of interference.
- F046: Counter-demonstrators beat Mr Lebedev and Mr Nasonov — concrete harm resulting from failure of positive obligation.
- F066: About thirty counter-demonstrators beat Mr Starov, Ms Pitenova and Mr Fedorov — concrete harm resulting from failure of positive obligation.
- F039: Mr Lebedev and Mr Nasonov asked the police for protection before the demonstration — establishing State had advance notice and failed to act.
- F041: Organisers notified police of counter-demonstration aimed at obstructing the event — establishing State had advance notice and failed to act.
