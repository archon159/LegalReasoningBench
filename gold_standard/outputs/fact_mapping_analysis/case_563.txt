LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court must establish at minimum: (i) that there was an assembly in which the applicants participated (scope), (ii) that there was a governmental interference (interference), and (iii) that this interference failed the necessity test. All three of these steps are grounded in F002, which identifies the applicants as organisers or participants of public assemblies and identifies the measures taken against them (arrest, dispersal, conviction). F002 is therefore the single irreducible atomic fact on which the entire conclusion rests. F001 is a background fact identifying the applicants but adds nothing to the legal conclusion. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
