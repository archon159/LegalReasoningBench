LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court's finding that Article 11 is applicable rests on: (1) the meetings being assemblies (implied by the nature of the events), (2) the applicants' participation or attempted participation, and (3) the peaceful character. The most critical atomic facts are:
- F008: Ilinden tried to organise meetings each year (establishes the pattern of assemblies)
- F026, F048, F064, F070, F096, F100, F124, F135, F146, F153, F170: Members actually tried to attend or attended meetings (participation)
- F009: All prior meetings were banned without finding of violent intent (supports peaceful character finding by continuity)
For the peaceful character specifically, the Court relies on the absence of evidence of violent intent. The most representative facts establishing the nature of the events as peaceful commemorations are F026 (members tried to approach monastery), F106 (members laid flowers and made speech), F124 (members assembled at Rozhen monastery), F153 (members marched toward memorial), F170 (members gathered for four-hour programme).

Narrowed to most critical: F008, F009, F026, F106, F124, F153, F170.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court's interference findings rest on:
- Formal mayoral bans with inhibiting effect: F098 (April 2000 ban), F108 (July 2000 ban)
- Seizure of materials: F101, F103 (April 2000 seizures)
- Physical diversion to different location: F135, F137, F138 (May 2001)
- Hostile group hampering meeting with inadequate police response: F154, F155, F157 (September 2002)
- Police physically blocking access: F027 (April 1998), F048 (August 1998), F064 (September 1998), F071 (April 1999), F081 (May 1999), F096 (August 1999), F120 (September 2000), F146 (July 2001), F186 (September 2003)

Most critical (one per occasion, establishing the interference):
F027, F048, F064, F071, F081, F096, F098, F101, F108, F120, F137, F146, F154, F157, F186.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on three distinct violations: (1) repeated unjustified bans/enforcement preventing Ilinden's commemorative meetings; (2) a post-Stankov ban with identical reasoning; (3) failure to protect participants from counter-demonstrators. The irreducible facts are:

- F009: All prior meetings 1990-97 were banned (establishes the pattern and the prior Strasbourg finding)
- F027: Police turned back members at checkpoints in April 1998 (representative enforcement of ban)
- F048: Roads blocked by police in August 1998 (representative enforcement of ban)
- F081: Police stopped members citing prosecutor decree in May 1999 (representative enforcement)
- F096: Police stopped members at roads in August 1999 (representative enforcement)
- F098: Mayor of Sandanski formally banned April 2000 meeting (formal ban with inhibiting effect)
- F101: Police seized stage decoration in April 2000 (concrete interference)
- F108: Mayor of Petrich formally banned July 2000 meeting (formal ban)
- F120: Police stopped members in September 2000 (enforcement)
- F137: Police diverted members from chosen site in May 2001 (concrete interference with place)
- F146: Group turned back by police in July 2001 (enforcement)
- F154: Hostile group surrounded memorial and prevented approach in September 2002 (positive obligation trigger)
- F155: Members physically attacked by hostile group in September 2002 (positive obligation trigger)
- F157: Police formed cordon but failed to prevent attacks in September 2002 (inadequate positive obligation discharge)
- F183: Mayor of Blagoevgrad banned September 2003 meeting after Stankov judgment (post-judgment ban, most egregious necessity finding)
- F186: Prosecutor decree read out to members on 12 September 2003 (enforcement of post-judgment ban)
- F213: Article 185 § 1 CCP extremely vaguely formulated (prescribed by law concern, accepted but noted)
