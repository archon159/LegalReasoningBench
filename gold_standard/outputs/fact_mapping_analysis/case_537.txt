LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

(a) Scope

The factual grounding quote was: "the decision to sanction the applicant with a fine for his organising of a public meeting on 21 September 2012"

Note: The reasoning text refers to "21 September 2012" but the atomic facts consistently refer to "21 September 2013" (F010, F011, F012). This appears to be a typographical error in the judgment's reasoning section. The atomic facts that correspond to this quote are:
- F010: The first applicant organised a public meeting for the former members of the kolkhoz Rodina on 21 September 2013.
- F011: The public meeting was held with a view to discussing the matter about the land.
- F012: Sixty-five persons participated in the public meeting.
- F013: On 23 September 2013, the Rîbniţa District Court imposed a fine of 145 MRT roubles on the first applicant.
- F014: The fine was imposed for breach of the rules of conducting public manifestations.
- F015: The court found that the first applicant had not obtained a prior authorisation.

(b) Interference

The factual grounding quote was: "the decision to sanction the applicant with a fine for his organising of a public meeting on 21 September 2012 amounted to an 'interference by [a] public authority' with the applicant's right to freedom of assembly under the first paragraph of Article 11"

Atomic facts:
- F010: The first applicant organised a public meeting on 21 September 2013.
- F013: The Rîbniţa District Court imposed a fine of 145 MRT roubles on the first applicant.
- F014: The fine was imposed for breach of the rules of conducting public manifestations.
- F015: The court found that the first applicant had not obtained a prior authorisation.
- F016: The first applicant challenged the decision before the MRT Supreme Court.
- F017: The MRT Supreme Court dismissed the appeal.
- F018: The MRT Supreme Court upheld the fine.

(c) Prescribed by law

The factual grounding quote was: "no elements in the present case allow the Court to consider that there was a legal basis for limiting the applicant's right to freedom of peaceful assembly"

This is a negative finding — the Court found an absence of legal basis. The relevant atomic fact is:
- F019: The relevant domestic law and practice were summarised in Mozer, which is the only reference to the applicable legal framework.

No atomic fact positively establishes the absence of a legal basis; the Court's finding is essentially that nothing in the case materials (including F019) provided a sufficient legal basis. There is no atomic fact that directly states "there was no legal basis," but F019 is the closest reference to the legal framework against which this assessment was made.

(d) Legitimate aim

Not discussed. No factual grounding identified. No atomic facts to map.

(e) Necessity

Not discussed. No factual grounding identified. No atomic facts to map.

(f) Positive obligation (Moldova)

First factual grounding quote: "Since the parties did not adduce any evidence to show that the Moldovan Government has changed its position concerning the Transdniestrian region in the years preceding the facts of the present case"

Atomic facts:

No atomic fact in the provided list directly establishes that the applicant failed to inform Moldovan authorities. This is a negative factual finding by the Court for which no corresponding atomic fact exists in the provided list.

LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of legal reasoning

(a) Scope
The Court's implied finding that an assembly existed and that the applicant participated rests critically on the fact that the applicant organised a public meeting attended by 65 persons. The most critical atomic facts are:
- F010: The first applicant organised a public meeting on 21 September 2013.
- F012: Sixty-five persons participated in the public meeting.

F011 (purpose of the meeting) and F015 (no prior authorisation) are contextual but not strictly necessary for the scope finding. The peaceful nature has no atomic fact grounding.

(b) Interference
The interference finding rests on the fact that a public authority imposed a sanction (fine) on the applicant for organising the meeting. The strictly necessary atomic facts are:
- F010: The first applicant organised a public meeting.
- F013: The Rîbniţa District Court imposed a fine on the first applicant.
- F014: The fine was imposed for breach of the rules of conducting public manifestations.

F016-F018 (the appeal) confirm the sanction was upheld but are not strictly necessary for the interference finding itself, which is grounded in the initial imposition of the fine.

(c) Prescribed by law
The Court's finding that there was no legal basis is a negative conclusion. The only atomic fact relevant to the legal framework is:
- F019: The relevant domestic law and practice were summarised in Mozer.

This is the reference point against which the absence of legal basis was assessed. No other atomic fact directly supports this element.

(d) Legitimate aim
Not discussed. Empty.

(e) Necessity
Not discussed. Empty.

(f) Positive obligation
For Moldova's positive obligation, the strictly necessary atomic facts are:
- F019: Relevant domestic law and practice summarised in Mozer (which established Moldova's prior conduct regarding re-establishing control, referenced by the Court).

The second aspect (applicant not informing Moldovan authorities) has no corresponding atomic fact in the provided list.

For Russia's responsibility (which leads to the violation finding), the strictly necessary atomic facts are:
- F006: Background including the Transdniestrian conflict set out in Ilaşcu (establishing the historical context for Russia's control).
- F019: Relevant domestic law and practice summarised in Mozer (which established Russia's effective control over the MRT).

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The case turned on: (1) the applicant organised a public meeting; (2) he was fined by an MRT court for doing so without prior authorisation; (3) there was no legal basis for this restriction under applicable law; and (4) Russia exercised effective control over the MRT and is therefore responsible. The irreducible atomic facts are:

- F010: The first applicant organised a public meeting on 21 September 2013. [establishes the assembly and participation — the trigger for the entire case]
- F013: The Rîbniţa District Court imposed a fine of 145 MRT roubles on the first applicant. [establishes the interference]
- F014: The fine was imposed for breach of the rules of conducting public manifestations. [establishes the nature of the interference]
- F019: The relevant domestic law and practice were summarised in Mozer. [the reference point for the prescribed-by-law finding and for Russia's effective control, which grounds the violation by Russia]
