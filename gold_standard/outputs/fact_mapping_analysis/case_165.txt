LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish that there was an assembly and that the applicant participated in it. The most critical fact is:
- F018: The Diyarbakır Assize Court established that the applicant had taken part in the demonstration of 13 December 2009.
This is the court-established fact of participation. F002 also captures this but F018 is the more precise judicial finding.

**Interference**
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's freedom of assembly. The conviction is the core interference:
- F002: The applicant was convicted on account of his participation in a demonstration.
- F011: The Diyarbakır Assize Court convicted the applicant on the basis of Articles 220 § 6 and 314 § 3.
F005 and F006 (arrest and detention) are also part of the interference but the conviction is the definitive act. F003 (sentence) reinforces the severity but is not strictly necessary to establish the existence of interference.

**Prescribed by law**
The Court's reasoning turns entirely on the application of Article 220 § 6 to the applicant. The strictly necessary fact is:
- F001: The application concerns the conviction of the applicant under Articles 220 § 6 and 314 of the Turkish Criminal Code.
- F011: Convicted on the basis of Articles 220 § 6 and 314 § 3.
No other case-specific atomic facts are necessary here, as the Court's analysis is purely jurisprudential.

**Legitimate aim, Necessity, Positive obligation**
Not discussed by the Court; no atomic facts are strictly necessary for these elements in the Court's reasoning.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two things: (1) the applicant was convicted for participating in a demonstration, and (2) the legal basis for that conviction (Article 220 § 6) was not foreseeable and thus not prescribed by law. The irreducible facts are:

- F018: The Diyarbakır Assize Court established that the applicant had taken part in the demonstration of 13 December 2009. — This is the factual basis for the conviction and for the Article 11 scope/interference analysis.
- F002: The applicant was convicted on account of his participation in a demonstration. — This directly links the conviction to the exercise of the Article 11 right.
- F011: The Diyarbakır Assize Court convicted the applicant on the basis of Articles 220 § 6 and 314 § 3. — This identifies the legal basis that the Court found not to be prescribed by law, which is the reason the violation was found.

F001 is also relevant as it frames the entire application, but F011 is more specific and subsumes it for the purpose of the conclusion. F018 and F002 together establish that the interference was with the Article 11 right specifically (participation in a demonstration), and F011 establishes the legal basis that failed the prescribed-by-law test.
