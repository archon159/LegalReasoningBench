LIST 1: Factual grounding quotes mapped to atomic facts

**Scope — assembly exists:**
The Court's grounding quote is: 'The Court refers to its finding that the gathering in question fell within the scope of Article 11 of the Convention (see Navalnyy v. Russia [GC], nos. 29580/12 and 4 others, §§ 110-12, 15 November 2018).'
This refers to the gathering outside the Zamoskvoretskiy District Court. The atomic facts pointing to this are:
- F012: 'On the morning of 24 February 2014 the applicants were among some 150 people who came to the Zamoskvoretskiy District Court of Moscow.'
- F016: 'The applicants remained outside the Zamoskvoretskiy District Court of Moscow among other members of the public who were also aspiring to attend the hearing, thus forming a gathering.'
- F017: 'Further details about the gathering outside the Zamoskvoretskiy District Court of Moscow on 24 February 2014 are described in Navalnyy v. Russia [GC], nos. 29580/12 and 4 others, §§ 35-42, 15 November 2018.' (This cross-reference mirrors the Court's own cross-reference to Navalnyy for the scope finding.)

**Scope — participation:**
The Court's grounding quote is: 'the applicants' intentions and conduct were also covered by the notion of peaceful assembly, including those of Ms Polyakova who claimed that she was acting as an observer for a group of human rights defender organisations.'
The atomic facts pointing to participation are:
- F012: The applicants were among those who came to the court.
- F016: The applicants remained outside and formed part of the gathering.
No atomic fact specifically records Ms Polyakova's observer role — this detail comes from her own claim as reported in the reasoning, not from the formalised atomic facts.

**Scope — peaceful:**
The Court's grounding quote is: 'the applicants' intentions and conduct were also covered by the notion of peaceful assembly' and 'the measures applied to the applicants as peaceful participants in the public assembly.'
No atomic fact in the provided list explicitly states that the assembly was peaceful or that no violence occurred. The closest atomic facts are:
- F016: The applicants 'remained outside... among other members of the public who were also aspiring to attend the hearing, thus forming a gathering.' This implies a passive, non-violent presence.
- F013: The applicants came to attend a public court hearing — a lawful civic purpose.

**Interference:**
The Court's grounding quote is: 'the applicants' arrests, transfer to the police station and the ensuing sanctions constituted an interference' and 'the applicants were arrested, transferred to the police station and charged with administrative offences for the sole reason that their gathering as such had not been authorised.'
The atomic facts pointing to this are:
- F003: 'The applicants were arrested during the gathering in front of the Zamoskvoretskiy District Court of Moscow on 24 February 2014.'
- F018: 'At different times the applicants were arrested and transferred to a police station following the gathering outside the Zamoskvoretskiy District Court of Moscow on 24 February 2014.'
- F004: 'The applicants were convicted of administrative offences following their arrest during the gathering on 24 February 2014.'

**Prescribed by law:**
Not discussed by the Court in the reasoning section. No factual grounding was identified. No atomic facts to map.

**Legitimate aim:**
Not discussed by the Court. No factual grounding was identified. No atomic facts to map.

**Necessity:**
The Court's grounding quotes are: 'the measures applied to the applicants as peaceful participants in the public assembly did not correspond to a pressing social need and were thus not necessary in a democratic society' and 'the applicants were arrested, transferred to the police station and charged with administrative offences for the sole reason that their gathering as such had not been authorised.'
The atomic facts pointing to this are:
- F003: Arrest of the applicants.
- F018: Arrest and transfer to police station.
- F004: Conviction for administrative offences.
- F016: The gathering was formed by people aspiring to attend a public hearing — implying no disorderly conduct.
- F013: The purpose was to attend a public court hearing — a lawful civic purpose, supporting the absence of pressing social need to disperse them.

**Positive obligation:**
Not discussed. No atomic facts to map.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope:**
The Court needed to establish that there was a gathering, that the applicants participated in it, and that it was peaceful.
- F016 is the most critical fact: it establishes the existence of the gathering and the applicants' participation in it ('thus forming a gathering').
- F013 establishes the purpose (attending a public court hearing), which supports the peaceful character and the assembly's civic nature.

**Interference:**
The Court needed to establish that state authorities took measures against the applicants in connection with the assembly.
- F018 is the most critical: it establishes arrest and transfer to police station.
- F004 establishes the ensuing administrative convictions.

**Prescribed by law:**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

**Legitimate aim:**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

**Necessity:**
The Court needed to establish that the sole reason for the measures was the absence of authorisation, and that the applicants were peaceful, in order to conclude there was no pressing social need.
- F016: The gathering arose from people aspiring to attend a public hearing — no disorderly conduct implied.
- F013: The purpose was attendance at a public court hearing — lawful civic purpose.
- F018: Arrest and transfer — the measures applied.
- F004: Administrative convictions — the sanctions imposed.

**Positive obligation:**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (1) there was a peaceful gathering; (2) the applicants participated in it; (3) they were arrested, transferred to a police station, and convicted of administrative offences; (4) the sole reason was the absence of prior authorisation, not any violent or disorderly conduct. These are the facts that made the violation inevitable under the Court's established case-law.

- F016: Establishes the gathering and the applicants' participation — the assembly element.
- F013: Establishes the lawful civic purpose of the gathering — supports peaceful character and undermines any pressing social need.
- F018: Establishes arrest and transfer — the core interference.
- F004: Establishes administrative conviction — the sanction completing the interference.

F003 is subsumed by F018 (which is more specific about both arrest and transfer), so F003 is not separately listed in List 3 as it adds nothing beyond F018. F016 and F013 together establish the assembly and its peaceful/civic nature, which is the factual premise for the necessity finding.
