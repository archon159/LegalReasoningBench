LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on: (i) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated or which they organised, and (ii) the fact that governmental measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) were taken against them in that capacity. These are the facts that trigger Article 11 protection and establish the interference that the Court found unjustified. The irreducible core is therefore F002 and F003.
