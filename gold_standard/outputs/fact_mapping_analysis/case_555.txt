LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on two facts: (1) that the applicants were organisers or participants of public assemblies (establishing scope and the factual basis for the Article 11 claim), and (2) that disproportionate governmental measures — arrest, dispersal, conviction — were taken against them (establishing interference and grounding the necessity finding). Both F002 and F003 are strictly necessary: F002 establishes the position of organisers and F003 that of participants, and together they cover the full applicant group. Without these facts, there is no assembly, no participation, and no interference to assess. F001 is administrative background and not part of the legal core. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
