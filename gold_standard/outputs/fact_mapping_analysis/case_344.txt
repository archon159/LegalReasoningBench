LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on the fact that State measures (arrest, dispersal, administrative conviction) were taken against people who were organising or participating in public assemblies. The irreducible factual core is:

- F002: establishes that organisers of public assemblies were subjected to disproportionate measures — this is essential because it identifies both the assembly context and the State's interference.
- F003: establishes the same for participants — equally essential for the same reasons.

F001 is procedural and not substantively necessary for the legal conclusion. F004 (other complaints) is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.

The final conclusion thus rests on F002 and F003 as the irreducible core.
