LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

Scope: F002 and F003 are strictly necessary. F002 establishes that some applicants were organisers of public assemblies (supporting scope_assembly_exists and scope_participation for organisers). F003 establishes that other applicants were participants of public assemblies (supporting scope_participation for participants). Together they are the minimum factual basis for the Court's implied finding that the assemblies existed and that the applicants participated. No atomic fact supports the peaceful character finding, so none can be listed here.

Interference: F002 and F003 are strictly necessary. The "disproportionate measures" described in these facts are the factual basis for the Court's identification of an interference (arrest and conviction). Without these facts there is no interference to assess.

Prescribed by law: No atomic facts. Not discussed.

Legitimate aim: No atomic facts. Not discussed.

Necessity: F002 and F003 are the only case-specific atomic facts the Court could have drawn on. The necessity conclusion is grounded primarily in prior case-law, but the link to the present case is made through the description of measures against organisers and participants, which maps to F002 and F003.

Positive obligation: No atomic facts. Not discussed.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned on the fact that state measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction) were taken against people acting as organisers or participants of public assemblies. The irreducible factual core is therefore:
- F002: establishes that applicants as organisers were subjected to disproportionate measures in connection with public assemblies — this is essential both for scope (assembly, participation) and interference.
- F003: establishes the same for applicants as participants.

F001 is merely a procedural pointer to the appended table and is not itself a substantive fact the conclusion rests on. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. The final conclusion therefore rests irreducibly on F002 and F003.
