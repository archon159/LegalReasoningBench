LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

(a) Scope: The Court's implicit finding that there were peaceful assemblies in which the applicants sought to participate is strictly grounded in F002 (prohibition on holding peaceful assemblies). F002 is the most critical fact: it establishes that there were assemblies, that the applicants sought to hold/participate in them, and that they were peaceful. F003, F004, F005 add detail about the nature of restrictions but are not strictly necessary beyond F002 for establishing scope.
- Strictly necessary: F002

(b) Interference: The existence of interference is grounded in the fact that the applicants faced prohibitions or restrictions on their assemblies. F002 is the core fact (outright prohibition), with F003, F004, F005 establishing additional forms of interference (location, time, manner restrictions). All four are relevant, but F002 is the most critical as it establishes the most direct form of interference (prohibition). For strict necessity, F002 alone establishes interference; F003-F005 are corroborative.
- Strictly necessary: F002

(c) Prescribed by law: The Court's finding that legal provisions did not meet "quality of law" requirements is not grounded in any specific atomic fact about the content of the law — the Court outsources this to the leading case. The only atomic facts available are F002-F005, which establish that restrictions existed (and thus that some legal basis was invoked), but do not speak to the quality of that legal basis. F002 is the minimum necessary fact to establish that a legal provision was applied.
- Strictly necessary: F002

(d) Legitimate aim: Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.
- Strictly necessary: none

(e) Necessity: The Court's finding that the interference was not necessary in a democratic society is grounded in the existence of the interference itself. F002 is the core fact. F003, F004, F005 add texture (restrictions on location, time, manner) but F002 is the irreducible minimum.
- Strictly necessary: F002

(f) Positive obligation: Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.
- Strictly necessary: none

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two findings: (1) the legal provisions did not meet quality of law requirements, or (2) the interference was not necessary in a democratic society. Both findings presuppose that there was an interference with the applicants' freedom of peaceful assembly. The single atomic fact that is absolutely necessary for the Court to reach its conclusion — that there was a prohibition on holding peaceful assemblies, which constituted an unjustified interference — is F002. Without F002, there is no assembly, no interference, and no violation. F003, F004, and F005 describe additional forms of restriction but are not strictly necessary for the conclusion given that F002 alone establishes a complete case of interference with peaceful assembly rights. The conclusion of violation flows from F002 as the irreducible factual core.
- Irreducible core: F002
