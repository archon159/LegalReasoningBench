LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality of the police intervention. The Court found the intervention disproportionate because: (1) the demonstration was mainly peaceful before the intervention; (2) the security intelligence did not specifically concern this demonstration and did not demonstrate violent intent; (3) the disruption to traffic lasted less than twenty minutes before the police intervened; (4) the police intervened swiftly — less than two minutes after the applicants joined the front lines; and (5) the manner of dispersal caused serious injury. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary for this conclusion:

F027: The police report of 2 June 2010 mentioned only that the group might, if provoked, attack — insufficient to justify dispersal (undermines security justification).
F020: The demonstration was organised by a political party (BDP), not directly by the PKK (undermines the Government's PKK-link argument).
F146: The vast majority of participants were not involved in any violent act before the police intervention (establishes peaceful character).
F144: At least three adolescents threw stones — but these were sporadic and not committed by the applicants (contextualises the limited violence).
F145: Some demonstrators reacted against the stone-throwing (reinforces peaceful character of the majority).
F069: Police records did not indicate whether vehicle damage occurred before or during the intervention (removes this as a pre-intervention justification).
F142: Traffic was not stopped until the demonstrators began heading towards the BDP bus (establishes start of disruption).
F032: People began gathering at about 3 p.m. (establishes timeline).
F050: Applicants joined front lines at about 4.40 p.m. (establishes timeline for swift intervention finding).
F143: Police intervention began less than two minutes after applicants joined the front lines (establishes swiftness of intervention — central to disproportionality finding).
F052: Police dispersed demonstrators using water cannon and tear gas (establishes nature of interference).
F048: Police warned participants several times before intervening (acknowledged by Court but insufficient to justify the swiftness).
F053: The second applicant fell and sustained hip injuries during the first moments of the police intervention (establishes serious consequence of manner of dispersal).
F055: Medical report confirmed fractured neck of femur (establishes severity of injury — cross-referenced from Article 3 finding).
F151: Section 22 prohibited demonstrations on public roads (establishes unlawfulness of demonstration — acknowledged by Court but held insufficient alone to justify dispersal).
F150: Section 10 required prior notification (establishes unlawfulness — same point).
