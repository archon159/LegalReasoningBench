LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of law

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish that there were assemblies, that the applicants participated, and that the assemblies were peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F003 and F004: Establish that demonstrations were planned and took place (the foundation of the entire case).
- F018: Establishes that each applicant attended one of the demonstrations (participation).
- F014 and F015: Establish the peaceful character of the demonstrations (adopted by the Court as a finding).

F013 is also necessary to show the demonstrations actually occurred despite the refusals, but F003/F004 combined with F019 (dispersal) sufficiently establish this. F013 is included as it bridges the planning and the actual holding.

**Interference**
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicants' freedom of assembly:
- F019: Dispersal by police — the primary interference with the assembly itself.
- F020: Arrest of all applicants — interference with individual liberty and assembly rights.
- F052: Conviction under Article 310.1 — the legal consequence constituting interference.
- F053: Sentence of administrative detention — the sanction imposed.

**Prescribed by law**
The Court's finding rests on the legislative framework as analysed in Gafgaz Mammadov, manifested in the present cases through the refusals:
- F007: Refusal to authorise the 20 October demonstration at the proposed location.
- F009: Refusal to authorise the 17 November demonstration.
- F064: Reference to Gafgaz Mammadov for the relevant legislative provisions (the basis for the Court's concerns about foreseeability and safeguards).

F008, F010, F011, F012 provide additional detail on the refusals but F007 and F009 are the core facts.

**Legitimate aim**
No atomic facts strictly necessary — the Court does not make a finding on this element.

**Necessity**
The Court's necessity analysis rests on four factual pillars:
- F005 and F006: Prior notification was given — the authorities failed to take this into account.
- F014 and F015: The demonstrations were peaceful — no sufficient reason for dispersal.
- F019: The police dispersed the demonstrations — the act whose proportionality is assessed.
- F025: The formal ground for arrest was Article 310.1 (failure to comply with police order) — the credibility of which the Court doubts.
- F052: Conviction under Article 310.1 — the domestic courts did not acknowledge Article 11 protection.

**Positive obligation**
Not discussed. No atomic facts.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following facts, which are essential to the conclusion that Article 11 was violated:

1. F003/F004: Demonstrations were planned and held — without this, there is no assembly and no Article 11 issue.
2. F018: Each applicant attended one of the demonstrations — establishes standing and participation.
3. F014/F015: The demonstrations were peaceful — essential because Article 11 only protects peaceful assembly, and the Court's finding that there were no sufficient reasons for dispersal depends on this.
4. F005/F006: Prior notification was given to the authorities — the Court explicitly found that the authorities failed to take this into account, which is central to the necessity analysis.
5. F019: The police dispersed the demonstrations — the primary act of interference.
6. F052: The applicants were convicted under Article 310.1 — the Court doubted the credibility of this formal ground and found no acknowledgment of Article 11 protection.
7. F053: The applicants were sentenced to administrative detention — the sanction imposed, confirming the seriousness of the interference.

F025 (the formal ground stated in the administrative offence reports) is also essential as it underpins the Court's doubts about the credibility of the Article 310.1 charge, but F052 (the conviction) is the more critical downstream fact. F025 is included in List 2 for necessity but F052 captures the essential point for List 3. However, since F025 is what the Court specifically pointed to regarding the 'formal ground,' it is retained.

Final irreducible core: F003, F004, F005, F006, F014, F015, F018, F019, F025, F052, F053.
