LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two distinct violations. For the first applicant (first application): the pre-demonstration arrest on dubious grounds, the politically motivated nature of the arrest, and the failure of domestic courts to examine the case properly. For the second applicant and first applicant (second application): the dispersal of peaceful demonstrations, the convictions under Article 310.1 CAO without acknowledgment of the Article 11 protection, and the failure to give relevant reasons for dispersal despite prior notification.

Irreducible core facts:
- F002 (first applicant was member of PFPA — establishes political profile making pre-demonstration arrest suspicious)
- F004 (both applicants participated in unauthorised peaceful demonstrations — establishes pattern and context)
- F018 (first applicant intended to participate in 11 March 2011 demonstration — establishes what the arrest prevented)
- F031 (demonstrations intended to be peaceful and conducted in a peaceful manner — establishes scope and undermines justification for dispersal)
- F035 (first applicant arrested on 4 March 2011, before the demonstration — core interference fact)
- F047 (first applicant told arrest was in connection with the 11 March 2011 demonstration — establishes political motivation)
- F058 (Nasimi District Court disregarded first applicant's statement — establishes failure of domestic review)
- F063 (first applicant sentenced to ten days' administrative detention — establishes severity of interference)
- F072 (Baku Court of Appeal upheld without proper examination — confirms failure of domestic review)
- F022 (organisers gave notice prior to 20 October 2012 demonstration — undermines justification for dispersal)
- F023 (organisers gave notice prior to 17 November 2012 demonstration — undermines justification for dispersal)
- F027 (BCEA noted only in general terms assembly would not comply with law — establishes lack of relevant reasons)
- F077 (demonstrations dispersed as soon as protesters began to gather — establishes nature and immediacy of interference)
- F104 (applicants convicted under Article 310.1 CAO — establishes the formal legal basis used and its problematic application)
- F106 (applicants sentenced to various periods of administrative detention — establishes severity of interference)
