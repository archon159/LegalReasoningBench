LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the legal reasoning

Scope: F002 is strictly necessary. It establishes that the applicants were organisers or participants of public assemblies, which is the minimum factual basis for the Court to treat the case as falling within the scope of Article 11. F001 is not strictly necessary for the legal reasoning itself — it is a procedural reference.

Interference: F002 is strictly necessary. The reference to "disproportionate measures" taken against the applicants as organisers or participants is the factual basis from which the Court infers that state action interfered with their freedom of assembly. No other atomic fact addresses this.

Prescribed by law: Not discussed. No atomic facts are necessary or relevant.

Legitimate aim: Not discussed. No atomic facts are necessary or relevant.

Necessity: F002 is the only atomic fact that could ground the necessity analysis, insofar as it describes the nature of the measures complained of. However, as noted above, the Court's necessity conclusion rests primarily on analogy with prior case-law rather than on specific facts stated in this decision. F002 remains the minimum factual anchor.

Positive obligation: Not discussed. No atomic facts are necessary or relevant.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court must at minimum establish: (1) that there were assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope), and (2) that the state took measures against them in that context (interference), which together form the basis for the necessity finding. Both of these points rest solely on F002. F001 is a procedural reference and adds no substantive factual content necessary for the conclusion. F003 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.

Therefore, the irreducible factual core is: F002.
