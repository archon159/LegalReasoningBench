LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts per element

--- SCOPE ---
For the finding that there was an assembly within the scope of Art. 11: F006 (demonstration held on 6 May 2012), F008 (demonstration had been approved by Moscow city authorities). These are the core facts establishing that a qualifying assembly took place.
For the finding that the applicant participated and was peaceful: F018 (applicant took part in the demonstration), F026 (applicant conducted herself peacefully), F055 (applicant stated she had been in the middle of the crowd which pulled her towards the police officers — the key fact explaining why she was beyond the cordon without deliberate intent), F011 (barriers narrowed entrance — the structural cause of the cordon rupture, adopted by the Court from the case file).

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The strictly necessary facts establishing interference are: F015 (police ordered meeting to finish early), F016 (police began to disperse participants), F019 (applicant was arrested at the venue). These three facts establish the concrete acts that interfered with the applicant's assembly rights.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
No atomic facts strictly necessary — element not discussed.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
No atomic facts strictly necessary — element not resolved by the Court.

--- NECESSITY ---
The strictly necessary facts are: F073 (District Court refused to examine the applicant's complaint — the core fact establishing absence of domestic balancing), F076 (City Court upheld the refusal — confirming the absence of any judicial review), F019 (applicant was arrested — the concrete measure whose proportionality was never assessed), F016 (dispersal — the broader measure whose proportionality was never assessed).

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
The strictly necessary facts are: F011 (barriers narrowed entrance to the meeting venue — the key fact establishing the authorities' role in creating the conditions for disorder), F013 (police cordon forced protesters to remain within the barriers — the operational measure that contributed to the deterioration), F014 (numerous clashes between police and protesters — the outcome showing the failure to ensure peaceful conduct).

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The case turned on three interlocking findings: (1) the applicant was a peaceful participant in a qualifying assembly; (2) the authorities terminated the assembly early and arrested her; and (3) no domestic court ever assessed whether those measures were justified. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary for the Court to reach its conclusion of a violation:

F006: There was a demonstration on 6 May 2012 — establishes the assembly.
F008: The demonstration had been approved — establishes it was an authorised, protected assembly.
F018: The applicant took part in the demonstration — establishes her participation.
F026: The applicant conducted herself peacefully — establishes she retained Art. 11 protection.
F011: Barriers narrowed the entrance to the meeting venue — establishes the authorities' role in creating the conditions that led to disorder and the cordon rupture, underpinning both the positive obligation finding and the context of the applicant's arrest.
F015: Police ordered the meeting to finish early — establishes the early termination as an interference.
F016: Police began to disperse participants — establishes the dispersal as an interference.
F019: Applicant was arrested at the venue — establishes the most direct interference with her assembly rights.
F073: District Court refused to examine the applicant's complaint — establishes the absence of domestic balancing, which is the pivot of the necessity finding.
F076: City Court upheld the refusal — confirms the complete absence of any judicial proportionality assessment at domestic level.
