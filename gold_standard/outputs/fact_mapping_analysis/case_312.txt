LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish that the applicants were participants in assemblies. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F002: all applicants were members of the PFPA (establishes their identity as opposition members)
- F014: all applicants participated in peaceful opposition demonstrations (establishes both assembly and participation)
- F015: in the course of many demonstrations, most applicants were arrested and convicted (establishes the link between assembly participation and the measures taken)

**Interference**
The Court's finding of interference rests on three pillars: (i) the applicants' known opposition affiliation, (ii) the dubious and coordinated nature of the arrests, and (iii) the failure of domestic courts to examine the political motivation. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F002: known PFPA membership (opposition affiliation generally known)
- F014: active participation in opposition demonstrations
- F027: all arrested within a short time frame (February or March 2014)
- F035: first applicant's arrest on implausible grounds (swearing aloud at no one)
- F036: second applicant approached for vague 'suspicious behaviour'
- F037: sixth applicant approached for vague 'suspicious behaviour'
- F038: third applicant approached on pretext of traffic accident
- F039: fifth applicant approached on basis of unspecified complaint
- F040: fourth applicant approached on basis of unspecified 'information'
- F061: all ultimately charged with the same offence (Article 310.1 - failure to comply with police order)
- F082: courts disregarded applicants' submissions contesting political motivation
- F085, F086, F087, F088: only police officers questioned as witnesses in respective hearings
- F089: police officers merely reiterated official version
- F110: PACE Resolution noting dubiously motivated criminal prosecutions
- F113: PACE calling on authorities to end systemic harassment
- F119: Amnesty International reporting on arrests of PFPA members in February-March 2014

**Prescribed by law (implied)**
The implied negative conclusion on lawfulness rests on the same facts as interference, specifically the arbitrariness of the arrests and the failure of domestic courts to scrutinise them:
- F035, F036, F037, F038, F039, F040 (dubious initial grounds)
- F061 (all charges collapsed into same offence)
- F082 (courts ignored political motivation arguments)
- F085, F086, F087, F088, F089 (one-sided evidentiary process)

**Legitimate aim:** Empty - no atomic facts support this element as the Court did not discuss it.

**Necessity:** Empty - no atomic facts support this element as the Court did not apply this test.

**Positive obligation:** Empty.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the finding that the arrests were politically motivated interference with freedom of assembly, not genuine law enforcement. The irreducible facts are:

- F002: all applicants were PFPA members (establishes opposition identity as the trigger)
- F014: all applicants had participated in peaceful opposition demonstrations (establishes the assembly context and their prior exercise of Article 11 rights)
- F027: all arrested within a short time frame in February-March 2014 (establishes coordinated pattern)
- F035: first applicant arrested on implausible grounds of swearing aloud at no one (establishes pretextual nature of arrests)
- F061: all charged with the same offence of failure to comply with police order, regardless of the varying initial pretexts (establishes the pattern of pretextual prosecution)
- F082: domestic courts disregarded applicants' submissions that arrests were politically motivated (establishes failure of domestic remedy and absence of genuine judicial scrutiny)
- F085 / F086 / F087 / F088: only police officers questioned as witnesses (establishes one-sided proceedings, supporting the finding of arbitrary and unlawful interference)
- F110: PACE Resolution noting dubiously motivated criminal prosecutions in Azerbaijan (establishes systemic context corroborating individual findings)
- F119: Amnesty International reporting on arrests of PFPA members in February-March 2014 (directly corroborates the pattern of targeting the specific party to which all applicants belonged at the exact time of the arrests)
