LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The case turned on the following: Nagibin joined an authorised assembly (not his own), was arrested and convicted for holding an unauthorised event, and the domestic courts failed to provide any adequate reasoning for why his presence constituted a separate event or why the lack of prior authorisation justified his removal and conviction. The irreducible facts are:

- F006: the LDPR event was already authorised at the same time and location — this is why Nagibin's own notification was refused, and it is the factual backdrop against which his joining the LDPR event must be assessed
- F008: Nagibin joined the LDPR event — the central factual dispute; the Court found the domestic courts failed to explain why this amounted to a separate event
- F014: Nagibin was convicted under Article 20.2 § 1 of the CAO — the act whose necessity the Court assessed and found unjustified
- F016: the domestic court dismissed Nagibin's argument that he had simply joined the LDPR event — establishes the inadequacy of domestic reasoning that the Court found fell short of Article 11
- F017: the domestic court dismissed Nagibin's argument that he had not staged his own event — same point, completing the picture of the domestic courts' failure to engage with the substance
