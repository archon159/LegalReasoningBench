LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on: (i) the existence of assemblies in which the applicants participated, and (ii) the fact that governmental measures (arrest, dispersal, administrative conviction) were taken against them as participants. The single atomic fact that is absolutely essential and irreducible is F002, which captures both the applicants' participation and the measures taken against them. F003 is also necessary to establish that the assemblies actually occurred (without which there is no assembly to protect under Art. 11). F004 is not strictly necessary for the legal conclusion — it is contextual background about the political setting, not a fact the Court's reasoning turned on.
