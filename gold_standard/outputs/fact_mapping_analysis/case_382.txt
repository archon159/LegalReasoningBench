LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion, the Court must at minimum establish: (i) that the applicants were involved in public assemblies (triggering Article 11 scope), and (ii) that governmental measures were taken against them (establishing interference). Both of these are grounded in F002 and F003. The necessity conclusion itself is drawn from case-law rather than case-specific facts, so no additional atomic facts are strictly necessary beyond those establishing scope and interference.
