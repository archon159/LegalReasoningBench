LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court found a violation of Article 11 on the basis that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion, the Court needed at minimum: (1) that there were public assemblies (scope), (2) that the applicants were organisers or participants (scope/participation), and (3) that measures were taken against them (interference). All three points are captured by F002 and F003 together. F002 covers organisers and F003 covers participants; both refer to "disproportionate measures" which grounds the interference. These two facts are the irreducible core. F001 merely references the appended table and F004 concerns other complaints unrelated to Article 11 — neither is necessary for the Article 11 conclusion.
