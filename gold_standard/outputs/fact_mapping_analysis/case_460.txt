LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on the following key facts:

1. The gatherings were peaceful and caused minimal or no disruption:
F018 (episode 1: not blocking roads), F041 (walkabouts peaceful), F054 (no banners, no nuisance on Bolshaya Nikitskaya), F083 (no banners, no noise at Kudrinskaya), F146 (pavement gathering), F147 (no sound equipment, applicant talking to journalist)

2. The applicant was arrested, detained and convicted solely because the gatherings lacked authorisation, without any assessment of actual disturbance:
F020 (arrest episode 1), F023 (report: irregular gathering), F030 (conviction based on lack of notification), F048 (arrest during group photo), F055 (arrest without warning), F084 (arrest without warning), F147 (arrest while talking to journalist), F158 (conviction based solely on lack of notification)

3. Episodes 5 and 6 specifically: the conduct could not reasonably have been foreseen as requiring notification, and no legitimate aim was served:
F110, F114, F115 (episode 5: walking away from demonstration), F135, F136, F137, F138 (episode 6: attending court hearing, denied entry)

4. Episode 7: spontaneous assembly in response to same-day judgment, with no legal allowance for spontaneous assemblies:
F144, F145, F179

5. The legal framework gave authorities unfettered discretion to arrest based on lack of notification alone, without requiring assessment of disturbance:
F175, F179, F180

6. Criminal-nature sanctions (imprisonment) were imposed without justification:
F102 (15 days imprisonment, episode 3), F160 (7 days imprisonment, episode 7)

7. The applicant is a well-known public figure whose repeated arrests had a chilling effect:
F003, F004, F005, F006
