LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts per element

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that there was an assembly, that the applicant participated, and that it was peaceful (at least as regards the applicant).
-> F003: establishes the existence of the gathering
-> F009: establishes the applicant's participation
-> F022: establishes that the applicant was not found to have injured police (key to peacefulness)
-> F024: establishes that the applicant was not found to have resisted police (key to peacefulness)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a state act interfered with the applicant's freedom of assembly.
-> F020: the conviction under section 7(2) is the interference
-> F030: the suspended pronouncement is still treated as an interference (this is the specific legal point the Court makes)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court identifies the legal basis but declines to examine lawfulness. The only strictly necessary fact is the identification of the legal basis.
-> F020: conviction under section 7(2) of Law no. 3713
-> F034: the content of section 7(2) as applicable at the material time

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts the aim tentatively. No atomic facts are strictly necessary from the case file — the Court's acceptance is not grounded in specific facts. However, the anti-terrorism context is implicit.
-> F005: PKK is an illegal armed organisation (background context for national security / prevention of disorder and crime)
-> F020: conviction under anti-terrorism law

--- NECESSITY ---
This is the decisive element. The strictly necessary facts are those that show (a) what the applicant actually did, (b) what the national court found (and failed to find), and (c) the absence of any link to violence.
-> F003: the gathering was on the anniversary of Öcalan's arrest (context of the assembly)
-> F007: a press statement was read out (the specific act)
-> F009: the applicant participated in the gathering
-> F020: conviction under section 7(2)
-> F021: not established that applicant attended in response to PKK calls
-> F022: not established that applicant injured police
-> F024: not established that applicant resisted police
-> F025: the sole factual basis the court relied on (going to DTP building to disseminate propaganda)
-> F026: conviction on that sole basis

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. Empty.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis. The Court found a violation because: (1) the applicant participated in a peaceful public gathering; (2) she was convicted solely on the basis of her presence/participation; (3) the national court gave no relevant and sufficient reasons and ignored the absence of any link to violence. The irreducible facts are:

-> F009: applicant participated in the gathering — establishes the assembly/participation nexus
-> F020: conviction under section 7(2) — the interference
-> F030: suspended pronouncement — confirms the conviction still counts as interference
-> F021: not established that applicant attended in response to PKK calls — undermines the court's reasoning
-> F022: not established that applicant injured police — confirms peaceful character and absence of violence
-> F024: not established that applicant resisted police — confirms peaceful character
-> F025: sole factual basis used by national court (going to DTP building to disseminate propaganda) — the inadequate reasoning
-> F026: conviction on that sole basis — the insufficiency of the national court's reasoning
