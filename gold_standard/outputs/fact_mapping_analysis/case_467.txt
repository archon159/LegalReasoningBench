LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on the finding that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The only atomic fact that is strictly necessary to reach this conclusion — given the doctrinal structure of Article 11 — is F002. It establishes: (i) that there were public assemblies (scope); (ii) that the applicants were organisers or participants (scope/participation); and (iii) that disproportionate measures were taken against them (interference and necessity). Without F002, none of the key doctrinal steps can be grounded in the facts of the case. F001 and F003 are not essential to the violation conclusion under Article 11.
