LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court implies assemblies existed, that applicants participated or attempted to participate, and that the assemblies were peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that Ilinden notified authorities of planned rallies (showing intent to assemble), that participants gathered, and that any violence risk came from counter-demonstrators rather than Ilinden participants.

Key facts: F003 (notification of rally at Rozhen), F048 (notification of rally at Samuilova Krepost), F024 (notification of rally in Blagoevgrad), F068 (participants gathered in Blagoevgrad on 12 Sept 2004), F074 (counter-demonstrators from VMRO, not Ilinden, were the source of confrontation), F057 (Ilinden held its rally without disturbance on 1 Aug 2004, showing peaceful character)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing the various categories of interference the Court identified: mayoral bans/restrictions, the Regional Governor's ban, police orders, arrests and seizures, and fines.

Key facts: F009 (Mayor of Sandanski restricted timing without reasons), F049 (Mayor of Petrich banned rally at Samuilova Krepost), F033 (Mayor of Blagoevgrad banned rally citing simultaneous municipal event), F224 (Regional Governor of Blagoevgrad imposed outright ban), F041 (police issued orders to Ilinden members not to organise rallies), F131 (police seized flags and placards and destroyed wreath), F280 (participants fined), F316 (second applicant arrested on 4 May 2009)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing the four specific concerns the Court raised: no legal basis for arrests/seizures, reliance on unregistered status as a ground not in the law, the Regional Governor acting outside his statutory role, and the vague formulation of the police powers provision.

Key facts: F131 (arrests and seizures without cited legal basis), F032 (Mayor relied on lack of registration as ground for ban), F341 (section 62(1) of 1997 Act formulated very generally), F344 (section 55(1) of 2006 Act has almost identical wording), F224 (Regional Governor acted outside his statutory role), F338 (1990 Act gives no role to regional governors in policing rallies — implied by the statutory framework), F280 (fines later annulled by courts)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary here as the Court explicitly declined to determine this element.

--- NECESSITY ---
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing: repetition of previously condemned grounds, absence of reasons for restrictions, police orders without reasons, the counter-demonstrator point, and the escalation of police conduct.

Key facts: F032 (unregistered status used as ban ground), F033 (simultaneous municipal event used as ban ground), F063 (Mayor cited risk of public order breaches), F064 (Mayor cited lack of registration), F065 (Mayor cited simultaneous municipal event), F010 (Mayor of Sandanski gave no reasons for restriction), F041 (police issued orders without reasons), F128 (police issued orders without reasons on 12 Sept 2005), F074 (counter-demonstrators present — police task was to separate groups), F057 (police were in fact able to ensure smooth rally, showing separation was possible), F278 (participants arrested without cited grounds), F280 (participants fined, later annulled), F175 (court acknowledged lack of registration was not sufficient ground, yet still upheld ban on other grounds already found problematic)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
The strictly necessary facts are those establishing that the risk of disorder came from counter-demonstrators and that the police were in fact capable of managing this.

Key facts: F074 (VMRO counter-demonstrators present at Gotse Delchev monument), F057 (Ilinden held rally without disturbance — showing police could ensure order)


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis. The Court found that the authorities repeatedly relied on the same grounds already found inadequate in prior judgments, gave no or insufficient reasons for restrictions, issued police orders without reasons, escalated to arrests and fines without legal basis or justification, and failed to discharge their positive obligation to protect the assemblies from counter-demonstrators. The irreducible factual core is:

1. The systematic pattern of bans and restrictions by the Mayor of Blagoevgrad, relying on the same grounds (unregistered status, simultaneous municipal events, risk of public order breaches) already condemned by the Court: F032, F033, F063, F064, F065, F104, F105, F154, F155, F169, F170, F171, F243, F244, F245, F270, F271
2. The Mayor of Sandanski imposing restrictions without giving reasons: F009, F010
3. The Mayor of Petrich banning rallies citing simultaneous VMRO events: F049, F116
4. The Regional Governor of Blagoevgrad imposing an outright ban outside his statutory authority and relying on irrelevant grounds: F224, F227
5. Police issuing orders to Ilinden members without reasons: F041, F128, F157, F252, F317, F322
6. Police arresting participants without cited grounds and without violent behaviour: F278, F316, F321
7. Police seizing materials without legal basis: F131, F305, F318, F323, F324, F325
8. Participants fined (later annulled — showing chilling effect): F280, F282
9. Counter-demonstrators present but police capable of managing order: F074, F057
10. Courts upholding bans on grounds already found problematic: F175, F176, F177, F250, F251, F276
