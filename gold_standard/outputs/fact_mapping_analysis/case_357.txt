LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

For the scope element, the factual grounding quote was: "disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies, namely the dispersal of these assemblies, as well as their arrest followed by their conviction for administrative offences." This maps to F002 ("The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies that took place in Moscow"), which is the closest atomic fact. F001 is also relevant as it establishes who the applicants are.

For the interference element, the same quote applies, mapping again to F002 for the same reasons.

For prescribed by law: no factual grounding was identified in the reasoning text, and none of the atomic facts speak to this element. Empty.

For legitimate aim: no factual grounding was identified, and none of the atomic facts speak to this element. Empty.

For necessity: the factual grounding was the Court's reference to leading cases and the absence of distinguishing facts. None of the three atomic facts directly correspond to this. F002 is the closest in that it establishes the general nature of the complaints, but the necessity reasoning was jurisprudential rather than factual. F002 is the best available mapping.

For positive obligation: not discussed, no factual grounding, no mapping.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each discussed element

Scope: F002 is strictly necessary — it establishes that the applicants were participants in public assemblies, which is the factual predicate for the scope analysis (assembly exists, participation, and by implication peaceful character since the Court proceeds with Article 11 analysis).

Interference: F002 is strictly necessary — it identifies the specific acts (dispersal, arrest, conviction) that constitute the interference with freedom of assembly.

Prescribed by law: not discussed, no atomic facts strictly necessary.

Legitimate aim: not discussed, no atomic facts strictly necessary.

Necessity: F002 is the only atomic fact that connects to the necessity finding, as it establishes the nature of the measures (disproportionate measures against assembly participants) that the Court found not necessary in a democratic society. However, the necessity reasoning was primarily jurisprudential.

Positive obligation: not discussed, no atomic facts strictly necessary.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated turned on: (1) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated, and (2) the State's interference with those assemblies through dispersal, arrest, and conviction. These are both captured in F002. F001 establishes the identity of the applicants but is procedural rather than substantive — it does not bear on whether Article 11 was violated. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, F002 is the sole atomic fact that is strictly necessary for the Court's conclusion, as it is the only fact that speaks to the substance of the Article 11 analysis.
