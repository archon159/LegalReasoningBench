LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on two things: (1) that there were public assemblies in which the applicants participated and against which the state took measures (dispersal, arrest, conviction) — this is the foundation for both scope and interference; and (2) that those assemblies were of a political nature (manifestations for fair elections), which is relevant to the weight of the right and thus to the necessity/proportionality conclusion. F002 is the single most essential fact — it establishes both that the applicants were assembly participants and that the state took measures against them. F003 adds the political character of the assemblies, which is relevant to why the interference failed the necessity test (political assemblies attract heightened protection). F001 and F004 are not necessary for the violation conclusion itself.
