LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each doctrinal element

--- SCOPE ---
The core question was whether the assembly existed, whether applicants participated, and crucially whether it was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- Existence of assembly and participation: F007 (group entered courthouse corridor), F010-F016 (slogans chanted — establishing the nature of the assembly), F019 (banner hung), F020 (leaflets thrown)
- Peacefulness: F028 (protesters warned others no need to be afraid — key witness evidence on intent), F032 (witnesses reported no harm), F033 (incident report: no material damage), F059 (statements given without lawyer — undermining the evidence of violent intent), F071 (reconstruction without lawyer — same), F149 (G.I.'s testimony that protesters said they would not harm anyone)
- Disturbance acknowledged but insufficient: F017, F018 (door closed and barricaded — the most serious conduct), F029, F030 (witnesses locked themselves in), F034 (protest lasted about an hour)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
Strictly necessary: F034 (arrest), F114 (pre-trial detention), F156, F157 (conviction and sentence — first round), F180, F181 (conviction and sentence — second round, which is the operative judgment)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
Strictly necessary: F190 (text of Article 113 § 1, establishing the legal basis), F156 or F180 (conviction under that provision)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
Strictly necessary: F008 (courthouse as location of essential public service), F017, F018 (barricading the corridor — the conduct that disrupted the service), F029, F030 (witnesses confined), F144 (hearing discontinued), F148 (proceedings postponed for the day)

--- NECESSITY ---
Strictly necessary:
- Non-violent nature of conduct: F032, F033
- Severity of sentence (the decisive proportionality factor): F157/F181 (1 year 8 months imprisonment)
- Length of pre-trial detention: F114 (detention ordered 22 November 2003), F153 (most released 1 August 2005)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts — element not discussed.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Art. 11 violated)

The case turned on two pivotal findings: (1) the assembly was peaceful (no violent intent or conduct), which kept it within Art. 11's scope and limited the state's margin; and (2) the sanctions imposed — particularly the prison sentence and pre-trial detention — were grossly disproportionate to non-violent conduct. The irreducible facts are:

- F032: Witnesses reported they came to no harm — establishes peacefulness
- F033: Incident report noted no material damage — establishes peacefulness
- F028: Protesters warned others no need to be afraid — establishes absence of violent intent
- F017/F018: Door closed and barricaded — the most serious conduct, establishing the degree of disturbance that the Court accepted but found insufficient to negate peacefulness
- F034: Protest lasted about an hour before police arrested applicants — establishes the limited duration of disruption
- F157/F181: Each applicant sentenced to 1 year and 8 months' imprisonment — the central disproportionality finding
- F114: Pre-trial detention ordered on 22 November 2003
- F153: Most applicants released on 1 August 2005 — together with F114, establishes the very long pre-trial detention period
- F190: Text of Article 113 § 1 — establishes the legal basis (prescribed by law element)
- F156/F180: Conviction under Article 113 § 1 — establishes that the interference was the prosecution and conviction for the courthouse conduct specifically
