LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on three demonstrations, each resulting in a violation. The irreducible core facts are those that establish: (a) there were assemblies the applicants participated in; (b) the state interfered; and (c) the interference was disproportionate. The prescribed-by-law and legitimate aim elements were left undecided, so the case turned entirely on disproportionality.

For 30 August: The violation turned on the fact that the applicants had a valid authorisation, produced it when asked, were nonetheless arrested and taken to a police station, and as a result could not continue their demonstration because the authorisation time lapsed. The subject matter of the protest (against a public figure/ministry) was also critical to the tolerance analysis.
- F021 (valid authorisation existed)
- F033, F034, F035, F036 (arrests made)
- F038 (demonstration ended — authorisation time lapsed)
- F020 (protest against Ministry of Internal Affairs — public figure tolerance)

For 4 September: The violation turned on the arrests during the demonstration and the disproportionately high fines (80% of maximum, over twenty times the Ziliberberg fine), combined with the absence of special circumstances justifying an immediate unauthorised response.
- F052, F053 (arrests in course of demonstration)
- F067, F068 (fines of 800 MDL)
- F112 (maximum fine 1,000 MDL — establishes proportionality benchmark)
- F045 (protest against suppression of 30 August — no special urgency)

For 10 September: The violation turned on the fact that a valid authorisation existed, was produced and checked by police, yet arrests were still made, and the demonstration was peaceful.
- F073 (valid authorisation existed)
- F075, F076, F077 (arrests made despite authorisation)
- F074 (demonstration was taking place peacefully)

Cross-cutting fact relevant to all three (context of repeated arrests cited by Court for 10 September necessity analysis):
- F033, F034, F035, F036 (30 August arrests — also establish interference for that demonstration)
- F052, F053 (4 September arrests — also establish interference for that demonstration)

Final irreducible list (removing duplicates, keeping only those that actually turned the case):
F020, F021, F033, F034, F035, F036, F038, F045, F050, F052, F053, F067, F068, F073, F074, F075, F076, F077, F112
