LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of doctrine

--- SCOPE ---
The Court implies an assembly existed, that the applicant participated, and that it was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F003: applicant organised a demonstration (establishes assembly and participation)
- F006: demonstration took the form of a march followed by a press statement (establishes nature of assembly)
- F008: forty to fifty persons gathered brandishing placards and signs (establishes assembly exists, its size, and its non-violent character)
- F007: demonstration held to protest against plans for F-type prisons (establishes the expressive/political purpose, supporting peaceful character)
- F015 and F016: demonstrators refused orders and attempted to continue marching (the Court acknowledges this but still finds the assembly peaceful — no violence)

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
- F012: police asked the group to disperse (the order to end the assembly)
- F017: police dispersed the group using pepper spray (the physical act of dispersal)
- F018: police arrested thirty-nine demonstrators including the applicant (the arrest constitutes the clearest interference with the applicant's assembly right)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The strictly necessary fact is:
- F033: section 22 of Law no. 2911 prohibits demonstrations and processions on public streets, in parks, etc. (this is the specific legal basis the Court identifies)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
- F031: section 10 of Law no. 2911 required seventy-two hours prior notification (establishes the notification requirement that was not complied with, underpinning the disorder prevention aim)
- F014: police informed the group that the demonstration was likely to cause public-order problems at a busy time of day (establishes the factual basis for the aim of preventing disorder and protecting others' freedom of movement)

--- NECESSITY ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
- F008: forty to fifty persons gathered brandishing placards and signs at about 12 noon (establishes the small scale and non-violent character of the gathering, and the time)
- F007: demonstration held to protest against plans for F-type prisons (establishes the topical political purpose)
- F018: police arrested thirty-nine demonstrators (establishes the severity of the intervention)
- F020: applicant released at 12.45 p.m. (establishes the short duration of the entire event, supporting the finding of authorities' impatience)
- F005 and F011: applicant is a member of the administrative board, and Eren Keskin is President, of the Istanbul Human Rights Association (establishes the organised, legitimate civil society character of the demonstration, which the Court specifically notes)
- F014: police cited public-order problems at a busy time of day (the only specific justification for intervention — the Court finds this insufficient)
- F015: demonstrators refused to obey the police order to disperse (the Government's main factual justification, which the Court weighs but finds insufficient)

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary here, as the Court's positive obligation discussion is purely normative and not applied to the specific facts of the case.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity assessment. The Court found the intervention disproportionate because: (1) the group was small and non-violent; (2) the only concrete harm was possible traffic disruption; (3) the intervention was swift and forceful; (4) the demonstration was organised by a legitimate civil society organisation. The irreducible facts are:

- F003: applicant organised a demonstration (establishes the assembly right was engaged)
- F008: forty to fifty persons gathered brandishing placards and signs at about 12 noon (establishes the small, non-violent scale and timing)
- F007: demonstration held to protest against plans for F-type prisons (establishes the political/expressive purpose)
- F017: police dispersed the group using pepper spray (establishes the forceful nature of the intervention)
- F018: police arrested thirty-nine demonstrators including the applicant (establishes the severity of the interference)
- F020: applicant released at 12.45 p.m. (establishes the brevity of the entire event — the Court's finding of "impatience")
- F014: police cited public-order problems at a busy time of day (the only stated justification — found insufficient)
- F033: section 22 of Law no. 2911 (the legal basis for the interference — necessary to establish prescribed by law)
- F031: section 10 of Law no. 2911 required seventy-two hours prior notification (establishes the notification requirement, the basis for the unlawfulness finding and the legitimate aim)
- F011: Eren Keskin is the President of the Istanbul Human Rights Association (together with F005, establishes the organised civil society character the Court specifically highlights)
