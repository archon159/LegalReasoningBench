LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of law

--- SCOPE ---
The Court's scope analysis rests on: (1) the existence of a public event (F027); (2) the applicants' participation as ordinary demonstrators (F042, F043, F063); (3) the peaceful nature of the event (F031). The classification dispute (F036, F028) is necessary to understand the "single event" finding but is more directly relevant to prescribed by law. For scope, the core facts are F027 (assembly existed), F042 or F043 (participation — one suffices to show conviction as ordinary demonstrator), F031 (peaceful nature).

Most critical: F027, F031, F042

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The interference consists of the administrative convictions. The strictly necessary facts are: the dispersal orders (F038, F039), non-compliance (F034, F037), and the resulting convictions (F042, F043 for Mr Raymov; F063 for Mr Ryabenko). Since the Court treats the convictions as the interference, F042 and F063 are the most critical (one per applicant). F034 and F037 establish the factual basis for the convictions.

Most critical: F034, F037, F042, F063

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court's analysis here turns on: (1) the hybrid event possibility under the PEA (F073); (2) the fact that the event was treated as a single event by all parties (F027, F036, F044); (3) the separate prosecution in two sets of proceedings (F040, F049). The question left open is whether separate prosecution for what was reclassified as a separate event was foreseeable.

Most critical: F073, F036, F040

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
No atomic facts strictly necessary — the Court does not make factual findings on this element.

--- NECESSITY ---
The necessity analysis turns on: (1) the peaceful and non-disruptive nature of the event (F031); (2) the sole basis for dispersal orders being lack of approval (F038, F039, F027); (3) the domestic courts' failure to balance — evidenced by their reasoning (F048, F068, F069, F070, F071 — the last three being the domestic courts' findings that the ECtHR implicitly rejects as insufficient); (4) the convictions themselves as the measure (F042, F043, F063).

Most critical: F027, F031, F038, F039, F042, F043, F048, F063, F068

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. No atomic facts.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis. The Court found a violation because: (a) the event was peaceful and caused no real disruption; (b) the sole basis for the dispersal orders and subsequent convictions was the lack of official approval; (c) the domestic courts failed to conduct any balancing exercise. The irreducible facts are:

- F027: establishes that the event was non-approved — the only formal basis for intervention
- F031: establishes the peaceful and non-disruptive nature of the event (walking on pavements to avoid traffic disruption)
- F038: establishes that the dispersal order at Respublika Square was based solely on the unlawful nature of the presence
- F039: establishes that the dispersal order at Chapayev Square was based solely on the unlawful nature of the presence
- F042: establishes the conviction of Mr Raymov (the interference)
- F063: establishes the conviction of Mr Ryabenko (the interference)
- F048: establishes that the domestic appeal court justified Mr Raymov's conviction solely by reference to non-compliance with dispersal orders under section 6 § 3 of the PEA — no substantive balancing
- F068: establishes that the domestic appeal court justified Mr Ryabenko's conviction solely by reference to violation of section 6 § 3 of the PEA — no substantive balancing
