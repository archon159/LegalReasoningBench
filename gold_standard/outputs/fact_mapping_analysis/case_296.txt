LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: the applicant (a minor) participated in a demonstration that was peaceful in its origins and intent; he was convicted of PKK membership and propaganda dissemination without any adequate reasoning by the domestic courts; the penalties imposed were grossly disproportionate given his age and the nature of his conduct; and even his conviction for stone-throwing (the one act of violence established on the evidence) attracted a disproportionate sentence. The deterrent effect of the suspended sentence further compounded the interference.

Irreducible core:
- F001 (applicant was born in 1992 — a minor at the time)
- F003 (demonstration took place on 14 July 2008)
- F004 (demonstration was a political protest, not inherently violent)
- F048 (applicant joined and chanted slogans)
- F049 (applicant threw stones at police)
- F053 (applicant had no particular violent purpose when joining)
- F054 (applicant denied connection to any illegal organisation)
- F068, F069 (applicant's consistent statements — no propaganda admission)
- F088 (assize court's unsupported claim that applicant admitted propaganda)
- F089 (assize court convicted of PKK membership without reasoning)
- F093 (conviction based solely on participation following general PKK call)
- F094, F095, F096 (PKK membership sentence — four years two months)
- F097, F098, F099 (propaganda sentence — six months twenty days)
- F103, F104 (resistance sentence — two years nine months ten days)
- F092 (total sentence seven years six months)
- F037, F059 (arrest and remand without consideration of alternatives)
- F113 (released only after over two years' deprivation of liberty)
- F126 (juvenile court also failed to explain propaganda conviction)
- F129 (suspended sentence with deterrent condition on future conduct)
