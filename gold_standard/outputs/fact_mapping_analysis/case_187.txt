LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each element of law

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish: (i) an assembly existed, (ii) the applicant participated, (iii) it was peaceful.
- F003: establishes both the existence of the assembly (press conference/demonstration) and the applicant's participation.
- F005: corroborates the existence of a group assembly.
- F044: establishes the nature of the assembly (press statement, intent to disperse peacefully).
- F047: establishes that the assembly did not go beyond the exercise of democratic rights (peaceful character).
- F048: establishes that the actions were an exercise of the right to freedom of assembly (peaceful character).

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's freedom of assembly.
- F004: the applicant was arrested — this is the core act of interference.
- F045: police kept demonstrators in a circle — part of the interference.
- F046: police arrested demonstrators without proper warning — part of the interference.

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court needs to establish a legal basis for the interference.
- F049: points to the relevant domestic law (Meetings and Demonstration Marches Act) as applicable law.
- F038: charges were brought under the Meetings and Demonstration Marches Act, confirming the Act was applied.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts the Government's characterisation without independent factual analysis. The closest factual anchors are:
- F027: factual basis for the disorder-prevention aim (demonstrators allegedly gathered illegally and disturbed public order).

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity analysis turns on: the demonstrators' lawful conduct, the peaceful nature of the assembly, and the disproportionate manner of police intervention.
- F040: acquittal establishes that the demonstrators did not breach the law.
- F047: no evidence of unlawful conduct beyond exercise of democratic rights.
- F048: actions were merely an exercise of the right to freedom of assembly.
- F045: police kept demonstrators in a circle (manner of intervention).
- F046: police arrested without proper warning (manner of intervention, disproportionality).

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary here, as the Court's reference is purely doctrinal with no case-specific factual application.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two pivotal factual clusters: (1) the demonstrators' conduct was lawful and peaceful, and (2) the police intervened forcefully without proper warning. These are the facts without which the Court could not have concluded that the interference was disproportionate and unnecessary.

- F003: establishes the applicant's attempt to participate in a peaceful press conference/demonstration — the foundation of the Article 11 claim.
- F004: the applicant was arrested — the core act of interference that triggers the entire analysis.
- F040: the Beyoğlu Assize Court acquitted the applicant — the domestic judicial finding that the demonstrators acted lawfully, which the ECtHR adopted as its factual basis.
- F047: no evidence that the accused committed the alleged offence or went beyond the exercise of democratic rights — directly establishes the lawfulness of the assembly and the absence of justification for intervention.
- F048: the actions of the accused were merely an exercise of the right to freedom of assembly — directly establishes the peaceful and legitimate character of the assembly.
- F046: police arrested demonstrators without giving a proper warning — establishes the disproportionate manner of the intervention, which is the decisive factor in the necessity analysis.
