LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that there was a planned assembly, that the applicant was its organiser, and that it was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F003: establishes the assembly and the applicant's role as organiser
- F012: establishes the anticipated number of participants (used directly in the reasoning)
The peaceful nature is a negative finding with no direct atomic fact support; the absence of any fact asserting violence is what matters.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicant's assembly rights. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F014: the ban was issued
- F019: the demonstration did not take place as a result

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court needs to establish that the restriction had a legal basis. The strictly necessary fact is:
- F015: the ban was issued under section 8(1) of Act no. III of 1989
(F011 is supporting context but F015 is the operative legal basis for the restriction.)

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
The Court accepts the aim without detailed factual analysis. The only fact that grounds the nature of the aim is:
- F017: the stated reason for the ban was traffic impediment (protecting rights of others / preventing disorder)

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity analysis turns on several specific factual points:
- F012: 200 anticipated participants (scale of the event)
- F006/F007: width of Venyige Street (five or eight metres) — the Court uses both figures
- F008: existence of the service lane adjacent to the main driveway
- F027: the expert opinion characterised Venyige Street as a dead end (the Court's key overlooked fact)
- F030: the expert opinion's speculation about Maglódi Road (which the Court dismisses as speculative)
- F025: the domestic court relied exclusively on traffic grounds

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed; no atomic facts.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis — specifically, whether the traffic-based ban was proportionate. The irreducible facts are:

- F014: the ban was issued (establishes the interference that is the subject of the violation finding)
- F019: the demonstration did not take place (confirms the ban had real effect on the applicant's rights)
- F015: the ban was issued under Act no. III of 1989 (establishes legal basis, which was not in dispute)
- F012: 200 anticipated participants (the Court uses this to assess whether the traffic concern was proportionate)
- F027: Venyige Street is a dead end (the Court's pivotal factual point — the authorities failed to account for this)
- F008: the service lane adjacent to the main driveway (the Court uses this to question whether accommodation was impossible)
- F030: speculation about Maglódi Road (the Court dismisses this as speculative, undermining the ban's justification)
- F025: the domestic court upheld the ban on traffic grounds alone (the Court finds this basis insufficient)
