LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the prescribed-by-law requirement. The Court found a violation because the interference with the assembly had no adequate legal basis. The irreducible facts are:

1. There was an assembly: F013 (meeting took place), F017 (twelve participants).
2. The assembly was peaceful: F019 (participants having tea and cake — no disturbance).
3. The authorities intervened and brought the meeting to a premature end: F018 (fifteen police officers entered), F028 (officials demanded participants leave citing non-notification), F031 (participants left).
4. The legal grounds invoked on the spot (non-notification of a public event) were subsequently found inapplicable: F058 (supervisory review found section 7 of Public Events Act exempted the gathering), F086 (section 7 exempts gatherings from notification requirement).
5. The only alternative legal basis (municipal instruction on school notifications for anti-terrorism purposes) was referenced only in civil proceedings and not relied upon by the Government before the ECtHR: F074.

These facts are strictly necessary: without F013/F017 there is no assembly; without F019 the assembly might fall outside Article 11 protection; without F018/F028/F031 there is no interference; without F058/F086 the original legal basis might have stood; without F074 there would be no alternative legal basis to consider and reject.
