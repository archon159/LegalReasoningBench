LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two findings: (1) the State violated its positive obligations by failing to protect the assembly from counter-demonstrators; (2) the State violated its negative obligations by arresting the applicant without lawful basis. The irreducible facts are:

- F016: No prior arrangements were made — establishes the absence of any preliminary protective measures, which is central to the positive obligation finding.
- F018: No crowd barriers installed — concrete evidence of failure to facilitate.
- F019: No perimeter fence erected — concrete evidence of failure to secure the venue.
- F021: Participants unable to gather because the place was blocked by counter-demonstrators — the direct consequence of police inaction, establishing that the assembly could not take place.
- F023: Counter-demonstrators insulted participants — establishes the threat the police failed to address.
- F024: Counter-demonstrators pushed and punched participants — establishes the physical threat the police failed to address.
- F026: Police did not react to requests for help — establishes the passivity of the police in the face of the threat.
- F027: Police stepped in only when counter-demonstrators insulted police officers — establishes the discriminatory/selective nature of police inaction.
- F030: Police surrounded and arrested group including applicant — the act constituting the negative obligation interference.
- F045: Administrative proceedings terminated for lack of evidence of guilt — establishes that the arrest lacked lawful basis, grounding both the prescribed-by-law finding and the non-violent nature of the applicant's conduct.
- F117: History of public hostility towards LGBTI people in Russia — establishes the vulnerability context that made the positive obligation of paramount importance.
- F118: Homophobic counter-demonstrators had violently disrupted many public events — establishes the foreseeable risk the authorities failed to address.
