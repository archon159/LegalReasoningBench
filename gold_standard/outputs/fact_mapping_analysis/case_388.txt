LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court needs at minimum: (1) that there were assemblies and the applicants participated in them (scope), and (2) that the state took measures against them in that context (interference/necessity). Both of these are grounded solely in F002. F001 is a procedural reference that points to the appended table for details but contains no substantive facts capable of independently supporting any element of the legal analysis. F003 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, F002 is the sole atomic fact strictly necessary for the Court's final conclusion.
