LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court implies an assembly existed, that the applicants participated, and that it was peaceful. The strictly necessary atomic facts are:
- F024: applicants began demonstration in Svoboda Square — establishes assembly and participation
- F025: about one hundred people participated — establishes assembly
- F026: demonstrators put up tents and remained day and night — establishes nature and continuity of assembly
- F027: demonstrators displayed placards and banners — establishes peaceful, expressive character
- F044: after dismantling, protesters re-erected tents and continued — confirms ongoing peaceful assembly

The Court identifies four acts of interference. The strictly necessary atomic facts are:
- F022: refusal to approve Svoboda Square as venue

- F042: police ordered protesters to disperse
- F043: police dismantled tents

The Court treats this as uncontested, identifying prevention of disorder and protection of rights of others. No atomic facts from the circumstances are strictly necessary — the Court does not adopt any specific facts to establish the aim.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (no violation of Article 11)

The Court's conclusion of no violation rests on the finding that the interference, while real, was proportionate because the authorities showed sufficient tolerance: the demonstration lasted long enough for the applicants to express their protest, the police did not use force, and the protesters were able to continue. The irreducible atomic facts are:

- F024: The demonstration began on 30 September 2003 in Svoboda Square — establishes the starting point of the relevant period and that the demonstration proceeded despite the refusal.
- F026: The demonstrators put up tents and remained day and night — establishes the sustained, continuous nature of the protest that the authorities tolerated.
- F027: The demonstrators displayed placards and banners — establishes that the expressive purpose of the assembly was fulfilled.
- F032: The Leninskiy District Court declared the demonstration unlawful — establishes the legal context against which the authorities' tolerance is measured.
- F042: The police ordered the protesters to disperse — establishes that an interference occurred during the assembly.
- F043: The police dismantled the tents — establishes the concrete act of interference.
- F044: After the police dismantled the tents, the protesters put them back up and continued — establishes that the police did not prevent continuation, i.e., the tolerance finding.
- F048: The demonstration ended in December 2003 — establishes the over-two-month duration, which is the linchpin of the Court's proportionality conclusion that the applicants had sufficient time to express their protest.
