LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

**Scope (assembly exists, participation, peaceful character)**
The Court's own language: "the dispersal of the public assembly on 16 October 2006" — this points to:
- F004: The planned picket was to be held on 16 October 2006 near the monument to the heroes of the Second World War on Bazorkina Avenue in Nazran.
- F011: On 16 October 2006, before 4 p.m., the applicants arrived at the site of the planned picket.
- F013: On 16 October 2006, Mr M. and others had also gathered at the site of the planned picket with the view to participating in it.
- F015: On 16 October 2006, the police and the men in civilian clothes ordered the picketers, including the applicants, to disperse.

The implied finding of participation is grounded in:
- F011: The applicants arrived at the site of the planned picket.
- F017: The third, fourth and fifth applicants were apprehended on their way to their car after leaving the site.
- F018: The sixth applicant was arrested at the site of the planned picket.

The peaceful character is implied (no factual grounding explicitly stated by the Court), but the following facts are consistent with it:
- F005: The purpose of the picket was to commemorate journalist Anna Politkovskaya.
- F016: When the applicants realised it would be impossible to proceed, they decided to leave the site.

**Interference**
The Court's own language: "the dispersal of the public assembly on 16 October 2006 and the arrest of the third, fourth, fifth and sixth applicants" — this points to:
- F015: The police ordered the picketers to disperse.
- F017: The third, fourth and fifth applicants were apprehended on their way to their car.
- F018: The sixth applicant was arrested at the site.
- F019: The third, fourth, fifth and sixth applicants were transferred to the Nazran police department.
- F029: They were released between 12.30 a.m. and 1.30 a.m. on 17 October 2006.

Also relevant to the ban (which preceded the dispersal):
- F007: On 15 October 2006, the director of Mashr received a handwritten letter from the Head of Nazran City Administration banning the planned picket.
- F008: The ban was on the grounds that Mashr, being a non-commercial organisation, had no right to organise it.

**Prescribed by law**
Not discussed. No factual grounding identified.

**Legitimate aim**
Not discussed. No factual grounding identified.

**Necessity**
The Court's language: "the dispersal of the public assembly on 16 October 2006 and the arrest of the third, fourth, fifth and sixth applicants were not 'necessary in a democratic society'" — same core facts as interference:
- F015: Police ordered dispersal.
- F017: Third, fourth and fifth applicants apprehended.
- F018: Sixth applicant arrested.
- F019: Transferred to Nazran police department.
- F029: Released between 12.30 a.m. and 1.30 a.m.
- F007: Ban issued on 15 October 2006.
- F008: Grounds for ban (non-commercial organisation).
- F031: Justice of the Peace discontinued administrative proceedings on the ground that no offence had taken place — this is particularly relevant to necessity as it undermines the justification for the arrests.

The Court also grounds its necessity conclusion procedurally by reference to leading cases (Frumkin, Navalnyy and Yashin, Kasparov), but these are legal rather than factual grounding.

**Positive obligation**
Not discussed. No factual grounding identified.

LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish that there was a public assembly, that the applicants participated (or attempted to), and that it was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F004: The planned picket was to be held on 16 October 2006 (establishes the assembly event).
- F011: The applicants arrived at the site (establishes participation/attempt).
- F013: Mr M. and others gathered at the site with a view to participating (corroborates assembly).
- F016: When the applicants realised it would be impossible to proceed, they decided to leave (consistent with peaceful character — no resistance or violence by participants).

**Interference**
The strictly necessary facts establishing that a governmental act interfered with the assembly are:
- F007: The Head of Nazran City Administration issued a ban on the picket on 15 October 2006.
- F015: The police ordered the picketers to disperse on 16 October 2006.
- F017: The third, fourth and fifth applicants were apprehended after leaving the site.
- F018: The sixth applicant was arrested at the site.

**Prescribed by law**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary for this element in the Court's reasoning.

**Legitimate aim**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary for this element in the Court's reasoning.

**Necessity**
The strictly necessary facts for the necessity assessment (that the interference was not necessary in a democratic society) are:
- F008: The ban was issued on the grounds that Mashr, as a non-commercial organisation, had no right to organise the picket — this reveals the weakness of the justification for the ban.
- F015: The police ordered dispersal.
- F017: The third, fourth and fifth applicants were apprehended.
- F018: The sixth applicant was arrested.
- F031: The Justice of the Peace discontinued administrative proceedings on the ground that no offence had taken place — this is the most critical fact undermining the necessity of the arrests, as it establishes that the domestic authorities themselves found no legal basis for the action taken.

**Positive obligation**
Not discussed. No atomic facts strictly necessary.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: there was a planned peaceful assembly; it was banned on a legally questionable ground; it was dispersed by police; participants were arrested and detained; and the domestic courts ultimately found no offence had been committed. The irreducible core is:

- F004: The planned picket was to be held on 16 October 2006 (establishes the assembly).
- F007: The Head of Nazran City Administration banned the picket on 15 October 2006.
- F008: The ban was on the grounds that Mashr, as a non-commercial organisation, had no right to organise it (reveals the legal basis and its weakness).
- F015: The police ordered the picketers to disperse on 16 October 2006 (the core interference with the assembly).
- F017: The third, fourth and fifth applicants were apprehended after leaving the site.
- F018: The sixth applicant was arrested at the site.
- F031: The Justice of the Peace discontinued administrative proceedings on the ground that no offence had taken place (the critical fact undermining the necessity and lawfulness of the interference).
