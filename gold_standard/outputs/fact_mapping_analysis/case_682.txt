The factual grounding quote was: "the applicants complained principally of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies, namely the dispersal of these assemblies, as well as their arrest followed by their conviction for administrative offence."

- F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies." — this is the closest atomic fact, capturing both the participation element and the assembly element.

**Interference (explicit)**

This maps to:
- F002: same reasoning as above — the complaint of disproportionate measures is the factual basis for the interference finding.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on the finding that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion, the Court needed at minimum: (1) that the applicants were participants in public assemblies (scope/interference), and (2) that governmental measures were taken against them in that context (interference/necessity). Both of these are captured exclusively in F002. F001 establishes that there are applicants but adds no substantive factual content relevant to the Article 11 analysis. F003 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.

The irreducible factual core is therefore: F002.
