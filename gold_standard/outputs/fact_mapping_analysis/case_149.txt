LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 was violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. For this conclusion, the Court needed to establish: (1) that there was an assembly in which the applicants participated (scope), and (2) that governmental measures were taken against them in connection with that assembly (interference), and (3) that those measures were disproportionate (necessity, resolved by reference to prior case law on similar Russian cases).

The irreducible factual core is therefore F002 and F003. F002 establishes both the existence of the protest and the measures taken against the applicants as participants — it is the single most critical fact. F003 establishes the specific context (protest against Russian military attack on Ukraine, 27 February 2022, Moscow), which situates the case within the pattern of similar Russian cases referenced in the necessity analysis. F001 merely identifies the applicants and is not a substantive fact driving the legal conclusion. F004 is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
