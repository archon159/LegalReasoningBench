LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needed to establish: (i) there was an assembly; (ii) the applicant participated; (iii) it was peaceful.

For the existence of the assembly: F017, F018, F019, F020 — these establish that large-scale protest rallies took place at Freedom Square over multiple days.
For the applicant's participation: F021, F023, F024 — these establish that the applicant, as an opposition MP, regularly attended and gave speeches at the demonstrations.
For the peaceful character: F028, F033 — the Court's finding that the assembly was peaceful is grounded in its rejection of the Government's weapons claim (F033) and its assessment of the police operation (F028) as aimed at dispersal rather than weapons inspection.

Core facts: F017, F018, F019, F020, F021, F023, F024, F028, F033

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needed to establish that the applicant's criminal conviction constituted an interference with his Article 11 rights.

Core facts: F023, F024 (participation in assembly), F106 (conviction under Article 225 § 1), F108 (sentence of eight years)

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: the applicant was an opposition MP who participated in a peaceful assembly (post-election protests at Freedom Square), was convicted and sentenced to eight years in prison for organising mass disorder in connection with that assembly, but the domestic courts failed to provide specific and sufficient evidence of any reprehensible acts — incitement to or instigation of violence — attributable to him personally. The Court found the assembly was peaceful, the arms allegations were unsubstantiated, the findings about the applicant's conduct were vague and abstract, and the prosecution followed a pattern condemned internationally as politically motivated.

The irreducible core facts are:

F021 — applicant was an opposition MP (heightened protection, political speech)
F023 — applicant regularly attended the demonstrations (participation in assembly)
F024 — applicant gave speeches at the demonstrations (expressive conduct within assembly)
F017 — nationwide protest rallies were held (existence of assembly)
F018 — Freedom Square was the main meeting place (existence and location of assembly)
F106 — applicant convicted under Article 225 § 1 (the interference: criminal conviction)
F108 — applicant sentenced to eight years (severity of the interference)
F109 — domestic court's finding that applicant prepared crowd for violence and disobedience, stated in vague terms (insufficiency of domestic reasoning)
F028 — police operation at Freedom Square on 1 March 2008 (context of dispersal; basis for Court's finding that assembly was peaceful)
F033 — Government's weapons claim rejected by the Court (peaceful character of assembly)
F045 — demonstrators relocated spontaneously after ejection from Freedom Square (spontaneous nature of later events)
F049 — tensions escalated and clashes occurred (undisputed violence, but not linked to applicant)
F138 — PACE condemned arrests as politically motivated (pattern of prosecution context)
F139 — PACE condemned charges under Articles 225 and 300 as artificial and politically motivated (political motivation context)
