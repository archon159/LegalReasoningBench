LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The final conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion under the Article 11 doctrinal structure, the Court must establish: (1) that there were assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope); (2) that there was a governmental interference (interference); and (3) that this interference was not justified (necessity). All three of these steps rest on F002, which is the single atomic fact that establishes the existence of public assemblies, the applicants' participation, and the nature of the governmental measures taken against them. F002 is therefore the irreducible factual core. F001 is not strictly necessary for the legal conclusion — it merely identifies the applicants administratively. F003 concerns other complaints under other Convention provisions and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
