LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on: (1) the existence of a peaceful assembly that was notified to authorities; (2) the state's dispersal and the applicant's arrest and conviction for participating in it; (3) the absence of sufficient justification given the peaceful nature and prior notification; and (4) the deficient legislative framework. The irreducible facts are:

- F004: A demonstration was planned and took place on 2 April 2011 — the assembly existed.
- F006: Prior notification was given to the Baku City Executive Authority — the notification that authorities failed to consider.
- F008: The authority refused to authorise the demonstration at the indicated location — the refusal that triggered the "unauthorised" status.
- F010: The organisers held the demonstration notwithstanding the refusal — establishes the "unauthorised" character that led to dispersal and conviction.
- F011: The demonstrations were intended to be peaceful — accepted by the Court as established.
- F012: The demonstrations were conducted in a peaceful manner — accepted by the Court as established.
- F015: The applicant attended the demonstration on 2 April 2011 — establishes participation.
- F017: The police dispersed the demonstration on 2 April 2011 — the primary interference.
- F019: The applicant was arrested during the dispersal — the arrest as interference.
- F055: The applicant was convicted under Article 310.1 — the conviction as interference and the act whose necessity is assessed.
- F056: The applicant was sentenced to seven days' administrative detention — the sanction, establishing the severity of the interference.
- F070: Reference to Gafgaz Mammadov for the legislative framework — grounds the prescribed-by-law concerns that underpin the violation finding.
