LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on the finding that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The only atomic fact that is strictly necessary to reach this conclusion is F002 — it establishes that (a) public assemblies took place, (b) the applicants were participants, and (c) measures were taken against them in that context. Without F002, there would be no factual basis for any element of the Article 11 analysis. F001 is formally necessary to identify who the applicants are, but it contains no substantive facts that bear on the legal conclusion. F003 is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore, F002 is the sole atomic fact in the irreducible core.
