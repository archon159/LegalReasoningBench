LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on whether the measures taken against the applicants — as organisers and participants of public assemblies — constituted a disproportionate interference with their freedom of assembly. The two facts that are absolutely essential are F002 and F003: F002 establishes that some applicants were organisers of the protests and were subject to disproportionate measures; F003 establishes that others were participants subject to the same. Without these two facts, there is no assembly, no participation, no interference, and no necessity analysis. F004 (other complaints) is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. F001 is purely administrative. The conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests entirely on F002 and F003.
