LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion is that Article 11 has been violated because the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. The case turned entirely on: (1) the applicants being participants in public assemblies (establishing scope and triggering Article 11 protection), and (2) the State having taken measures against them in that capacity (establishing interference and grounding the necessity analysis by reference to the leading cases). Both of these points are captured exclusively in F002. F001 merely identifies the applicants and adds no substantive content relevant to the legal conclusion. F003 concerns Article 6 complaints which are irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.

The irreducible factual core is therefore: F002.
