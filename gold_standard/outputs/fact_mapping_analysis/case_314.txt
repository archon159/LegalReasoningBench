LIST 2: Strictly necessary atomic facts for each doctrinal element

--- SCOPE ---
The Court's implied finding that there was a peaceful assembly within the scope of Article 11 rests critically on:
-> F027 (Hyde Park applied to hold a peaceful demonstration — establishes that an assembly was planned and that the applicant sought to participate)
-> F028 (the demonstration was intended to protest against the non-transparent manner of organising Eurovision — establishes the nature and purpose of the assembly, supporting the peaceful character)

F049 is background law rather than a fact about the case; the Court does not cite it as a factual basis. The peaceful character is established by F027 ("peaceful demonstration") and confirmed by the absence of any public order concern, which the Court derives from F028.

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The strictly necessary fact is:
-> F029 (the Municipal Council rejected Hyde Park's application on 21 February 2006 — this is the act of interference)

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
-> F073 (section 12(6): the only lawful grounds for rejection are evidence that sections 6 and 7 will be breached with serious consequences — this is the legal standard against which the Municipality's conduct is measured)
-> F030 (ground given: Parliament not responsible for organising Eurovision — this is the actual reason given, which does not correspond to sections 6 and 7)
-> F032 (ground given: protest groundless because it concerned past events — second actual reason given, also not corresponding to sections 6 and 7)

F031 (Eurovision took place in Ukraine) is part of the same rejection decision but is subsumed within F030 and F032 as context; F030 and F032 are the operative grounds. F036-F038 (new reasons in court) are relevant to prescribed by law but the Court folds this into necessity; for the prescribed-by-law element specifically, the core mismatch is between F073 and F030/F032.

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary — the Court explicitly declined to decide this element.

--- NECESSITY ---
The strictly necessary facts are:
-> F029 (the rejection of the application — the interference whose necessity is assessed)
-> F030 (ground: Parliament not responsible — a reason going to the merits of the protest, not to sections 6 and 7)
-> F032 (ground: protest groundless — a reason going to the merits of the protest, not to sections 6 and 7)
-> F073 (section 12(6): only lawful grounds are sections 6 and 7 — establishes that the reasons given were outside the statutory framework)
-> F028 (intended to protest against the non-transparent manner of organising Eurovision — establishes the peaceful, political nature of the protest and the absence of any public order concern)
-> F042 (Court of Appeal gave a new reason: failure to correctly indicate the aim — the additional reason the Court explicitly disregards)
-> F073 also covers sections 11-12 (exclusive authority of local authorities), which is the basis for disregarding F042; no separate fact ID covers sections 11-12 exclusively, but F073 is the closest available fact about the statutory framework

Note: F074 (section 14(1): decision must be reasoned in writing) is referenced in the prescribed-by-law discussion but is not independently necessary for the necessity analysis. F039 and F040 (Court of Appeal dismissed the action and found the rejection lawful) provide context for F042 but F042 is the operative fact the Court addresses under necessity.

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
Not discussed. No atomic facts are strictly necessary.


LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: Hyde Park applied to hold a peaceful demonstration (F027, F028); the Municipal Council rejected the application (F029) on grounds — Parliament not responsible for Eurovision (F030) and the protest was groundless (F032) — that had nothing to do with the only lawful statutory grounds for rejection, namely evidence that sections 6 and 7 would be breached with serious consequences (F073); and the Court of Appeal added a new reason (F042) that the Court disregarded because only local authorities have the power to authorise assemblies under the Act (also grounded in F073). The absence of any public order concern is grounded in F028 (the peaceful, political nature of the protest). These facts are the irreducible core:

-> F027 (application to hold a peaceful demonstration — establishes scope, participation, and the act that triggered the interference)
-> F028 (intended to protest against the non-transparent manner of organising Eurovision — establishes peaceful character and absence of public order concern)
-> F029 (rejection of the application — the interference)
-> F030 (ground: Parliament not responsible — a merits-based reason incompatible with Article 11)
-> F032 (ground: protest groundless — a merits-based reason incompatible with Article 11)
-> F073 (section 12(6): only lawful grounds are sections 6 and 7 — establishes both the prescribed-by-law problem and the necessity failure)
-> F042 (Court of Appeal's new reason: failure to correctly indicate aim — the additional reason the Court explicitly disregards, necessary to explain why it did not save the violation finding)
