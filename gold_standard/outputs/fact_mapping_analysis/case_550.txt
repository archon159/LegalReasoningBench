LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

**Scope**
The Court needs to establish that there was an assembly, that the applicant participated, and that it was peaceful. The strictly necessary facts are:
- F007: establishes the assembly (30-35 persons gathered with candles and banners)
- F017: establishes the applicant's participation (he arrived to participate in the press release)
- F012 and F014: establish the peaceful nature (demonstrators dispersed after warnings, no violence)

**Interference**
The Court needs to establish that the police intervened and arrested the applicant:
- F016: police used force to arrest the applicant (core interference fact)
- F012: police warned demonstrators to disperse (part of the intervention)

**Prescribed by law**
The Court needs to establish a legal basis and the absence of prior notification:
- F055: the domestic prosecutor found the force used was in line with Article 24 of Law no. 2911 (legal basis)
- F004: official documents refer to an 'unauthorised demonstration' (basis for the Court's presumption about absence of notification)

**Legitimate aim**
No atomic facts strictly necessary — the Court makes no factual findings to ground this element.

**Necessity**
The Court's necessity analysis turns on: the small and peaceful nature of the group, the absence of danger to public order, the immediacy of dispersal, and the applicant's inability to manifest his views:
- F007: small group of 30-35 people with candles and banners (scale and nature of demonstration)
- F018: police intercepted the group immediately and told them they would not permit the demonstration (immediacy of intervention, adopted as undisputed)
- F004: official documents drafted at 9.15 p.m. (timing, used to establish promptness of dispersal)
- F012: police warned demonstrators many times to disperse (context of dispersal)
- F014: group started to disperse (demonstrators' compliance)

**Positive obligation**
Not discussed. No atomic facts.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity analysis — specifically whether the police intervention was proportionate given the nature of the demonstration and the speed of dispersal. The irreducible facts are:

- F007: The assembly consisted of only 30-35 people carrying candles and banners — establishing the small, non-threatening scale of the demonstration, which is central to the disproportionality finding.
- F018: The police immediately intercepted the group and told them they would not permit the demonstration — establishing the immediacy of the intervention, which the Court found meant the applicant had insufficient time to manifest his views. This was adopted as undisputed.
- F004: The official police documents were drafted at 9.15 p.m. — establishing the timeline from which the Court inferred the dispersal was "quite prompt" and the applicant had insufficient time to manifest his views.
- F012: The police warned the demonstrators many times to disperse — establishing the nature of the intervention.
- F014: The group started to disperse — establishing that the demonstrators were compliant and peaceful, reinforcing disproportionality.
- F016: The police used force to arrest the applicant — the core act constituting the interference whose proportionality is in question.
