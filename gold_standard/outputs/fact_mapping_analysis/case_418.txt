LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

Scope (assembly exists, participation, peaceful):
The factual grounding quote was: "the applicants complained principally of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers or participants of public assemblies, namely their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."
This maps to:
- F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers of public assemblies." — directly corresponds to the "organisers" part of the quote.
- F003: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of public assemblies." — directly corresponds to the "participants" part of the quote.
Note: F001 is background only (it refers to the appended table of applicant details) and does not correspond to the factual grounding quote. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant here.

Interference:
The factual grounding quote was: "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."

- F002 and F003 again, as these are the only atomic facts that describe the applicants as organisers or participants subject to measures — the specific acts (arrest, dispersal, conviction) are mentioned in the reasoning text but are not broken out as separate atomic facts in the provided list. Necessity:
As noted previously, the necessity conclusion is not grounded in any specific facts stated in the reasoning text — it rests entirely on prior case-law. The only atomic facts that could be said to underlie it are again F002 and F003, since the Court's reference to "the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly" presupposes that the applicants were organisers or participants subject to disproportionate measures. No atomic fact specifically addresses the proportionality analysis.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the finding that governmental measures taken against the applicants as organisers or participants of public assemblies were not necessary in a democratic society. The irreducible factual core is:
- F002 and F003: These establish that the applicants were organisers or participants of public assemblies who were subjected to disproportionate measures. Without these facts, there is no assembly, no participation, no interference, and no basis for the necessity analysis. These are the only atomic facts that are strictly necessary for the violation conclusion.

F001 (list of applicants) and F004 (other complaints) are not necessary for the Article 11 conclusion.
