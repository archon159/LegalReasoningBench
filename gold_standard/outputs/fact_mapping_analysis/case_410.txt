LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

(a) Scope — implied
To imply that scope is satisfied (assembly exists, participation, peaceful), the Court needs at minimum the fact that there were public events subject to restrictions. The most critical atomic facts are F002, F003, and F004, since together they establish that public events took place and that the applicants were subject to authority-imposed restrictions on them. F001 is background and not strictly necessary for the legal reasoning.

(b) Interference — explicit
The finding of interference rests on the existence of authority-imposed restrictions on the applicants' assemblies. The strictly necessary facts are F002, F003, and F004 — these directly establish that the authorities imposed restrictions on location, time, and manner. All three are necessary because the Court's own framing in the reasoning section uses all three categories.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on two findings: (1) that there were interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly, and (2) that those interferences either failed the "quality of law" test or the necessity test. The first finding — interference — is the only element that is actually grounded in the atomic facts provided. The second finding (prescribed by law / necessity) is delegated entirely to Lashmankin and is not independently grounded in the atomic facts here.

For the Court to reach the conclusion that Article 11 was violated, it needed at minimum to establish that there were public events and that the authorities imposed restrictions on them. This is what F002, F003, and F004 establish. Without these facts, there would be no interference to assess, and the violation conclusion could not be reached.

F001 (the appended table of applicants) is background administrative information and not strictly necessary for the legal conclusion itself.

F005 (other complaints under the Convention) is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.

The irreducible factual core is therefore: F002, F003, F004.
