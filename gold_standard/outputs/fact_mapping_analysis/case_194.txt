LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court found a violation of Article 11 on the basis that the interferences with the applicants' freedom of assembly were not necessary in a democratic society. To reach this conclusion, the Court needed at minimum: (i) that there were public assemblies (scope), (ii) that the applicants were organisers or participants (scope/interference), and (iii) that governmental measures were taken against them (interference/necessity). All three of these points are jointly captured by F002 and F003 — F002 for organisers, F003 for participants. These are the only atomic facts that are strictly necessary. F001 is purely procedural and F004 concerns unrelated complaints. Therefore the irreducible core is F002 and F003.
