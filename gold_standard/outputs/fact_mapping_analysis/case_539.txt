LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion

The Court's conclusion that Article 11 was violated rests on: (i) the existence of public assemblies in which the applicants participated (scope, implied); (ii) the fact that governmental measures — dispersal, arrest, and conviction — were taken against them (interference, implied); and (iii) the similarity of these facts to those in the leading cases, which drives the necessity conclusion. All three of these points are grounded exclusively in F002. F001 provides background identification but is not what the legal conclusion turns on. F003 is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion. Therefore the irreducible factual core is F002 alone.
