LIST 2: Atomic facts strictly necessary to support each element of the Court's reasoning

--- SCOPE ---
The Court needs to establish that an assembly took place, that the applicants participated, and that it was peaceful.
-> F011: establishes that an assembly took place (46 persons gathered) and that the applicants participated, and identifies the subject matter (protest against F-type prisons)
-> F015: establishes the nature of the assembly (marching, chanting slogans, reading a press declaration — no violence)
-> F023: the incident report refers only to refusal to obey warnings as the basis for force, not to any violence by demonstrators — supports the peaceful character of the assembly

--- INTERFERENCE ---
The Court needs to establish that a governmental act interfered with the applicants' freedom of assembly.
-> F016: police dispersed the group using truncheons and tear-gas — the core act of interference
-> F017: the applicants were arrested — a further act of interference
-> F037: a bill of indictment was filed — the subsequent prosecution as part of the interference
-> F040: acquittal delivered almost 5 years after the incident — relevant to the chilling effect and victim status analysis

--- PRESCRIBED BY LAW ---
The Court needs to identify the legal basis for the interference.
-> F047: section 22 of Law No. 2911 — the specific provision the Court identifies as the legal basis
-> F046: section 10 of Law No. 2911 — the notification requirement, which is the background legal context for the unlawfulness finding

--- LEGITIMATE AIM ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary here — the Court accepts the Government's characterisation without grounding it in specific facts. List is empty.

--- NECESSITY ---
The Court's necessity analysis turns on several key facts:
-> F008: intelligence reports gave authorities prior knowledge of the demonstration — critical to the Court's finding that preventive measures could have been taken
-> F009: police officers were deployed in advance — confirms prior knowledge and ability to take preventive measures
-> F010: Rapid Intervention Force was deployed in advance — same point
-> F011: 46 persons gathered to make a press declaration about F-type prisons — establishes the small scale and the subject matter (topical issue)
-> F014: demonstrators refused to obey the police order to disperse — the Court acknowledges this but finds it insufficient to justify the intervention
-> F015: demonstrators attempted to march, chanting slogans and reading a press declaration — establishes the non-violent nature of the conduct
-> F016: police dispersed the group within half an hour using truncheons and tear-gas — establishes the speed and force of the intervention
-> F044: Article 34 of the Turkish Constitution guarantees the right to hold peaceful meetings without prior permission — contextualises the notification requirement
-> F046: section 10 required 72-hour notification but not authorisation — the Court distinguishes notification from authorisation

--- POSITIVE OBLIGATION ---
No atomic facts are strictly necessary — the Court states this as a general principle only, without applying it to the facts of the case. List is empty.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the necessity/proportionality analysis. The Court found a violation because the forceful and rapid police intervention was disproportionate given: (a) the small, non-violent nature of the assembly; (b) the authorities' prior knowledge which meant they could have taken less intrusive preventive measures; (c) the speed of the intervention (within 30 minutes); and (d) the subsequent prosecution lasting nearly 5 years. The following atomic facts are strictly necessary:

-> F008: authorities had prior knowledge via intelligence reports — essential to the Court's finding that preventive measures could have been taken instead of forceful dispersal
-> F011: 46 persons gathered peacefully to make a press declaration about F-type prisons — establishes the small scale, peaceful purpose, and topical subject matter
-> F015: demonstrators marched, chanted slogans and read a press declaration — establishes the non-violent nature of the conduct
-> F016: police dispersed the group within approximately 30 minutes using truncheons and tear-gas — the core act whose proportionality is assessed
-> F017: the applicants were arrested — part of the interference
-> F037: a bill of indictment was filed against the applicants — the subsequent prosecution as part of the overall interference
-> F040: acquittal delivered on 19 September 2005, almost 5 years after the incident — essential to the chilling effect finding and victim status
-> F046: section 10 required notification but not authorisation — essential to the Court's point that the legal framework was less restrictive than the authorities' response implied
-> F047: section 22 of Law No. 2911 — the legal basis for the interference (prescribed by law element)
