LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the finding that the applicants — as organisers and participants of public assemblies — were subjected to disproportionate governmental measures (arrest, dispersal, conviction), and that these measures were not necessary in a democratic society, by analogy with prior leading cases. The irreducible factual core is:
- F002: establishes that organisers of public assemblies were subjected to disproportionate measures — essential for the scope, interference, and necessity findings.
- F003: establishes that participants of public assemblies were subjected to disproportionate measures — equally essential for the same reasons.

Without F002 and F003, the Court could not have established that there was an assembly, that the applicants participated, that there was an interference, or that the necessity test was failed. These are the only atomic facts that the final conclusion actually turns on, given the extremely compressed reasoning and the sparse factual record provided.
