LIST 1: Mapping factual grounding quotes to atomic facts

(a) Scope

The factual grounding quote was: "The applicants complained principally of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers or participants of public assemblies."

This maps to:
- F002: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as organisers of nationwide protests in support of the jailed opposition leader, Aleksey Navalnyy." — covers the 'organisers' dimension and the existence of assemblies (protests).
- F003: "The applicants complained of disproportionate measures taken against them as participants of nationwide protests in support of the jailed opposition leader, Aleksey Navalnyy." — covers the 'participants' dimension and the existence of assemblies (protests).

For the peaceful character sub-element, no factual grounding was identified in the reasoning text, and no atomic fact addresses this either.

(b) Interference

The factual grounding quote was: "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences."

This maps to:
- F002: The reference to "disproportionate measures" taken against organisers points to the same governmental acts (arrest, dispersal, conviction).
- F003: The reference to "disproportionate measures" taken against participants points to the same governmental acts.

Note: The atomic facts do not independently specify arrest, dispersal, or conviction — these details appear only in the reasoning section's characterisation of the complaints. F002 and F003 are the closest available atomic facts, as they describe the disproportionate measures complained of.

(c) Prescribed by law

No factual grounding was identified, and no atomic facts correspond to this element.

(d) Legitimate aim

No factual grounding was identified, and no atomic facts correspond to this element.

(e) Necessity in a democratic society

The factual grounding quotes were: "their arrest in relation to the dispersal of these assemblies and their conviction for administrative offences" and "Having examined all the material submitted to it, the Court has not found any fact or argument capable of persuading it to reach a different conclusion."

The same atomic facts apply as under interference:
- F002: Disproportionate measures against organisers.
- F003: Disproportionate measures against participants.

The second quote is a negative statement and does not point to any specific atomic fact.

LIST 3: Irreducible factual core for the final conclusion (Article 11 violated)

The case turned on the following: (i) there were public assemblies (protests); (ii) the applicants were organisers or participants; (iii) the state took measures against them (arrest, dispersal, conviction); and (iv) those measures were found not necessary in a democratic society. The atomic facts that are strictly necessary for this conclusion — and that the case actually turned on — are F002 and F003. These establish both the existence of the assemblies and the governmental measures taken, which are the indispensable factual predicates for the Article 11 violation finding. F001 is purely administrative. F004 concerns other complaints and is irrelevant to the Article 11 conclusion.
